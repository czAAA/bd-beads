# 319: Ruler numbers share the dots' line

**What to build:** Each Ruler number was pushed away from its bead line by half its own size, so a 1-digit row number (1 to 9) stood closer to the beads than a 2-digit one (10 and up), and than the Ruler dots, which stand on one line set by the widest number. Every number is now centred on that shared line, whatever its digits.
