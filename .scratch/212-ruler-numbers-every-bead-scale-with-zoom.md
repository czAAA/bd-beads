# 212: Ruler numbers every bead, scaling with zoom

**What to build:** The row and column rulers always number every bead from 1 to the last one, at every zoom level, instead of thinning the labels out with a step. The numbers scale with the Pattern when zoomed, so each stays beside its own bead row/column. Clicking a number still marks out that whole row/column as a Selection.
