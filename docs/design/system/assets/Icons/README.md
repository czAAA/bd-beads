# Icons

Icons v2: 90 single-ink SVGs on a 24-unit grid, stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, no fill, drawn in `ink` #1f1f1f because `<img>` cannot inherit colour; inline them in UI with `stroke: currentColor`. The bead (a zero-length stroke at 2.6) is the signature. Which icon goes where is in the brand book's Iconography section. Phase E added `device` (Match device) and `contrast` (High contrast) for the theme control; ticket 188 added `bead` (the app's own compact-header mark), `library` (Saved Patterns) and `paste` (the phone Edit sheet's Paste, no longer reusing import).

v15 added `menu` (three lines, the same 1.75 stroke, round caps and 4–20 span as `row-progress`): the HeaderMenu button next to the logo.
v15 also added `pattern` (a 12×17 board, radius 3.5, with two columns of three beads at stroke 2.6): the Dock's Pattern button, so it no longer reads as Save.
v16 added three for the open canvas: `frame` (four crossing lines, two upright and two level, like crop marks: the Frame row, every Set Frame button and the Dock's Frame button), `ruler` (a rounded bar with three ticks, the middle one longer: the Rulers toggle) and `hand` (an open hand, one stroke: the Hand tool). `size` no longer marks a Toolbox row; it stays for the phone header's bead and technique selector.
v16 also redrew `paint` as a pencil (a body, a rounded eraser end and a wood cone, one 1.75 stroke, no bead) so the Paint tool looks the way a drawing tool is expected to, and added `palette` (a round palette with a thumb notch and four bead dabs at stroke 2.6): the Colors button's own icon, so Colors and Paint no longer share one glyph.

Ticket 324 added `pen-mode` (a tablet, a stylus and three square bead pixels under the nib: Pen mode) and `pen-mode-off` (the same with a slash through it: Mouse mode), the faces of the input mode toggle. The pixels are stroke-only squares (2.4, square caps), so the no-fill rule holds.

Ticket 455 added `reset` (a circular arrow, one 1.75 stroke plus its head): Reset progress.

Ticket 0457 added `move-frame` (four arrowheads on short arms round a bead at 2.6): the Frame's Move handle.

`more` (three dots) is reserved: no card places it yet, so the app does not draw it. `pattern` is the Dock's Project button; `delete` is Clear.

Ticket 413 added five for the Selection work. `piece` (the Piece tool) is a polygon of five bead corners, drawn at 3.2 so they read as the points you tap, joined by dashed bead edges (2.4, `0 4.09`), like Select's. `select-all` is Select's dashed bead square with the word "all" inside, drawn in strokes at 1.5 (a circle for the a and two stems), so it needs no font; the letters are small, so check them at 14 to 16px. `cut` is scissors: two ring handles and two crossing blades. `fill-selection` is Select's dashed bead square with a drop inside, the Selection bar's Fill, distinct from `fill`, the tool's bucket. `share` is an arrow leaving a rounded square; no card places it yet. Frame (from Selection) reuses `frame` and Save keeps `save`.

One later addition: `name-piece` (a tag with a bead for its string hole, one closed stroke plus the bead at 2.6) is the Selection bar's Name button, so Name no longer shares `piece` with the Piece tool.

Ticket 0474 added `row-highlight` (a rounded bar between two rules, one row picked out): the Progress bar's Row highlight toggle.

Ticket 0553 added four for the panels redesign. `coffee` (a cup with a handle and two steam strokes): Menu's Buy me a coffee row, the one icon drawn in `accent-strong` there. `external` (a short diagonal arrow): the trailing mark on a row that leaves the app (Source code, Buy me a coffee). `cloud` (a cloud with a check inside) and `cloud-off` (the same cloud with a slash): the Project panel's cloud save state, beside `save` (the floppy disk) for the local one.

Ticket 0686 redrew `fill` (the Fill tool) because early users didn't read it as a bucket: the tilted bucket and its paint line keep their shape, a wire handle (one 1.75 stroke) runs from outside the top edge to the middle, and the bead beside it is now a teardrop drop, one closed 1.75 stroke, since a bead reads as a bead rather than as paint.

Spec 0702 (2026-10-30) added four for Mirror. `mirror` (the Tool pill's Mirror tile) is a bead split by its axis: the set's dotted line (2.4, `0 4.5`, as Select and Piece draw theirs) down the middle, with a half-bead, one closed 1.75 stroke, on either side; it replaces the retired `mirror-horizontal`, `mirror-vertical` and `mirror-copy-mode`. `axis-left-right`, `axis-top-bottom` and `axis-centre` were the open Canvas's Axis and Radial-centre handles; since ticket 0750 they are the Mirror bar's Axis chips, Left–right, Top–bottom and Radial, drawn at 16px (18px on touch) (ContextBar card). Redrawn on 2026-11-01 (claude.ai canvas "Mirror mode icons", round 2, variant 1) so each shows what you draw and what the Mirror makes of it: the part you draw is solid ink, the part the Mirror copies is the same ink at `stroke-opacity` .35. `axis-left-right` is a level line with a head at each end, its left half solid and its right half faint; `axis-top-bottom` is the same upright, the top half solid; `axis-centre` is an asterisk of six rays from the centre, the two upper rays (the slice you draw) solid and the other four faint. The faint part is opacity, not a second colour, so the icons stay single-ink and follow `currentColor` on and off the chip.

Ticket 0757 added two for the Convert image Size step's chain between Width and Height. `link` is two open capsules end to end, a short bar across their meeting (a rounded stadium split in two, the halves joined): Width and Height linked. `unlink` is the same two halves drawn short and apart, with no bar: unlinked. One 1.75 stroke each, no bead, so they read at 16px.

Ticket 0778 added `arrow-left` and `arrow-right`, `arrow-up` turned a quarter (the same 15-unit shaft and 6-unit head): Convert image's Back and Next on its Progress bar, which move along the steps sideways, so they point the way the steps go (ProgressBar card).

Ticket 0790 added `reference-image`: the `image` picture (the same 17×15 frame, radius 2.5) with its edge dashed (2.4 on, 2.6 off), a lower, shorter hill and the bead sun: the Canvas strip's Reference image toggle. The dash is the Select tool's way of saying "not beads"; the picture inside stays solid so it reads at 18px.

Ticket 0829 made `palette` the Tool pill's colour swatch: there it is drawn at 28px with its body filled with the chosen colour (the one place an icon carries a fill, and the fill is the user's colour, not ink). The tile fills the file's first path and recolours its last, so `palette.svg` keeps that order: the body first, the dabs last.

Ticket 0862 added two for Convert image. `flip` is the picture turned over left to right: the set's dotted axis (2.4, `0 4.5`, as `mirror` draws it) between two right triangles, their upright sides along it and their slopes facing out, one 1.75 stroke each: the floating bar's Flip, icon only. `camera` is a rounded body (17 × 12.5, radius 2.5) with the viewfinder's hump on top, a round lens and the bead as its flash: the No picture row's Take photo, which had no icon.

Ticket 0883 added `convert-to-beads`: a small picture (a 9 × 9 frame, radius 2, with the `image` hill) at the top left, a curved arrow turning down from it, and four beads in a square at the bottom right (stroke 3.2, as `grid` draws them). The reference image's picture bar and its Convert popover's primary button: "this picture becomes beads". Chosen from seven drawn options on the ticket 0883 design canvas, because the bead grid alone read as Grid or Pattern, not as an action.

Ticket 0900 added `clean-up`: two beads side by side (7 × 9 rounded rectangles, radius 2.5, at the bottom) and a small plus-shaped sparkle over the right one, which is the bead that was tidied. The Convert image page's Clean up button under Colors (DS-3): "stray beads were tidied into their neighbours' color". Drawn on the ticket 0900 design canvas.

Ticket 0948 added `eye-off`: an eye (one closed 1.75 stroke, pointed at both ends, 17 wide), a ring for its pupil and the set's slash from corner to corner, as `cloud-off` and `pen-mode-off` draw theirs. The coffee screen's **No ads or tracking**: nothing watches what you do. It replaces `close` there, which read as Close.

Ticket 1009 added `picture-over-beads`: a picture (a 13.5 × 13 frame, radius 2.5, with a hill and the bead sun at 2.6) laid over a square of four beads at its lower left (stroke 3.2, as `grid` draws them) in the same ink at `stroke-opacity` .35, the faint part being what lies under, as the Mirror's axis icons draw theirs; one bead sits inside the picture's corner. The picture bar's Picture over the beads toggle (ReferenceImage card).

Ticket 1082 added one drawing per bead Type for the Canvas strip's Rotate canvas, chosen by the owner on 2026-11-18 (style A, Thread, from the design canvas "Rotate canvas icon by bead shape"): `rotate-canvas-round`, `rotate-canvas-delica` and `rotate-canvas-cube`. Each keeps the `rotate` arc and puts in the square's place the bead as the Canvas draws it (its proportions and its corners) with a thread out of both ends of its hole, and the same thread at `stroke-opacity` .35 where it passes inside the bead. The bead and its thread are the file's `<g data-turns>` group: the app turns that group by the Canvas's orientation about the bead's own centre, (9, 15), over the base duration, a press always a quarter further clockwise (from 270° on to a whole turn, never unwinding), a jump with reduced motion; the arc never turns. Upright, the thread runs left to right, the way the Bead is laid. The icon goes by the woven layer's Form factor, never its size or Brand. `rotate-canvas-cube` is the one icon with mitred corners (`stroke-linejoin="miter"` on its outline), the one exception to round joins, so it reads as square beside the Delica. A new Form factor adds its drawing here as `rotate-canvas-<id>`, in this style: its outline as the Canvas draws it, one thread per hole, the way its holes run; a test fails while a Form factor in the catalog has none. A Bead whose Form factor has no drawing (an unknown Bead, a file from a newer version) keeps `rotate-canvas`, which stays in the set for that.

Ticket 1087 added two for signing in (approved 2026-11-18; ticket 0078 places them). `account` is a person: a round head (radius 3.75) over the arc of the shoulders, one 1.75 stroke each, no bead; it stands before the Menu's "Sign in" row and the signed-in email. `sign-out` is an open door frame (2.5 corners) with an arrow leaving it to the right: the Menu's "Sign out" row. Google's "G" on the Google button is not an icon of this set: it keeps Google's own four colors and lives with the button (SignIn card).
