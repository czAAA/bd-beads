# Icons

Icons v2: 84 SVGs on a 24-unit grid, single-ink but for the AI mark (below), stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, no fill, drawn in `ink` #1f1f1f because `<img>` cannot inherit colour; inline them in UI with `stroke: currentColor`. The bead (a zero-length stroke at 2.6) is the signature. Which icon goes where is in the brand book's Iconography section. Phase E added `device` (Match device) and `contrast` (High contrast) for the theme control; ticket 188 added `bead` (the app's own compact-header mark), `library` (Saved Patterns) and `paste` (the phone Edit sheet's Paste, no longer reusing import).

v15 added `menu` (three lines, the same 1.75 stroke, round caps and 4–20 span as `row-progress`): the HeaderMenu button next to the logo.
v15 also added `pattern` (a 12×17 board, radius 3.5, with two columns of three beads at stroke 2.6): the Dock's Pattern button, so it no longer reads as Save.
v16 added three for the open canvas: `frame` (four crossing lines, two upright and two level, like crop marks: the Frame row, every Set Frame button and the Dock's Frame button), `ruler` (a rounded bar with three ticks, the middle one longer: the Rulers toggle) and `hand` (an open hand, one stroke: the Hand tool). `size` no longer marks a Toolbox row; it stays for the phone header's bead and technique selector.
v16 also redrew `paint` as a pencil (a body, a rounded eraser end and a wood cone, one 1.75 stroke, no bead) so the Paint tool looks the way a drawing tool is expected to, and added `palette` (a round palette with a thumb notch and four bead dabs at stroke 2.6): the Colors button's own icon, so Colors and Paint no longer share one glyph.

Ticket 324 added `pen-mode` (a tablet, a stylus and three square bead pixels under the nib: Pen mode) and `pen-mode-off` (the same with a slash through it: Mouse mode), the faces of the input mode toggle. The pixels are stroke-only squares (2.4, square caps), so the no-fill rule holds.

Ticket 455 added `reset` (a circular arrow, one 1.75 stroke plus its head): Reset progress.

Ticket 0457 added `move-frame` (four arrowheads on short arms round a bead at 2.6): the Frame's Move handle.

`more` (three dots) is reserved: no card places it yet, so the app does not draw it. `pattern` is the Dock's Project button; `delete` is Clear.

Ticket 413 added five for the Selection work. `piece` (the Piece tool) is a polygon of five bead corners, drawn at 3.2 so they read as the points you tap, joined by dashed bead edges (2.4, `0 4.09`), like Select's. `select-all` is Select's dashed bead square with the word "all" inside, drawn in strokes at 1.5 (a circle for the a and two stems), so it needs no font; the letters are small, so check them at 14 to 16px. `cut` is scissors: two ring handles and two crossing blades. `fill-selection` is Select's dashed bead square with a drop inside, the Selection bar's Fill, distinct from `fill`, the tool's bucket. `share` is an arrow leaving a rounded square; no card places it yet. Frame (from Selection) reuses `frame` and Save keeps `save`.

One later addition: `name-piece` (a tag with a bead for its string hole, one closed stroke plus the bead at 2.6) is the Selection bar's Name button, so Name no longer shares `piece` with the Piece tool.

Ticket 0474 added `row-highlight` (a rounded bar between two rules, one row picked out): the Progress bar's Row highlight toggle.

Ticket 0553 added four for the panels redesign. `coffee` (a cup with a handle and two steam strokes): Menu's Buy me a coffee row, the one icon drawn in `accent-strong` there. `external` (a short diagonal arrow): the trailing mark on a row that leaves the app (Source code, Buy me a coffee). `cloud` (a cloud with a check inside) and `cloud-off` (the same cloud with a slash): the Project panel's cloud save state, beside `save` (the floppy disk) for the local one.

Ticket 0686 redrew `fill` (the Fill tool) because early users didn't read it as a bucket: the tilted bucket and its paint line keep their shape, a wire handle (one 1.75 stroke) runs from outside the top edge to the middle, and the bead beside it is now a teardrop drop, one closed 1.75 stroke, since a bead reads as a bead rather than as paint.

Spec 0702 (2026-10-30) added four for Mirror. `mirror` (the Tool pill's Mirror tile) is a bead split by its axis: the set's dotted line (2.4, `0 4.5`, as Select and Piece draw theirs) down the middle, with a half-bead, one closed 1.75 stroke, on either side; it replaces the retired `mirror-horizontal`, `mirror-vertical` and `mirror-copy-mode`. `axis-left-right`, `axis-top-bottom` and `axis-centre` were the open Canvas's Axis and Radial-centre handles; since ticket 0750 they are the Mirror bar's Axis chips, Left–right, Top–bottom and Radial, drawn at 16px (18px on touch) (ContextBar card). Redrawn on 2026-11-01 (claude.ai canvas "Mirror mode icons", round 2, variant 1) so each shows what you draw and what the Mirror makes of it: the part you draw is solid ink, the part the Mirror copies is the same ink at `stroke-opacity` .35. `axis-left-right` is a level line with a head at each end, its left half solid and its right half faint; `axis-top-bottom` is the same upright, the top half solid; `axis-centre` is an asterisk of six rays from the centre, the two upper rays (the slice you draw) solid and the other four faint. The faint part is opacity, not a second colour, so the icons stay single-ink and follow `currentColor` on and off the chip.

Ticket 0757 added two for the Convert image Size step's chain between Width and Height. `link` is two open capsules end to end, a short bar across their meeting (a rounded stadium split in two, the halves joined): Width and Height linked. `unlink` is the same two halves drawn short and apart, with no bar: unlinked. One 1.75 stroke each, no bead, so they read at 16px.

Ticket 0764 (spec 0761, ADR 0087) added six for Convert image's Cut out, drawn from the grilling session's proposal sheet of 2026-11-01 (marker A, colored). `cut-out` is a subject, a head (a circle, r 2.75) and shoulders (one arc), inside Select's dashed bead frame (2.4, `0 4`) at `stroke-opacity` .35. `tap-add` and `tap-remove` are a tapped bead (a circle, r 3) inside a faint ripple (a three-quarter arc at .35, open towards the sign) with a + or − at the bottom right. Each has an **AI form** with the **AI mark**, `cut-out-ai`, `tap-add-ai` and `tap-remove-ai`: a four-point sparkle centred on 17.5, 6.5 with arms of 3 and its sides curved in, one closed 1.75 stroke (which fills it at icon sizes), the one part of any icon in a second colour, `ai`. To make room the drawing moves down and left (the frame to 3.5, 4.5, the subject 1 left and 1 down; the bead to 9.5, 12.5, its ripple a shorter arc), and what would run under the mark is left open: the frame's top-right corner (three beads) and the ripple's top-right arc. In the file the mark is `<path class="ai-mark" stroke="#6e44ff">`, the light `ai`, so the file reads alone; AppIcon drops that stroke and draws the mark in the theme's `ai` (README, Iconography). Ticket 0765 gave plain `cut-out` its first place: Fit to subject's button, the part round the subject.
