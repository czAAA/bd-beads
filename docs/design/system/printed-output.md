# Printed output

The PDF and PNG exports of a framed Schema: the chart is the main thing on every page, every bead countable, with the facts and the beads to buy set clearly around it. Signed off in the design session of 2026-10-26 (ticket 0575, "Gallery").

## Look and feel

- **The chart comes first.** Page 1 shows the whole piece across the full width of the sheet, as the working chart when it fits there and as the overview of its parts when it does not; every chart page shows one part with every row and column numbered. Everything else is small and sits around the board, so the page works at the craft table.
- **The canvas, on paper.** On the overview the Schema sits on a pale rounded board (`print-board`) like the app's canvas. A chart, on page 1 or a chart page, has no board fill, only a thin rounded rim 1 mm clear of its beads (`print-pad-line`, `print-pad-radius`, `print-rim-gap`; ticket 1126), so only beads take toner. The technique word is set in Instrument Serif italic in `accent` next to the title (the Frame's name, else the Project's).
- **Open, with no watermarks.** Nothing is drawn behind or over the chart or the text: no background maker's name, no X1 marks, no accent line, no signature on the beads. The PDF and PNG are meant to be shared. The maker's name is still printed as information (Made by, the header, "by {maker}").
- **Accent marks structure only**: the technique word, the lines where the chart parts meet, the part labels and the current part on the mini map. Text is `ink` or `muted`. A black-and-white printer turns the accent gray and loses no information: every bead on a chart page carries its letter.
- **On every page**: the X1 mark and "openbeads" (small, in the header or footer, never as a background), technique and name, the maker's name, the export date and time, "page 2 / 9".
- Inter for text, DM Mono for numbers and meta, Instrument Serif italic for the technique word and the maker's name, loaded before drawing. A4 portrait, 10 mm margins (`print-margin`); nothing under 6.5 pt but a chart bead's Swatch letter, which scales with the bead, and a ruler number where the page's widest needs it (ticket 1129); Row progress fades never print.

## The order of the pages

1. **The Cover page** (ticket 1096; PrintCover card), while the Export panel's **Cover page** switch is on (it is on at first): the technique word, the title, the maker, the piece's colors and the tech line at the top, then the finished piece in color on the board under them (ticket 1141). It counts as a page, so the chart's first page is "2 / 2". This brings back a first page that shows the piece, which ticket 0914 had removed; unlike that Cover it holds no chart parts and no Beads needed, so nothing is printed twice. Switched off, the PDF opens on page 1 as before.
2. **Page 1** (ticket 0914): the title block, the whole piece and Beads needed, the table at the foot of the page and the piece centred in the room over it (ticket 1141). When the chart fits there at a working bead it is the chart itself, and for most pieces (up to about 100 × 100, a bracelet in strips) **the PDF ends here**: a 20 × 20 is the Cover page and 1 page, not 3. Beads needed that does not fit continues on **list pages** straight after it.
3. **Chart pages**, only when the chart does not fit page 1: the parts, in reading order (across, then down), after the overview on page 1.
4. **Word chart** pages, only when **Row by row** is on in the Export panel (tickets 0574, 0916). Letters adds or removes no page: every printed chart is lettered.

**Black and white** (Export panel, ticket 1096; PrintBlackAndWhite card), off at first, draws every page after the cover in one ink for a black-and-white printer: each bead on paper, a 10%, 20% or 30% tint or solid black, every letter and line black, so they print sharp on an office printer. The cover and the picture stay in color, and it adds no page.

**Large beads** (Export panel, ticket 0916) raises the smallest bead from `print-bead-min` to `print-bead-large` (3 mm, the chart size before ticket 0913), on page 1's whole chart, its strips and every chart page, at the cost of more pages: a 100 × 100 becomes the overview and parts. It acts, and is offered, only where the plan without it prints a bead under 3 mm (ticket 1125). The Export panel's Printable chart row counts the pages before the export ("PDF ≈ 1.2 MB · 5 pages").

The PDF's file name says which print switches are on, `{project}-{frame}-{maker}_large-beads_row-by-row.pdf`, and the Export panel shows it, renamable for one export, before the export (ExportPanel card, ticket 1096).

There is no cover that repeats the chart and no Specification that repeats the bead list (ticket 0914): Beads needed is one table, printed once, and the Schema's facts are page 1's tech line.

## Trimmed to the beads (ticket 1128)

The chart, the overview, the PNG picture and the Cover page show only the rows and columns from the first bead to the last; empty positions inside the drawing stay. The tech line, Row by row, the page count and the File size estimate give the trimmed size ("13 × 30", not "30 × 39"), and the plan sizes the bead from it. A Custom frame's chart, overview and Cover page are trimmed to the rectangle round its colored beads, while its tech line keeps its shape's size and Row by row its whole rounds and rows; a Schema with no beads prints whole.

## Approved 2026-11-19 (tickets 1126, 1127; in the app)

Signed off by the owner on 2026-11-19 (ticket 1123; the claude.ai page "PDF chart, beads only", linked on the ticket). The PrintChartPage card has the detail; the rest of this file describes the chart with Empty grid and Chart border on, and where it says otherwise this section wins.

- **Lines** (1126, in the app): no lines inside the chart and no bold edge. The lines every 5 and 10 and the chart's 0.3 mm outer edge are gone (`print-line-five` stays for a Custom frame's Seams); each colored bead keeps its own slim border, `print-line-cell` in `line-strong`, so neighbours share one hairline and a staggered row is outlined bead by bead.
- **One rounded rim** (approved 2026-11-20; 1126; its switch 1127): the pad's rounded outline (`print-pad-line`, `print-pad-radius`) moves in to `print-rim-gap` (1 mm) clear of the outermost beads, and the ruler numbers stand outside it. It is the only line round the chart, a Custom frame's too; no bead touches it. The Export panel's **Chart border** (approved 2026-11-20), on at first, shows it; off, nothing goes round the chart and the numbers keep their places. It is a rim where the owner first wanted no edge; the bold edge stays gone.
- **Empty grid** (1127), a switch in the Export panel, off at first: off, an empty position on a chart or the overview is bare paper or bare board; on, it gets today's outline and small `faint` cross. Colored beads look the same either way.
- **Letters always** is unchanged: every bead carries its Swatch letter. An empty position has no letter and no fill, and with Empty grid off no cross either: bare paper. A White bead keeps its border and its `ink` letter, which tell it from an empty position.

## Lifted Frame in exports (approved 2026-11-21, ticket 1177; not yet in the app)

The Frame's Lift (MarginSheet card, Lift; BeadBoard card, Lifted Frame) carries into the PDF and PNG. An export follows the **saved Lift** of the Frame, never Row highlight, and draws it in the light theme whatever the screen shows. A Schema with no Frame, and a Frame with no Lift (every Frame saved before it), print as before. Not drawn in a mockup yet: ticket 1177 tries a printed sample and sets the stacked shadow's steps.

- **The Cover page, the overview and the PNG picture** (the finished piece on the `print-board`): the piece is lifted as on screen, outer border only: ground, outline and the two soft shadows (BeadBoard card, values), their sizes in proportion to the bead as printed, as they follow the zoom on screen; holes lie on the piece like beads, empty positions inside the drawing staying as they are (Trimmed to the beads).
- **Page 1's whole chart and every chart part:** the one rounded rim (`print-pad-line`, 1 mm clear of the beads) stays the only line, and each pad lifts off the paper by a soft shadow under it: a PDF's drawing has no blur, so the shadow is three or four stacked, nearly clear black copies of the rim, each a little lower and wider. No ground (the paper is the ground) and no second outline. Never along a cut between parts, since each part is its own pad with its own rim.
- **Not drawn** with **Chart border** off (the shadow hangs on the rim), with **Black and white** on (every page after the cover is one ink and toner is not spent on a shadow; the cover and the picture keep it), on Beads needed, list pages or Word chart pages.
- **No change to the chart:** the beads, letters, rulers and lines of a chart page are exactly as before, and the ruler numbers keep their places outside the rim; the shadow lies on the margin, clear of them.

## Page 1

One A4 portrait page inside a 10 mm margin on all four sides (`print-margin`), the PrintPage1 card.

- **Title block**: the Frame's name as the title, else the Project's (Inter 700 22, `ink`), "by {Maker's name}" under it (Instrument Serif italic 13, `muted`), then one tech line (DM Mono `muted`: technique, grid, Bead, Estimated size). An empty name leaves out the title, an empty maker the "by" line; the Bead's name is never the title.
- **The chart, whole** (it fits page 1): drawn as a chart page draws a part (Chart pages below), PDF drawing with a letter on every bead, rulers on all four sides and the lines every 5 and 10, at the bead the plan gives (ticket 0913): as big as page 1 allows up to `print-bead-max` (9 mm), down to `print-bead-stretch` (1.6 mm) only to keep it there. A small piece fills the page width, so a page is never mostly empty: a 20 × 20 at 9 mm; a 5 × 5 is centred at 9 mm. A long narrow piece is folded into strips side by side, each under its label (PrintStrips). Page 1 leaves the chart the room under the title block and over Beads needed: all of Beads needed when it takes no more than 40% of the page under the title block, else its heading and first row, the rest of the colors going under the chart as they fit and on list pages.
- **The overview** (it does not fit): the whole piece on the board, scaled to the room it has with no minimum bead, without letters, rulers with a number every 5 (10 when the beads are small) and a hairline every 10, an empty position bare board (under a small `faint` cross with Empty grid on, ticket 1127). The chart parts are marked on it: solid 2 px `accent` lines with a white keyline, run through the rulers, with "101 →" and "101 ↓" in `accent-strong` where each cut is, and a white pill with an `accent` border on each part: "part A · page 2", "B · p 3". Beads needed takes at most 40% of the room under the title block.
- **Beads needed**, one table (ticket 1096): the heading, "Beads needed · Miyuki Delica 11/0" (an unknown Bead leaves the name out), over a 1.5 px `ink` rule, then the heads (DM Mono 6.5 `muted`: color, code, beads, grams) and the rows on `line` hairlines: a letter swatch (the color with its Swatch letter, as every chart prints it), color name, brand code, beads, grams. One color to a row across the page when all the colors fit under the chart that way, else two columns that read down; the chart's room is worked out from the two columns, so it is never smaller for it. Under a 1.5 px rule the Total ("Total · 8 colors" on the left; "10 000 beads · ≈ 50 g" on the right) and the grams note. No to buy column and no tubes on paper. An unknown Bead shows no grams and no note.
- **List pages**: colors that do not fit go on pages straight after page 1 (so chart pages start later), each with the title, small, and the same table in two columns; the Total and the grams note end the last.
- **Footer**: the X1 mark, "openbeads" and the date and time on the left, the page number on the right ("1 / 1", "1 / 3"), on the Cover page, page 1 and the list pages, every one counted.

## Chart pages

- **The bead is as big as the sheet allows** (ticket 0913), the same on every chart page of a PDF. The whole Schema is the chart on page 1 when it fits there at `print-bead-stretch` (1.6 mm) or more (ticket 0914); 1.6 to 1.8 mm is used only for that. Up to about 100 × 100 fits page 1. A small Schema fills the width up to `print-bead-max` (9 mm), and one too narrow to fill it at 9 mm is centred.
- **Otherwise equal parts**: the fewest sheets that keep the bead at `print-bead-min` (1.8 mm) or more, then the largest bead those sheets allow, at most 3 mm. A 150 × 100 is two parts of 75 columns at about 2.3 mm, not 100 + 50; a 150 × 150 is four parts. Cuts fall on a multiple of 10, as the rulers count (a part starts at 11, 21… 61), where the parts still fit at that bead on as many sheets. With more than 26 colors (two-letter Swatch letters) the floor is 2.2 mm and there is no stretch. Every part fits the chart area with its rulers and overlap, under a color key whose height is set once per PDF from the page with the most colors. Exact layout: the PrintChartPage card.
- **Parts share a sheet** when they fit at that bead, each on its own pad, inside its own rim, with its label above it ("part B · columns 101–130 · rows 1–100"), 6 mm apart; a long narrow Schema under 3 mm a bead whole (a bracelet) is folded into strips side by side on page 1 when that gives a bigger bead: a 35 × 120 is two strips at about 2.2 mm (PrintStrips card). Sheets are filled in reading order.
- **Vector**: chart pages, and the chart on page 1, are PDF drawing (ADR 0043): fills, lines, Swatch letters and ruler numbers in the PDF's built-in Helvetica, sharp at any zoom. The translated text (header, part label, key names, footer; page 1's title block and Beads needed) is one 300 dpi picture under the drawing.
- **Rulers number every row and column** (ticket 1129), on all four sides, 1, 2, 3… from the left and from the top for every turn, starting at the trimmed chart's first column and row; a part from column 61 counts on 61, 62… (its overlap's 59, 60 `faint`). Upright, in a `print-ruler` band, every number Helvetica regular in `muted`, the 10ths too: nothing is bold or set apart. The part labels, the continuation line, the overview's cut pills and Row by row use these numbers; the editor keeps its own. The overview keeps a number every 5 (10 when the beads are small) and its hairline every 10, counted the same way.
- **The 6.5 pt rule** (ticket 1129): every ruler number on a page is one size (approved 2026-11-20), the largest, at most `print-letter` (6.5 pt), at which the page's widest number fits (`print-ruler-fit`, 80% of its column's width, or of its row's height and its band): 6.5 pt on most pages, 3.1 pt on part B of a 150 × 100 at 2.3 mm, 2.4 pt on a 100 × 100 at 1.8 mm. A sheet of several parts or strips sets them all in one size. The overview's numbers follow the same one-size rule. Every number is printed. It is the one exception to the 6.5 pt floor besides a chart bead's Swatch letter.
- **Lines** (ticket 1126): each square bead's own border round its cell, `print-line-cell` in `line-strong`, one hairline between neighbours, an empty position's too while Empty grid is on; no line every 5th or 10th and no edge. **The rim**: one rounded outline (`print-pad-line` in `faint`, `print-pad-radius`) `print-rim-gap` (1 mm) clear of the outermost beads, the ruler numbers outside it, the only line round the chart, a Custom frame's too.
- **Overlap**: a part repeats `print-part-overlap` (2) columns or rows of the part before it, tinted (`print-overlap-tint`, 60% toward white; letters `muted`, numbers `faint`), with a `print-overlap-line` (0.5 mm) `accent` line where the part itself begins, through the rim and the rulers. The overlap counts in the size of a part, so a part always fits its sheet.
- **Letters always**: every bead carries its Swatch letter, whatever the Letters toggle says and in every Technique: Helvetica-Bold, 6.5 pt on a 3 mm bead and scaled with it (about 4.3 pt at 1.8 mm), never wider than its cell; `ink` on a bead lighter than luminance 0.1791 and white on a darker one. An empty position is plain paper under a small `faint` cross (ticket 0818), no fill and no letter; a White bead is filled and lettered, which tells it from empty.
- **Header**: the technique word and name; the part label ("part A · columns 1–60 · rows 1–75 · by {maker}"); on the right a mini map of the parts (the current one in `accent`) and the page number, large. No chart-style or date line. **Under the board**: "Colors on this page", each a swatch drawn as a bead with its letter and the color name, no counts, in `print-key-columns` (6) columns. **Footer**: the mark, the brand and the date and time, and "Continues right on page 3, below on page 4 →" or "Last part" (writing.md).

## Word chart

- With Row by row on (ticket 0916; it was Letters until then), one or more pages of written rows follow the chart (format: ticket 0574 and the glossary's Word chart; PDF only, no PNG). Each page is titled with its own rows ("Row by row 56–79"). An outlined strip with the color key sits at the top; each row is one line at `print-word-row` (10 pt), `print-word-pitch` apart: a tick box, the row number in DM Mono 500 and its runs, each its count and its bead's swatch with its letter, an empty position the empty swatch (ticket 1096), a hairline under it (stronger every 5th). When the longest row fits half the width the page runs in two columns of 91 mm with a `print-word-gap` gutter and no rule. A long row wraps with a hanging indent and never splits across columns or pages. Exact layout: the PrintWordChart card.

## Beads and grams

- **Beads and grams, side by side**: every color and the Total show the count and the weight, because beads are bought by the gram. The Total and the PNG say it both ways ("16 900 beads · ≈ 84.5 g").
- **How grams are worked out**: count ÷ the Bead's beads per gram, rounded **up** to 0.1 g, trailing ".0" dropped ("24 g", "3.9 g"); the Total is rounded up from the total count, not added up from the rounded rows.
- **Beads per gram is a catalog field** on each Bead: Miyuki Delica 11/0 about 200, TOHO Round 11/0 about 105 (TOHO's Beads Per Kg Chart, 105,300 a kg); TOHO Cube 1.5 mm needs a weighed figure before it ships.
- A note under the Total says where the number comes from and suggests a spare: "≈ 200 Delica 11/0 a gram, rounded up. Buy about 10% more for spares." An unknown Bead shows no grams and no note.
- The Beads needed box in the app has the same grams column, so the screen and the paper agree.

## The maker's name

- **Where the name comes from**: the Project's own optional field, **Your name** (ADR 0060; "Printed on this Project's PDF and PNG exports.").
- Set on a Project, it is printed on every export of that Project as information: in the chart page header, and "by {maker}" under the title of page 1 and of the Cover page (and so of the PNG). It is never a watermark.
- Empty: the exports leave it out. Names longer than 32 characters are cut with an ellipsis in the header.

## Wide and long Schemas

- The PDF's charts are vector; its other pages (the overview, list pages, Word chart) and the text under a chart are lossless pictures at 300 dpi (`print-dpi`), or 150 where a device cannot allocate a 300 dpi page. Export quality sets the PNG only (QualitySlider card, ADR 0076). The PDF is always A4 portrait. A Schema wider than tall gets a shorter chart on page 1, which leaves more room for Beads needed; the chart pages are the same full-page parts.
- Long thin Schemas (bracelets) stack their parts on shared sheets (Chart pages above). See the PrintWide and PrintStrips cards.

## PNG image

- **The Cover page, as a picture** (ticket 1096; PngExport card): the same page drawn by the same code, in color, drawn at 150, 300 or 450 DPI by the Export quality (QualitySlider card); a device that cannot hold a 450 DPI page draws High at 300. It is saved whatever the Cover page switch says, and no switch changes it. One file, whatever the piece: no list pages follow it, and its footer reads "1 / 1". Until ticket 1096 the PNG was page 1 of the PDF as a picture, its list pages further files (ticket 0915).
- Drawn in the app's language. No watermark. The PNG and PDF are named `{project}-{frame}-{maker}.png|pdf` and the project file `{project}-{maker}.openbeads`, each part made filename-safe and left out when it leaves nothing to write (ticket 0720).

The sizes are the `print-*` tokens; the board color is `print-board`. The PrintPage1, PrintChartPage, PrintStrips, PrintWordChart and PngExport cards show the pages. The signed-off canvas is in the claude.ai design project "Export sheets — PDF & PNG".
