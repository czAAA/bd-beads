# Homepage copy

The copy approved for the Homepage area's strings (`homepage`, `releaseNoteKinds`: `src/i18n/translations/homepage.ts` and each language's `homepage.ts`), ticket by ticket in the order it was approved. The rules it follows are in [`writing.md`](../writing.md). A new ticket's approved copy goes at the end of this file.

## Copy added in v15

The Homepage and the header menu, as signed off. The same rules: plain and short, sentence case, product nouns capitalized, no exclamation marks, emoji, em dashes or "please". The Homepage's slogan and its handwritten note are the one place with a lighter, drawn voice.
