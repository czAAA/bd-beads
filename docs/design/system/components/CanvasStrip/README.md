# CanvasStrip

The header strip at the top of the canvas box, one component on every App page (ADR 0085): the editor, the Homepage and Convert image: three slots: the panels (Menu, in the editor Project and Frame) aligned left, the Brand centred, and the controls aligned right: Undo and Redo, then the tools (Reference image, Rotate Canvas, Rulers) with the zoom and Language, then Export. Its height is its tallest item plus 1px above and 1px below (ticket 544): the Menu button (34px) plus 2px with a mouse, the 44px touch targets plus 2px (46px) on a coarse pointer; `--strip-height` (controls.css) is the same rule for the Homepage. It has a 1px `box-line`-tint edge on its left, right and bottom, and its top corners take the canvas box's radius (`radius-lg`, `--canvas-box-radius`). It has no title or size line (tickets 425, 431, 0443, 0473, 0540, 0543, ADR 0046, ADR 0073); the one name it shows is the open Project's, on the Project button (ticket 0858).

- The canvas box: `box` fill, 1px `box-line`, radius-lg, `elevation-2` (light only), overflow hidden; it takes all the width right of the left column.
- Strip: padding 0 10 0 16, gap 8, bottom divider `box-muted` at 22%. Three slots in a grid (`1fr auto 1fr`): Menu, Project and Frame on the left, the Brand in the middle, the canvas controls on the right.
- **No title, size line, printed-size estimate, grid icon or keyboard-focus hint** (ticket 425 removed them; before it the strip read "Canvas · 3 pieces · no Frame" or "Pattern · Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm"). The Project's size lives in the Project panel and the Frame's size tooltip.
- **Menu and Project (tickets 431, 0858):** text buttons with their icon (`menu`, `pattern`): Menu with the word "Menu", Project with the open Project's name (one line, cut with an ellipsis at `--strip-project-name-max`, 192px), before the zoom cluster, in that order. The Project button's accessible name and Tooltip name are "Project: {name}", whole, so a cut name is still read and shown in full. Menu's Tooltip lists its rows: "New Project, Convert image, Projects library, Theme." (ticket 0844). They open the Menu and Project panels (MenuPanel and ProjectPanel cards): a dropdown on every screen size. They sit on `ink` while theirs is open. Menu, Project, Frame and Export open panels, so they are `aria-expanded` (false while closed), never `aria-pressed`; Project, Frame and Export, whose panels are dialogs, are also `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, and Menu's is a plain region (ticket 0995). Padding 0 14 0 12 and an 8px gap between icon and word keep the label off the button's edge. Menu is always there, with no Project open too (the strip then holds Menu alone); Project is hidden, not disabled, with no Project. The New Project / Import bar has no Menu of its own any more.
- **Brand (ticket 540):** the X1 mark (22px, `accent`) and "openbeads" in the `brand` text style (Instrument Serif italic 24px, −0.3px, `ink`), 8px apart, the middle slot, centred on the strip whatever the sides hold. In the editor it is a link that opens the Homepage; the Homepage shows its rolling slogan in its place (ticket 0819). Under 1024px the wordmark hides and the logo stays. On a phone (under 744px) the editor's strip has no Brand; the Homepage's keeps its slogan. The Homepage's demo strip has a **Demo** tag after it, where the editor has Buy me a coffee (Homepage card, What's inside; ticket 0841).
- **Labels (ticket 540):** under 1024px the words of Menu, Frame and the Project's name hide and their icons stay (the words remain for screen readers and Tooltips), so on a phone the name never pushes Export off the strip. Where it hides, the name is said elsewhere (ticket 0986): a Project opened from the library says so once in an info toast, "Now in “{name}”." (Message card), and the Frame and Export panels' title lines carry it. The tab's title is "{name} · openbeads" at every width.
- **The Homepage's strip (tickets 0819, 0822, 0830)** holds Menu and beside it the way into the editor (`sm` buttons: Make your first Project, or Open the editor and Projects library · {n}, its name hidden under 1000px; each its own Tab stop; under 744px a row of its own under the rest, centred; not shown on the Welcome section, which has its own, its place kept and the buttons fading in over `duration-base` from the second section, out of the Tab order and hidden from screen readers while not shown), the rolling slogan in the Brand's place (after the X1 mark, where the wordmark stands) (on the Homepage the slogan is the brand; `tagline` at `--strip-slogan-size`, Homepage card), and the zoom (in, out, Fit), which scales the page's content 75–200% in the same 10% steps; Menu opens the editor's Menu panel, with Homepage as its current page. On a phone it holds Menu, the slogan (`--strip-slogan-size-phone`) and, with Projects saved, a library button (the `library` icon and the count); no zoom.
- **Controls, in this order:** Menu, Project, Frame on the left; in the right slot Undo, Redo, a divider, Reference image (ticket 0790), Rotate Canvas, the Rulers toggle, zoom in, zoom out, Fit, Language, a divider, Export. Undo and Redo are the first of the right slot and Export its last, both always visible; only what lies between the two dividers scrolls. The dividers are 1px by `--strip-divider-height` (18px), `box-muted` at 22%, flush between the neighbouring buttons' boxes (it cancels the cluster's 2px gaps, so a 800px tablet still fits), hidden from screen readers; the first shows with a Project open, the second where Export does. They show only with a Project open (not while a picture is framed). There is no zoom level in percent, at any size (ticket 543). Zoom runs 10% to 400% in 10% steps; the tier's smallest-bead floor wins where it is higher. Icon buttons 30px (44px on a coarse pointer), 18px icons, radius-sm, no fill.
- **Language:** the MenuPanel's Language picker, moved out of the Menu into the strip (the same PanelPicker, its `toolbar` look): from 1024px up the `globe` icon (18px), the code ("EN") and a 12px `chevron-down`, padding 0 4 0 6; under 1024px the code alone, centred, padding 0 4, at least an icon button wide, so the cluster still fits an 800px tablet; plain like the icon buttons, 30px tall (44px and at least 44px wide on a coarse pointer), `hover-fill` on hover, on `ink` with a `canvas` text while its list is open. Named "Language: EN, English", Tooltip "Language". Its list is the PanelPicker's, each language by its own name with a check on the chosen one, right edges aligned under the button. It sits after Fit and before Export, in the scrolling row (so on a phone it scrolls with the tools); ←/→ step past it like the other strip buttons, ↓, Enter or Space open its list. On every App page (the editor, with or without a Project, the Homepage and Convert image), not in the Homepage's demo.
- **Fit (tickets 0850, 0964):** puts the whole Frame with its Margin sheet in the room the Tool pill and the Progress bar leave, centred there; with no Frame, the drawing whole, or the origin in the middle of an empty Canvas. With a Frame it zooms in past 100% until the sheet fills that room one way, but no further than a bead 48px from the next along a row or down a column (`LARGEST_FIT_BEAD_PX`), so a small piece is the largest thing on screen and a tiny one is not blown up; never under 100% for a sheet that would fit at it. With no Frame it never goes above 100%. Zoom's 400% ceiling still holds. The Project opens at its fit. While the Frame is expanded (MarginSheet card, Expand the Frame, ticket 1168) the room is the whole space between the strip and the Progress bar, as the expanded sheet takes it.
- **At every width, phones included.** When the strip is too narrow for the cluster it scrolls sideways (no visible scrollbar) and the buttons are never squeezed; Menu and Project stay at the left edge, Undo and Redo stay whole at the start of the right slot and Export at the right end, and only the tools between the dividers scroll (ticket 0843). The scrolling row fades over `--strip-fade` (24px) on each edge where a control is cut, so a phone shows the row goes on; a swipe scrolls it, and once it is scrolled to its end only the start edge fades. Where the cluster fits (tablets and laptops) nothing fades and nothing moves.
- **Convert image (tickets 0649, 0756):** an App page (ConvertImage card). The strip keeps Menu, so Theme and About work as usual, and Language at its right end, and a **Close** (×, the `close` icon, plain, 30px, name "Close") takes the place of Project and Frame. There are no tools, zoom or Export besides Language (the page has its own zoom), and the Brand is not a link, so the page can only be left through Close, Escape or a Menu action, each of which asks first once a picture is set. On a phone the strip is the editor's: no Brand.
- **Buy me a coffee (ticket 560):** the `coffee` icon, plain look, first in the right slot, a link to the Coffee link opening in a new tab; Tooltip and name "Buy me a coffee". It shows only from 1024px up, where the strip has room; under that the Menu row is the way in. Absent while the app has no link.
- **Save ring (tickets 0796, 0922, 1111; SaveReminder card):** not on the strip: it sits over the drawing area just under Export, its right edge on Export's.
- **Rotate Canvas (ticket 0471, ADR 0052):** the icon is the woven layer's bead Type (ticket 1082; the Frame's Bead when a Frame is set, else the Canvas's, never its size or Brand): `rotate-canvas-round`, `-delica` or `-cube`, the `rotate` arc with that bead and a thread through its hole (Icons README). The bead and its thread turn with the Canvas's saved orientation about the bead's centre, so the button shows which way the thread runs, left to right or up and down; the arc stays. A press turns them a quarter clockwise over the base duration (from 270° on to 0°, never unwinding), a jump with reduced motion; on a two-finger twist they stay still while the fingers turn and take the Canvas's angle, lean included, as they lift. A Bead whose Type has no drawing shows `rotate-canvas` (the `rotate` square ruled into four). Disabled, it shows the Type in the disabled ink. Plain look, between Reference image and Rulers at every width. Each press turns the whole picture a quarter clockwise (0, 90, 180, 270, 0); no bead, Frame or name moves. Name "Rotate Canvas", no key, disabled with the reason "There are no beads to turn." while the Canvas is empty, and still available once a Row progress pass is done. Saved with the Project, no Undo step.
- **Export (tickets 0652, 0843, 0847):** the `export` icon (the Homepage's Exports row's; `share` read as share or open elsewhere, ticket 0847), plain look, the strip's last button, after Language, never scrolled away on a phone. It opens the Export panel (ExportPanel card), which hangs 4px under it with right edges aligned; it sits on `ink` with a `canvas` icon while its panel is open, like Menu and Project. Name and Tooltip "Export", no key; shown with a Project open, with or without a Frame.
- **Rulers toggle:** the `ruler` icon; pressed is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon. Name "Rulers", with the Tooltip line "Rulers number your beads." (ticket 0844), `aria-pressed`. Shortcut `R`. Off until the person switches it on, then kept on the device (ticket 0921). While the Canvas has no bead, with or without a Frame (the Frame's own rulers are always on, so there is nothing to switch, ticket 1012) it is disabled, plain and not pressed, with the reason "There are no beads to number." in its Tooltip, as Rotate Canvas is, and `R` does nothing; it can be pressed once a bead is on the Canvas, and never turns on by itself. The `ink` fill shows only while rulers are on.
- **Reference image (ticket 0790, ReferenceImage card):** the `reference-image` icon, plain look, the first of the scrolling tools, just before Rotate Canvas. With no picture in the Project it opens the file picker (Tooltip "Add a reference image", no `aria-pressed`); with one it shows and hides it, `aria-pressed`, the Rulers look when shown, Tooltip "Hide reference image" or "Show reference image" with the key chip `I`. Shown with a Project open.
- **No Canvas color button (ticket 0443, spec 0440).** The background color is set in Project (ProjectPanel card, Canvas section); in high contrast that section holds one line saying the canvas stays white. Every button left on the strip is a control-registry icon button, so its Tooltip carries the key chip (`R`, `G`, `I`, `Ctrl/Cmd+Z`, `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`, `Ctrl/Cmd+−`, `Ctrl/Cmd++`, `Ctrl/Cmd+0`) and says why it is disabled.
- The Zoom pill (a draggable pill with the same buttons, ticket 79) is gone; its buttons moved here.
