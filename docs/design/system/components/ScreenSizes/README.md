# ScreenSizes

The four screen tiers side by side: Phone, iPad 13″, MacBook Air and 24″ and larger (the MacBook Air layout, wider).

- Tiers by width: phone up to 1023px (portrait and landscape alike), `bp-tablet-lg` 1024px, `bp-laptop` 1280px, `bp-desktop` 1920px.
- The MacBook Air tier is DESIGN.md's layout; every other tier is described as a change from it. The full rules are in the Responsive section of the brand book.
- **v16:** the canvas is open at every tier, and the Frame, piece rulers and the CanvasHint behave the same everywhere (the Responsive section lists what differs per tier). The five previews here still draw the v15 board; the signed-off v16 screens for every tier are in the "bd-beads · Open canvas and frame" mockups.
