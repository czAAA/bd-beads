# Rulers

The row and column numbers around the Pattern (approved variant A). Every number is drawn, 1 upwards: on all four sides of the Frame, and above and left of each piece before a Frame is set.

- **Whose rulers (v16):** until a Frame is set, every piece carries its own rulers: column numbers above and row numbers on the left, counted from 1 at the piece's top-left corner, 3px from its rectangle. Once the Frame is set, piece rulers hide and only the Frame's show, on all four sides, counted from the Frame's top-left corner.
- **Piece areas (ticket 288):** the box that carries piece rulers is the Piece area, not each piece's own rectangle. A piece's area, counted as everything within one bead outside its bounds, that overlaps, lies inside or touches (the cell ring around it, side or corner, by the Technique's neighbour geometry) another's joins it, repeated until none do (ticket 293: the margin only decides what joins, the drawn rectangle stays around the beads), so a large piece never shows smaller pieces' rectangles inside or against its own. The area is the union's bounding box; its numbers run from 1 across the whole area, a number selects that row or column across the whole area, and the highlighted (`muted`) rectangle for the piece being drawn is the area containing it. Areas merge and split live as beads are painted and erased. Pieces with one empty bead between them join (a triangle of beads, each one bead from the next, is one area); two or more empty beads keep them apart.
- **Rulers toggle:** rulers are on by default. The canvas strip's Rulers button (`ruler` icon; pressed is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon; first in the ZoomPill on a phone) or `R` hides and shows them. Off hides every number and size marking, and (v19) each piece's rectangle with them; the Frame's line is never hidden by the toggle.
- **Type:** the `ruler` role: DM Mono 11 on every tier, 12px on a phone (below `bp-tablet`).
- **Every 5th number** is bold in `body`; the rest are regular in `ruler`. The current row's number is weight 700 in `marker` and the keyboard cursor's is bold `ink` with a 2px `focus-ring` line under it (BeadBoard and BeadCursor cards); both win over the 5th-number style.
- **Column numbers up to 99 are horizontal.** From 100 they are turned a quarter turn and read upward, so three digits take no more width than two. Row numbers always stay horizontal.
- **Placement:** column numbers sit at the bead-side end of their 28px gutter, 3px from the Frame line or the piece rectangle, horizontal or turned; offset techniques shift them by half a bead as before. Each number keeps its own bead at every zoom level.
- **Ruler step:** the smallest of every bead, 5, 10, 50, 100 beads (and so on) that leaves every number clear of the next at the current zoom (4px of air, measured on the longest numbers, 3-digit ones turned), for Piece rulers and Frame rulers alike. Beads between numbers are blank until ticket 299 adds the Ruler dots. The Zoom floor is 10% on every screen (ADR 0033); `bead-min-phone` 16px and `bead-min-tablet` 15px are where a 12px or 11px number needs more room, not a floor. **From 50% zoom up only (ticket 303):** below 50% a ruler shows only its last number (the column count along the columns, the row count along the rows; on a Frame each of its four sides shows its own), the cut is sharp at 50%, the last number stays clickable and the hidden ones are not; the cursor and Row progress highlights appear only on a drawn number.
- **Contrast:** `ruler` is 5.2:1 in light and 4.9:1 in dark on `box`; `body` is stronger still.
