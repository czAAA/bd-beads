# Rulers

The row and column numbers around the Pattern (approved variant A). Every number is drawn, 1 upwards: along the left and the top of the Frame and of each piece as seen, whatever is turned (ticket 1170).

- **Whose rulers (v16):** until a Frame is set, every piece carries its own rulers: column numbers above and row numbers on the left, counted from 1 at the piece's top-left corner, 3px from its rectangle. Once the Frame is set (ticket 1012), the Frame's show too, on its left and top as seen (ticket 1170), counted from the Frame's top-left corner, and every piece area of more than 4 beads on the Canvas around it keeps its own, as without a Frame. A bead the Frame or its keep-out margin covers is not drawn and not counted, so it carries no ruler and no rectangle. The Frame's numbers stay at its margin sheet's scale and a piece's follow the zoom, in the same view.
- **Which way they count (ticket 1165):** every ruler counts as a page is read, left to right and top to bottom as seen on screen, so 1 stands at the top-left. The numbers do not turn with the Canvas or the Frame: a ruler that a turn shows running right to left or bottom to top counts from its other end. The way the Frame is woven does not change the numbers; woven bottom to top or right to left, the current pass's pill starts on the last number and moves towards 1, and the counter's row on peyote names the number it stands on. A Frame lying turned has no pill, as its rulers count the cells it covers and not its own rows (ticket 0667): the counter counts its own rows the same way, from the left or the top as seen. Every 5th number is bold as counted, and the one number left below 50% zoom is the last, at the right or the bottom end.
- **Piece areas (ticket 288):** the box that carries piece rulers is the Piece area, not each piece's own rectangle. A piece's area, counted as everything within one bead outside its bounds, that overlaps, lies inside or touches (the cell ring around it, side or corner, by the Technique's neighbour geometry) another's joins it, repeated until none do (ticket 293: the margin only decides what joins, the drawn rectangle stays around the beads), so a large piece never shows smaller pieces' rectangles inside or against its own. The area is the union's bounding box; its numbers run from 1 across the whole area, a number selects that row or column across the whole area, and the highlighted (`muted`) rectangle for the piece being drawn is the area containing it. Areas merge and split live as beads are painted and erased. Pieces with one empty bead between them join (a triangle of beads, each one bead from the next, is one area); two or more empty beads keep them apart.
- **Rulers toggle:** rulers are off until switched on, and the choice is kept on the device (ticket 0921); while the Canvas has no bead, with a Frame or without, the toggle is disabled (CanvasStrip card). The canvas strip's Rulers button (`ruler` icon; pressed is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon; first in the strip at every width) or `R` hides and shows the pieces' rulers. Off hides every piece number and, (v19) each piece's rectangle with them. The Frame's rulers (numbers, dots and line) and its size tooltip while it is dragged or stepped are always shown and are not the toggle's to hide (ticket 1012).
- **Type:** the `ruler` role: DM Mono 11 on every tier, 12px on a phone (below `bp-tablet`).
- **Every 5th number** is bold in `body`; the rest are regular in `ruler`. The current pass's number is weight 700 in `canvas` on a `marker` pill, 2px round the number and fully rounded (ticket 0685); it wins over the 5th-number style. On Row done the pill glides to the next pass's number over `duration-base` with `ease-standard` while the two numbers crossfade their color; under reduced motion it jumps (ticket 0729).
- **Column numbers up to 99 are horizontal.** From 100 they are turned a quarter turn and read upward, so three digits take no more width than two. Row numbers always stay horizontal.
- **Placement:** column numbers sit at the bead-side end of their 28px gutter, 3px from the Frame line or the piece rectangle, horizontal or turned; offset techniques shift them by half a bead as before. Each number keeps its own bead at every zoom level.
- **Scale with the zoom (ticket 377):** the rulers' parts follow the zoom so they look alike at every zoom. At 100% the numbers are 11px (12px on a phone), the line is 7px (Frame) or 5px (piece) off the beads, the gap between numbers and line is 3px and the Ruler dots are 1px (5th 1.75px). From 100% down to 12.5% the numbers grow to 1.3 times, the line draws in to 45% (3px, 2px), and nothing changes below 12.5%. From 100% up to 400% the numbers grow to 1.6 times, the line moves out to twice (14px, 10px), the gap to three times and the dots to 2.5 times, and nothing changes above 400%. The gap is half as wide at 12.5% and below. The dots stand on the numbers' line but no further out than a 2-digit number's half band; below 50% zoom, where only the last number is drawn, they hug the line instead, a gap and a dot's radius off it (ticket 378). A Piece area carries rulers once its rectangle spans more than 4 beads in total.
- **Ruler step:** the smallest of every bead, 5, 10, 50, 100 beads (and so on) that leaves every number clear of the next at the current zoom (4px of air, measured on the longest numbers, 3-digit ones turned), for Piece rulers and Frame rulers alike. Beads between numbers get a **Ruler dot** (below). The Zoom floor is 10% on every screen (ADR 0033); `bead-min-phone` 16px and `bead-min-tablet` 15px are where a 12px or 11px number needs more room, not a floor. **From 50% zoom up only (ticket 303):** below 50% a ruler shows only its last number (the column count along the columns, the row count along the rows; on a Frame each of its four sides shows its own), the cut is sharp at 50%, the last number stays clickable, and the beads between are Ruler dots that select their row or column (ticket 299); the Row progress highlight appears only on a drawn number.
- **Ruler dots:** every bead without a number gets a dot on the line the numbers' centres follow, 2px across in `ruler`; every 5th is 3.5px across in `body`. Below 6px of bead pitch only the 5th-bead dots are drawn. A dot, like a number, selects its row or column; a press anywhere on the ruler picks the nearest bead along it (a number under the pointer picks its own), so a ruler works at 10%, where a bead is 2px. Dots use the number colours, so they keep the number contrast on every Canvas color.
- **Contrast:** `ruler` is 5.2:1 in light and 4.9:1 in dark on `box`; `body` is stronger still.

- **No Lifted piece (removed 2026-11-20):** every Piece keeps its 1px rectangle where the Rulers toggle shows it (BeadBoard card); with Rulers off a piece has no line.

The BeadBoard and Frame cards show the piece and Frame rulers in place.

- **Which sides (ticket 1170):** every ruler, the Frame's and a piece's, stands along the left and the top as seen on screen, at every Canvas turn and for a Frame lying turned: the box's side whose outward step points most to the left, and the one pointing most up. A turned Canvas never moves a ruler to the right or the bottom, and the Frame's title block keeps the bottom-left corner below them (MarginSheet card). Until ticket 1170 the Frame's stood on three sides, none along the title block's edge (ticket 0728), and a piece's on its own top and left, which a turned Canvas showed at the bottom or the right.

**On the margin sheet (ticket 0655; MarginSheet card):** with a Frame its rulers stand in the left and top bands (ticket 1170). They sit on the margin's color, so they take the nearest grey to the look's `ruler` at 4.5:1 or more against it (the 5th numbers at 7:1), the rule a Custom Canvas color uses. The current pass is marked on its number in the band that numbers it (the left for a pass across the screen, the top for one down it), never by a pointer outside the numbers, so the mark never meets the tools of the top band (ticket 0685). In the margin's compact and folded forms no numbers are drawn; the pass is a tick on the Frame's line instead (MarginSheet card). **Expanded (ticket 1168):** while the Frame is expanded the sheet is drawn at scale 1 and its numbers are the 100% ones (DM Mono 11, 12 on a phone), with the Ruler step and Ruler dots, in every case, never dots alone (MarginSheet card, Expand the Frame).

## As built (ticket 0730)

- **Gliding pill:** drawn on the canvas, the pill moves from the number of the pass left to the next one's in each band that numbers both, and the two numbers crossfade by drawing each in both styles at opposite opacities. In a band that numbers only the next pass the pill shows there at once. Once every pass is done the pill and its number fade back to the plain number over 150ms where they were.
