# BeadBoard

How a Pattern is drawn: beads on an open canvas you can draw on anywhere, in pieces that carry their own rulers.

- **Open canvas (v16):** there is no board. The drawing area is one background (CanvasBackground) covered edge to edge with the positions of the chosen Technique, each marked by a Position mark in `position-mark` (starts from `bead-empty`), at the bead pitch. A Position mark is one shared 1 CSS px line in the 1px gap between neighbouring positions (not one line each), clear inside so the Canvas color shows through, round every position once a bead is drawn on it and inside the Frame too (the keep-out margin has none), with the Technique's bead corner radius (rounded for peyote, square otherwise) and the half-bead row shift of peyote and brick stitch. It uses the same color in each theme, and is never saved with a Project or drawn in exports. Painting works at every position. The canvas has no edge and no size; it is moved, never resized.
- **Moving and zooming:** the wheel or two fingers move the canvas, and so do Space + drag and the Hand tool (`H`). ⌘ or Ctrl + wheel and pinch zoom, as do the canvas strip's buttons. The CanvasHint names these in the bottom-left corner.
- **Pieces:** beads that touch by a side or a corner form one piece. Until a Frame is set, each piece has a 1px `line-strong` rectangle 5px outside its beads (radius 6; it does not change while you draw) and its own rulers above and on the left (Rulers card).
- **The Frame** marks the part of the canvas that is the Pattern (Frame card). Inside a set Frame, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads instead of marks.
- **Beads:** 1px gap (each bead stands 0.5px in from its cell; ticket 0458). Shape comes from the Bead's form factor and Technique; a rounded bead has corners at 22% of its width. Every theme draws a faint `bead-rim` at 0.75px just inside the bead's edge, so two beads of one color stay apart (ticket 0460): a dark tint in light and high contrast, a white tint in dark; a near-black bead gets the white tint in light too, since a dark tint vanishes on it.
- **Finished rows** (inside the Frame, ticket 352): every bead keeps its own color and is only slightly dimmed, in light and dark alike: 50% color, 50% canvas background, never grey. While a mouse or an Apple Pencil hovers a finished row (not the empty canvas or a row still to weave) all finished rows draw at normal color, and a click or tap on a finished row toggles them between normal and dimmed until the next one (a view setting: not saved, not an Undo step).
- **Current row** (ticket 352): its beads are drawn 10% brighter (lifted toward white) than the todo rows, and carry a `marker` ring (royal blue in light; see Beads in progress). A rotated Project turns the rings with the beads.
- **Beads in progress** (tickets 375, 376): the beads woven in the current pass (the whole row or column; on peyote's later lines the half that pass adds) are drawn lifted above their neighbours, so their corners are not covered, each with its own 2px `marker` ring drawn inside its cell, its outer edge on the cell's edge and its curve the bead's corner radius less 1px, so a ring never reaches a neighbour and two rings side by side stay two. Only those beads are ringed: no line joins the rings, and the beads still to weave in the line carry nothing.
- **Zoomed out (draft look, ticket 0676):** where a bead is under 5 device px across (below about 25% on a 1× screen, 12% on a 2× one), the editor draws each layer as one flat pixel a bead: its own color, faded or lit by Row progress, empty Frame positions in `bead-empty`, no gap, rim, rounding or letter. At that size the gap and rim are a fraction of a pixel and showed only as streaks; peyote's and brick stitch's shifted rows keep their half-bead offset. Exports and pictures keep the full bead at every size.
- **While it moves (ticket 0717):** a pan or pinch of a big pattern moves the drawn picture and draws it sharp 120 ms after the gesture settles (tickets 349, 0689), and never shows a bare patch meanwhile. The empty canvas is laid under the drawing: the Background with its Position marks, repeated at the zoom, scroll and quarter turn the beads are drawn at (a lean turns it with them), so whatever the moving picture uncovers, and the drawing area before a Project's first frame is drawn, already looks like empty canvas, in every theme and Background. The drawing covers it wherever it lies; the Frame, its margin sheet and the beads keep their look from the drawing, and a strip uncovered over the Frame shows empty canvas until the redraw. In the draft look the picture also reaches half a screen past the drawing area on every side (less on a screen where that passes 4096 × 4096 device pixels), so a pan of half a screen or a zoom out to about 60% uncovers nothing at all; the full look keeps a picture the size of the drawing area. Reduced motion changes none of this.
- Empty positions use `bead-empty`; the hover outline with no color `bead-outline`.
- **PNG and PDF exports always use the light values**, and still draw the Frame's beads on a board (`print-board`).
