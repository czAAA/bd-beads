The one page outside the editor, as signed off: full-length pages at each size; the dashed line is the fold. The live page is interactive (menu, theme, language, carousel).

Structure, top to bottom

  • Header as in the editor: mark and "bd-beads", the menu button, EN / RU, the theme control (one icon on the phone). No Keyboard shortcuts.
  • Hero, centred: the mark and wordmark; the slogan Draw. Joy. Weave. (tagline, last word in accent-strong) with the note "you" on its first word; the line save. count. export in mono with the note "on us"; Make your first Pattern (primary) and Open the editor, with "eleven small steps" beside them from 1024; the Tour Pattern lying across as a band on its board, no ruler, and "you'll make this" centred under it in note-gold. With Patterns saved: only Open the editor, "Patterns saved: 3", no notes.
  • What's inside (serif-heading, no label above it): a carousel. From 1024 a list of the seven features on the left (the selected one on panel with a 2px accent bar and its line) and a large example on the right with ‹ › and "3 / 7". Below 1024 the examples swipe as cards with dots. Examples are drawn as beads: hearts in loom, peyote and brick stitch; a poppy with Select; a picture and its beaded version; rows done and the current row; Beads needed; a PDF page with the technique word across it; the Saved Patterns gallery.
  • The coffee tile: a beaded cup with rising steam, "Made by one person", one line, and Buy me a coffee linking to the maker's own page.
  • Three ways to use it: No account (works now, "you are here", primary Open the editor), Free account and Pro (designed, switched off, "coming later"). Each: a strand of one to three beads, the name in serif-heading, price and meta, one line, the button, and "everything in …, plus" with checks. Plan contents are placeholders.

The drawn layer and rules

  • Notes are note (serif italic 15–19px) in muted, or accent-strong for "you" / "on us", with thin hand-drawn arrows (1.25px muted, round caps). Never more than one note per region; hidden where they would crowd (below 1024 for the button note).
  • Bead drawings: a flower, heart, bee, spool, leaf, rhombus and sparkles in Palette colours, 3–6px beads, at the page edges; large ones (9–11px beads) at 16% opacity. They sit behind content and never touch text.
  • Background marks: three to five X1 marks per section at 8% accent, sizes 34–300px, as on the PDF exports.
  • No new colours except note-gold and tour-highlight; Palette colours are used only as beads.
  • The page scrolls; the editor never does. Everything decorative is aria-hidden; one h1; the carousel is a tablist from 1024 (arrows move) and a swipe list below. Steam and scroll animate only with reduced motion off.
  • note-gold #c99a14 on white is about 2.5:1: fine for this decorative note, never for text that carries meaning.
