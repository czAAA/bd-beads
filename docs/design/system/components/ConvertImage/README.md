# ConvertImage

The framing step of Convert image, inside the canvas box.

- Runs inside the canvas box: the strip names the step; the picture fills the drawing area with the frame at the Pattern's size and the bead lattice showing through; outside the frame the picture is dimmed (black at 45%).
- Drag (or one finger) moves the picture; pinch or the zoom controls scale it. The hint sits on the picture, bottom-left.
- The Progress bar's place holds the conversion controls: Colors at most (2–14, default 12), Colors found with their swatches, Cancel and Create Pattern.
- Phone: the same, full screen, with the controls as a bar above the dock area.
- **v16:** the frame of this step becomes the Pattern's Frame. The converted picture is the first piece on the open canvas, inside that Frame, and drawing outside it stays possible.

Hand-written from the Phase A sign-off (forms, screens and states); static rendition.
