# ConvertImage

The Convert image page: an App page (ADR 0085, ADR 0086) where a picture becomes a new Project, a live bead preview beside the steps, every change shown at once (spec 0645, ADR 0010). Designed in ticket 0646 as a large popup, made an App page in ticket 0756, and **redesigned for desktop in ticket 0778** (design canvas "Convert image, desktop redesign", https://claude.ai/artifact/7YWhy6JfdHCwbf8cM3x322): one scrolling pane of steps that the Progress bar follows, Picture and Bead & Technique as one step, Background in place of Cut out, a floating bar of tools under the picture, Keep the picture's shape in place of the chain, Make empty as a tool in place of Treat as empty, and a part of the beads kept with Select.

Built in ticket 0780 (As built, below). The phone and tablet layout of the redesign are still to be designed; until then the rules under 1024 px below are 0756's, applied to the one pane.

## Frame

- **A page of the canvas box**, at every size: the Canvas strip on top (Menu kept; a Close × in place of Project and Frame, and none of the editor's tools, zoom or Export), the page between, and the Progress bar below (ProgressBar and CanvasStrip cards). It takes the Canvas's cell; the editor's side panels (Project, Frame, Export) are hidden, and the editor stays as it was beneath it.
- **From 1024 px wide in landscape:** the preview on the left, taking the rest; the **step pane** on the right, `convert-sections-width` (440; `--convert-sections-width-narrow`, 400, while the page is under 1100 wide), a 1px `panel-rule` between them. The pane scrolls on its own and holds every step, one after another (Steps). The preview is on `box`, the pane on `canvas`.
- **Under 1024 px, or in portrait:** the preview above the pane, at most `--convert-preview-phone` (300) tall and never under `--convert-preview-min` (160), 440 on a portrait tablet (`--convert-preview-tablet`), a 1px `panel-rule` under it; the pane fills the rest and scrolls (PhoneForms card). On a short page (a phone on its side, the Homepage's demo editor) the preview takes at most half the page and gives way down to 40% of it, so the pane always has room (ticket 0820). The band and the floating bar on a phone are to be designed.
- Opens from New Project's Convert image, the Menu's Convert image row, or by dropping or pasting a picture on the editor, which opens it with the picture set. Escape belongs to the page: it asks to leave, like Close, except while a preview tool is on, when it turns the tool off first.

## Leaving
- **Close (×) in the strip, Escape, the browser's Back, and every Menu action that leaves** (New Project, a pick from the Projects library, Import, Homepage) go through the page's leave guard (ADR 0085). With no picture set, the page closes at once. Over the page the Menu opens on its root list, never on the New Project form, even with an empty library, so the form's buttons are only reached through New Project's question (ticket 0861).
- **The browser's Back is Close** (ticket 0861): opening the page puts one entry on the tab's history, so Back stays in the app and asks as Close does; Back to converter keeps Back the page's Close, and a page that goes any other way takes its entry off, so the next Back goes where it went before. **A reload or closing the tab** asks the browser's own "Leave site?" while a picture is set, and nothing with none.
- **Once a picture is set** the guard asks in a confirmation (ConfirmDialogs card): the title "Leave the converter?", the message "Everything you set up here will be lost.", **Back to converter** (the default: it takes focus, and Escape means it) and **Leave** (danger-filled). Leave closes the page and the Menu action goes on; Back to converter stays with everything as it was.
- **Close with no Project open** (Close, Escape or Back, then Leave) lands on the New Project form, where the person came from, not on the empty Canvas (ticket 0861). A Menu action that leaves goes where it says.
- **Either way the person lands on the Project that was open before**, with its Undo and Redo untouched: converting makes no Undo step, and nothing the page does touches the open Project. Create Project adds the new Project and opens it, as New Project does.

## Steps

- **Five steps in one pane:** 1 Picture & bead, 2 Background, 3 Size, 4 Colors, 5 Review. The pane is one scrolling column: each step follows the last, so the pane never ends in white space while a step is left to see, and the person can read on or jump.
- **A step** is a section with a 1px `panel-rule` under it (none under the last), its body padded 4 24 28 with 16 between its rows; under 1024 px padded 4 16 24.
- **Its header** is `--convert-step-head` (52) tall, padded 0 24, on `canvas`, and **pinned to the pane's top** (`position: sticky`) while its step is in view, the next step's header pushing it out; while pinned it has a 1px `line-soft` edge under it. It holds the step disc, the title in `control` at 600, and at the right a `meta` summary of what the step holds now, cut with an ellipsis rather than wrapped: "toho 11/0 · loom", "found · 38%" / "removed · 38%" / "off", "46×100 · 7.4 × 16 cm" (or "34×38 kept · of 46×100"), "9 colors · 1 empty", "4 210 beads".
- **Step disc** (`--step-disc`, 20): the number in `meta-small` inside a 1px `line-strong` ring. Picture & bead's disc becomes a 12px check on `ink` once a picture is set.
- **Until a picture is set,** steps 2 to 5 are shut: their headers show in `faint` with the summary "add a picture first", their bodies are not drawn, and their dots on the Progress bar are `aria-disabled`. Picture & bead is open, so Bead and Technique can be chosen first.
- **The Progress bar follows the pane** (ProgressBar card): the counter ("2/5"), the track with a named dot per step, ← Back and Next →. Scrolling the pane moves the fill and its spark continuously between the dots; the step whose header is nearest the top is current, its dot the ring, and reaching a step flares it. A dot, Back and Next scroll the pane to their step (smooth, a jump under reduced motion) and move focus to its heading. Once a picture is set the last step is at least as tall as the pane, so every step's header reaches the pane's top and Next lands with the new step's header first (ticket 0862); a part that opens or grows inside a step (Color by color, a Change picker) moves neither the current step nor the counter. Were the last steps too short to reach the top, the last stretch of scroll would be shared out evenly between them, so each still became current in turn. On Review, Next becomes **Create Project** (primary), disabled without a picture. There is no footer line or Cancel: Close is in the strip, and the result is in the Size step's box and its summary.

## Preview pane

- `box` fill, in two parts: the **band** on top and the picture under it.
- **The band** (`--convert-band-height`, 48): padded 0 24, a 1px `line-soft` edge under it, `meta` in `muted`. At the left, the facts that used to be the size tag over the picture: the grid size in `ink` at 500 ("46×100 beads"), the measured size ("7.4 × 16 cm") and the colors ("9 colors"), `·` between them in `faint`; with a part kept, "keeping 34×38" and a small **Keep all** button (24 tall, `small`) that clears it; with A part of the picture, "part of picture 160×231 px". At the right, one line on what the preview is showing now, if anything: the hatch swatch and "background found · 38%", "background removed", the Select icon and "drag on the beads to keep only a part" (or "drag to crop the picture"), the eyedropper and "tap a color to leave it out", or "original picture · let go to see the beads". It never covers the picture, so nothing is cut off at the top.
- **The picture:** drawn as beads by the Project renderer in the Frame's space (in the Bead's own cell, the dragging draft look), redrawn on every change, fitted into the room left: 20 above, 32 at the sides and 76 below, so the floating bar never covers it at Fit. The pane is `role="img"` named "Preview: 46×100 beads, 9 colors"; its controls are buttons.
- **Empty places** (a removed background, a color made empty) are drawn as the Canvas draws an empty position: the bead's outline in `position-mark`, no fill.
- **The floating bar** sits centred 18 above the preview's bottom edge: `elevated`, a 1px `line` edge, `radius-lg`, `elevation-3`, padding 4, 2 between items, 1 × 20 `line` dividers with 4 either side. Buttons are 32 tall, `radius-md`, icon and `tab` label 6 apart (Turn 90°, Flip, Undo, Redo and the zoom buttons icon only, 32 square, each named by its Tooltip). In groups:
  1. **Turn 90°** (`rotate` icon) and **Flip** (`flip` icon, left to right), icon only since ticket 0862, so the bar fits the iPad's 870px preview with Zoom in and Fit whole; their Tooltips keep the names: change the picture's pixels before sampling, and put A part of the picture back to the whole picture.
  2. **Undo** and **Redo** (`undo`, `redo` icons, 32 square, icon only): take back and put back the background decisions, one step each: the Remove background switch, a Tolerance change (a slider drag is one step), Remove what was found, Show what was found, a Background tool step and Start over. Each is disabled while it has nothing to do, with the editor's "Nothing to undo." and "Nothing to redo." Tooltips. They have no key. A turn, a flip or a new picture empties their history.
  3. **Select**, **Make empty** and **Background** (ticket 0787), the preview's tools: one or none is on, the one on drawn `ink` with a `canvas` label and icon. Pressing the tool that is on turns it off; Escape does too.
  4. **Compare** (`contrast` icon): held (pointer, Space or Enter), the preview shows the original picture, smoothed, in the beads' place, until let go. It is a button of its own so a keyboard can reach it.
  5. Zoom out, the level ("100%", `meta`), zoom in and Fit, the Canvas strip's registry actions (ticket 0650): their Tooltips with the key chips; at a limit zoom out or zoom in is disabled with the strip's reason, still hoverable and focusable. Zoom is the view only. Ctrl/⌘ + plus, minus and 0 zoom the preview and Fit it while the page is open, never the canvas beneath, and the browser's page zoom stays claimed. Pinch and wheel too.
  Until a picture is set the bar is not shown.
- **Select** on the preview (the editor's own Select gesture and rubber band, ContextBar card): a drag draws a rectangle from the bead it began on to the one it ends on (a plain tap draws nothing); a drag that begins inside it, the `move` pointer over it, moves it whole, kept on the picture; a drag outside it draws a new one. On touch a drag on the beads draws or moves (`touch-action: none`) rather than scrolling. What it draws depends on the weave:
  - **Whole picture** (the default): Select picks **a part of the beads**, snapped to whole beads, drawn as the editor draws a Selection, a 1.5px `marker` rectangle round the picked beads. Only the picked beads become the Project; nothing is converted again, so the size and colors stay exactly as shown, and the beads outside are dimmed (`canvas` at 60%). The band says "keeping 34×38" with Keep all; the Size step's result box and summary give the kept size beside the whole ("34×38 beads · 5.4 × 6.1 cm · kept from 46×100"); Beads needed counts only the kept beads. Keep all, or changing Width or Height, clears it.
  - **A part of the picture** (1 Picture & bead): Select **crops the picture** before it becomes beads, in whole picture pixels: the crop is drawn with the crop handles (the Handle card's eight, four corners and four edge bars, in `accent`, 44px to hit on touch, each named for its edge or corner, "Crop: top-left corner"), a 1.5px `ink` line with a 4px `canvas` halo; inside it the beads, outside it the original picture dimmed by `--framing-dim`. A focused handle moves one pixel per arrow, ten with Shift; the crop is at least 8 pixels a side. Choosing A part of the picture puts the crop 10% in from each edge and turns Select on. The crop only chooses *what* is woven: Width and Height in beads stay as they were, dropping only when the crop has fewer pixels than they allow, and the colors are then counted from the crop alone.
- **Make empty** (the eyedropper, the Canvas's own pointer): the bead under the pointer is ringed (2px `ink` with a 4px `canvas` halo); a tap makes that bead color empty everywhere (its row of Color by color, every found color it joins; ticket 0863), and a tap on an empty place of that color brings it back. It works from any step; the Colors step's Color by color shows the same state on each row's Empty button. The crop handles hide while it is on.
- **Background** (the `erase` icon; ticket 0787): with it on, the bead under the pointer is ringed as Make empty's is, and a tap toggles the patch under it: on a found bead it **keeps** the patch (the found beads joined to it whose color is within twice the Tolerance of the tapped one: held by what it touches and by the beads' state, the patch can reach wider than the edge fill, so a tap on fur takes a patch), on a kept bead it makes the patch **background**. A drag does the same for every bead it crosses, in the sense of the bead it began on, as one step. It works at both stages: on Find the hatch follows, on Remove the empty places do. Picking it turns Remove background on; turning Remove background off turns it off. The band reads "tap or drag over beads to keep them or take them away". The crop handles hide while it is on.
- **Background found** (2 Background, stage Find): every bead the background takes is faded with `--found-dim` (`canvas` at 60%, as beads outside a kept part are; ticket 0787) and hatched with `--found-hatch` (the `marker` color at 70% in 1.1px diagonal lines every 4px, over `marker` at 10%), over its own color, so the person sees what will go before it goes, whatever the bead's color.
- **Empty:** the pane is the drop zone, inset 24 (16 on a phone): a 1.5px dashed `line-strong` edge, `radius-lg`, the 32px `image` icon, "Drop a picture here" in `control` and "or paste one with ⌘V" (Ctrl+V off Apple devices) in `meta`; on touch (writing.md, A help line fits the device in hand) "Your picture shows here" and "Choose a file, paste or take a photo", as nothing can be dropped there (ticket 0846). The band reads "no picture yet".
- **A picture dragged over the page:** a 2px dashed `accent-strong` edge inset 12 on `surface` at 92%, the icon and "Drop to use this picture" in `accent-strong`, the limits in `meta`. Dropping replaces the picture and keeps every other setting; the weave goes back to Whole picture and a kept part is cleared.
- **A picture dragged over the app** (page closed): the scrim and a centred 400 × 220 card (`--picture-drop-width`, `--picture-drop-height`) in the same drop look, "Drop to convert image" and "PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP · up to 10 MB". Dropping opens the page with the picture set.

## 1 Picture & bead

- **No picture:** Choose file (primary, `import` icon, the FileButton's `accept`), Paste (`paste` icon; the async Clipboard API, and Ctrl/Cmd+V works anywhere on the page), and Take photo on a touch device (a file input with `capture`, the `camera` icon; ticket 0862), all `field-height` (Paste too), side by side in one row sharing it evenly (Choose file primary); a button is never narrower than its label, so a pane too narrow for the row (a phone) stacks them. A button never wraps sideways.
- **Paste with nothing to paste** says nothing: no line appears (ticket 0756).
- **With a picture:** a row of the 64 × 48 thumbnail (`--convert-thumb-width`, `--convert-thumb-height`; the picture contained in it, 1px `line` edge), the file name in `control` and "289×630 px · jpg" in `meta`, and at the right a small (28) **Replace** button with a chevron, a menu of Choose file and Paste. Turn 90° and Flip are in the preview's floating bar, not here.
- **bead** and **technique:** the New Project form's own pickers in panel rows (ProjectPanel card), the `label` at the left (88 wide) and the PanelPickers at the right: Brand, Type and Size; Technique, with the Drop row for peyote and brick stitch. They start from the form's values and are the page's own copy.
- **convert:** the `label` "convert" (ticket 0863; it was "weave", a word taken for the Technique) over two choices as radio cards (a 1px `line` edge, `radius-md`, padding 10 12; the chosen one a 1px `ink` edge doubled inside, its radio filled `ink`), each a title in `control` over a line in `small` `muted`:
  - **Whole picture** (the default): "Every part of the picture becomes beads."
  - **A part of the picture:** "Crop the picture with Select before it becomes beads. The rest of the picture is left out." Choosing it draws the crop and turns Select on (Preview pane).
  Keeping a part of the beads is not a choice here: Select on the preview does it, with Whole picture (Preview pane).
- **Note**, only until the first picture is set: "Drop a picture anywhere on the app, choose a file or paste one with ⌘V. PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP, up to 10 MB and 16 megapixels." - **Errors** (HEIC, SVG, too big, too many pixels, unreadable, wrong format) take writing.md's Error pattern (ticket 0863), in an inline Message under the buttons (`danger` edge, the warning icon, `role="alert"`): "This file can't be converted. ## 2 Background

Takes away what is round the subject, in two stages, so the person sees what was found before anything goes. Replaces Cut out (spec 0761, rolled back): there is one way to find the background, and no AI choice.

- **Remove background:** a row on `surface` with a 1px `line-soft` edge, `radius-md`, padding 10 12: the label in `control` over a line in `small`, and an AppSwitch at the right (SwitchAndFileButton card). Off (the default): "Takes away what is round the subject, so the table or the wall is not woven. You see what is found first." On: "Turn it off to keep the whole picture." Turning it off at any stage puts the background back.
- **The two stages,** shown while it is on as one plain numbered line under the row, in `control` `ink`, naming the stage in hand (ticket 0863: two chips looked like a switch that did nothing): "Step 1 of 2: check what was found" (1 Find) and "Step 2 of 2: check what is left" (2 Remove). The buttons below move between them.
- **1 Find** (turning the switch on lands here): the preview hatches the background found (Preview pane) and the band says how much. In the step: the hatch swatch (20, `radius-xs`, a 1px `marker` edge) beside "The hatched beads are the background found: 38% of the picture. Check it on the preview before you remove it."; a **Tolerance** ValueSlider (2 to 40, ends "only close colors" and "more colors" (ticket 0863); ticket 0787; an Info button beside its name, "What Tolerance does", whose Tooltip says it is how far a color may be from the background's colors and still be background: low takes the background with its noise and soft shading, high also colors that only look alike and may reach into the subject; ticket 0817) that widens or narrows what is found, live; the line pointing at the tool, with the 16px `erase` icon, "To fix what was found, pick **Background** under the preview: tap or drag over hatched beads to keep them, or over kept beads to take them away."; **Start over** (small, secondary; ticket 0787), shown once the tool has made a step (Undo and Redo are on the floating bar); and **Remove what was found** (primary).
- **2 Remove:** the background's beads become empty places on the preview. In the step: a box on `surface` with the `check` icon, "Background removed" in `control` and "1 742 beads left out, shown as empty places on the preview." in `small`; then **Show what was found** (small, secondary), which goes back to 1 Find with nothing removed, and the same Start over once the tool has made a step.
- The background is found from the picture's edges inward (ticket 0787): the colors touching the edge are grouped into **families** (within 0.07 of each other in OKLab); a family covering under 2% of the border is the subject touching the edge, and so is one more present in the picture's middle third than on its border (its share of the middle over 1.5× its share of the border: a close-up of a cat on concrete keeps the cat); at most 16 families. The fill takes a bead within Tolerance of a family, in OKLab (the slider in hundredths of ΔEOK), as far as it joins up with the edge, through places with no color. Colors are then counted from what is left, so the background does not spend the color count.
- **The tool's steps** (ticket 0787): each tap or drag of the Background tool is one step, kept in picture pixels, so a new Tolerance, Width, Height or crop replays it on the beads that then stand there; a turn, a flip or a new picture drops them all. Undo and Redo (on the floating bar) walk every background decision, the steps among them; Start over takes every step back as one decision, so Undo puts them all back. Turning Remove background off keeps them for the next time it is on.

## 3 Size

- **units:** a panel row, the `label` "units" at the left (ticket 0863; it was "slide in") and a small mono SegmentedControl at the right, beads | mm (beads is the default). It changes only how Width reads: the slider moves in whole beads either way.
- **Width and Height:** two ValueSliders (ValueSlider card), Width first, with Keep the picture's shape between them. Each head is "Width" or "Height" and the value in both units, the chosen one first: "32 beads · 51 mm", or "51 mm · 32 beads".
- **Keep the picture's shape:** between the sliders, a row on `surface` with a 1px `line-soft` edge, `radius-md`, padding 10 12: "Keep the picture's shape" in `control` over a line in `small` saying what the state does, and an AppSwitch at the right, on by default. On: "Width and height change together, so the picture is never squashed." Off: "Width and height are set apart, so the picture stretches." It replaces the chain and its `link` / `unlink` IconButton (ticket 0757), whose icon did not say what it did.
- **Shape kept (switch on):** moving either slider sets the other through the picture's (or the part's) shape and the Bead's aspect, so the picture is never distorted by accident. The slider moved last leads: it keeps exactly the value it is dragged to, and the other is rounded from it, so every Height is reachable on a tall picture. Both sliders show the same marks: the Width marks, and on Height the heights their grids come to. If the other side would pass its slider's end, it stays at the end and the one moved is recalculated so the shape is kept. Turning it off keeps the sizes on show; turning it on again takes the shape back from the slider moved last.
- **Shape free (switch off):** each side is its own, so the picture stretches on purpose. Each slider keeps its own marks (Width by the part's width, Height by the part's height), each range by the same rule. The second line gives one block only where both sides stand for the same one, else "About 8 × 4 px of your picture = 1 bead".
- **The ends set themselves, on each slider.** The min is 8 beads. The max is the picture's (or the part's) width or height in pixels, one bead per pixel, at most 300 beads (the Large Schema line); a picture or part under 75 px on a side goes up to 4× that, still at most 300, so small pixel art can be made bigger. On Width the ends read "8" and "256 beads (all the picture's detail)", or in mm through the Bead ("13" and "410 mm (all the picture's detail)"); Height keeps "max 256". With the shape kept, a Height the Width's shape takes past 300 shows its real value with the thumb at the end, and the Large Schema note below.
- **Marks** where one bead is a whole number of pixels: n × n px for n = 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 where the width divides by n, and, when enlarging, every pixel 2 × 2 or 4 × 4 beads, each inside the range. Each mark is labelled with the size it stands for in the chosen unit ("16 beads", "40 mm"), never the source pixels per bead; the mark at the max (one bead per pixel) has the max's label only.
- **On a mark the other side snaps to the pixel grid too (shape kept):** the Schema is the picture's pixels divided by n both ways, exact ("32×24 beads", "Each 8×8 px square of your picture = 1 bead"), as 8:1 used to be. Between marks the other side follows the picture's physical shape through the Bead, rounded to whole beads ("About 4.3 × 3.5 px of your picture = 1 bead"). So on a mark a non-square Bead makes the piece a little wider or taller than the picture, which the cm shows.
- Under the Width slider in `small`: "Fewer beads, bigger blocks, less detail. More beads, smaller blocks, more detail. The marks are widths where the picture divides cleanly."
- **The result box:** a box on `surface` (`elevated` in dark, a 2px `line` edge in high contrast), `radius-lg`, padding 10 12, the `label` "result" over a `meta` line in `body`: the grid size in `ink` at 500 ("32×24 beads": writing.md's Grid size, no spaces), the measured size through the Bead ("5.1 × 3.1 cm"), . A second line in `small`, `muted`, says what one bead takes from the picture, pixels first and live while sliding ("Each 8×8 px square of your picture = 1 bead" on a mark; "1 px of your picture = 2 × 2 beads" on an enlarging mark; "About 4.3 × 3.5 px of your picture = 1 bead" between marks). `role="status"`, read once after a change settles. With a part of the beads kept, its first line is the kept size and its measure, then "kept from 46×100".
- With A part of the picture, under the result box in `small`: "Colors follow the crop. Width and height stay as set, and drop only when the crop has fewer pixels than they allow."
- **Large Schema:** a Note, "Large Schema: more than 300 beads on a side. Converting and drawing take longer." Each slider stops at 300, so it shows when a side the shape sets passes 300 (a tall picture or part at a wide Width).
- **Opening size** (ticket 0863): a small piece a beginner would start with, not the most detailed one. Width opens on the mark nearest 60 beads when one is within a fifth of it (a 240 px picture at 60, each 4×4 px one bead; 1920 px at 60, each 32×32), else at 60 beads; a picture narrower than 60 px opens at one bead per pixel. Height follows in the picture's shape. All the picture's detail is one drag away, at the slider's end. Choosing A part of the picture later keeps both as set. (It opened at the maximum, so a small photo came out 240 × 160, 38 400 beads.)
- Retired with ticket 0747: By Pixel to bead ratio (its presets, Custom and the explainer; PixelToBeadRatio card), By size (the cm unit, Width and Height fields, Keep picture proportions, picture zoom and the pan under the Frame) and the slow-framing warning that went with the pan.

## 4 Colors

- **Colors:** a ValueSlider (ValueSlider card) from 1 to every color the picture (or the part) has, at most 256: the distinct colors its beads sample, counted before the match (the build, ticket 0747: counting after the match would match every sampled color on every change, for a number the slider could not reach anyway). Its head "Colors" and "8 colors", its ends "1" and "max 9". It starts at 12, or at the max where that is smaller, and a part with fewer colors pulls it down to them. Under it in `small`: "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: every color the picture has."; with more colors than 256, "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: the picture has more, reduced to as many." Under Exact, whose colors are no shop's, it begins "Each color is one bead color." instead (ticket 0863). It replaces Colors at most. The 256 (a palette image's count) was set with the build: a photo under Exact had 55 000 colors at the max and took two minutes there, and a pattern of that many colors is the picture, not a pattern; at 256 every move is under half a second.
- **Leaving a color out** is the preview's **Make empty** tool (Preview pane), not a switch here: one line in `body` with the 16px `eyedropper` icon, "To leave a color out, pick **Make empty** under the preview and tap that color." Color by color's Empty buttons do the same (FoundColorRow card).
- **match to:** under the line, the `label` "match to" over one SegmentedControl across the pane, "{Bead} palette" | Default Palette | Exact, opening on the **Bead's palette** (ticket 0863; it opened on Exact, ticket 0758, so a stranger got hex codes no shop sells), or on Default Palette for a Bead with no Color catalog. The first option names the Bead as the palette's Bead colors entry does, its maker, name and shape and no finish ("Toho Round 11/0 palette"; in Russian "Палитра Toho Round 11/0"), and is there only with a Color catalog. Its options share the row by the width of their text, not equally, so the long name wraps to two lines at most and the others stay on one.
- **Under the Bead's palette only:** one line in `meta-small` counting the codes the match uses, "codes in 11/0: 1 211": the codes made in the Bead's size, discontinued ones left out. The match itself has no filter (spec 0645's Include discontinued is left out of this design); the Bead colors list in a row's Change picker has the BeadColors card's filters (ticket 0713).
