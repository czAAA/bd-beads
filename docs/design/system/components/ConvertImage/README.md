# ConvertImage

The Convert image page: an App page (ADR 0085, ADR 0086) where a picture becomes a new Project, a live bead preview beside one of five steps at a time, every change shown at once (spec 0645, ADR 0010). It replaces the size dialog (ticket 342) and the framing step in the canvas box (ticket 58). Designed in ticket 0646 (design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/STCJ6XW9qBXTDJZUEHvJuD) as a large popup, built in ticket 0649 (`src/components/import/ConvertImageWizard.vue`); Picture, Size and Colors reworked in ticket 0747 (design canvas "Convert image wizard · sliders", https://claude.ai/artifact/W8sTQApy6JYsF2RBkGFdDA); made an App page in ticket 0756, which retired the Modal, the phone sheet, the footer and the step rail.

## Frame

- **A page of the canvas box**, at every size: the Canvas strip on top (Menu kept; a Close × in place of Project and Frame, and none of the editor's tools, zoom or Export), the page between, and the Progress bar below (ProgressBar and CanvasStrip cards). It takes the Canvas's cell; the editor's side panels (Project, Frame, Export) are hidden, and the editor stays as it was beneath it.
- **From 1024 px wide in landscape:** the preview on the left, taking the rest; the step on the right, `convert-sections-width` (440; `--convert-sections-width-narrow`, 400, while the page is under 1100 wide), a 1px `panel-rule` between them, scrolling on its own. The preview is on `box`.
- **Under 1024 px, or in portrait:** the preview above the step, at most `--convert-preview-phone` (300) tall and never under `--convert-preview-min` (160), 440 on a portrait tablet (`--convert-preview-tablet`), a 1px `panel-rule` under it; the step fills the rest and scrolls (PhoneForms card).
- Opens from New Project's Convert image, the Menu's Convert image row, or by dropping or pasting a picture on the editor, which opens it with the picture set. Escape belongs to the page: it asks to leave, like Close.

## Leaving

- **Close (×) in the strip, Escape, and every Menu action that leaves** (New Project, a pick from the Projects library, Import, Homepage) go through the page's leave guard (ADR 0085). With no picture set, the page closes at once.
- **Once a picture is set** the guard asks in a confirmation (ConfirmDialogs card): the title "Leave the converter?", the message "Everything you set up here will be lost.", **Back to converter** (the default: it takes focus, and Escape means it) and **Leave** (danger-filled). Leave closes the page and the Menu action goes on; Back to converter stays with everything as it was.
- **Either way the person lands on the Project that was open before**, with its Undo and Redo untouched: converting makes no Undo step, and nothing the page does touches the open Project. Create Project adds the new Project and opens it, as New Project does.

## Steps

- Five steps, one at a time: 1 Picture, 2 Bead & Technique, 3 Size, 4 Colors, 5 Review. The step in hand is a section padded 14 20 18 (12 16 16 under 1024 px) with a 1px `panel-rule` under it. Its header (28 or more) holds the step disc, the title in `control` at 600, and at the right a `meta` summary of what it holds now: "delica 11/0 · loom", "32×24 · 5.1 × 3.1 cm", "bead colors · 8", "≈ 1.8 g". A new step starts at the top of its pane and moves focus to its heading.
- **Step disc** (`--step-disc`, 20): the number in `meta-small` inside a 1px `line-strong` ring on `overlay-fill`. Picture's disc becomes a 12px check on `ink` once a picture is set.
- **The Progress bar holds the steps** (ProgressBar card): a counter ("2/5"), the track with a dot per step and its name under it (the registry names: picture, bead, size, colors, review; `meta-small`, `meta-tiny` on a phone), Back and Next. A dot jumps to its step. **Until a picture is set, the steps after Picture are shut:** their dots are `aria-disabled` in `faint`, Next is disabled with "Add a picture first", and none can be reached. On Review, Next becomes **Create Project** (primary), disabled without a picture. There is no footer line or Cancel: Close is in the strip, and the result is in the Size step's box and its summary.

## Preview pane

- `box` fill. The picture drawn as beads by the Project renderer in the Frame's space (in the Bead's own cell, the dragging draft look), redrawn on every change; the Frame line in `ink` (1.5px, with a 1.5px `canvas` halo so it reads over any picture); the size tag "32×24" in `meta-small` on `ink` above the Frame's top-left corner.
- **Whole picture** (weave, 1 Picture): the whole picture is the Frame. No handles.
- **A part** is drawn like a Selection (ticket 0757): drag on the preview to draw the rectangle, from the pixel the drag began on to the one it ends on (a plain tap draws nothing); a drag that begins outside it draws a new one; a drag that begins inside it, the `move` pointer over the Frame, moves it whole, kept on the picture; its handles adjust it. It only chooses *what* is woven: the Width and Height in beads stay as they were, dropping only when the new part has fewer pixels than they allow. On touch a drag on the beads draws or moves the part (`touch-action: none`) rather than scrolling the view. The whole picture stays drawn, dimmed outside the part by `--framing-dim`, and the part is the Frame, drawn as beads. Four 16px corner handles (`--crop-handle`, `canvas` fill, 2px `ink` edge, radius 3, `--crop-handle-radius`) and four 28 × 8 bars at the edges' middles (`--crop-bar-length`, `--crop-bar-width`) move its edges, each named for its edge or corner ("Crop: top-left corner", a control registry entry), in whole picture pixels. Choosing A part puts the handles 10% in from each edge, so there is something to drag at once. A focused handle moves one pixel per arrow, ten with Shift. The part is at least 8 pixels a side.
- **Zoom** (the view only; it never changes what is framed): zoom out, the level, zoom in and Fit, floating at the bottom right on `overlay-fill` with `elevation-3`, the Canvas strip's buttons from the same registry actions (ticket 0650): their Tooltips with the key chips, and at a limit zoom out or zoom in is disabled with the strip's reason ("Already zoomed out as far as it goes."), still hoverable and focusable. Ctrl/⌘ + plus, minus and 0 zoom the preview and Fit it while the page is open, never the canvas beneath it, and the browser's page zoom stays claimed. Pinch and wheel too. On a phone it is compact and sits above the picture (PhoneForms card).
- **Treat as empty** (ticket 0758) is a switch in the Colors step (4 Colors), never over the picture, so it cannot overflow it. The preview only takes it up: while it is on, the pointer is the eyedropper (the Canvas's own), the bead under it is ringed (2px `ink`, `canvas` halo), the part's handles hide, and a tap sets that found color to empty. Escape ends it, leaving the Colors step ends it, and so does turning the switch off.
- **Empty:** the pane is the drop zone, inset 24 (16 on a phone): a 1.5px dashed `line-strong` edge, `radius-lg`, the 32px `image` icon, "Drop a picture here" in `control` and "or paste one with Ctrl+V" ("or choose a file below" on touch) in `meta`.
- **A picture dragged over the page:** a 2px dashed `accent-strong` edge inset 12 on `surface` at 92%, the icon and "Drop to use this picture" in `accent-strong`, the limits in `meta`. Dropping replaces the picture and keeps every other setting; weave goes back to Whole picture.
- **A picture dragged over the app** (page closed): the scrim and a centred 400 × 220 card (`--picture-drop-width`, `--picture-drop-height`) in the same drop look, "Drop to convert image" and "PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP · up to 10 MB". Dropping opens the page with the picture set.
- The pane is `role="img"` named "Preview: 32×24 beads, 8 colors"; its controls are buttons.

## 1 Picture

- **No picture:** Choose file (primary, `import` icon, the FileButton's `accept`), Paste (`paste` icon; the async Clipboard API, and Ctrl/Cmd+V works anywhere on the page), and Take photo on a touch device (a file input with `capture`; the icon set has no camera, so it has no icon), all `field-height`, in aligned equal-width columns (a grid with 160 minimum columns, so a narrow pane stacks them one under another); a button never wraps sideways.
- **Paste with nothing to paste** says nothing: no line appears (ticket 0756).
- **With a picture:** a 64 × 48 thumbnail of the pixels (1px `line` edge; `--convert-thumb-width`, `--convert-thumb-height`), the file name in `control` and "256×192 px · png" in `meta`; then two aligned rows of small (28) in-box buttons, equal columns: Turn 90° (`rotate` icon) and Flip (left to right; no icon in the set); then the `label` `replace` over Choose file and Paste. Turn and Flip change the pixels before sampling and set weave back to Whole picture.
- **weave:** a panel row, the `label` "weave" at the left and a small SegmentedControl at the right, Whole picture | A part (Whole picture is the default). A part shows the part and its handles on the preview and adds one line under the row in `small`: "Drag on the preview to draw the part to weave, drag inside it to move it, or pull its handles to adjust it." Colors then follow the part; Width and Height stay as set.
- **cut out** (spec 0761, ticket 0764; built by tickets 0765 and 0767 to 0769): a panel row under weave, shown once a picture is set, built like the weave row: the `label` "cut out" at the left and at the right a small SegmentedControl (SegmentedControl card: `role="radiogroup"` named by the label, one Tab stop, ←/→/Home/End move the choice, which follows focus) of three chips, **Off** · **Simple** · **the AI chip**, in that order. Off is the default every time the page opens; a choice applies at once and the preview follows. The chips stay enabled while a model works: choosing Off or Simple then stops it, as Cancel does.
  - **The chips:** Off and Simple are words, `tab` (Inter 500 13); the AI chip is icon only, `cut-out-ai` at 16 with its AI mark, and no text. Every chip is `--cut-out-chip` (76) wide, whatever its content, so the three are one width in every language: the widest word is Russian «Простая» (54 px), which with the chip's 10 + 10 padding needs 74, and 76 leaves 2 px to spare. The control is 238 px: three 76 chips, two 2px gaps between them (`space-2`), the 2px inset (`space-2`) on each side and the 1px `line-strong` edge on each side (SegmentedControl card).
  - **Fit:** the row wraps. Where the pane cannot hold the label, a 12 gap and the control on one line (Polish "wycinanie" is the widest label, 70 px, so the row needs 320 px: a 320 px phone's 288 px pane wraps it, a 390 one's 358 does not), the control goes under the label, at the pane's left; a chip never wraps or cuts its word.
  - **Until ticket 0767** the control holds Off and Simple only, each still 76 wide.
  - **Simple** shows its **Tolerance** slider under the row (ticket 0765): a ValueSlider (ValueSlider card) as wide as the pane, its head "Tolerance" and the value, a plain whole number from 1 to 40 (hundredths of an OKLab distance from the border's colors, opening at 10), its ends "1" and "max 40", no marks, and under it in `small`: "Higher takes away more of a shaded background, lower keeps more of the subject." Off shows nothing under it. The preview follows every step of the slider at once.
  - **What Simple takes away:** the colors covering the picture's border are its background (a color covering under 2% of the border is the subject touching it, and stays); a fill from every border pixel spreads to its four neighbours while they are within Tolerance of one of those colors, and through transparent pixels. What it reaches is empty: no bead, and its colors are never found, so the Colors step counts and matches the subject's colors alone.
- **Fit to subject** (ticket 0765): under the cut out row and what it shows (the Tolerance slider), while Cut out is on, a small (28) `in-box` button at its own width at the pane's left, the `cut-out` icon at 16 before "Fit to subject" (the subject in Select's frame: the part round what was kept). Its Tooltip is "Fit to subject" over "Set the part to weave round what the cut out kept."; when the cut out kept nothing it is disabled (`aria-disabled`, still focusable) with the reason "The cut out kept nothing to fit to." A press sets weave to A part, the part the smallest box of whole pixels round the subject (grown evenly to the part's 8 pixels a side where smaller); the Width and Height follow A part's rule (they stay, dropping only when the part has fewer pixels). Turn, Flip and a new picture work the cut out out again and set weave back to Whole picture.
  - **The AI chip's name:** its Tooltip is the name alone, "Cut out with AI · runs on this device", and so is its accessible name (`aria-label`). It is the one place the converter says AI; the screen never does (README, Iconography). Chosen, the chip is the SegmentedControl's `ink` fill and its whole icon is `canvas`, the mark included: `ai` falls under 3:1 on `ink` in dark and high contrast, and the sparkle's shape still marks it.
  - **The warning dot** (ticket 0768): on a device whose last run stopped (the run-in-progress flag found still set when the page opens), a `--ai-warning-dot` (7) dot in `warning`, ringed 1.5 px in the chip's own fill so it stands off the chip's edge, its centre 6 px in from the chip's top-right corner. The Tooltip gains the body "Cut out with AI stopped last time, probably for lack of memory on this device. Try Simple, or try again.", and the chip's accessible description (`aria-describedby`) is that sentence, so the dot is never the only sign (like the Message's warning icon, it is not held to 3:1 in light). The chip stays enabled and is never hidden; a run that finishes clears the dot.
- **Under the AI chip** (ticket 0769), once the subject is found: one row, under the cut out row and lined up with the chips' left edge (the pane's left once they wrap), of the **tap add** chip, the **tap remove** chip, an 8 gap, and **Reset**.
  - **Tap add and tap remove:** 28 square icon-only toggles (`aria-pressed`), the `secondary` IconButton (`elevated`, 1px `line-strong` edge, `radius-sm`), `tap-add-ai` and `tap-remove-ai` at 16 with their marks, 4 apart. At most one is on: pressing one turns the other off, pressing the one that is on turns it off. On, it is `ink` with its icon `canvas`, mark included, as the chosen AI chip. While one is on a tap on the preview adds the region under it to the subject, or takes it away; while neither is, the preview does what it does without AI (A part's drawing). Tooltips: "Tap to add, with AI" over "Tap a part the cut out missed to keep it."; "Tap to take away, with AI" over "Tap a part the cut out kept by mistake to take it away." Their accessible names are the Tooltip names.
  - **Reset:** a small (28) `in-box` text button, "Reset", with no icon and no mark (it runs no model: it goes back to the automatic result). Tooltip "Reset" over "Drop every tap and go back to the first cut out."; with no taps yet it is disabled (`aria-disabled`, still focusable) with the reason "No taps to reset yet."
- **The download note** (tickets 0767, 0768): the first press of the AI chip on a device without the model, and the first press of tap add or tap remove without the tap model, runs nothing yet. The chip shows as chosen and, under the cut out row, in the place of what the choice shows under it, the note asks first: a block like a Note (Note card: `surface`, `radius-sm`, padding 8 10, `meta` in `muted`, as wide as the pane's content), here with buttons, as it asks before a download.
  - **Text:** "Runs on this device: nothing is uploaded. Downloads {size} once. On older or smaller devices it can take a while." {size} is the model's in whole MB with a no-break space ("44 MB"; writing.md, Numbers and units). On a browser that reports under 4 GB of memory, a second line in `body`: "This device reports under 4 GB of memory, so it may be slow." The line warns and never blocks.
  - **Buttons**, under the text, 8 apart, left-aligned: **Download**, a small (28) `in-box` button with `cut-out-ai` at 15 (its mark in `ai` on `elevated`) before the label, the one AI-marked button; and **Cancel**, a small text button. Download is not primary: the accent fill would put `ai` on orange, and the page's one primary stays Next or Create Project. The note takes focus on Download when it shows; Escape stays the page's (it asks to leave). Download starts the AI Loading's download in the note's place (Loading card, AI variant); Cancel takes the choice back to the one before (Off or Simple, or no tap chip on).
- **While a model works** the AI Loading (Loading card, AI variant) sits in the same place, under the cut out row: its line and MB while downloading, its beads and "Finding the subject…" while thinking, with the shimmer over the preview and Cancel. Cancel stops it at once and takes the choice back to the one before. Once found, the AI result stays while the picture does, so flipping between Off, Simple and the AI chip is instant.
- **Note**, only until the first picture is set: "Drop a picture anywhere on the app, choose a file or paste one with Ctrl+V. PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB and 16 megapixels." On touch: "Choose a file, paste a picture or take a photo. …"
- **Errors** (HEIC, SVG, too big, too many pixels, unreadable, wrong format) keep the app's sentences, in an inline Message under the buttons (`danger` edge, the warning icon, `role="alert"`). A picture already set stays set.

## 2 Bead & Technique

- The New Project form's own sections in panel rows (ProjectPanel card): bead (Brand, Type and Size PanelPickers), technique (PanelPicker, with the Drop row for peyote and brick stitch). They start from the form's values and are the page's own copy. No Note: nothing here needs explaining.

## 3 Size

- **slide in:** a panel row, the `label` "slide in" at the left and a small mono SegmentedControl at the right, beads | mm (beads is the default). It changes only how Width reads: the slider moves in whole beads either way.
- **Width and Height:** two ValueSliders (ValueSlider card), Width first, with the chain between them (ticket 0757). Each head is "Width" or "Height" and the value in both units, the chosen one first: "32 beads · 51 mm", or "51 mm · 32 beads".
- **The chain:** a 28 `secondary` IconButton centred between the sliders, on a 1px `line` rule across the pane's width either side of it, with the `link` icon at 16 (linked, the default) or the `unlink` icon (unlinked); a press toggles it. Its Tooltip says what the state does: linked, "Linked: the picture's shape is kept; width and height change together, following the picture's proportions."; unlinked, "Unlinked: width and height are set separately, so the picture stretches." The names are "Linked" and "Unlinked" (registry actions `size-linked`, `size-unlinked`).
- **Linked:** moving either slider sets the other through the picture's (or the part's) shape and the Bead's aspect, so the picture is never distorted by accident. The slider moved last leads: it keeps exactly the value it is dragged to, and the other is rounded from it, so every Height is reachable on a tall picture. Both sliders show the same marks: the Width marks, and on Height the heights their grids come to. If the other side would pass its slider's end, it stays at the end and the one moved is recalculated so the shape is kept. Unlinking keeps the sizes on show; linking again takes the shape back from the slider moved last.
- **Unlinked:** each side is its own, so the picture stretches on purpose. Each slider keeps its own marks (Width by the part's width, Height by the part's height), each range by the same rule. The second line gives one block only where both sides stand for the same one, else "About 8 × 4 px of your picture = 1 bead".
- **The ends set themselves, on each slider.** The min is 8 beads. The max is the picture's (or the part's) width or height in pixels, one bead per pixel, at most 300 beads (the Large Schema line); a picture or part under 75 px on a side goes up to 4× that, still at most 300, so small pixel art can be made bigger. On Width the ends read "8" and "256 beads (all the picture's detail)", or in mm through the Bead ("13" and "410 mm (all the picture's detail)"); Height keeps "max 256". Linked, a Height the Width's shape takes past 300 shows its real value with the thumb at the end, and the Large Schema note below.
- **Marks** where one bead is a whole number of pixels: n × n px for n = 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 where the width divides by n, and, when enlarging, every pixel 2 × 2 or 4 × 4 beads, each inside the range. Each mark is labelled with the size it stands for in the chosen unit ("16 beads", "40 mm"), never the source pixels per bead; the mark at the max (one bead per pixel) has the max's label only.
- **On a mark the other side snaps to the pixel grid too (linked):** the Schema is the picture's pixels divided by n both ways, exact ("32×24 beads", "Each 8×8 px square of your picture = 1 bead"), as 8:1 used to be. Between marks the other side follows the picture's physical shape through the Bead, rounded to whole beads ("About 4.3 × 3.5 px of your picture = 1 bead"). So on a mark a non-square Bead makes the piece a little wider or taller than the picture, which the cm shows.
- Under the Width slider in `small`: "Fewer beads, bigger blocks, less detail. More beads, smaller blocks, more detail. The marks are widths where the picture divides cleanly."
- **The result box:** a box on `surface` (`elevated` in dark, a 2px `line` edge in high contrast), `radius-lg`, padding 10 12, the `label` "result" over a `meta` line in `body`: the grid size in `ink` at 500 ("32×24 beads": writing.md's Grid size, no spaces), the measured size through the Bead ("5.1 × 3.1 cm"), . A second line in `small`, `muted`, says what one bead takes from the picture, pixels first and live while sliding ("Each 8×8 px square of your picture = 1 bead" on a mark; "1 px of your picture = 2 × 2 beads" on an enlarging mark; "About 4.3 × 3.5 px of your picture = 1 bead" between marks). `role="status"`, read once after a change settles.
- With A part, under the result box in `small`: "Colors follow the part. Width and height stay as set, and drop only when the part has fewer pixels than they allow."
- **Large Schema:** a Note, "Large Schema: more than 300 beads on a side. Converting and drawing take longer." Each slider stops at 300, so it shows when a linked side passes 300 (a tall picture or part at a wide Width).
- **Opening size:** Width opens at its maximum for the picture, and Height at its own, linked: the most detailed result the ranges allow, which for pixel art is one bead per pixel. A picture wider than 300 px opens at 300 beads, which makes a tall one a Large Schema at once. Choosing A part later keeps both as set.
- Retired with ticket 0747: By Pixel to bead ratio (its presets, Custom and the explainer; PixelToBeadRatio card), By size (the cm unit, Width and Height fields, Keep picture proportions, picture zoom and the pan under the Frame) and the slow-framing warning that went with the pan.

## 4 Colors

- **Colors:** a ValueSlider (ValueSlider card) from 1 to every color the picture (or the part) has, at most 256: the distinct colors its beads sample, counted before the match (the build, ticket 0747: counting after the match would match every sampled color on every change, for a number the slider could not reach anyway). Its head "Colors" and "8 colors", its ends "1" and "max 9". It starts at 12, or at the max where that is smaller, and a part with fewer colors pulls it down to them. Under it in `small`: "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: every color the picture has."; with more colors than 256, "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: the picture has more, reduced to as many." It replaces Colors at most. The 256 (a palette image's count) was set with the build: a photo under Exact had 55 000 colors at the max and took two minutes there, and a pattern of that many colors is the picture, not a pattern; at 256 every move is under half a second.
- **Treat as empty:** after the slider, a row with the label "Treat as empty" in `control` and an AppSwitch (SwitchAndFileButton card) at the right, with the Tooltip "Tap a color on the preview to leave its beads out." (ticket 0650). While it is on, the preview shows the eyedropper (Preview pane) and each tap makes that color empty; the summary of Color by color counts it.
- **match to:** under it, one SegmentedControl, "{Bead} palette" | Default Palette | Exact, opening on **Exact** (ticket 0758). The first option names the Bead as the palette's Bead colors entry does, its maker, name and shape and no finish ("Toho Round 11/0 palette"; in Russian "Палитра Toho Round 11/0"), and is there only with a Color catalog. Its options share the row by the width of their text, not equally, so the long name wraps to two lines at most and the others stay on one.
- **Under the Bead's palette only:** one line in `meta-small` counting the codes the match uses, "codes in 11/0: 1 211": the codes made in the Bead's size, discontinued ones left out. The match itself has no filter (spec 0645's Include discontinued is left out of this design); the Bead colors list in a row's Change picker has the BeadColors card's filters (ticket 0713).
- **A Note**, only under the Bead's palette: "Each color in the picture is matched to the closest color in {Bead}’s catalog." Default Palette and Exact have none.
- **Color by color:** a DisclosureRow (DisclosureRow card) with no icon, closed every time the page opens: the label "Color by color", the summary "8 colors · 1 empty" (the colors the beads use, as the step's summary counts them, then the found colors set to empty; the empty part left out when nothing is empty), the chevron. Escape is the page's (it asks to leave), not the row's. Open, it holds colors found, the count at the right, the heading row "From picture | Becomes | Beads | Change | Empty" (it stays at the top of the step as the list scrolls) and one FoundColorRow per found color, most beads first (FoundColorRow card), pushing Review down. Treat as empty works with it closed; its summary counts the change.

## 5 Review

- Name and Maker's name, TextFields stacked (the build stacks them at every width: side by side, a long label such as Spanish "Nombre de quien lo hace" and its "optional" did not fit the 400px pane), each "optional"; Name's placeholder is the file's name, and a Name left blank is the file's name.
- **Beads needed** (BeadsNeeded card), the app's own, for the Project Create Project would make: one box (`surface`, `radius-lg`, padding 4 0) holding the section row, collapsed every time Review is shown, with the total on the closed row ("128 · ≈ 0.64 g"). Pressed, it opens in place to the usual colors, counts and grams, five colors then "Show all", with the weight Note; a real catalog color reads "code name" (ColorLabel), named from the Bead's Color catalog the page already holds. There is no second buy list: no size, colors, weight or to-buy rows, no per-color tubes and no Show details.

## Keyboard and screen readers

- The page is a `region` named "Convert image". Focus enters on Choose file (or, with a picture set, the first control of Picture).
- Tab order: the strip (Menu, Close); the preview's controls (the crop handles with A part, zoom); the step in hand (in Picture: weave, cut out, then the Tolerance slider, or the tap chips and Reset, or the download note's buttons, or Cancel while a model works, then Fit to subject); the Progress bar (one Tab stop, arrows along its dots and buttons).
- The step is a `region` named by its title; a new step moves focus to its heading. The result line and the found-color count are `role="status"`, read once after a change settles. Errors are `role="alert"`.
