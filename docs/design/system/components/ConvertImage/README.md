# ConvertImage

The Convert image page: an App page (ADR 0085, ADR 0086) where a picture becomes a new Project, a live bead preview beside one of five steps at a time, every change shown at once (spec 0645, ADR 0010). It replaces the size dialog (ticket 342) and the framing step in the canvas box (ticket 58). Designed in ticket 0646 (design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/STCJ6XW9qBXTDJZUEHvJuD) as a large popup, built in ticket 0649 (`src/components/import/ConvertImageWizard.vue`); Picture, Size and Colors reworked in ticket 0747 (design canvas "Convert image wizard · sliders", https://claude.ai/artifact/W8sTQApy6JYsF2RBkGFdDA); made an App page in ticket 0756, which retired the Modal, the phone sheet, the footer and the step rail.

## Frame

- **A page of the canvas box**, at every size: the Canvas strip on top (Menu kept; a Close × in place of Project and Frame, and none of the editor's tools, zoom or Export), the page between, and the Progress bar below (ProgressBar and CanvasStrip cards). It takes the Canvas's cell; the editor's side panels (Project, Frame, Export) are hidden, and the editor stays as it was beneath it.
- **From 1024 px wide in landscape:** the preview on the left, taking the rest; the step on the right, `convert-sections-width` (440; `--convert-sections-width-narrow`, 400, while the page is under 1100 wide), a 1px `panel-rule` between them, scrolling on its own. The preview is on `box`.
- **Under 1024 px, or in portrait:** the preview above the step, at most `--convert-preview-phone` (300) tall and never under `--convert-preview-min` (160), 440 on a portrait tablet (`--convert-preview-tablet`), a 1px `panel-rule` under it; the step fills the rest and scrolls (PhoneForms card).
- Opens from New Project's Convert image, the Menu's Convert image row, or by dropping or pasting a picture on the editor, which opens it with the picture set. Escape belongs to the page: it asks to leave, like Close.

## Leaving

- **Close (×) in the strip, Escape, and every Menu action that leaves** (New Project, a pick from the Projects library, Import, Homepage) go through the page's leave guard (ADR 0085). With no picture set, the page closes at once.
- **Once a picture is set** the guard asks in a confirmation (ConfirmDialogs card): the title "Leave the converter?", the message "Everything you set up here will be lost.", **Back to converter** (the default: it takes focus, and Escape means it) and **Leave** (danger-filled). Leave closes the page and the Menu action goes on; Back to converter stays with everything as it was.
- **Either way the person lands on the Project that was open before**, with its Undo and Redo untouched: converting makes no Undo step, and nothing the page does touches the open Project. Create Project adds the new Project and opens it, as New Project does.

## Steps

- Five steps, one at a time: 1 Picture, 2 Bead & Technique, 3 Size, 4 Colors, 5 Review. The step in hand is a section padded 14 20 18 (12 16 16 under 1024 px) with a 1px `panel-rule` under it. Its header (28 or more) holds the step disc, the title in `control` at 600, and at the right a `meta` summary of what it holds now: "delica 11/0 · loom", "32×24 · 5.1 × 3.1 cm", "bead colors · 8", "≈ 1.8 g". A new step starts at the top of its pane and moves focus to its heading.
- **Step disc** (`--step-disc`, 20): the number in `meta-small` inside a 1px `line-strong` ring on `overlay-fill`. Picture's disc becomes a 12px check on `ink` once a picture is set.
- **The Progress bar holds the steps** (ProgressBar card): a counter ("2/5"), the track with a dot per step and its name under it (the registry names: picture, bead, size, colors, review; `meta-small`, `meta-tiny` on a phone), Back and Next. A dot jumps to its step. **Until a picture is set, the steps after Picture are shut:** their dots are `aria-disabled` in `faint`, Next is disabled with "Add a picture first", and none can be reached. On Review, Next becomes **Create Project** (primary), disabled without a picture. There is no footer line or Cancel: Close is in the strip, and the result is in the Size step's box and its summary.

## Preview pane

- `box` fill. The picture drawn as beads by the Project renderer in the Frame's space (in the Bead's own cell, the dragging draft look), redrawn on every change; the Frame line in `ink` (1.5px, with a 1.5px `canvas` halo so it reads over any picture); the size tag "32×24" in `meta-small` on `ink` above the Frame's top-left corner.
- **Whole picture** (weave, 1 Picture): the whole picture is the Frame. No handles.
- **A part** is drawn like a Selection (ticket 0757): drag on the preview to draw the rectangle, from the pixel the drag began on to the one it ends on (a plain tap draws nothing); a drag that begins outside it draws a new one; a drag that begins inside it, the `move` pointer over the Frame, moves it whole, kept on the picture; its handles adjust it. It only chooses *what* is woven: the Width and Height in beads stay as they were, dropping only when the new part has fewer pixels than they allow. On touch a drag on the beads draws or moves the part (`touch-action: none`) rather than scrolling the view. The whole picture stays drawn, dimmed outside the part by `--framing-dim`, and the part is the Frame, drawn as beads. Four 16px corner handles (`--crop-handle`, `canvas` fill, 2px `ink` edge, radius 3, `--crop-handle-radius`) and four 28 × 8 bars at the edges' middles (`--crop-bar-length`, `--crop-bar-width`) move its edges, each named for its edge or corner ("Crop: top-left corner", a control registry entry), in whole picture pixels. Choosing A part puts the handles 10% in from each edge, so there is something to drag at once. A focused handle moves one pixel per arrow, ten with Shift. The part is at least 8 pixels a side.
- **Zoom** (the view only; it never changes what is framed): zoom out, the level, zoom in and Fit, floating at the bottom right on `overlay-fill` with `elevation-3`, the Canvas strip's buttons from the same registry actions (ticket 0650): their Tooltips with the key chips, and at a limit zoom out or zoom in is disabled with the strip's reason ("Already zoomed out as far as it goes."), still hoverable and focusable. Ctrl/⌘ + plus, minus and 0 zoom the preview and Fit it while the page is open, never the canvas beneath it, and the browser's page zoom stays claimed. Pinch and wheel too. On a phone it is compact and sits above the picture (PhoneForms card).
- **Treat as empty:** a 34px `box` Button at the top left (Button card: the `eyedropper` icon at 15 and its name; an IconButton, icon only, on a phone or a shrunk preview), with the Tooltip "Tap a color on the preview to leave its beads out." (ticket 0650). Pressed: `accent-strong` icon and text and a 2px inset `accent-strong` edge, `aria-pressed`. While it is on, the pointer is the eyedropper, the bead under it is ringed (2px `ink`, `canvas` halo), and a banner on `ink` says "Tap a color on the preview to make it empty" (an `esc` key chip with a keyboard). A tap sets that found color to empty; Escape or the button ends it.
- **Empty:** the pane is the drop zone, inset 24 (16 on a phone): a 1.5px dashed `line-strong` edge, `radius-lg`, the 32px `image` icon, "Drop a picture here" in `control` and "or paste one with Ctrl+V" ("or choose a file below" on touch) in `meta`.
- **A picture dragged over the page:** a 2px dashed `accent-strong` edge inset 12 on `surface` at 92%, the icon and "Drop to use this picture" in `accent-strong`, the limits in `meta`. Dropping replaces the picture and keeps every other setting; weave goes back to Whole picture.
- **A picture dragged over the app** (page closed): the scrim and a centred 400 × 220 card (`--picture-drop-width`, `--picture-drop-height`) in the same drop look, "Drop to convert image" and "PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP · up to 10 MB". Dropping opens the page with the picture set.
- The pane is `role="img"` named "Preview: 32×24 beads, 8 colors"; its controls are buttons.

## 1 Picture

- **No picture:** Choose file (primary, `import` icon, the FileButton's `accept`), Paste (`paste` icon; the async Clipboard API, and Ctrl/Cmd+V works anywhere on the page), and Take photo on a touch device (a file input with `capture`; the icon set has no camera, so it has no icon), all `field-height`, in aligned equal-width columns (a grid with 160 minimum columns, so a narrow pane stacks them one under another); a button never wraps sideways.
- **Paste with nothing to paste** says nothing: no line appears (ticket 0756).
- **With a picture:** a 64 × 48 thumbnail of the pixels (1px `line` edge; `--convert-thumb-width`, `--convert-thumb-height`), the file name in `control` and "256×192 px · png" in `meta`; then two aligned rows of small (28) in-box buttons, equal columns: Turn 90° (`rotate` icon) and Flip (left to right; no icon in the set); then the `label` `replace` over Choose file and Paste. Turn and Flip change the pixels before sampling and set weave back to Whole picture.
- **weave:** a panel row, the `label` "weave" at the left and a small SegmentedControl at the right, Whole picture | A part (Whole picture is the default). A part shows the part and its handles on the preview and adds one line under the row in `small`: "Drag on the preview to draw the part to weave, drag inside it to move it, or pull its handles to adjust it." Colors then follow the part; Width and Height stay as set.
- **Note**, only until the first picture is set: "Drop a picture anywhere on the app, choose a file or paste one with Ctrl+V. PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB and 16 megapixels." On touch: "Choose a file, paste a picture or take a photo. …"
- **Errors** (HEIC, SVG, too big, too many pixels, unreadable, wrong format) keep the app's sentences, in an inline Message under the buttons (`danger` edge, the warning icon, `role="alert"`). A picture already set stays set.

## 2 Bead & Technique

- The New Project form's own sections in panel rows (ProjectPanel card): bead (Brand, Type and Size PanelPickers), technique (PanelPicker, with the Drop row for peyote and brick stitch). They start from the form's values and are the page's own copy. No Note: nothing here needs explaining.

## 3 Size

- **slide in:** a panel row, the `label` "slide in" at the left and a small mono SegmentedControl at the right, beads | mm (beads is the default). It changes only how Width reads: the slider moves in whole beads either way.
- **Width and Height:** two ValueSliders (ValueSlider card), Width first, with the chain between them (ticket 0757). Each head is "Width" or "Height" and the value in both units, the chosen one first: "32 beads · 51 mm", or "51 mm · 32 beads".
- **The chain:** a 28 `secondary` IconButton centred between the sliders, on a 1px `line` rule across the pane's width either side of it, with the `link` icon at 16 (linked, the default) or the `unlink` icon (unlinked); a press toggles it. Its Tooltip says what the state does: linked, "Linked: the picture's shape is kept; width and height change together, following the picture's proportions."; unlinked, "Unlinked: width and height are set separately, so the picture stretches." The names are "Linked" and "Unlinked" (registry actions `size-linked`, `size-unlinked`).
- **Linked:** moving either slider sets the other through the picture's (or the part's) shape and the Bead's aspect, so the picture is never distorted by accident. The slider moved last leads: it keeps exactly the value it is dragged to, and the other is rounded from it, so every Height is reachable on a tall picture. Both sliders show the same marks: the Width marks, and on Height the heights their grids come to. If the other side would pass its slider's end, it stays at the end and the one moved is recalculated so the shape is kept. Unlinking keeps the sizes on show; linking again takes the shape back from the slider moved last.
- **Unlinked:** each side is its own, so the picture stretches on purpose. Each slider keeps its own marks (Width by the part's width, Height by the part's height), each range by the same rule. The result line says "1 bead = 8 × 8 px" only where both sides stand for the same block, else "1 bead ≈ 8 × 4 px".
- **The ends set themselves, on each slider.** The min is 8 beads. The max is the picture's (or the part's) width or height in pixels, one bead per pixel, at most 300 beads (the Large Schema line); a picture or part under 75 px on a side goes up to 4× that, still at most 300, so small pixel art can be made bigger. The ends read "8" and "max 256", or in mm through the Bead ("13" and "max 410 mm"). Linked, a Height the Width's shape takes past 300 shows its real value with the thumb at the end, and the Large Schema note below.
- **Marks** where one bead is a whole number of pixels: n × n px for n = 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 where the width divides by n, and, when enlarging, every pixel 2 × 2 or 4 × 4 beads, each inside the range. Labels: "8×8 px", "4×4 px"; "×2", "×4" for enlarging; the mark at the max (one bead per pixel) has the max's label only.
- **On a mark the other side snaps to the pixel grid too (linked):** the Schema is the picture's pixels divided by n both ways, exact ("32×24 beads", "1 bead = 8 × 8 px"), as 8:1 used to be. Between marks the other side follows the picture's physical shape through the Bead, rounded to whole beads ("1 bead ≈ 4.3 × 3.5 px"). So on a mark a non-square Bead makes the piece a little wider or taller than the picture, which the cm shows.
- Under the Width slider in `small`: "Max: one bead per picture pixel. On a mark, one bead covers a whole n × n pixels."
- **The result box:** a box on `surface` (`elevated` in dark, a 2px `line` edge in high contrast), `radius-lg`, padding 10 12, the `label` "result" over one `meta` line in `body`: the grid size in `ink` at 500 ("32×24 beads": writing.md's Grid size, no spaces), the measured size through the Bead ("5.1 × 3.1 cm"), and the pixels per bead ("1 bead = 8 × 8 px" on a mark; "1 px = 2 × 2 beads" on an enlarging mark; "1 bead ≈ 4.3 × 3.5 px" between marks). `role="status"`, read once after a change settles.
- With A part, under the result box in `small`: "Colors follow the part. Width and height stay as set, and drop only when the part has fewer pixels than they allow."
- **Large Schema:** a Note, "Large Schema: more than 300 beads on a side. Converting and drawing take longer." Each slider stops at 300, so it shows when a linked side passes 300 (a tall picture or part at a wide Width).
- **Opening size:** Width opens at its maximum for the picture, and Height at its own, linked: the most detailed result the ranges allow, which for pixel art is one bead per pixel. A picture wider than 300 px opens at 300 beads, which makes a tall one a Large Schema at once. Choosing A part later keeps both as set.
- Retired with ticket 0747: By Pixel to bead ratio (its presets, Custom and the explainer; PixelToBeadRatio card), By size (the cm unit, Width and Height fields, Keep picture proportions, picture zoom and the pan under the Frame) and the slow-framing warning that went with the pan.

## 4 Colors

- **Colors:** a ValueSlider (ValueSlider card) from 1 to every color the picture (or the part) has, at most 256: the distinct colors its beads sample, counted before the match (the build, ticket 0747: counting after the match would match every sampled color on every change, for a number the slider could not reach anyway). Its head "Colors" and "8 colors", its ends "1" and "max 9". It starts at 12, or at the max where that is smaller, and a part with fewer colors pulls it down to them. Under it in `small`: "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: every color the picture has."; with more colors than 256, "Each color is a bead to buy. Max {n}: the picture has more, reduced to as many." It replaces Colors at most. The 256 (a palette image's count) was set with the build: a photo under Exact had 55 000 colors at the max and took two minutes there, and a pattern of that many colors is the picture, not a pattern; at 256 every move is under half a second.
- **match to:** under the slider, one SegmentedControl, Bead colors | Default Palette | Exact. A Bead without a Color catalog shows Default Palette | Exact; with one, Bead colors is the default.
- **Under Bead colors only:** one line in `meta-small` counting the codes the match uses, "codes in 11/0: 1 211": the codes made in the Bead's size, discontinued ones left out. The match itself has no filter (spec 0645's Include discontinued is left out of this design); the Bead colors list in a row's remap picker has the BeadColors card's filters (ticket 0713).
- **A Note** says what the source does: Bead colors repeats the BeadColors approximate note; Default Palette "Matches each color to the nearest of the Bead’s 16 default colors." (12 for a Brand without its own set); Exact "Keeps the picture’s own colors. Each joins the Palette as a Custom color."
- **Color by color:** a DisclosureRow (DisclosureRow card) with no icon, closed every time the page opens: the label "Color by color", the summary "8 colors · 1 empty" (the colors the beads use, as the step's summary counts them, then the found colors set to empty; the empty part left out when nothing is empty), the chevron. Escape is the page's (it asks to leave), not the row's. Open, it holds colors found, the count at the right, and one FoundColorRow per found color, most beads first (FoundColorRow card), pushing Review down. Treat as empty on the preview works with it closed; its summary counts the change.

## 5 Review

- Name and Maker's name, TextFields stacked (the build stacks them at every width: side by side, a long label such as Spanish "Nombre de quien lo hace" and its "optional" did not fit the 400px pane), each "optional"; Name's placeholder is the file's name, and a Name left blank is the file's name.
- The summary box (`surface`, `radius-lg`, padding 4 12): size, colors, beads needed, estimated weight and to buy as rows, the `label` at the left and the value in `meta` `ink` at the right; under a 1px `line`, one row per color: a 14px swatch, the code or name, beads, grams and tubes ("1 × 5 g", PrintSpecification's rule). On a phone the per-color rows sit behind Show details / Hide details.
- The grams Note: "≈ 200 Delica 11/0 a gram, rounded up. Buy about 10% more for spares."

## Keyboard and screen readers

- The page is a `region` named "Convert image". Focus enters on Choose file (or, with a picture set, the first control of Picture).
- Tab order: the strip (Menu, Close); the preview's controls (Treat as empty, the crop handles with A part, zoom); the step in hand; the Progress bar (one Tab stop, arrows along its dots and buttons).
- The step is a `region` named by its title; a new step moves focus to its heading. The result line and the found-color count are `role="status"`, read once after a change settles. Errors are `role="alert"`.
