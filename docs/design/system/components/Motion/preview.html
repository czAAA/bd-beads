--duration-instant80msPress feedback, bead hover outline.
--duration-fast120msHover and colour changes, tooltips, the Remove × on thumbnails.
--duration-base200msMenus, popovers, toasts, modals, disclosure rows and expandable panels, the current-row marker. Leaving uses 150ms.
--duration-slow280msBottom sheets and the Drawer coming in (they leave in 200ms).
--ease-outcubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)Anything arriving: it starts fast and settles.
--ease-incubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)Anything leaving: it gathers speed and goes.
--ease-standardcubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)Anything moving from one place to another: the row marker, a panel growing.

Rules

  • Only transform and opacity animate over the canvas (ADR 0018): sheets, the Drawer, menus, toasts and modals slide or fade over it; the drawing surface never resizes during an animation. Height animates only inside the left column (disclosure rows, expandable panels).
  • Arrive with --ease-out, leave with --ease-in, a little faster than arriving. Nothing bounces or overshoots.
  • Row done moves the current-row marker to the next row in 200ms and grows the progress line with it: the one moment of motion on the Pattern itself, because it confirms the most repeated action.
  • No motion on painting, the theme switch (colours change at once, so the canvas redraws once) or zoom steps (the Pattern jumps to the new scale; pinch follows the fingers directly).
  • Toasts leave after 5s unless hovered or focused; errors never leave by themselves.
  • Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce): nothing slides or scales: sheets, the Drawer, menus and modals fade in 120ms; the row marker jumps; the loading beads stand still. Colour and opacity feedback stay.
