How it works

  • A third theme, contrast, light-based. It switches on with the system's "increase contrast" (prefers-contrast: more) until the person picks a theme, and from the theme menu ("High contrast").
  • Every text at least 7:1, every border and mark at least 3:1 and drawn 2px; boxes are white with dark borders instead of beige with shadows (every elevation is none); the focus ring is 3px black.
  • The Pattern's bead colours never change (they are the person's work); only the board, empty beads and rulers get stronger.
  • Windows high contrast (forced-colors: active): system colours win, every outline and border stays, the focus ring is Highlight, and no meaning hides in a background colour alone.
