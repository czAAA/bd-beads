# Homepage

The one page outside the editor, at `/homepage`: the front door for a new visitor (empty Pattern library), reachable from the Menu panel, drawn lighter and more by hand than the editor.

- **How it sits with the first principle:** the editor stays an editor; this is a single introductory page with the same tokens and voice, three Homepage-only type styles (`tagline`, `serif-heading`, `note`), no marketing phrases and no screenshots. It is one canvas box like the editor's (ticket 540, ADR 0073), in the middle of the page.
- **The scroll story (ticket 0739):** the page is not a long page that scrolls down. The box stands in the middle of the page, at most `--homepage-box-width` (1120px) by `--homepage-box-height` (880px), 8px from the screen's edge on a phone and 24px from 744; the page around it is `page-ground` (black in dark, a grey a step darker than the box in light and high contrast), so the box always stands off it. In the box: the Canvas strip on top, the hero fixed under it, one section at a time, the Progress bar at the bottom. Scrolling (wheel, trackpad, swipe, keys) never moves the box: the page is a viewport of scroll per section and snaps to each (`scroll-snap-type: y mandatory`), and each one turns the box to the next section, the old one sliding up 56px (`--section-shift`), shrinking to 98.5% and fading while the next rises in from below, over `--section-turn` (600ms) on `ease-standard`. Three sections, in order: **What's inside**, the **coffee tile**, **Three ways to use it**. The page holds the focus when it opens, so Page Down, Space and the arrows turn it; a section's own controls take the keys once focused. A section taller than the room left scrolls inside itself (a phone, a tablet, a laptop under about 900px tall, a large zoom); it fits whole from 1440 × 900.
- **Canvas strip, not a header (ticket 540):** the editor's own CanvasStrip on top: Menu on the left, the Brand (X1 mark and "openbeads", the current page, no link) centred, zoom out · level · zoom in · Fit on the right. Menu opens the editor's Menu panel (Language, Theme, About; Homepage shown as the current page; the Projects section and the editor's own settings are left out). The page's own header, Language switcher and Theme toggle are gone.
- **Zoom:** the buttons scale the box's content (the hero and the sections) 75–200% in the editor's 10% steps; the layout is made for the width the zoom leaves, so text reflows and nothing scrolls sideways. Remembered on the device; Fit is 100%.
- **The drawing area:** today's Canvas background with its peyote position marks, behind the hero and the sections.
- **Hero, centred, never scrolling away:** the rolling slogan **open**_beads_ in `tagline` (40px phone, 58px from 744, 60px from 1024, 70px at 24″), described below; **save. count. export** / «сохраним. посчитаем. экспортируем» in `mono` 15px `muted`, with the note "on us" / «это мы», always shown, in the same style; **Make your first Pattern** (primary) and **Open the editor**.
- **Rolling slogan (ticket 550):** one lowercase line, no full stops: **open** in `ink`, then a word in `accent-strong` set straight after it with no space, so it first reads as the brand name, **openbeads**. The words, in order: beads, draw, joy, weave, create, art, canvas, possibility, play, wonder, pattern, ideas, then beads again. Each word holds 2.2s (`--slogan-hold`), then rolls: it slides down out of a one-line slot while the next drops in from above, over 650ms (`--slogan-roll`) with `--ease-standard`, the slot's top and bottom edges faded so the words seem to pass through it. **open** never moves: the slot is as wide as the longest word ("possibility") in the font as drawn, and the line is centred as if it held that word, so short words leave room on their right. The loop closes on beads, so it never jumps. The line is the brand, not copy: the same English words in every language, and the Russian 20% reduction no longer applies. Screen readers get one stable label, "openbeads", and the rolling words are `aria-hidden`; the roll pauses while the page is hidden. Reduced motion: no roll, the line stays **openbeads**.
- **With Patterns saved:** **Open the editor** (primary) and beside it **Projects library · {n}** (secondary, `library` icon; the Menu row's name and the count, ticket 0716), no notes. It opens the editor at `?open=saved-projects`: no Project open, the Menu on its Projects library subpanel. The Homepage's Menu has the Projects section with that row alone while the library is not empty, and its subpanel is the editor's list (MenuPanel card), Remove asking first; a pick opens the editor on that Project (`?project=<id>`).
- **The Progress bar (ticket 0739):** the editor's own bar (ProgressBar card), not a look-alike, with the sections in place of rows: the counter ("2/3", `control`), the section's name (`meta`, `box-muted`, lowercase; left out on a phone), the track filling a third per section with its spark, **Back ↑** (`box`, large) where Row not done sits and **Next ↓** (primary, large) where Row done sits. Back is disabled on the first section and Next on the last, each Tooltip saying why ("This is the first section." / "This is the last section."). Each step forward flares the spark as Row done does, a step back moves it with no flare, and reaching the last section plays Pattern done's burst at the track's end (its three flashes along a short span there, no shimmer or stars). The track is a `progressbar` named "Sections", its value text "2/3 · Made by one family". On a phone Back and Next take the bar's second row, half each, as Row not done and Row done do.
- **What's inside:** `serif-heading`, no label above it. A carousel of the seven features (Techniques, Pattern editing, Convert image, Row progress, Beads needed, Exports, Saved Patterns; Mirror not listed), each with an example drawn as beads, filling the section's height. Always a `tablist`, the arrows moving along it. From 1024: the list left (selected item on `panel` with a 2px `accent` bar and its line), the stage right with ‹ › and "3 / 7" top-right and the caption inside it. Below 1024 (ticket 0739): a row of tabs, the names alone, scrolling sideways, above the stage; ‹ › and the count top-right of the stage; the feature's name and line under it. The swipe cards and their dots are gone.
- **Examples:** hearts in loom, peyote and brick stitch; a poppy with Paint active and a Selection frame; a picture beside its 6-colour beaded version; rows done faded with the current row marked and a mini Progress bar; Beads needed for a gold-and-black pattern; a PDF page with the technique word filling its width (uniform scale, never stretched); the Saved Patterns gallery.
- **Coffee tile:** as wide as the section, centred in it: a beaded cup with three beads of steam, "Made by one family" in `serif-heading` 30px, one line of text with no width cap (one line where it fits, two on a tablet or a narrow laptop, more only on a phone), **Buy me a coffee** (secondary; a link opening the page in a new tab, inert while the app has no link). The whole tile is that link (ticket 0718): a click or tap anywhere on it opens the page, and hovering it shows the button's hover; keyboards and screen readers meet one link, Buy me a coffee, with the focus ring on the button. No donation-service name, logo or colours.
- **Three ways to use it:** the heading on the left and the reason ("Accounts and Pro aren't open yet. Everything works without them.") on the right, wrapping under it where there is no room; no "plans" label. No account works now: 2px `ink` edge on `elevated`, "you are here" note from 744, a strand of one bead, the name in `serif-heading`, **Free** and `meta` "no sign-up", one line, primary **Open the editor**, then the free list. On a phone the tiles scroll sideways, one and a bit in view; from 744 the three side by side, top-aligned, so No account grows on its own.
- **Free account and Pro, switched off (ticket 0740):** nobody knows yet what they will hold, so they list nothing. Each is greyed out: `surface` fill, a 1px dashed `line-strong` edge, every word in `subtle` (the name in `serif-heading`, the price with no `meta`, the line), the bead strand (two beads, three) in grey at 45%, the button disabled. The line says only what it builds on: "Same as No account, plus more" and "Same as Free account, plus more". Where the list was, a **Coming soon** stamp, centred: a rubber stamp in `accent-strong` at 85%, `--coming-soon-width` (236px) wide, a 5px rounded border with a 1.5px one 8px inside it, "COMING SOON" in Inter 600 26px, 3px tracking, tilted `--coming-soon-tilt` (−8°, Pro's −5°), its ink worn by a noise mask (holes where the paper didn't take it), each tile's wear its own. An image named "Coming soon"; the tile `aria-disabled`.
- **The free list (tickets 0739, 0740):** No account's list is everything the app does free, nearly all of it without an account, under "forever free:" (`meta`, `muted`, lowercase), each row a check in `accent-strong` and its line in `body`, the last row "More on the way" in `muted` with a plus. The app's real features in its own words, in this order: runs in the browser with the work kept on the device; the open Canvas; the Techniques; any number of Projects; picture to beads; one Frame per Project; many Schemas per Project; Row progress; the whole editor; PDF; PNG; Beads needed; the built-in catalogs; the `.openbeads` file; the Word chart; Mirror; the Starters (`writing.md`, the free list). The counts and names in it come from the app, never typed into the copy: the Techniques from `TECHNIQUES` and their names, the catalogs' brands and codes from the Color catalogs, the Starters and their categories from the Starters' registry, so a new one shows up on its own. The tile shows the first `--free-start-rows` (4) rows, the rest faded out at its foot over `--free-fade` (28px). There is no window of its own and no scroll bar: scrolling the page grows the tile. It grows `--free-growth` (2.5) times as fast as the page scrolls, so its bottom edge runs down the screen; once it reaches the screen's foot (40px above it) it grows exactly as fast as the page scrolls, so it stays screen-tall while its top, with Free account and Pro, scrolls up out of view, until the whole list is out and the fade is gone. The page is as tall as the open tile needs, and the section stops snapping while the tile is growing. Scrolling back shrinks it the same way. The list is whole in the DOM at every height, so screen readers and keyboards get every row. Reduced motion: no difference, it follows the scroll.
- **Decoration:** the drawn layer is gone (its bead drawings, X1 marks and notes beside the column). Only two faint X1 marks stay, on the page outside the box, in `accent`: a large one (420px, 1.4 stroke, 7%) left of the box and a small one (120px, 2.6 stroke, 16%) low on its right, cut at the screen's edge, drifting a little as the page scrolls (a scroll-driven animation where the browser has one; still under reduced motion). The hero's "on us" and the plans' "you are here" notes stay, in `note`, with their 1.25px hand-drawn arrows. All `aria-hidden`.
- **Reduced motion:** the sections swap without sliding, the marks stand still, and the spark follows the bar's rules (the fill steps; no flare, tail or burst).
- **Layout:** the box as above; each section at most 1080px wide, centred in the box, padding 4 8 12 on a phone, 8 24 24 from 744, 8 32 24 from 1024. The hero: padding 20 16 12, from 744 24 24 16. No footer.
- Copy in every shipped language: the Writing section, "Copy added in v15", and the scroll story's lines after it.

Hand-written from the v15 sign-off; the scroll story from the signed prototype, the claude.ai canvas "bd-beads homepage — scroll story", its dark rendition the reference; the plan tiles from the claude.ai canvas "bd-beads — forever free tile" (ticket 0740).

**Where the app differs, until ticket 0740's code lands:** the app still has the free list in a hand-scrolled window that lights three beads and unrolls into a paper roll on the third scroll (`--free-window`, `--free-roll-curl`, still in `src/styles/design-values.css` for it), and Free account and Pro still list their placeholder features.
