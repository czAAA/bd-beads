# Homepage

The one page outside the editor, at `/homepage`: the front door for a new visitor (empty Pattern library), reachable from the Menu panel, drawn lighter and more by hand than the editor.

- **How it sits with the first principle:** the editor stays an editor; this is a single introductory page with the same tokens and voice, three Homepage-only type styles (`tagline`, `serif-heading`, `note`), no marketing phrases and no screenshots. It is a canvas box like the editor's (ticket 540, ADR 0073): the page never scrolls, its content does, between the strip and the footer.
- **Canvas strip, not a header (ticket 540):** the editor's own CanvasStrip on top: Menu on the left, the Brand (X1 mark and "openbeads", the current page, no link) centred, zoom out · level · zoom in · Fit on the right. Menu opens the editor's Menu panel (Language, Theme, About; Homepage shown as the current page; the Projects section and the editor's own settings are left out). Below the content, an empty Canvas footer at the Progress bar's height, so the box is the same on both pages. The page's own header, Language switcher and Theme toggle are gone.
- **Zoom:** the buttons scale the page's content 75–200% in the editor's 10% steps; the layout is made for the width the zoom leaves, so text reflows and the page never scrolls sideways. Remembered on the device; Fit is 100%.
- **Hero, centred:** the mark and wordmark; the rolling slogan **open**_beads_ in `tagline` (40px phone, 58px from 744, 60px from 1024, 70px at 24″), described below; **save. count. export** / «сохраним. посчитаем. экспортируем» in `mono` 15px `muted`, with the note "on us" / «это мы», always shown, in the same style; **Make your first Pattern** (primary) and **Open the editor**.
- **Rolling slogan (ticket 550):** one lowercase line, no full stops: **open** in `ink`, then a word in `accent-strong` set straight after it with no space, so it first reads as the brand name, **openbeads**. The words, in order: beads, draw, joy, weave, create, art, canvas, possibility, play, wonder, pattern, ideas, then beads again. Each word holds 2.2s (`--slogan-hold`), then rolls: it slides down out of a one-line slot while the next drops in from above, over 650ms (`--slogan-roll`) with `--ease-standard`, the slot's top and bottom edges faded so the words seem to pass through it. **open** never moves: the slot is as wide as the longest word ("possibility") in the font as drawn, and the line is centred as if it held that word, so short words leave room on their right. The loop closes on beads, so it never jumps. The line is the brand, not copy: the same English words in every language, and the Russian 20% reduction no longer applies. Screen readers get one stable label, "openbeads", and the rolling words are `aria-hidden`; the roll pauses while the page is hidden. Reduced motion: no roll, the line stays **openbeads**. The "you" note that sat on the old slogan's first word is gone.
- **With Patterns saved:** only **Open the editor** (primary), "Patterns saved: {n}" in `meta-small`, no notes.
- **What's inside:** `serif-heading`, no label above it. A carousel of the seven features (Techniques, Pattern editing, Convert image, Row progress, Beads needed, Exports, Saved Patterns; Mirror not listed), each with an example drawn as beads. From 1024: the list left (selected item on `panel` with a 2px `accent` bar and its line), the example right, ‹ › and "3 / 7" top-right; a `tablist`, arrows move. Below 1024: swipe cards with dots and ‹ ›. On a MacBook Air the whole carousel is above the fold.
- **Examples:** hearts in loom, peyote and brick stitch; a poppy with Paint active and a Selection frame; a picture beside its 6-colour beaded version; rows done faded with the current row marked and a mini Progress bar; Beads needed for a gold-and-black pattern; a PDF page with the technique word filling its width (uniform scale, never stretched); the Saved Patterns gallery.
- **Coffee tile:** as wide as the main column (the same edges as "What's inside" and the plan tiles), stacked and centred: a beaded cup with three beads of steam, "Made by one family" in `serif-heading` 30px, one line of text with no width cap (one line where it fits, two on a tablet or a narrow laptop, more only on a phone), **Buy me a coffee** (secondary; a link opening the page in a new tab, inert while the app has no link). The whole tile is that link (ticket 0718): a click or tap anywhere on it opens the page, and hovering it shows the button's hover; keyboards and screen readers meet one link, Buy me a coffee, with the focus ring on the button. No donation-service name, logo or colours.
- **Three ways to use it:** No account (works now, 2px `ink` edge, "you are here" note, primary **Open the editor**), Free account and Pro (switched off: disabled buttons, "coming later", the reason written once). Each tile: a strand of one to three beads, the name in `serif-heading`, price and `meta`, one line, the button, then "everything in …, plus" and checks. Plan contents are placeholders; no integration.
- **The drawn layer:** notes in `note` (`muted`, or `accent-strong` for "on us") with 1.25px hand-drawn arrows; bead drawings (flower, heart, bee, spool, leaf, rhombus, sparkles) in Palette colours beside the main column, large ones at 16% opacity with a clear margin from text and controls; three to five X1 marks per section at 8% `accent`, as on the PDF exports, each drawn whole even where it is taller than what is left of its section (it passes behind the next one). The hand-fitted ones sit a fixed gap (24-48px) outside the main column's edge at every screen band, measured from the column, not the page; the rest (ticket 540) are measured from the section's middle and fill the gaps across its whole width, small ones only where nothing is near, large faint ones also behind text but never over a control; where a band has no room beside the column (phone, tablet, the low end of laptop) the small ones tuck into the gutter or into blank space beside the hero's text. They thin out per band, counting from the laptop and desktop amount (from 1440): about two thirds from 744 to 1439, about one third on a phone. The strip's logo is not part of the layer. All `aria-hidden`, behind content, never touching text.
- **Layout:** container 1080px (1200px at 24″), padding 16 / 24 / 32 / 40. No footer.
- Copy in English and Russian: the Writing section, "Copy added in v15".

Hand-written from the v15 sign-off; the preview is the signed page laid out with container queries at each tier's width.
