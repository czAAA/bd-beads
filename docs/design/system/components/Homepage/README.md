# Homepage

The one page outside the editor, at `/homepage`: the front door for a new visitor (empty Pattern library), reachable from the Menu panel, drawn lighter and more by hand than the editor.

- **How it sits with the first principle:** the editor stays an editor; this is a single introductory page with the same tokens and voice, three Homepage-only type styles (`tagline`, `serif-heading`, `note`), no marketing phrases and no screenshots. It is one canvas box like the editor's (ticket 540, ADR 0073), in the middle of the page.
- **The scroll story (ticket 0739):** the page is not a long page that scrolls down. The box stands in the middle of the page, at most `--homepage-box-width` (1120px) by `--homepage-box-height` (880px), 8px from the screen's edge on a phone and 24px from 744; the page around it is `page-ground` (black in dark, a grey a step darker than the box in light and high contrast), so the box always stands off it. In the box: the Canvas strip on top, holding the slogan and the way into the editor (no hero since ticket 0819), one section at a time, the Progress bar at the bottom (on a phone, the phone's own bar). Scrolling (wheel, trackpad, swipe, keys) never moves the box and never scrolls the page: the section shown is the one truth of where the page is, and each way of scrolling turns the box to the next section (ticket 0801), the old one sliding up 56px (`--section-shift`), shrinking to 98.5% and fading while the next rises in from below, over `--section-turn` (600ms) on `ease-standard`. Three sections, in order: **What's inside**, the **coffee tile**, **Three ways to use it**. The page holds the focus when it opens; Page Down, Page Up, Space (Shift+Space up) and the arrows turn it from wherever the focus is, except in a text field, a list or slider the arrows move in, the demo editor, a button Space presses, and while the Menu or a dialog is open. A section taller than the room left scrolls inside itself (a phone, a tablet, a laptop under about 900px tall, a large zoom); it fits whole from 1440 × 900.
- **One scroll, one section (tickets 0784, 0801):** a wheel or trackpad never scrolls the page itself, and a section turns on its first scroll, however short. A scroll is a gesture: wheel events with no 300ms quiet between them, so a notch rolled on its own is one, and so is a whole trackpad swipe and its momentum; the rest of a gesture that turned, its momentum too, turns nothing and scrolls nothing, unless the beader pushes again in it (an event more than twice as strong as the one before, at least 20px, once the turn has stood its while) or scrolls the other way. A swipe turns once it goes 48px up or down, more than it goes sideways; a swipe in the demo editor is the editor's. A section taller than its room scrolls inside itself first: a wheel, swipe or key reaching its end turns nothing, and the next one turns. A section stays at least `--section-turn` (600ms) before a scroll, swipe or held key turns it again, however it was reached; a wheel scroll in that while turns once it is over. Home and End go straight to the first and last section, the box turning once, as a step does. (Ticket 0793 turned on the second scroll, leaning the section 8–16px on the first; ticket 0801 dropped it, as the rest already keeps a turn from being an accident, and a first scroll that did nothing read as broken.)
- **The nudge (ticket 0784):** the box fits the screen and the scroll bar is hidden, so a first-time visitor is shown the page scrolls. After 1.5s with no input on the first section, it lifts `--nudge-lift` (12px) fading to `--nudge-fade` (82%) and settles back, over `--nudge-length` (1100ms), rising on `ease-out` and settling on `ease-standard`, while Next's arrow dips `--nudge-dip` (4px) twice. Once per device: any scroll, wheel or swipe first means the beader knows, and it never plays; a press or a key first holds it off for that visit. Never under reduced motion. Variant Lift of the claude.ai prototype "Homepage scroll nudge" (https://claude.ai/artifact/M8txn3t7BVG7tfQ148ngZv).
- **Canvas strip, not a header (tickets 540, 0819, 0822):** the editor's own CanvasStrip on top. On the left Menu, then the way into the editor, `sm` buttons 8px apart: **Make your first Project** (primary) with no Projects saved; **Open the editor** (primary) and **Projects library · {n}** (secondary) with some. In the middle the rolling slogan, in the Brand's place: on the Homepage the slogan is the brand, set after the Brand's X1 mark where the editor sets the wordmark. On the right the zoom (out, in, Fit). The left slot is as wide as Menu and the way in need: the slogan stays centred on the strip while there is room and moves right where there is not (the longest languages, with Projects saved), the zoom then scrolling as the strip's controls do. Under 744px (the box's width) the way in has no room beside Menu and the slogan: it takes a row of its own in the strip under them, centred; on a phone, until the phone bar carries it (ticket 0827). Menu opens the editor's Menu panel (Language, Theme, About; Homepage shown as the current page; the Projects section and the editor's own settings are left out). The page's own header, Language switcher and Theme toggle are gone. On a phone the strip holds Menu, the slogan and, with Projects saved, the library button (the `library` icon and {n}, `box-button`, 36px tall, named "Projects library, {n}"); no zoom, and the way in is the phone bar's main button.
- **Zoom:** the buttons scale the box's content (the sections) 75–200% in the editor's 10% steps; the layout is made for the width the zoom leaves, so text reflows and nothing scrolls sideways. Remembered on the device; Fit is 100%. Not on a phone.
- **The drawing area:** today's Canvas background with its peyote position marks, behind the sections.
- **No hero (ticket 0819):** the slogan and the buttons that stood centred over the sections moved into the strip, so the sections, What's inside's demo first, have the box's whole height under it.
- **Rolling slogan (tickets 550, 0819):** in the strip's middle, `tagline` at `--strip-slogan-size` (24px; `--strip-slogan-size-phone`, 22px, on a phone), one lowercase line, no full stops: **open** in `ink`, then a word in `accent-strong` set straight after it with no space, so it first reads as the brand name, **openbeads**. The words, in order: beads, draw, joy, weave, create, art, canvas, possibility, play, wonder, pattern, ideas, then beads again. Each word holds 2.2s (`--slogan-hold`), then rolls: it slides down out of a one-line slot while the next drops in from above, over 650ms (`--slogan-roll`) with `--ease-standard`, the slot's top and bottom edges faded so the words seem to pass through it. **open** never moves: the slot is as wide as the longest word ("possibility") in the font as drawn, and the line is centred as if it held that word, so short words leave room on their right. The loop closes on beads, so it never jumps. The line is the brand, not copy: the same English words in every language, and the Russian 20% reduction no longer applies. Screen readers get one stable label, "openbeads", and the rolling words are `aria-hidden`; the roll pauses while the page is hidden. Reduced motion: no roll, the line stays **openbeads**.
- **With Patterns saved:** **Open the editor** (primary) and beside it **Projects library · {n}** (secondary, `library` icon; the Menu row's name and the count, ticket 0716), in the strip; under 1000px its name hides, the icon and · {n} stay (the name stays for screen readers). It opens the editor at `?open=saved-projects`: no Project open, the Menu on its Projects library subpanel. The Homepage's Menu has the Projects section with that row alone while the library is not empty, and its subpanel is the editor's list (MenuPanel card), Remove asking first; a pick opens the editor on that Project (`?project=<id>`).
- **Into the editor (ticket 0776):** every way into the editor (Make your first Project, Open the editor, Projects library, a Saved Project) carries the box across, so the editor reads as the same box opening up. First the box stays where it is while its content (the section shown), the page around it and the X1 marks fade away (`duration-base`), leaving the box with its Canvas strip and its canvas marks on `page-ground` (never an empty box: ticket 0783); then the box grows into the editor's canvas box over `--homepage-opening` (450ms) with `--ease-standard`, the editor's content shown in it at its own size (never stretched), its Canvas strip along the box's top edge, cross-fading from the Homepage's strip and marks over `duration-slow` (`--ease-in` out, `--ease-out` in), the editor's other parts fading in around it, and a panel the way in opens (New Project, the Menu's Projects library) fading in above the box. The box's corners are drawn, never scaled. Only this way in moves: a reload or any other way into the editor is instant. Reduced motion, or a browser without View Transitions: no move, the editor simply opens. An editor that hasn't started 4s after the box appeared gives it up for the Loading splash. The strip and the marks are the Homepage's own (its strip's markup and its marks' tile, handed over with the box's place), so they are drawn in the beader's language, theme and zoom before the editor's code runs.
- **The Progress bar (tickets 0739, 0743):** the editor's own bar (ProgressBar card), not a look-alike, with the sections in place of rows: the counter ("1/3", `control`), the track with a named step per section, **Back ↑** (quiet, 32px `sm`) where Row not done sits and **Next ↓** (primary, 32px `sm`) where Row done sits. Signed off as variant A of the claude.ai canvas "Homepage progress bar" (https://claude.ai/artifact/NhEega7RYGk5JXsax8QsKn). Since ticket 0780 it is the same bar of steps as Convert image's (ProgressBar card): 64 tall with room at both ends, every name centred under its dot inside the track, and the fill and spark moving to each step as the box turns (ticket 0801; they followed the page's scroll while it had one).
  - **The steps are the sections.** One step per section, in the page's order, named by the section's own heading ("What's inside", "Made by one family", "Three ways to use it" today). Nothing in the bar counts them: a section added, removed or moved shows up as a step added, removed or moved, the spacing and the counter following.
  - **Where each step sits:** of n sections, step i (from 0) sits at i / (n − 1) of the track: the first at its start, the last at its end, the rest evenly between. A dot centred on the track, its name under it (`meta-small`, lowercase); the first name starts at the track's start, the last ends at its end, the others are centred under their dot.
  - **The fill starts empty:** it is section / (n − 1), so the track is empty on the first section, reaches each step's dot as you arrive on it and is full on the last (it used to fill a third per section, so the first already showed a third done).
  - **The step's look:** passed, a filled `--step-dot` (8px) in `accent`; ahead, the same dot in `track`; each with a 2px `box` edge so it stands off the fill. The current one, a `--step-dot-current` (14px) ring, `--step-ring` (3px) of `accent-strong` round `box`, which takes the spark's glow once the fill has started. Names: current in `ink`, passed in `body`, ahead in `muted`. The spark's tail runs into the current ring; its own dot isn't drawn, the ring is the spark, flaring on each step forward.
  - **A step is a button:** a click or tap on its dot or name turns the page to that section (a jump of several sections turns once, not through each), named "Go to {section}" with `aria-current="step"` on the current one. The track stays a `progressbar` named "Sections", its value text "2/3 · Made by one family". The section's name beside the counter is gone: the steps name themselves.
  - Back is disabled on the first section and Next on the last, each Tooltip saying why ("This is the first section." / "This is the last section."). Each step forward flares the ring as Row done flares the spark, a step back moves it with no flare, and reaching the last section plays Pattern done's burst at the track's end (its three flashes along a short span there, no shimmer or stars).
  - **Height:** the bar is `--step-bar-height` (64px; ticket 0780), the track 8px down and the names from 20px.
  - **On a phone (480 and under; ticket 0819)** this bar gives way to the phone's own, below.
  - Styles: `.bb-section-steps`, `.bb-section-step` and its `--first`, `--last`, `--passed`, `--current` and `is-lit` in `bundle.css`.

- **The ghost:** a pointer that is not the beader's: the system arrow, white (`--ghost-fill`) with a 1.5px dark edge (`--ghost-edge`) and a soft shadow, 28 × 32, `aria-hidden`. It moves where a beader's hand would over `--ghost-move` (840ms, `ease-standard`; a short hop quicker, from 280ms, and each bead along a stroke 140ms) and presses what a beader would press, each press leaving a ripple: a 30px ring, white at 30% over white at 5%, growing from 30% to 130% and fading over `--ghost-ripple` (840ms, `ease-out`). Calm: one thing moves at a time, nothing flashes, nothing appears that the ghost did not make.
- **Everything the ghost does is seen (ticket 0819):** before each press, and before each rest on a control:
  - it waits for the control to stop moving (a bar or a panel still sliding in), so it never aims where a control is about to leave;
  - it scrolls the control's panel or list so all of the control shows, `--ghost-room` (24px) clear of the edges where there is room and below any sticky header, over 200–450ms (`ease-standard`, longer for a longer way); a control taller than the room shows from its top;
  - it moves onto the middle of the part that shows, inside every clip and the demo's box;
  - on a control in a panel, a popover, a menu or a dialog it rests `--ghost-pause` (300ms) before it presses, so the beader sees what is pressed;
  - if the control moved meanwhile it follows it, so what is pressed is always under the ghost.
  A Selection's drag rests `--ghost-pause` on its first bead, then the Selection grows bead by bead behind the ghost over `--ghost-drag` (1.4s), its size counting up in the Selection bar, and stays whole `--ghost-drag-hold` (1.2s) before the ghost moves on. The ghost paints above the editor's menus and pickers (the browser's top layer, where they open) while one is open. A panel or popover in the demo is never taller than the room under it in the demo's box, and scrolls inside itself past that.

- **Choosing a row:** a press on any row, earlier or later than the one playing, plays from there (ticket 0802): the row lights at once, the editor starts afresh, every row before it is applied at once (no ghost, no motion, no wait for a frame where no control needs time to appear), then the ghost plays on from that row. - **Taking over:** a press, a key or a drag into the editor stops the ghost for the rest of the visit, leaving the editor as it is for the beader to try, the tabs unlit and the caption still. A press on a row starts the tour again from there.
- **Only while seen:** the tour plays only while What's inside is the section shown and the page is visible. Turning away stops it; coming back starts it again from the first row, unless the beader has taken over, whose editor is kept as they left it.
