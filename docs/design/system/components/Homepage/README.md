# Homepage

The one page outside the editor, at `/homepage`: the front door for a new visitor (empty Pattern library), reachable from the Menu panel, drawn lighter and more by hand than the editor.

- **How it sits with the first principle:** the editor stays an editor; this is a single introductory page with the same tokens and voice, three Homepage-only type styles (`tagline`, `serif-heading`, `note`), no marketing phrases and no screenshots. It is one canvas box like the editor's (ticket 540, ADR 0073), in the middle of the page.
- **The scroll story (ticket 0739):** the page is not a long page that scrolls down. The box stands in the middle of the page, at most `--homepage-box-width` (1120px) by `--homepage-box-height` (880px), 8px from the screen's edge on a phone and 24px from 744; the page around it is `page-ground` (black in dark, a grey a step darker than the box in light and high contrast), so the box always stands off it. In the box: the Canvas strip on top, holding the slogan and, past the first section, the way into the editor, one section at a time, the Progress bar at the bottom (on a phone, the phone's own bar). Scrolling (wheel, trackpad, swipe, keys) never moves the box and never scrolls the page: the section shown is the one truth of where the page is, and each way of scrolling turns the box to the next section (ticket 0801), the old one sliding up 56px (`--section-shift`), shrinking to 98.5% and fading while the next rises in from below, over `--section-turn` (600ms) on `ease-standard`. Four sections, in order: **Welcome** (ticket 0830), **What's inside**, the **coffee tile**, **Three ways to use it**. The page holds the focus when it opens; Page Down, Page Up, Space (Shift+Space up) and the arrows turn it from wherever the focus is, except in a text field, a list or slider the arrows move in, the demo editor, a button Space presses, and while the Menu or a dialog is open. A section taller than the room left scrolls inside itself (a phone, a tablet, a laptop under about 900px tall, a large zoom); it fits whole from 1440 × 900.
- **One scroll, one section (tickets 0784, 0801):** a wheel or trackpad never scrolls the page itself, and a section turns on its first scroll, however short. A scroll is a gesture: wheel events with no 300ms quiet between them, so a notch rolled on its own is one, and so is a whole trackpad swipe and its momentum; the rest of a gesture that turned, its momentum too, turns nothing and scrolls nothing, unless the beader pushes again in it (an event more than twice as strong as the one before, at least 20px, once the turn has stood its while) or scrolls the other way. A swipe turns once it goes 48px up or down, more than it goes sideways; a swipe in the demo editor is the editor's. A section taller than its room scrolls inside itself first: a wheel, swipe or key reaching its end turns nothing, and the next one turns. A section stays at least `--section-turn` (600ms) before a scroll, swipe or held key turns it again, however it was reached; a wheel scroll in that while turns once it is over. Home and End go straight to the first and last section, the box turning once, as a step does. (Ticket 0793 turned on the second scroll, leaning the section 8–16px on the first; ticket 0801 dropped it, as the rest already keeps a turn from being an accident, and a first scroll that did nothing read as broken.)
- **The nudge (ticket 0784):** the box fits the screen and the scroll bar is hidden, so a first-time visitor is shown the page scrolls. After 1.5s with no input on the first section (Welcome since ticket 0830), it lifts `--nudge-lift` (12px) fading to `--nudge-fade` (82%) and settles back, over `--nudge-length` (1100ms), rising on `ease-out` and settling on `ease-standard`, while Next's arrow dips `--nudge-dip` (4px) twice. Once per device: any scroll, wheel or swipe first means the beader knows, and it never plays; a press or a key first holds it off for that visit. Never under reduced motion. Variant Lift of the claude.ai prototype "Homepage scroll nudge" (https://claude.ai/artifact/M8txn3t7BVG7tfQ148ngZv).
