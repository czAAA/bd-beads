# Homepage

The one page outside the editor, at `/homepage`: the front door for a new visitor (empty Pattern library), reachable from the Menu panel, drawn lighter and more by hand than the editor.

- **How it sits with the first principle:** the editor stays an editor; this is a single introductory page with the same tokens and voice, three Homepage-only type styles (`tagline`, `serif-heading`, `note`), no marketing phrases and no screenshots. It is one canvas box like the editor's (ticket 540, ADR 0073), in the middle of the page.
- **The scroll story (ticket 0739):** the page is not a long page that scrolls down. The box stands in the middle of the page, at most `--homepage-box-width` (1120px) by `--homepage-box-height` (880px), 8px from the screen's edge on a phone and 24px from 744; the page around it is `page-ground` (black in dark, a grey a step darker than the box in light and high contrast), so the box always stands off it. In the box: the Canvas strip on top, the hero fixed under it, one section at a time, the Progress bar at the bottom. Scrolling (wheel, trackpad, swipe, keys) never moves the box: the page is a viewport of scroll per section and snaps to each (`scroll-snap-type: y mandatory`), and each one turns the box to the next section, the old one sliding up 56px (`--section-shift`), shrinking to 98.5% and fading while the next rises in from below, over `--section-turn` (600ms) on `ease-standard`. Three sections, in order: **What's inside**, the **coffee tile**, **Three ways to use it**. The page holds the focus when it opens, so Page Down, Space and the arrows turn it; a section's own controls take the keys once focused. A section taller than the room left scrolls inside itself (a phone, a tablet, a laptop under about 900px tall, a large zoom); it fits whole from 1440 × 900.
- **Turning deliberately (tickets 0784, 0793):** a wheel or trackpad never scrolls the page itself between sections, and a section turns on its second scroll. A scroll is a gesture: wheel events with no 300ms quiet between them, so a notch rolled on its own is one, and so is a whole trackpad swipe and its momentum. The first scroll on a section reaches its end: a section taller than its room scrolls there inside itself, and the scroll that gets there counts as the first; one that fits leans the way it went, 8px at once and up to 16px over a 100px notch, and settles back when the scroll stops. The next scroll the same way turns the box at once, however short it is and however long after: it counts scrolls, not distance, since a slow notch is only a few px on macOS. A scroll the other way counts afresh that way, and so does each new section, however it was reached. A section stays at least `--section-turn` (600ms) before the next turn; the rest of a gesture that turned, its momentum too, turns nothing and scrolls nothing. Past the plans the wheel scrolls the page as the free list needs, and that scroll counts as the first back up. Keys and swipes scroll the page as before, snapping at every section (`scroll-snap-stop: always`, so a fling never carries past one); Home and End go straight to the first and last section, the box turning once, as a step does.
- **The nudge (ticket 0784):** the box fits the screen and the scroll bar is hidden, so a first-time visitor is shown the page scrolls. After 1.5s with no input on the first section, it lifts `--nudge-lift` (12px) fading to `--nudge-fade` (82%) and settles back, over `--nudge-length` (1100ms), rising on `ease-out` and settling on `ease-standard`, while Next's arrow dips `--nudge-dip` (4px) twice. Once per device: any scroll, wheel or swipe first means the beader knows, and it never plays; a press or a key first holds it off for that visit. A turn by any means, not only the wheel, stands `--section-turn` before a wheel gesture turns again. Never under reduced motion. Variant Lift of the claude.ai prototype "Homepage scroll nudge" (https://claude.ai/artifact/M8txn3t7BVG7tfQ148ngZv).
- **Canvas strip, not a header (ticket 540):** the editor's own CanvasStrip on top: Menu on the left, the Brand (X1 mark and "openbeads", the current page, no link) centred, zoom out · level · zoom in · Fit on the right. Menu opens the editor's Menu panel (Language, Theme, About; Homepage shown as the current page; the Projects section and the editor's own settings are left out). The page's own header, Language switcher and Theme toggle are gone.
- **Zoom:** the buttons scale the box's content (the hero and the sections) 75–200% in the editor's 10% steps; the layout is made for the width the zoom leaves, so text reflows and nothing scrolls sideways. Remembered on the device; Fit is 100%.
- **The drawing area:** today's Canvas background with its peyote position marks, behind the hero and the sections.
- **Hero, centred, never scrolling away:** the rolling slogan **open**_beads_ in `tagline` (40px phone, 58px from 744, 60px from 1024, 70px at 24″), described below, and under it **Make your first Pattern** (primary) and **Open the editor**. Since ticket 0797 there is nothing between them: the **save. count. export** line and its "on us" note are gone, so the hero is a line shorter and What's inside has the room.
- **Rolling slogan (ticket 550):** one lowercase line, no full stops: **open** in `ink`, then a word in `accent-strong` set straight after it with no space, so it first reads as the brand name, **openbeads**. The words, in order: beads, draw, joy, weave, create, art, canvas, possibility, play, wonder, pattern, ideas, then beads again. Each word holds 2.2s (`--slogan-hold`), then rolls: it slides down out of a one-line slot while the next drops in from above, over 650ms (`--slogan-roll`) with `--ease-standard`, the slot's top and bottom edges faded so the words seem to pass through it. **open** never moves: the slot is as wide as the longest word ("possibility") in the font as drawn, and the line is centred as if it held that word, so short words leave room on their right. The loop closes on beads, so it never jumps. The line is the brand, not copy: the same English words in every language, and the Russian 20% reduction no longer applies. Screen readers get one stable label, "openbeads", and the rolling words are `aria-hidden`; the roll pauses while the page is hidden. Reduced motion: no roll, the line stays **openbeads**.
- **With Patterns saved:** **Open the editor** (primary) and beside it **Projects library · {n}** (secondary, `library` icon; the Menu row's name and the count, ticket 0716), no notes. It opens the editor at `?open=saved-projects`: no Project open, the Menu on its Projects library subpanel. The Homepage's Menu has the Projects section with that row alone while the library is not empty, and its subpanel is the editor's list (MenuPanel card), Remove asking first; a pick opens the editor on that Project (`?project=<id>`).
- **Into the editor (ticket 0776):** every way into the editor (Make your first Project, Open the editor, Projects library, a Saved Project) carries the box across, so the editor reads as the same box opening up. First the box stays where it is while its content (the hero and the section), the page around it and the X1 marks fade away (`duration-base`), leaving the box with its Canvas strip and its canvas marks on `page-ground` (never an empty box: ticket 0783); then the box grows into the editor's canvas box over `--homepage-opening` (450ms) with `--ease-standard`, the editor's content shown in it at its own size (never stretched), its Canvas strip along the box's top edge, cross-fading from the Homepage's strip and marks over `duration-slow` (`--ease-in` out, `--ease-out` in), the editor's other parts fading in around it, and a panel the way in opens (New Project, the Menu's Projects library) fading in above the box. The box's corners are drawn, never scaled. Only this way in moves: a reload or any other way into the editor is instant. Reduced motion, or a browser without View Transitions: no move, the editor simply opens. An editor that hasn't started 4s after the box appeared gives it up for the Loading splash. The strip and the marks are the Homepage's own (its strip's markup and its marks' tile, handed over with the box's place), so they are drawn in the beader's language, theme and zoom before the editor's code runs.
- **The Progress bar (tickets 0739, 0743):** the editor's own bar (ProgressBar card), not a look-alike, with the sections in place of rows: the counter ("1/3", `control`), the track with a named step per section, **Back ↑** (quiet, 32px `sm`) where Row not done sits and **Next ↓** (primary, 32px `sm`) where Row done sits. Signed off as variant A of the claude.ai canvas "Homepage progress bar" (https://claude.ai/artifact/NhEega7RYGk5JXsax8QsKn). Since ticket 0780 it is the same bar of steps as Convert image's (ProgressBar card): 64 tall with room at both ends, every name centred under its dot inside the track, and the fill and spark following the page's scroll between the sections.
  - **The steps are the sections.** One step per section, in the page's order, named by the section's own heading ("What's inside", "Made by one family", "Three ways to use it" today). Nothing in the bar counts them: a section added, removed or moved shows up as a step added, removed or moved, the spacing and the counter following.
  - **Where each step sits:** of n sections, step i (from 0) sits at i / (n − 1) of the track: the first at its start, the last at its end, the rest evenly between. A dot centred on the track, its name under it (`meta-small`, lowercase); the first name starts at the track's start, the last ends at its end, the others are centred under their dot.
  - **The fill starts empty:** it is section / (n − 1), so the track is empty on the first section, reaches each step's dot as you arrive on it and is full on the last (it used to fill a third per section, so the first already showed a third done).
  - **The step's look:** passed, a filled `--step-dot` (8px) in `accent`; ahead, the same dot in `track`; each with a 2px `box` edge so it stands off the fill. The current one, a `--step-dot-current` (14px) ring, `--step-ring` (3px) of `accent-strong` round `box`, which takes the spark's glow once the fill has started. Names: current in `ink`, passed in `body`, ahead in `muted`. The spark's tail runs into the current ring; its own dot isn't drawn, the ring is the spark, flaring on each step forward.
  - **A step is a button:** a click or tap on its dot or name turns the page to that section (a jump of several sections turns once, not through each), named "Go to {section}" with `aria-current="step"` on the current one. The track stays a `progressbar` named "Sections", its value text "2/3 · Made by one family". The section's name beside the counter is gone: the steps name themselves.
  - Back is disabled on the first section and Next on the last, each Tooltip saying why ("This is the first section." / "This is the last section."). Each step forward flares the ring as Row done flares the spark, a step back moves it with no flare, and reaching the last section plays Pattern done's burst at the track's end (its three flashes along a short span there, no shimmer or stars).
  - **Height:** the bar is `--step-bar-height` (64px; ticket 0780), the track 8px down and the names from 20px. On a phone (30rem and under) the names are `meta-tiny` and wrap at `--step-name-max` (92px) to two lines, the track row is 52px, and Back and Next take the bar's second row, half each, as Row not done and Row done do (about 96px in all).
  - Styles: `.bb-section-steps`, `.bb-section-step` and its `--first`, `--last`, `--passed`, `--current` and `is-lit` in `bundle.css`.
- **What's inside (ticket 0797):** `serif-heading`, no label above it and no hint beside it. Under it four cards, then the list of what's inside on the left and the editor on the right, playing each one in turn, in a loop. Signed off as variant A+D, "Tiles that play", of the claude.ai prototype "What's inside prototypes" (https://claude.ai/artifact/EVV6zb61kYqCpQgZnY2M5j), its Russian rendition at 1440 × 900 the reference. It replaces the carousel of seven drawn examples (ticket 0739): the carousel, its examples, its ‹ › arrows and its "3 / 7" count are gone.
- **The cards:** four in a row from 1024, two by two from 481, one column on a phone. Each is a quiet tile that never plays and takes no press: `panel` fill, a 1px `panel-rule` edge, `radius-lg`, no glow and no hover change; `--inside-card-height` (92px) tall (as tall as its text on a phone), padding 11 14; a 20px icon in `muted`, then the name (`--type-inside-name`, Inter 600 14, one line) over up to three lines of text (`--type-inside-text`, Inter 12/16, `body`). In order: `device` **Yours, on this device** · `pen` **For iPad and Pencil** (no word of the mouse) · `check` **Free forever** (all you need is free, nothing is paid now, and professional extras come later) · `keyboard` **Keyboard shortcuts** (every editing action has one, all listed in Menu → Help → Keyboard shortcuts; the path's three names are the app's own labels, not typed into the copy). Copy: `writing.md`, What's inside (ticket 0797).
- **The list:** `--inside-list-width` (272px) wide, 16px from the editor, 13 rows 2px apart, each a button: an 18px icon and the name, in `muted`; hover `hover-fill` and `ink`. The row playing is on `panel` with a 2px `accent` bar at its left (inset 7px top and bottom), its icon in `accent-strong`, its name in `ink`, and its line under the name in `body` (`--type-inside-line`, Inter 13/18); the other rows show their name alone. In order, each with the icon the editor uses for it: Canvas and Frame (`select`) · Techniques (`grid`) · Project editing (`paint`) · Mirror (`mirror`) · Reference image (the reference image icon) · Convert image (`image`) · Colors built in (`palette`) · Letters (`pattern`) · Starters (`frame`) · Row progress (`turn`) · Beads needed (`bead`) · Exports (`export`) · Saved Projects (`library`).
- **The editor:** the editor's own parts, never a picture or a copy of them, at the editor's own size: the Canvas strip (Menu, Project and Frame; Reference image, Undo and Redo), the Canvas and its Position marks, the Frame on its Margin sheet, the Tool pill and its color popover, the Selection bar, the Mirror bar, the Adjust bar, the Progress bar, the Menu, Frame and Project panels, the Starter gallery, Convert image and the Export view. It holds a demo Project of its own, an 11 × 11 loom Frame on Delica 11/0 named "Kaleidoscope", which is never saved and never touches the beader's Projects, settings, Palette or Undo. Every press in it works: what the beader does there is real editing of the demo Project, and an export from it is a real file of it. Its Menu holds the Projects section (New Project empties the demo Canvas; Convert image; Saved Projects, listing the demo Project and two more of the demo's own, "Heart" and "Bracelet", never the beader's) and the Help section's Keyboard shortcuts; the language, theme and the editor's settings are left out, so nothing in the demo changes the app. It fills the section's height beside the list.
- **The ghost:** a pointer that is not the beader's: the system arrow, white (`--ghost-fill`) with a 1.5px dark edge (`--ghost-edge`) and a soft shadow, 28 × 32, `aria-hidden`. It moves where a beader's hand would over `--ghost-move` (420ms, `ease-standard`) and presses what a beader would press, each press leaving a ripple: a 30px ring, white at 30% over white at 5%, growing from 30% to 130% and fading over `--ghost-ripple` (700ms, `ease-out`). Calm: one thing moves at a time, nothing flashes, nothing appears that the ghost did not make.
- **The tour:** the rows in order, each shown by doing it in the editor, its row lit while it plays, 350ms before and 450ms after. After the last the ghost hides for 1.8s and the tour starts again on an empty Canvas. What each row does:
  1. **Canvas and Frame:** the open Canvas with no Frame (the Progress bar's no-Frame state). With Paint the ghost draws a small kaleidoscope in the middle, then a rhombus on its left and a flower on its right, small and well clear of it, each one color at a time, the color picked on the Tool pill first (a recent swatch, or the color popover for one that isn't recent). Then Select: a Selection round the middle drawing alone, the Selection bar, and Set Frame, held with its Tooltip, then pressed. The Selection becomes the Frame on its Margin sheet and Row progress starts; the rhombus and the flower stay where they were, on the Canvas, outside the Frame. Nothing zooms and nothing fades in.
  2. **Techniques:** the title block's Technique, peyote, then brick stitch, then loom again, each held 1.15s.
  3. **Project editing:** Paint, three beads, then Undo.
  4. **Mirror:** Mirror from the Tool pill over the whole Frame, Radial with 4 folds (a kaleidoscope); a few beads, each followed by its counterparts; blue picked; a few more.
  5. **Reference image:** Close mirror; Select over the whole Frame and Delete on the Selection bar; the Canvas strip's Reference image button; the picture (a landscape: sun, hills, sky and ground) lands in Adjust, the ghost makes it a little smaller from its corner and presses Done; then it traces the picture, Paint for the sun, the hills' edge and the ground, Fill for the sky and the grass.
  6. **Convert image:** Menu on the Canvas strip, then Convert image in the Menu's Projects section: Convert image opens at its first step, held 3.4s, and closes.
  7. **Colors built in:** the color popover, the Miyuki Delica palette, DB55 picked, a few flowers painted in the meadow.
  8. **Letters:** the title block's Letters switch: each color's letter on its beads.
  9. **Starters:** the Frame panel, Replace Frame, the Starter gallery: Earrings, V fringe, its setup scrolled through, Replace. The earring is the Frame now; the landscape that was the Frame stays on the Canvas beside it.
  10. **Row progress:** Row done on the Progress bar for each of the earring's 13 steps, the first three slower; the spark flares each time and Pattern done plays at the end.
  11. **Beads needed:** the Frame panel opened on Beads needed.
  12. **Exports:** the Frame panel's Export, then PDF: the Export view, with page 1, held 3.8s.
  13. **Saved Projects:** Menu, then Saved Projects: the list, held 3.2s.
- **Choosing a row:** a press on a row plays from there: the editor starts afresh, every row before it is applied at once (no ghost, no motion), then the ghost plays on from that row. The tour starts by itself 900ms after the section first shows; a row pressed sooner wins.
- **Taking over:** a press, a key or a drag into the editor stops the ghost for the rest of the visit, leaving the editor as it is for the beader to try, the list unlit. A press on a row starts the tour again from there.
- **Only while seen:** the tour plays only while What's inside is the section shown and the page is visible. Turning away stops it; coming back starts it again from the first row, unless the beader has taken over, whose editor is kept as they left it.
- **The demo's sheet:** the band on the demo Frame's Margin sheet has no Row not done; the Progress bar under it keeps its own. It is the one place the sheet differs from the MarginSheet card, which says so. Otherwise the sheet follows that card, its band and title block inside it at its scale.
- **On a tablet (481 to 1023):** the list is a row of tabs, the names alone, scrolling sideways, above the editor; the tour and the ghost are as above.
- **On a phone (480 and under):** no editor and no tour. The cards in one column, then the 13 rows as a plain list, each with its icon, its name and its line, nothing to press.
- **Reduced motion:** no ghost and no loop. The editor shows the end of the first row (the Frame set, the rhombus and the flower beside it); a press on a row shows the end of that row at once.
- **Screen readers:** the list is a list of buttons, each named by its row, the one playing `aria-current="step"`; the editor is a region named "What's inside: the editor" that the ghost never takes the focus from. The ghost and its ripples are `aria-hidden`.
- **Coffee tile:** as wide as the section, centred in it: a beaded cup with three beads of steam, "Made by one family" in `serif-heading` 30px, one line of text with no width cap (one line where it fits, two on a tablet or a narrow laptop, more only on a phone), **Buy me a coffee** (secondary; a link opening the page in a new tab, inert while the app has no link). The whole tile is that link (ticket 0718): a click or tap anywhere on it opens the page, and hovering it shows the button's hover; keyboards and screen readers meet one link, Buy me a coffee, with the focus ring on the button. No donation-service name, logo or colours.
- **Three ways to use it:** the heading on the left and the reason ("Accounts and Pro aren't open yet. Everything works without them.") on the right, wrapping under it where there is no room; no "plans" label. An image named "Coming soon"; the tile `aria-disabled`.
- **The free list (tickets 0739, 0740):** No account's list is everything the app does free, nearly all of it without an account, under "forever free:" (`meta`, `muted`, lowercase), each row a check in `accent-strong` and its line in `body`, the last row "More on the way" in `muted` with a plus. The app's real features in its own words, in this order: runs in the browser with the work kept on the device; the open Canvas; the Techniques; any number of Projects; picture to beads; one Frame per Project; many Schemas per Project; Row progress; the whole editor; PDF; PNG; Beads needed; the built-in catalogs; the `.openbeads` file; the Word chart; Mirror; the Starters (`writing.md`, the free list). **Where the app differs (ticket 0742):** the "screen" the free list grows down is the plans section's room in the canvas box, under the hero and above the Progress bar, not the window: the box never moves, so past the plans the page reaches on as far as the whole list needs and the plans scroll up inside their section as the page scrolls (DESIGN.md §4.7). The counted rows take each language's counted forms ("3 Techniques: …"; Russian "3 техники: …" in place of "Техник: 3 — …", as the Russian copy has no em dash), and the names are joined as the language joins a list (English with a serial comma: "loom, peyote, and brick stitch").
