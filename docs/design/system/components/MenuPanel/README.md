# MenuPanel

The body of the Menu: what is about the app and the device. It renders as a dropdown under its strip button on every screen size (ADR 0046). The Project body is the ProjectPanel card; the Frame panel is in the Frame card; the Export panel is the ExportPanel card. All four open in the same shell, one at a time, close the same way and are built from the same four parts (below), so they look and space alike. (Spec 0440, tickets 0443, 0475; redesigned in ticket 0553, design canvas link on ticket 0553 in the private docs repo.)

## Panel (the dropdown)

- Opens 4px under the Menu button on the Canvas strip, left edges aligned. `panel-width` (520px, 585px at the app's 18px root) wide, `radius-lg`, `overlay-fill` with a 1px `overlay-line` edge (2px `line-strong` in high contrast), `elevation-3`, padding 8, `z-popover`. Taller than the box: its body scrolls inside, with no scrollbar of its own (a tablet shows none), so it shows that it scrolls two quiet ways: the edge where more lies fades over `--panel-edge-fade` (24px), as the Canvas strip's scrolling row does (top, bottom or both), and a slim thumb, `--panel-thumb-width` (3px) of `line-strong` with `radius-full`, stands centred in the 8px right padding beside the body. Its share of the body's height is the share that shows (never under `--panel-thumb-min`, 24px), and it runs from the body's top to its foot as the body scrolls. It is only a look, not a handle: a finger, the wheel or the keys scroll the body. Where everything fits, nothing fades and there is no thumb. It ends 8px above the canvas box's bottom bar (the Progress bar, or Convert image's steps; ticket 0850), never over it; where it hangs from no canvas box, 16px above the window's bottom edge. This holds for every panel hung from the strip (Menu, Project, Frame, Export).
- Closes on Escape, a press outside it (the press only closes), or the lit Menu button. Focus returns to the button. A press on the bottom bar is no press outside: it works at once (one press on Row done marks the row) and the panel stays open, so Beads needed can be read while weaving (ticket 0850).
- Motion: `duration-base` in, 150ms out, opacity and a 4px move; a fade only under reduced motion (as the Menu card).

## Density (ticket 0553)

About half the air of v18: every row is `panel-row` (28px; `panel-row-touch`, 36px, on a coarse pointer), padding 0 10, a 16px icon and an 8px gap. Sections are split by a 1px `line-soft` rule with 4px above and below it. The whole Menu, all sections open, is about 440px tall (was about 660). The 36px touch row is a deliberate exception to `touch-target` (responsive.md).

## The four shared parts

- **PanelSection:** a collapsible section. Its header is one `panel-row` button: the label in `label` style (DM Mono, lowercase) and a 14px chevron at the right (down when open, right when closed), `aria-expanded`. A collapsed section can show a short summary in `meta` before the chevron (Project and Frame use this: "Miyuki Delica 11/0 · real", "24 × 30 · ≈ 38 × 39 mm"). Open or closed is remembered on the device, per panel and section; on a first visit every section is open. A section whose help is read once has a 16px Info button right after its label (ButtonCard's Info button; the reference image section, ticket 0790). **Folded parts stand out (ticket 1121):** a closed section's label and chevron, a closed fold row's label and mark (PanelItem with `expanded` false), Beads needed's "Show all colors" while the rest are hidden and a folded QualitySlider's name and chevron are `accent-2` (teal in light, mint in dark), never the one accent; open, they are `muted` / `ink` again. A section that must never fold (the Frame panel's export) is `alwaysOpen`: the same `label` heading, no button and no chevron.
- **PanelItem:** a row: icon, label (`control`), an optional value at the right in `meta`, and an optional trailing 14px mark in `muted`: `chevron-right` (drawn as `chevron-left` turned) when it opens a subpanel, `external` when it leaves the app, or a PanelSection's fold mark (right when folded, down when unfolded, `aria-expanded`) when it unfolds rows right under it in the panel and closes nothing (the Project panel's Export, ticket 1058). `accent` text for the panel's main action, `danger` for a destructive one. Hover `hover-fill`, pressed `press-fill`, the 2px focus ring 2px away. A disabled item keeps its Tooltip with the reason and shows `faint` text.
- **PanelPicker:** (its list is a Menu: the first and last option's outer corners run parallel to the list's, 7px, 6px in High contrast; ticket 1080) a setting shown as its current value: icon and label on the left, a small button on the right (`picker-height`, 24px; 32 on touch; padding 0 6 0 8, `radius-sm`, `button` fill, 1px `button-line`) holding an optional 14px icon, the value word (`pill` type) and a 12px chevron. It opens a menu under it, right-aligned, 184px wide, items `panel-row` tall with a check on the chosen one (`panel-row-touch` on a coarse pointer wherever the picker sits, the converter's page too, each item its own hit area since the items touch; ticket 0836); the button's edge turns `ink` while it is open. It replaces SettingChoice and the stacked SegmentedControls: every preference with three or more choices, and Input and Writing hand, is a PanelPicker. A two-choice toggle stays a small SegmentedControl on the row.
- **PanelListRow:** a two-line row, `panel-row-two-line` (40px; 48 on touch), padding 0 6: a 28px thumbnail (`radius-sm`, on `board`), the name (`control`, one line, ellipsis) over a second line in `small` / `muted`. Used by the Projects library, where the row is a button that wraps: a 44px thumbnail in place of the 28px one (`library-thumbnail-size`, the SavedProjects card's circle size), its name on up to two lines before the ellipsis, its second line wrapping, and 4px above and below (ticket 0982); still (no button, no hover, one line each) for the reference image's picture (ticket 0790).

## Sections, in this order

0. **Account** (the Menu's user section; tickets 1087, 0078, 1086). The first section, above Projects, a PanelSection like the others (its label "Account", folding, remembered). Absent in the Homepage's demo and in the demo tour.
   - **Guest:** one PanelItem, **Sign in** (`account`, ›, `ink`: New Project stays the panel's one `accent` row), which pushes the Sign in Subpanel (SignIn card). Under it, from the label column, one line in `small` / `muted`: "Everything works without an account."
   - **Signed in:** a row that is a fact, not a button: the `account` icon, the verified email in `control` / `ink` (selectable), and the plan at the right in `meta` / `muted`: "Free account" or "Pro". No name and no picture: neither is stored. A long email keeps to one line and cuts at its end with an ellipsis, the plan always whole; the whole address is the row's Tooltip and accessible name. On a phone the plan drops under the email, from the label column, and may wrap. Under the row, from the label column, the settings line in `small` / `muted`, ending in a 16px Info button: "Your settings are the same on all your devices." The Info button ("Which settings are the same on all your devices") lists them: "Language, theme and Lifted pieces. Every other setting stays on this device." The line never changes; the list in the note grows as more settings are kept in step. Then **Sign out** (`sign-out`), a PanelItem with no confirm, since nothing on the device is lost. Under it **Delete account** (`delete`, the `danger` PanelItem, ticket 0082), which asks "Delete your account?" first (ConfirmDialogs card); once the account is gone the section shows the Guest's state and a toast says "Your account is deleted. Your Projects are still here." When the backend can't be reached nothing changes and a toast says when to try again, as for Sign in.
   - **No answer from the backend** (offline, or the backend down): the device shows the email it remembers, exactly as signed in, with no mark.
   - **The session ended** (after its 90 days): the section quietly shows the Guest's state again. No toast.
   - **The backend can't be reached** when a Guest presses Sign in: the row stays enabled, and the press shows a toast and opens nothing (Message card, Sign-in unavailable).
   - Back from Google signed in, the Menu opens on this section; no toast, also when signing in changed the language or theme.
   - With this section the Menu is taller than a short screen (820px) and scrolls inside, as the Panel rule above says; sections fold.
   - Later, without changing this: the email row gains a › and pushes an Account Subpanel for passkeys (ticket 0389) and Sign out on all devices (ticket 0323); Delete account moves into it then. At the release (2026-11-20) it is a row of the section, and the privacy policy is a row of About, for everyone (ticket 0082).

## Projects library (subpanel)

Pushed by the Projects library row, under the header "Projects library" (Back icon button, the title in `title`, the count in `meta` at the right).

- **Open Project file** (`import`, value `.openbeads`), **Save all Projects to a Project file** (`export`, value `.openbeads`) and, in the editor, **Delete all Projects** (`danger`, `delete`) as PanelItems, then a `line-soft` rule (ticket 0859: the file's one name, "Open" in and "Save" out). The two rows line up: each label from the same column, each `.openbeads` at the row's right edge. Open's result or error (ImportResult card) is a line of its own under its row, never in the row. Where the device has no touch, Open's file picker offers `.openbeads` (and an older build's `.json`) files; on iPad and phones it offers every file (ADR 0048).
- The list, newest first, one PanelListRow per project: the thumbnail (the whole project, as SavedPatterns drew it), the name, and on the second line what tells one Project from another: the Frame's size and the last save, then the maker's name when set ("24 × 14 beads · saved Nov 12 · Ada"; no size without a Frame, "saved Nov 12"; ticket 0982). The row's name is these words, nothing hidden added (ticket 0940). At the right only a 20px × Remove shown on hover or keyboard focus (always shown without hover; on a coarse pointer the same × sits in a `touch-target` box, so its whole hit area lies inside the row, clear of the row's own button; ticket 0836). The open project's thumbnail is ringed in `accent` and its name is `accent-strong`. A press opens the project and closes the panel. Hovering the second line (a long press on touch) shows a Tooltip: "3 pieces" and the named Pieces ("Petal · Stem · 1 unnamed").
- **Delete all Projects** sits under Save all, above the rule, never at the foot of the list, so it is not the nearest thing under the last Project (ticket 0984). Its confirmation names how many go and offers Save all (ConfirmDialogs card).
- Empty: Save all Projects to a Project file and Delete all Projects are disabled with their Tooltips, and the list's place says "No Projects yet" over "Projects you make are kept in this browser. Open a Project file to bring one in."
- × Remove asks the Project panel's Delete question, "Delete “{name}”?" with Save Project file (ConfirmDialogs card, ticket 0842); deleting the open Project opens the most recently updated other one.
- A **sealed** Project (over the size gate, or the one that didn't open last time, ticket 0716) is never decoded for its row: its thumbnail slot is a plain `board` square with the `library` icon in `muted`, the second line its bead count and last save ("31 771 464 beads · saved Oct 30"), its Tooltip both dates ("made Oct 1, 2026 · saved Oct 30, 2026"). A project whose Canvas has more than 250,000 beads has the Tooltip "Counting pieces" until idle time has counted them, and "too big to count" over the size gate.

## Subpanel (spec 0478)

A panel can push **subpanels** over its root, as a stack (ticket 0493): New Project's Pick a starter pushes the Starter gallery's categories, a category its options, and an option its setup (StarterGallery card, ticket 0595), each under its own name; Back and Escape pop one level, and focus returns to the card that opened it. The header is a `chevron-left` Back icon button (plain, 8px gap) and the subpanel's title in `title`, 8px above the body. Back returns to the list and puts focus on the row it came from; Escape does the same, and closes the panel when the subpanel was the opening view (an empty library, or the empty-canvas bar's New Project button). Focus enters on the first field. The dropdown keeps `panel-width`, with one exception: the Keyboard shortcuts Subpanel widens it to `panel-width-wide` and Back narrows it again (ShortcutsHelp card; approved 2026-11-20, not yet in the app, ticket 1159). The header stays pinned while the body scrolls. A panel's `footer` slot (a pinned row, a 1px `panel-rule` above, padding 4 8 8) holds the Starter setup's Create Project. Closing the panel, or swapping to another, resets to the root and drops a draft. The panel is a `dialog` while a subpanel shows.

Nothing repeats a tool: Rulers, Undo, Redo and zoom live on the Canvas strip, Mirror in the pill, its bar and the Frame's margin.

## Keyboard and screen readers

- The strip button is "Menu" / «Меню», `aria-expanded`. Enter, Space or Down opens on the first item, Up on the last. Arrows, Home and End move; Enter chooses; Tab and Shift+Tab wrap (ADR 0075); Escape closes.
- The dropdown is a plain `region` named "Menu", its rows buttons, not menu items: it holds headings and pickers, which a `menu` may not (ticket 0856; Project and Frame hold fields, so they are plain `dialog`s, as is every subpanel); rules are `separator`; a section header is a button with `aria-expanded` and the section a `group` named by it. A PanelPicker is a button with `aria-haspopup="menu"`, named "{label}: {value}"; its menu items are `menuitemradio`. Escape in an open picker closes the picker only.
- A Version row is read as text.
