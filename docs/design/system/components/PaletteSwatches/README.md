# PaletteSwatches

The Colors group: the Pattern's Palette as an 8-column grid of square swatches that grows by rows from 12 to 40, then Custom.

- **Grid:** 8 columns, gap 6, square swatches, radius-sm, inset 1px `swatch-edge` so ivory and black do not vanish.
- **Contents:** the 12 built-in swatches first, then every Custom color that joined the Palette by painting a cell, in the order it was added. Up to 28 can be added, so the group holds 12 to 40 swatches.
- **Grows by rows beyond twelve:** the 12 built-in swatches take a row and a half; added swatches 13 to 16 complete the second row, and each further eight add a row, up to five full rows at 40. The grid wraps; it never clips and never scrolls sideways. The Toolbox grows with it.
- **Added swatches** look and behave exactly like built-in ones: the same size, the same inset edge, the same selected ring, the same focus ring.
- **Selected:** `0 0 0 2px panel, 0 0 0 4px ring`. **Focus:** 2px `focus-ring`, 2px away (the app now matches; it was 4px).
- **Touch** (`pointer: coarse`): `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(36px, 1fr))` instead of 8 columns, the same 6px gap, so swatches stay at least 36px.
- **One Swatch (ticket 333; `AppSwatch` in the app):** the Palette, and the Canvas color popover use the same chip. It takes a color, `selected`, an optional key chip and, in Edit palette, a ×. The ring's gap is the surface it sits on (`panel` in the Toolbox, `overlay-fill` in a popover).
- **Tooltip:** the name "Color" (Russian «Цвет»), the hex as the body and the key chip where there is one (see the Menu card). Only the first seven Palette swatches that are not one of the pill's three recents have a key (Shift+4 to 9, Shift+0; Shift+1 to 3 are the recents, see the ToolPill card); added swatches and Canvas colors show just the name and hex.
- **Accessible names:** every swatch has one. A built-in swatch is "Color 1, black"; an added swatch is named by its hex ("#6b3fa0").
- **Removing an added swatch (ticket 0531, replaces tickets 228 and 304's hover ×):** at rest no swatch shows a ×; the only ways to remove are **Edit palette** (below) and Delete or Backspace on a focused added swatch (`aria-keyshortcuts="Delete Backspace"` on added swatches only; they act on the swatch, not on the Erase tool or the Selection, and work on any focused added swatch, selected or not).
  - **Confirmation (ticket 304, unchanged):** every route opens a confirmation in the ConfirmDialogs style, and nothing is removed until Confirm. English: title "Remove color?", message "{hex} will leave the Palette. Painted cells keep their color.", buttons "Cancel" and "Remove". Russian: «Убрать цвет?», «Цвет {hex} уйдёт из палитры. Закрашенные ячейки сохранят свой цвет.», «Отмена» и «Убрать». Cancel, Escape and the backdrop leave the swatch alone.
  - After Confirm the grid closes the gap and a toast offers Undo (see the Message card). Undo puts the swatch back in its place, selected again if it was selected.
- **Edit palette (ticket 0531):** a mode of the Palette in the colour popover (ToolPill card holds the pencil that enters it). While it lasts:
  - **Added swatches** show a **×** centered on the swatch, always, not only on hover: the `close` glyph at `--swatch-edit-remove-size` (16px, about 60% of a 27px swatch) with `--swatch-edit-remove-stroke` (2.5), round-ended, drawn `swatch-mark-dark` or `swatch-mark-light` whichever reads on that swatch's hex, with the same thin `swatch-mark-halo` of the other color. No background, border or badge: just the glyph, appearing with a fade of `duration-fast`, none under reduced motion. The whole swatch is the hit area (the 36px floor on touch), so a click, tap, Enter or Space anywhere on it asks to remove it with the confirmation above; Delete and Backspace do too. The ×  is a picture, not a second button or Tab stop. The swatch's name is "Remove #6b3fa0 from the Palette" («Убрать #6b3fa0 из палитры») instead of the hex alone. Choosing a color does not happen in this mode and the popover stays open.
  - **Built-in swatches** are faded to `--swatch-faded-opacity` (0.35), keep their place and can still be focused and dragged. A Tooltip (AppTooltip, on hover and focus) says "Default colors can't be removed" («Стандартные цвета убрать нельзя»); the swatch is `aria-disabled="true"` and the Tooltip text is its description.
  - **Pick color from canvas** and **Custom color** are disabled (the disabled look in forms-and-states.md) and take no press.
  - **Reorder:** dragging a swatch to another slot (and Alt+Left/Right) works as in normal mode; a press that moves past the drag threshold never counts as a click, so it never asks to remove.
  - **Exit:** the pencil again, Escape (leaves the mode only; the popover stays), closing the popover, or removing the last added color. The popover always opens in normal mode.
  - **Remove all custom colors:** see the ToolPill card (bin after the word "Palette"). Confirmation English: title "Remove all custom colors?", message "{count} colors will leave the Palette. Painted cells keep their color." (one: "1 color will leave…"), buttons "Cancel" and "Remove all". Russian: «Убрать все свои цвета?», «Цвета ({count}) уйдут из палитры. Закрашенные ячейки сохранят свой цвет.», «Отмена» и «Убрать все». Cancel is the focused button. One toast offers a single Undo that puts every removed color back in its slot.
- **No cap (ticket 0525):** any number of Custom colors can be added; the grid grows by rows and the popover or sheet scrolls. The Palette belongs to its Project, so it is the same on every device.
- **Pick color from canvas (tickets 398, 403):** the grid's last tile, a swatch's size (the same 36px floor on touch) holding the 22px `eyedropper` icon, with the Toolbox button's look and `aria-pressed` while it is on. It is not a slot: it isn't dragged and has no Shift+key. While it is on the canvas pointer is the eyedropper, hot spot on its tip, in place of the bead marker and paint preview; a chip below and right of the tip (flipping at the canvas edge) shows the hex of the bead under it, and none over an empty cell or for a finger. Esc or choosing a tool ends it.
- **Tools sit on this grid (v18):** the Tools group above uses the same 8 columns, 6px gap and square size, so tool tiles and swatches line up (ToolTabs card).
- 12px below: the **Custom** button (a 14px hatched square in `muted`, always, never a fill of the chosen colour, then the label; the hatch stands for "pick any color") Unchanged by the longer grid.
- The swatch colors are the Pattern's bead colors (user data), never brand colors. The consumer provides them and the selected index.
