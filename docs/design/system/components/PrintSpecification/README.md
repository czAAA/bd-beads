# PrintSpecification

The Specification page of the PDF, after the chart pages: everything needed to buy the beads and find each part.

- Header: "loom · {name} · {maker}" in DM Mono `muted`, "Specification" in Inter 600, the page number on the right.
- Beads needed as a full table under a 1.5 px `ink` rule: letter (Inter 600), swatch (bead-shaped, the letter on it), color name, brand code (DM Mono), beads, grams, to buy. Rows on `line-soft` hairlines; a Total row under a 1.5 px rule with beads, grams and the tubes added up; the grams note.
- To buy: 5 g tubes from the grams with about 10% spare, rounded up ("2 × 5 g"). An unknown Bead shows no grams and no tubes.
- Under it, two columns: the Schema's facts (made by, technique, bead, size, finished size, beads, chart style, pages), and a map of the parts with their pages and a line on the 100-bead parts, shared sheets and overlap.
- Footer: brand, date and time, "row by row on page 6 →" when a Word chart follows.
