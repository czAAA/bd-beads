# CanvasBackground

The one background of the drawing area: the rulers, the technique word, the curve and the beads all sit on it, and the board has no second fill. The person picks one from the Canvas color button in the CanvasStrip: five in light, six in dark. The preview shows both themes with a different choice in each: Linen in light, Ash (the lightest dark canvas) in dark.

- **Colors made for the app.** Warm neutrals and faint tints taken from the app's own palette, in the spirit of Excalidraw's canvas picker. Light: Studio `#fafafa` (the default, equal to the `box` fill, so nothing shifts), Linen `#f7f3ec` (the export board color), Sage `#f1f5ee`, Mist `#eff4f8`, Blush `#fbf0ea`. Dark: Night `#1a1a1a` (the default, equal to `box`), Ink `#0e0e0e`, Midnight `#12161c`, Olive `#171608` (a nod to the yellow accent), Umber `#1c1714`, and Ash `#2c2b29`, a warm mid-dark grey, the lightest of the dark choices and still well inside the dark palette (between `box` and `line-strong`). Tokens `canvas-bg-1` to `canvas-bg-6`.
- **One choice, two palettes.** The app stores the number, not the color, so switching theme keeps the position: Sage (3) becomes Midnight (3). The light theme has no choice 6; a stored 6 shows Studio there and comes back when the theme does. The choice is remembered on the device, like the theme.
- **What it colors.** The whole drawing area, board included: the Pattern sits directly on the chosen color, so there is one background, not two (the `board` fill is set to the choice inside the drawing area). The strip, the Progress bar and every panel keep their own fills. Set `data-canvas-bg="1".."5"` on the canvas box; `components/bundle.css` maps it to `--canvas-bg`.
- **Picker.** A popover under the Canvas color button: a `canvas color` label (`meta-small`), five (light) or six (dark) 32px swatches in a row (radius-sm, 8px gap, 1px `field-line` edge), and the current name and hex beneath. The chosen swatch has a 2px `ring` outline 2px outside it and a check mark, so the choice is never shown by color alone. It is a radio group: Tab lands on the chosen swatch, arrows move and apply at once, Escape or a click outside closes and returns focus to the button. Each swatch is named "Sage, 3 of 5" (of 6 in dark). Coarse pointers get 40px swatches and a 44px button.
- **Contrast.** Rulers are at least 4.8:1 and the row marker at least 13:1 on all eleven backgrounds. On Ash (dark choice 6) the rulers use `#a0a0a0` (5.4:1, since `ruler` is 4.0:1 there), the empty beads `#3d3b38` (so they stay visible against the canvas), the technique word `#353432`, and the beads get a 16% white rim (dark has none elsewhere), so a black bead does not vanish. The marker keeps its yellow (13:1). The bead colors never change. With no board edge, each bead's rim (`bead-rim`, 24% ink in light, 16% white on Ash, none on the other dark choices) separates beads from the canvas.
- **High contrast.** All six are white, so the control is hidden and the drawing area stays white.
- **Not in exports.** PNG and PDF always print on `print-board`.
- **Phone and iPad mini.** The strip is not shown, so the same picker opens from the Pattern sheet's header row (the Canvas color button sits beside the zoom control there). This placement is proposed, not yet signed off.
- **Copy.** "Canvas color" (Russian "Цвет холста"); the choice names are in the Writing section.
