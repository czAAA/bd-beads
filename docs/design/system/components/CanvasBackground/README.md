# CanvasBackground

The one background of the drawing area: the rulers and the beads sit on it, and the board has no second fill. The person picks one in Project, under Canvas (ProjectPanel card; the Canvas color button left the strip in ticket 0443): five in light, five in dark (the palette approved in ticket 0554, below; the sections above still describe the first list of eleven, which that ticket replaced). The preview shows both themes with a different choice in each: Linen in light, Ash in dark.

- **Colors made for the app.** Warm neutrals and faint tints taken from the app's own palette, in the spirit of Excalidraw's canvas picker. Light: Studio `#fafafa` (the default, equal to the `box` fill, so nothing shifts), Linen `#f7f3ec` (the export board color), Sage `#f1f5ee`, Mist `#eff4f8`, Blush `#fbf0ea`. Dark: Night `#202020` (the default, equal to the dark `box` and `board`; the card first said `#1a1a1a`, but the app's dark canvas has always been `#202020` and the default must not shift), Ink `#0e0e0e`, Midnight `#12161c`, Olive `#171608` (a nod to the yellow accent), Umber `#1c1714`, and Ash `#2c2b29`, a warm mid-dark grey, the lightest of the dark choices and still well inside the dark palette (between `box` and `line-strong`). Tokens `canvas-bg-1` to `canvas-bg-6`.
- **One choice, two palettes.** The app stores the number, not the color, so switching theme keeps the position: Sage (3) becomes Midnight (3). The light theme has no choice 6; a stored 6 shows Studio there and comes back when the theme does. The choice is remembered on the device, like the theme.
- **What it colors.** The whole drawing area, board included: the Pattern sits directly on the chosen color, so there is one background, not two (the `board` fill is set to the choice inside the drawing area). The strip, the Progress bar and every panel keep their own fills. The six colors are the `canvas-bg-1` to `canvas-bg-6` tokens (light, dark and contrast; light's sixth repeats Studio, contrast's are all white). The drawing area takes `canvas-bg-N` for the stored number N; the app copies them into `src/rendering/canvasBackgrounds.ts`, and `canvasBackgrounds.test.ts` keeps the copy equal to `tokens.json`.
- **Picker.** The shared MenuButton's popover (ticket 338: the popover's fill, border, shadow and open and close come from the one popover look, with no transition of its own) under the Canvas color button: a `canvas color` label (`meta-small`), five (light) or six (dark) 32px swatches in a row (radius-sm, 8px gap, 1px `field-line` edge), and the current name and hex beneath. The chosen swatch has a 2px `ring` outline 2px outside it and a check mark, so the choice is never shown by color alone. It is a radio group: Tab lands on the chosen swatch, arrows move and apply at once, Escape or a click outside closes and returns focus to the button. Each swatch is named "Sage, 3 of 5" (of 6 in dark). Coarse pointers get 40px swatches and a 44px button.
- **Contrast.** Rulers (`ruler`, `#8c8c8c` in dark, 4.9:1 on Night) are at least 4.8:1 and the row marker at least 13:1 on the six dark backgrounds (in light it is royal blue, at least 4.5:1 on the five light ones, ticket 369). On Ash (dark choice 6) the rulers use `#a0a0a0` (5.4:1, since `ruler` is 4.0:1 there), the empty beads `#3d3b38` (so they stay visible against the canvas), and the technique word `#353432`. The marker keeps its yellow (13:1). The bead colors never change. With no board edge, each bead's rim (`bead-rim`: 12% ink in light, 16% white on every dark choice, so a black bead does not vanish) separates beads from the canvas.
- **High contrast.** All six are white, so the swatches are gone and Project's Canvas section holds one line saying so; the drawing area stays white.
- **Not in exports.** PNG and PDF always print on `print-board`.
- **Where it lives (ticket 0443).** Not a popover any more: five (light) or six (dark) round swatches in a row inside Project's Canvas section, 28px, 8px apart, the chosen one ringed. The same radio group, names and remembered choice as before. The popover paragraph above describes the earlier form.
- **Copy.** "Canvas color" (Russian "Цвет холста"); the choice names are in the Writing section.

**Approved palette (ticket 0554; in the tokens and the app since that ticket, and it wins over what is written above):** neutral steps from near-white to mid-grey, with no tints, since a tinted background shifts how bead colors look and the v18 light set kept white beads at about 1.0:1. Five in each theme.
- Light: Studio `#fafafa` (default), Linen `#f7f3ec`, **Stone** `#e6e3de` (new), **Fog** `#e3e7ec` (replaces Mist), **Pebble** `#cdcac4` (new). Sage and Blush go.
- Dark: Night `#202020` (default), Ink `#0e0e0e`, Midnight `#12161c`, Ash `#2c2b29`, **Slate** `#3b3e43` (new, for designs heavy in black). Olive and Umber go.
- Each background has its own empty-bead and ruler color, rulers at 4.5:1 or more: Studio and Linen `#d8d2cc` / `#6a6a6a`; Stone `#cbc6bf` / `#5c5c5c`; Fog `#c9ced5` / `#5c5c5c`; Pebble `#b3afa8` / `#444444`; Night `#3c3c3c` / `#8c8c8c`; Ink `#2c2c2c` / `#8c8c8c`; Midnight `#262c35` / `#8c8c8c`; Ash `#3d3b38` / `#a0a0a0`; Slate `#50545a` / `#b4b4b4`.
- The stored choice moves to the new list by name, not position: a dropped choice falls back to the theme's default, Mist becomes Fog, and Ash (dark 6) becomes dark 4. `canvas-bg-6` is gone. Pebble's strong ruler (every 5th number) is `#262626`.
- In Project the swatches are 26px `radius-sm`, 6px apart, ringed `ink` (ProjectPanel card).

**Custom color (ticket 0653; wins over the above where they differ):** the swatches are the standalone background color picker (`BackgroundColorPicker`), which Project's Canvas section uses and other places may later. After the five swatches comes the Palette's own Custom color tile (ColorPickers card), the same 26px size, 6px on; it is a button of its own after the radio group, ringed (`ring`) while the Custom color is the background. It expands the Palette's Custom color picker under the row, opening on the remembered Custom color, else on the background shown. Taking a color (a press outside, Enter, Escape after a change, or a second press on the tile) makes it the background at once; a press on one of the five swatches picks that one and takes nothing.
- One Custom color, remembered on the device, serves light and dark alike; picking a swatch keeps it on the tile. High contrast stays white: no swatches, no tile.
- The name line reads "Custom color" and its hex; the Canvas section's folded summary shows its chip and "Custom color".
- On a Custom color the Project is drawn in the light look on a light color and the dark look on a dark one (white or black reading better on it, `--print-letter-balance`), whatever the theme. Its empty bead is the color 12.5% toward black (light) or white (dark), Night's own step; its rulers are the nearest grey to the look's own at 4.5:1 or more, the strong ones at 7:1 (or black or white where none reaches it). The row marker keeps the look's color where it reads at 3:1 or more, and takes the look's ink (`bead-outline`) where it does not.
- With no radio checked, Tab lands on the first swatch.

**Second use (ticket 0655; MarginSheet card):** the Frame panel's margin section and the margin's Margin color button use the same BackgroundColorPicker with an **Auto** swatch first (drawn split, Canvas color and margin color), then the theme's five Canvas colors and the Custom color tile; the swatch equal to the Canvas color carries a dot. The margin color is remembered on the device, as the Canvas color is.

**Each layer's own (ticket 0666, ADR 0079; wins over the above where they differ):** the background is no longer one device choice but each layer's **Background**, saved with the Project, so a Frame can sit on another color than the Canvas and a Project looks the same on every device. The same list, numbers and Custom color: a layer stores its position (kept across light and dark) or its Custom color.
- Each layer's is picked in its Layer settings (ticket 0670, LayerSettings card): the Canvas's in Project's canvas section, the Frame's in the Frame panel's settings section (Frame card), the same picker, its radio group named "Frame background". The New Project form has none (ticket 0881): a new Project starts on the device's last pick. The 12px chip summary of ticket 0666 is gone: a folded section's summary is the layer's Technique and Bead.
- On the Canvas the Frame's box is filled in the Frame's background, a Custom frame's Off-shape included, and the Frame's beads, empty beads and position marks take that background's look (its empty bead, and on a Custom color the look it is drawn in). The rulers, the margin sheet and what lies round the Frame keep the Canvas's.
- A view: changing one is no Undo step, and Row progress never holds it.
- The device keeps the last one picked, for any layer, as the background a new Project's layers start with; a Project saved before ticket 0666 shows it, and the first pick on such a Project saves what each other layer showed, so a pick never moves another layer. Undo and Redo leave every layer's background as it is. The Custom color tile opens on the layer's own Custom color, else the last one taken on the device.
- High contrast keeps its one white canvas for every layer, and exports still print on `print-board`.

**Three per theme (ticket 1000; approved 2026-11-14, in the tokens and the app since that ticket; wins over the above where they differ):** three backgrounds in each theme in place of five. No color is new or changed.
- Light: 1 Studio `#fafafa` (default), 2 Stone `#e6e3de`, 3 Pebble `#cdcac4`. Dark: 1 Night `#202020` (default), 2 Ink `#0e0e0e`, 3 Slate `#3b3e43`. Tokens `canvas-bg-1` to `canvas-bg-3`; `canvas-bg-4` and `canvas-bg-5` go.
- **Why three.** Four of the ten were close copies of another (color distance, CIEDE2000: Ash 3.8 from Night, Midnight 4.7 from Ink, Linen 3.5 from Stone, Fog 4.9 from Studio). The three are three clearly different lightness levels, each with a job: the default; the deepest dark and a softer paper; and the mid tone, where the theme's hardest bead shows (a black bead in dark stands 2.05:1 off Slate's tiles against 1.27:1 on Night; a white bead in light 1.98:1 off Pebble's against 1.23:1 on Studio).
- **A stored choice moves to its nearest kept color, by one map for both themes:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 become 1, 2, 2, 1, 3. Linen goes to Stone, Midnight to Ink, Ash to Night and Fog to Studio; Ink, Stone, Slate and Pebble keep their color. It is applied once to everything that stores a position: the device's last pick and the margin color move to keys of their own (the old keys stay for older builds), and a saved Project, in the library or in a file, marks the list its layers' positions are in (`backgroundPositions: 3`), so a Project without the mark is moved when it opens and never again. A file written now and opened in an older build shows that build's color for the number.
- **Picker:** three swatches and the Custom color tile; a swatch is named "Stone, 2 of 3". The margin's picker is Auto, the three, and Custom color (MarginSheet card). Custom color is unchanged, and is the way to any other ground.
- **Each background's grid is made by one rule** (BeadBoard card, Tonal tiles, ticket 1001), so the per-background empty-bead colors above no longer color the full look: only the draft look (zoomed out) and the Canvas under a reference image, which keep the outline and the empty bead, still use them. Rulers keep their own color per background: Studio `#6a6a6a`, Stone `#5c5c5c`, Pebble `#444444` (strong `#262626`), Night and Ink `#8c8c8c`, Slate `#b4b4b4`.
- The strip, the Progress bar and the panels keep their own fills, as before. High contrast stays white.
