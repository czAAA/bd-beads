TourPattern · the Pattern every Tour builds

10 columns × 75 rows, loom, the default Bead, Palette Yellow #f2c94c and Black #1a1a1a. Five gold rhombuses with a black eye on black, like a Belarusian rushnik band; built with Fill and copy and paste.

How the Tour builds it

  • Step 2: Fill the empty board black.
  • Step 3: Paint the 22-bead outline of the first rhombus in yellow, rows 3–13.
  • Step 4: Fill inside the outline in yellow. Fill spreads to the four neighbours of a bead (not diagonally), so the diagonal outline holds it.
  • Step 5: Paint the eye in black: 16 beads, an inner ring and a 2-bead centre.
  • Step 6: Select rows 3–13, Copy, and paste at rows 18, 33, 48 and 63.
  • Step 7: the Tour adds the small gold beads between the rhombuses and rounds the ends (28 beads, one Undo step), leaving row 1, column 1 yellow.
  • Step 8: Erase row 1, column 1. Counts: Yellow 294, Black 452, empty 4.

Data and themes

  • The cell map below is the source; rows, counts and each step's cells are in components/TourPattern/tour-pattern.json. Rows and columns count from 1.
  • Marked beads use the Tour's gold outline; the Selection frame is the bead frame in accent; paste targets are dashed gold outlines.
  • Dark theme: Black #1a1a1a matches the dark board, so the black ground blends into it, but the gold rhombuses carry the design and read clearly.
