# ProgressBar

The bar along the canvas box's bottom edge holding every Row progress control. It is always shown, at every width including under 1024px, whatever the Pattern's shape; with no Project open it is there too, empty (ticket 0748). Its height is its tallest item (the 34px box icon buttons, Turn row direction and Reset progress, or Fit to drawing) plus 1px above and 1px below, 36px (ticket 0788; 40px while Row done and Row not done were 38px, 48px before ticket 544); `--progress-height` (controls.css). Where the screen has a bottom safe-area inset (an iPad's or iPhone's home indicator) the canvas box runs to the screen's bottom edge and the bar grows by the inset, padding its content above it, so no dead band shows under the bar (ticket 0788; the app shell's `--bar-bottom-inset`). It has a 1px edge on its left, right and top, and its bottom corners take the canvas box's radius (`radius-lg`, `--canvas-box-radius`).

Left to right, padding 0 24 (every bar, ticket 0780: room at both ends), gap 12, top divider `box-muted` at 22%:
1. **Remove frame (ticket 426)** was a box button here until ticket 0455: it is now the cross on the Frame's corner (Frame card), and still asks first. Row progress is on whenever the Project has a Frame, so there is no switch and no `P` key.
1a. **Edit Frame (ticket 443)** was an icon button here until ticket 0452 (ADR 0054): the edit toggle now sits on the Frame's margin outline (Frame card), so the bar has none.
2. **Turn row direction:** icon button, first in the bar and away from the do/undo pair, so it can't be hit by accident while doing or undoing rows. Pressed while rows run left to right. Turning it shows a short success toast naming the new direction ("Rows now run top to bottom."), once per change, the `D` key included. Its Tooltip's line: "Turn the way the rows run: top to bottom, left to right, bottom to top or right to left." (ticket 0844).
2a. **Row highlight (ticket 0474, ADR 0055):** Switch (30×18, as in the SwitchAndFileButton card; no visible label, its Tooltip names it, with the line "Show or hide the marks on the current row and the rows marked done.", ticket 0844; accent track while on) between Turn row direction and Reset progress, on at every width. On (the default) the grid looks as before; off, it draws no dimming on finished rows and no current-row ring, lift or brightening. View only: the pointer, Row done / Row not done, the counter, the track, Reset progress and the finished-row lock keep working, and a press on a locked bead still gives the locked feedback. Kept on the device, not in the Project, not an Undo step. Absent while there is no Frame.
2b. **Reset progress (ticket 0455):** icon button (`reset`, `box` look) right after Turn row direction. Asks first ("Reset progress?": every row marked done is cleared, you start again from the first row, the beads and the direction stay), then takes both pointers back to the first row; not an Undo step. Disabled (`aria-disabled`) until a row is done, its Tooltip saying "No rows are marked done yet."
3. **Counter** "5/30" (`control`), then the **track:** fills the free width, 4px, radius-full, `track`, with a fill showing the finished share: `linear-gradient(90deg, #fa520f, #ffa110)` in light, `#faff69` in dark (`--track-fill` in bundle.css). Once every pass is done the track is completely full (on loom, brick stitch and peyote, in both directions) and the counter shows the last row; a pass still open never fills it.
4. **Row not done:** the quiet 32px `sm` `text` button (no fill or edge until hovered) with a leading up arrow and its label, the bar of steps' Back (ticket 0788); moves the current row back one. Its key is Shift+Enter.
5. **Row done:** the 32px `sm` primary button with the label and a trailing down arrow, the bar of steps' Next (ticket 0788); marks the row finished and moves on. Disabled once every pass is done. Its key is Enter.

The keys are Enter and Shift+Enter only, never Space: Space + drag moves the Canvas, and a pan must not mark rows done (ticket 0835). Holding either key acts once; its repeats do nothing. The Tooltips' key chips and the Keyboard shortcuts list say the same.

**The lock, in one sentence (ticket 0848).** A row marked done is locked against drawing. A drawing it holds back says so in one `info` toast, once a stroke: "This row is marked done. Press Row not done to change it." ("This column …, Column not done" while rows run left to right). What else waits while a row is marked done (the Frame's size, Select a Frame, Replace Frame, the Technique and its settings) names the smaller step, never Remove Frame: "Rows are marked done. Reset progress to change the size." (the Frame card's line, with this Reset progress beside it), "… to change the Frame.", "… to start another Frame.", "… to change the Technique." The state is always "marked done" and the action "Row done", never "finished" or "marked as done".

Row done and Row not done show text and follow Row direction: top to bottom they read "Row not done ↑" and "Row done ↓"; left to right the arrows turn to point left and right and the words become "Column not done" and "Column done" (in every shipped language). Tooltips and screen-reader names are the visible labels.

**Motion (ticket 0729):** the track fill grows over `duration-base` (`ease-standard`, `transform` only) with a **spark** riding its leading edge: a 10px `--spark-core` dot with a `--spark-glow` halo and a 70px tail fading back along the fill (`.bb-spark` in `bundle.css`). It flares to 2.2 times and back over 520ms on each Row done and moves back with no flare on Row not done. It is hidden before the first pass and stays at the end once every pass is done, with no glow round the track. The press that marks the last pass plays Pattern done over the Frame (Motion section, Row progress). Under reduced motion the fill steps and the spark neither flares nor trails.

**Phone widths (30rem and under).** Row not done and Row done do not fit one row with Turn row direction, Reset progress and the track in every language at 360px, so they take a second row of their own, half the bar each (`type-small`), and the buttons sit 4px apart with 6px side padding and 2px between icon and label, so that the longest labels fit at 320px (ticket 556), and the bar is its two rows (34px, then the 32px pair) plus 4px between and 1px above and below, 72px (`--progress-height`), instead of 36px; everything above it follows that height.

**No Frame (v16).** Row progress works on the Frame: its rows are the Frame's rows. Until a Frame is set the bar shows the hint "[Select icon] Select to set a [Frame icon] Frame" (`meta`, `muted`; each icon, 14px, in a 20px hint chip like the CanvasHint's Kbd chips: `elevated`, 1px `line-strong` edge with a 2px bottom, `radius-xs`) and, right-aligned, a Fit to drawing button (secondary in a box, `frame` icon); Fit to drawing is shown only while there is no Frame (ticket 426). Row done and Row not done are not offered, and the bar keeps its height so the canvas does not jump.

**A Custom frame (ticket 0594).** Row progress steps through it by its Row order: a Triangle or Warped Square round by round from the centre, a Kite row by row from its point, a Star or Kaleidocycle part by part in making order and then through the joins, a Fringe earring (ticket 0597) through its top row by row from the base and then strand by strand from the left. The position reads its pass in place of "Row 3 of 20 · Top to bottom": "Round 3 of 9" or "Row 4 of 21", and for a part or a join "Part 2, round 3 · 8 of 20" or "Zip 1–2 · 16 of 20", on a Fringe earring "Row 2 · 2 of 13" or "Strand 3 · 9 of 13", counted of all its passes (same spans and type as the row's). Turn row direction is not offered. On the Canvas its finished rounds fade and the current one is lit and ringed as rows are, each bead's ring turned with it.

**On the Homepage too (tickets 0739, 0743, 0780; Homepage card):** the same bar, with the Homepage's sections in place of rows: the counter, the track with a named step per section (a dot at its place and the section's heading under it, the current one a ring the spark's tail runs into), Back ↑ where Row not done sits and Next ↓ where Row done sits (Next secondary there since ticket 0830, so the way into the editor is the page's one primary button). The fill starts empty on the first section and is full on the last, following the page's scroll between them; a step is a button to its section. A step forward flares the ring, a step back moves it with none, and the last section plays Pattern done's burst at the track's end.

**A bar of steps (ticket 0780):** the Homepage's bar and Convert image's are one component (`StepBar`), so what the 0778 redesign gave Convert image they both have: the room, the names inside the track, the fill following the scroll, the quiet 32px Back and Next. Only the arrows differ, by the way the page's steps run: down for the Homepage, across for Convert image. The editor's Row progress bar keeps its own height (36px), and shares the frame's room at both ends, the track, and since ticket 0788 the back and forward pair: one component (`ProgressMoves`) draws Back / Next and Row not done / Row done, so they are the same size and look on every page.

**On Convert image too (tickets 0756, 0778; ConvertImage card):** the same bar again, `StepBar` shared with the Homepage, holding the page's five steps in place of rows: the counter ("2/5"), the track with a named dot per step (picture & bead, background, size, colors, review; lowercase, under the dots), Back and Next. Redesigned in ticket 0778 so it has room and follows the page:
- **Room:** the bar is `--step-bar-height` (64) tall, padded 0 24, `--step-bar-gap` (28) between the counter, the track and the buttons, so nothing touches the screen's edge. The first and last dots stand `--step-names-inset` (52) in from the track's ends and every name is centred under its dot, so no name runs past the track or meets the counter or Back. The names are `meta-small`, the current one in `ink` at 500. **Where the names don't fit side by side** (two of them closer than 6px as laid out: the Homepage's four under about 950px, longer in a long language; ticket 0830) only the current step's name shows. The other steps keep their dots and stay buttons with their names for a screen reader, and each shows its name, on `box` over the current one's, while it is pointed at or focused. The names are measured, not counted, so a bar whose names fit shows them all.
- **It follows the scroll:** the steps are one scrolling pane, so the fill and its spark move continuously with the pane's scroll, the spark between two dots while the pane is between two steps and hidden on a dot. The current step is the one whose header is nearest the pane's top; reaching it flares its ring (no flare going back, none under reduced motion). A dot is a button that scrolls the pane to its step.
- **Back and Next point along the bar:** 32 (`sm`), "← Back" (a quiet button: no fill or edge until hovered) and "Next →" (primary), with the `arrow-left` and `arrow-right` icons, because the steps run sideways here. The Homepage keeps Back ↑ and Next ↓: its sections turn vertically. Back is disabled on the first step; on Review, **Next becomes Create Project** (primary, in Next's place, no arrow), disabled without a picture.
- **Until a picture is set** every step after the first is shut (`aria-disabled`, `faint`) and Next is disabled with "Add a picture first".
- No Pattern done burst. On a phone the names are `meta-tiny`, wrapped, the first starting at the track's start and the last ending at its end (there is no room for the inset), and Back and the forward button take a second row, half the bar each, until the phone layout of ticket 0778 is designed.

**In the margin too (ticket 0655; MarginSheet card):** the Frame's margin sheet carries the same Row progress component in its top band, compact: Turn row direction, Reset progress, Row highlight, the counter and a 64px track, Row not done (icon only) and Row done. It is one component in two places tracking the same progress: a press in either moves the one pointer, and both show it. The bar stays.

## As built (ticket 0730)

- **Track:** a groove inside the track's box holds the fill and the spark's tail, clipped to its rounded ends; the spark's dot sits in a copy outside it, so its halo and flare are not cut. The fill is the track's width moved left by the share still to weave (`translateX`), so it grows by `transform`; the spark moves the same way.
- **Flare:** two names for one animation are swapped on each Row done so the next press restarts it; a track shown afresh (the margin changing form) does not flare until the next press.
- **One track (ticket 0739):** the track, its fill and spark are one component (`ProgressTrack`), told the share done and counting the steps forward; Row progress's bar and margin and the Homepage's bar all draw it. The bar's frame takes other content in place of Row progress's, so the Homepage's bar is this bar.

## As built (ticket 0780)

- **One bar of steps:** `StepBar` takes the step in view and, when the page gives it, where the scroll is in steps (`position`, 1.4 is four tenths from the second to the third); the fill's share is that place over the last step, and the spark's dot shows only off a dot (more than 0.04 of a step from one). Convert image's pane and the Homepage's page both give it. A page that cannot scroll smoothly (reduced motion) gets there at once, and so does the fill.
- **Back** is the `text` button (no fill or edge until hovered), **Next** primary in Convert image and secondary on the Homepage (ticket 0830), both the 32px `sm` button (the Button card's `md` is 34); `arrows` picks ↑ ↓ (the Homepage) or ← → (Convert image). The pair is `ProgressMoves`, the same component the editor's Row not done and Row done are (ticket 0788), phone layout included.
- **Following a scroll, no ease (ticket 0786):** a track given the scroll's position (`following`) moves its fill and spark with no transition: the share is set on every scroll event, and Safari restarts an interrupted transform transition from a stale place, so with the 200ms ease the fill flickered back and forth under a touch scroll on an iPad. The editor's Row progress bar keeps the ease, its share changing once per row; a jump from Back, Next or a dot still runs smoothly, since the smooth scroll feeds the position per frame.
