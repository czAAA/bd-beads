# Message

A notice or toast: "couldn't save", import results and errors, confirmations of an action. (Derived in DESIGN.md.)

- **Shape:** `panel` fill (light) / `elevated` (dark), 1px `panel-line` (light) / `line-strong` (dark), radius-lg, `elevation-3`, padding 12 16, max width 420. A 3px left edge in the tone: `accent` for info and success, `warning`, or `danger`. The edge and the icon are always the one tone color, in every theme (ticket 0855). The `danger` tone is `--message-danger`: `danger` red in light and high contrast, gold (`accent-strong`) in dark.
- **Content:** a 16px icon in the tone color, the text (`body`), optional text-button actions, and a 28px close ×. A word longer than the line, such as a Project name with no spaces, breaks where it has to (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), so the text never runs out of the toast (ticket 0988).
- **Placement:** library-wide notices use the notice row under the header, full width, at elevation 0. A notice whose line names a Project ("“Scarf” didn't open last time.", `warning`, closable, ticket 0716) puts its two `in-box` actions, Remove and Save Project file (ticket 0859), on their own line under the text, so a long name keeps its room on a phone. Short-lived results show as a toast at the top-right of the canvas box, 16px in and under the strip (the save ring, which once pushed them down, is on the strip since ticket 0922; SaveReminder card), stacking downward on the toast layer, and go after 5s unless hovered or focused. Errors stay until closed. While a strip panel open over that corner covers their column (the Export panel above all; on a phone any panel, as it spans the width), they start 8px under the panel instead, so a result never covers the row just pressed (ticket 0859, F-02-08, F-06-11); a panel so tall that they would leave the canvas box leaves them on top.
- **Open in another tab** (notice, `warning`, not closable; ticket 0834): while the open Project is open in another tab, which opened it first. English: "This Project is open in another tab. Changes made here aren't saved while it is open there." Its one in-box action, "Work here instead" (ticket 0937), takes the Project over: this tab draws and saves it from what the other stored, and the other tab shows the notice in its turn. Meanwhile the Canvas is read-only: every Tool pill tile is off, its Tooltip saying "Off while this Project is open in another tab. Work here instead to draw.", and a press on the Canvas announces the notice's line again. It goes by itself once that tab opens another Project or closes.
- **Projects couldn't be opened** (full-page notice, `danger`, not closable; ticket 1020, spec 1018): when the Projects kept on this device can't be read from the browser's database, the app shows this alone, never the Homepage or the editor, centred on the page background, with its one in-box action, Reload. English: "The Projects on this device couldn't be opened. Reload to try again." with "Reload". The Homepage's Saved Projects list shows the same message and action in place of the list. The other languages are in `src/i18n`.
- Import results and errors show beside the header button that caused them as a compact one-line message (no shadow, no close).
- **Color removed** (toast, info tone, with Undo): after an added swatch is removed from the Palette. English: "Color removed from the Palette." with the text action "Undo". Russian: «Цвет удалён из палитры.» with «Отменить». The action is a text button (`control`, `ink`, underlined) before the close ×. Like other toasts it goes after 5s unless hovered or focused; Undo puts the swatch back. Russian reviewed in ticket 283 (matches Delete wording).
- **Frame stays** (toast, info tone, two actions; ticket 0935, UX-018): after a change in the Project panel's canvas section leaves a Frame on another Technique or Bead. English: "The Canvas is {canvas} now. The Frame stays {frame}." ("The Canvas is Loom now. The Frame stays Peyote."; a Bead by its label, "Miyuki Delica 11/0"), with the text actions "Change the Frame too", which runs the Frame panel's own change (a Technique at once, one Undo step; a Bead through its Replace bead? question), and "Open Frame", which opens the Frame panel with its settings unfolded, in view and focused. A toast with two actions puts them on their own line under the text, 16px apart, as a notice's in-box ones are (one action stays beside the text). Where the Frame can't take the Technique (a pass done, a Custom frame's shape), only Open Frame shows. With no Frame, a Frame that matches, or a change that changed nothing, no toast. It goes after 5s like other toasts. The other languages are in `writing.md` (Frame stays copy).
- **Project deleted** (toast, success tone, with Undo; ticket 0983, UX-068): after Delete “{name}”?. When the deleted Project was open and another opens: "“{name}” was deleted. Now in “{open}”." ("“Summer bracelet” was deleted. Now in “Loom cuff”."); when the open Project stays, from the Projects library's ×, or none is left: "“{name}” was deleted.". Undo brings the Project back whole (its Row progress, Beads since last file, reference image) and opens it again if it was open. The Project is held in memory until the toast goes, 5s unless hovered or focused, then it is gone from the device; a second delete replaces the toast and lets the first go. The other languages are in `writing.md`.
- **Update ready** (toast, info tone, two actions, stays until answered or closed; tickets 69, 1041, 1048, spec 1043): once a newer Build has downloaded behind the open editor, never in the demo. It says what Reload switches to. The first line is the name, "Update ready" (`control`), and after it the waiting Version in the mono face (`meta`, `muted`, tabular figures, 8px apart; under the name when the line is too short). Under it, 4px down: with one note waiting, its kind label (KindLabel card) and its line (`body` color), the line wrapping under the label; with several, 6px down, a kind label and its count (`control`, tabular) for each kind, in the order New, Changed, Fixed, 14px apart, a kind with none left out, no plural forms. A waiting Version with no note above the page's shows the name and the Version alone. When the app can't learn what waits (no answer from the waiting copy within 2s, or one it can't read) the toast says today's plain "Update ready". Every state has the two actions on their own line: Reload, which switches to the waiting Build, and What's new, a link that opens the Homepage at What's new, unfolded and in view. With counts (two or more notes waiting; ticket 1091) What's new is instead a button that opens the list of what waits right in the toast, with no navigation: the word as the same text action, a chevron after it (16px, `muted`, 4px apart) turned up while the list is open, and it tells a screen reader whether the list is open (`aria-expanded`). Reload keeps its place. The list comes under the actions, 10px down, after a 1px line in the toast's own line color (`--message-line`: `panel-line` in light, `line-strong` in dark and high contrast), then 10px: one row per note, 8px apart, its kind label and its line (`body`), wrapping under the label, as the one-note state draws it; New, then Changed, then Fixed, newest first inside a kind; at most 10 rows, while the counts above keep counting every waiting note. When more than 10 wait, a link under the list, 10px down: "{count} more in What's new" (`control`, underlined) with the 16px `external` mark of a link that leaves (`muted`), opening the Homepage at What's new as the link does; with 10 or fewer there is none. Opened, the toast grows to its widest (to the left), and it never leaves the canvas box: the toasts' room ends 16px above the box's foot, and on a short screen the list scrolls inside the toast while the name, counts, actions and the link under the list stay in view. A newer Build's toast shows closed; the toast stays until answered or closed, open or not. A Build with the page's own Version raises no toast; it arrives when every tab is closed and the app opened again. A newer Build taking the waiting one's place replaces the toast with its own. How the page learns what waits: ADR 0045. The words are in `writing.md` (Update ready copy).
- **Fill refused** (toast, info tone, no action; ticket 0715): after a Fill tool press on the Canvas that Fill reach refuses. Open space: "Nothing encloses this spot. Select an area to fill it." Over the Fill cap: "Too big to fill. Zoom in, or select an area." One toast for both (showing it again replaces it); it goes after 5s like other toasts. The other languages are in `writing.md` (Fill reach copy).

- **Kept** (toast, success, no action; tickets 0839, 0936): once, when the browser agreed to keep the Projects at the device's first Project file or export, asked with no question first. English: "This browser will keep your Projects." It goes after 5s like other toasts. A no says nothing (the Save reminder carries the advice); the Menu row "Kept by this browser" shows it. The other languages are in `writing.md` (Keep Projects copy).
- **Made from a picture** (toast, success, no action; ticket 0968, UX-055): once, after Convert image's Create Project has added the Project and the editor shows it. English: "Project “{name}” made from your picture. It is kept in this browser." with the new Project's name ("Project “holiday” made from your picture. It is kept in this browser."). Like every toast it is `role=status`, so a screen reader hears it once and nothing else announces it. Add to Canvas, which makes no Project, says nothing. It goes after 5s like other toasts. The other languages are in `writing.md` (Made from a picture copy).
- **Saved** (toast, success, no action; ticket 0859): the one toast every file that reaches the device shows, a Project file, a PNG or a PDF, by download, share or into a picked file, made by one shared maker so no save words its own: "Saved: {file}" with the file's name ("Saved: sunflower.png"). The next save replaces it; a PNG's further list pages each stack one of their own. Where a share needs a fresh tap (iPhone, iPad), "Ready to save: {file}" with the text action "Save", which hands it over and then says Saved. It replaces "PNG ready", "PDF ready" and "File ready". The other languages are in `writing.md` (Project file copy).
