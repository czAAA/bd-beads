# PrintWide

A framed Schema wider than tall keeps the portrait A4 layout: a shorter picture on page 1, the same full-page chart parts after it.

- The PDF is always portrait. On page 1 the board stays at the full page width and loses height, which leaves more room for Beads needed.
- A 240 × 60 Schema: page 1, then three equal parts of 80 beads across and 60 down at about 2.2 mm, the largest bead three sheets allow (ticket 0913; PrintChartPage card). A wide flat one under 3 mm a bead whole, such as a 300 × 8, is cut into strips stacked on one sheet (PrintStrips).
