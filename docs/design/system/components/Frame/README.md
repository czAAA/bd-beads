# Frame

The rectangle that marks which beads are the Pattern: set over the open canvas before or after drawing, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **One Frame per canvas. It limits drawing in one place only: its margin.** Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas, stay editable, and can be exported later by moving the Frame over them.
- **Margin (v18):** six bead positions of clear space on all four sides of the Frame (three until ticket 427, ADR 0056), measured from its outermost beads (102px at the smallest bead, 15px plus the 2px gap). No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there. Six holds the ruler gutter, the Frame line and handles and, in Edit Frame, the margin steppers at every bead size.
- **Margin look:** flat, no fill, nothing dimmed, no panel. At rest it is only a quiet gap in the grid: the empty-position dots do not draw inside it. While the Frame is being edited, and for 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin move out.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves every bead found in the margin to the nearest empty position outside it, with no travel animation, and a Message says what moved with Undo: "Frame set. 1 bead was in the margin and moved outside it." ("Frame moved.", "Frame resized."; "2 beads were in the margin..."; the count is beads, not Pieces). Beads inside the Frame are the Pattern and never move. Not decided: a piece with beads both inside the Frame and in the margin.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (ticket 417, ADR 0054):** the only mode with the Frame's handles, rulers, dashed margin outline, size tooltip and margin steppers; a Selection never shows them. It exists only with a Frame and while the Frame lock of ticket 411 is open (no Row progress pass done), and not otherwise. It is entered from the Project sheet's Edit Frame button, or after Frame from Selection (ContextBar card), and left with Esc, Enter or a tap outside the Frame (no Done button, ticket 427). There is no key, Dock slot or Frame sheet. Dragging inside the Frame moves it, snapped to whole beads. Eight handles resize it (9px, 1.5px `ink`, radius 2, filled with the canvas background), and a Tooltip at its bottom-right corner shows the size: "13×13 · 2.1 × 2.1 cm". The handles are grabbed from further away than they look (ticket 414): within 14px of a handle's centre for mouse and pen, 24px for touch, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; the nearest wins, corners beat edges, and a press inside the Frame within a corner's reach takes that corner. A Selection carries the same eight handles. On touch: four 16px corner handles. The arrow keys still move the Frame and Shift + arrows resize it.
- **Margin steppers (ticket 417):** a mini − and + on each of the four edges, centred on the edge in the keep-out margin, **between the ruler numbers and the margin's outer edge** (midway between them), clamped so the pair stays inside the canvas box (ticket 427, ADR 0055), **shrinking with the zoom** (full size at 100% and up, half size at 50% and below) and **hidden once the rulers show fewer than every 5th number** (a Ruler step above 5, or Rulers off), 28px square at full size (36px on touch; `--selbar-height`), `box` look, 2px apart (side by side above and below the Frame, stacked left and right). A press moves that edge one bead out (+) or in (−) and is one Undo step, announced like any Frame change; where rows alternate the top edge goes by a pair of rows. An edge stops at one bead (the − is disabled with the reason "The Frame can’t get any smaller on this side."). Each is named for its edge ("Grow the Frame at the top"). While a Row progress pass is done they do not exist, as Edit Frame itself does not.
- **Edit Frame has no bar** (ticket 427, ADR 0055): Fit to drawing and Remove frame are in the Progress bar, and the size is the tooltip at the Frame's corner.
- **Frame controls in the Project sheet:** Technique, Edit Frame, Fit to drawing and Remove Frame, then the Estimated size and its Note. The size steppers, the beads | mm switch and the side choice of ticket 410 are gone: each edge changes itself on the canvas.
- **Frame number:** a 22px chip ("1"; DM Mono 12 on `elevated`, 1px `line-strong`, radius 6) in the Frame row. Pressing it brings the Frame into view. Name: "Frame 1, bring it into view".
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. Piece rulers hide and the Frame's rulers show on all four sides, inside the margin (Rulers card). The header reads "Star · 21×19" and the canvas strip "Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm". Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame only.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Row not done). Without one, Export ▾ opens "Set Frame to export" (SaveBox card) and the Progress bar shows "Set Frame to start" with a Fit to drawing button. A Frame is made from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the beads inside the Frame. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
