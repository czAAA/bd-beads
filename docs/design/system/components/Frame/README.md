# Frame

A sheet of its own beads laid over the open canvas (ADR 0066), which holds the Pattern: made round a drawing before or after it, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **One Frame per canvas. It owns its beads and is opaque.** It hides what the canvas holds under it without changing or deleting it, and the canvas beads show again when the Frame moves off them. A press inside it paints the Frame, one outside paints the canvas, and a stroke stays on the layer it began on. It limits drawing on the canvas in one place only: its margin. Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas, stay editable, and are woven later by making a Frame round them (Frame from Selection, Fit to drawing), which lifts them into it.
- **Margin sheet (ticket 0655; wins over the Margin look, Gutter, Edit Frame, Rotate Frame and Move handle bullets below where they differ):** the margin is drawn as a sheet of drafting paper in its own color (Auto: the Canvas color one step off), with an inner border, the Row progress, the Move bar and the tools (Rotate Frame, Edit Frame, Export, Margin color, Remove Frame) in its top band, a title block (name, Technique, maker, sizes, the lettered legend, Letters) in its bottom-right corner, rulers on three sides and hot-corner resize handles while Edit Frame is on. Nothing of the Frame stands in the gutter any more. It has a full, a compact and a folded form by zoom. All of it is the MarginSheet card; built by tickets 0656 to 0658.
- **Margin (v18, back in v19, ticket 0501, ADR 0066):** four bead positions of clear space (six until ticket 0656) on all four sides of the Frame's rectangle on the canvas, at every zoom. No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there; Erase still works. The Frame's own sheet is unaffected.
- **Margin look:** the Margin sheet above (ticket 0656): its own color, no Position marks in it, nothing dimmed. For 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin stay.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves no bead and shows no Message: a canvas bead that lies in the margin stays on the canvas, covered by it (not drawn), and shows again if the Frame moves off it.
- **Gutter (ticket 0490):** the band just outside the Frame line where its controls stand: the edit pencil, Remove Frame, the Move handle and the resize handles. It is measured in screen pixels, so the controls keep their size and their distance from the line at every zoom. A resize handle's square stands half its size plus 3px from the line (7.5px, 11px on touch); the pencil and the Move handle stand 48px (`--frame-control-offset`) from it, past the handles' reach, so none covers another. Each is kept inside the canvas box and flips to the other side of the Frame's line (a resize handle to the inside of its edge, the pencil and the Move handle to below the Frame) when there is no room outside it. A press in a control's hit area wins over the tool's press there.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (tickets 417, 0452, 0473, 0490; ADR 0054):** a toggle, not a mode. The **edit pencil** in the gutter above the Frame's top-right corner shows the eight resize handles; off, they are hidden and the Frame can still be moved (Move handle). The pencil does not open the **Frame panel** (below), which has its own opener, the **Frame** button in the Canvas strip, after Menu and Project (CanvasStrip card; ticket 0510). The pencil is the `pencil` icon on a 32px `box` chip, 44px to hit for every pointer, centred on the corner's column (the Move handle slides clear of it on a small Frame), kept inside the canvas box (below the Frame when there is no room above) and never shrinking with the zoom. Open is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon (like the Rulers toggle), closed is the `box` look. The handles go off whenever a Project opens; the choice is kept for the session only. Once a Row progress pass is done no handles show, while the Frame button still opens the bar, which then holds the size and Remove Frame alone (ticket 0475). The rulers are always shown with a Frame; the size Tooltip at its bottom-right corner ("13×13 ≈ 2.1 × 2.1 cm") only while dragging or stepping. Tools work inside the Frame and right up to its edge with the handles on. Eight handles resize it, each in the gutter (9px, 1.5px `ink`, radius 2, filled with the canvas background), grabbed within 14px of a handle's centre for mouse and pen, 24px for touch, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; the nearest wins and corners beat edges. On touch: four 16px corner handles. **Remove Frame (ticket 0498):** a second `box` chip (`delete` icon, same 32px chip and 44px hit) right after the pencil, one hit area to its right (to its left when the canvas box has no room), with its Tooltip; it runs the same command as the Frame panel's Remove Frame, with the same confirmation (asked only when a Row progress pass is done). The pencil, Remove Frame and the Move handle are always there while a Frame is in view: with any tool, with a Selection, and after Row progress has started (only the resize handles go then). A Selection carries the same eight handles, standing on its own edge; where its handles and a control overlap, the Selection's handle wins the press. There is no key. Escape or a press outside the bar closes it, as it does Menu and Project, and that press does nothing else (ticket 0475); that includes a press on the Move handle, the pencil or a resize handle (ticket 0510).
- **Rotate Frame (ticket 0471, ADR 0052):** a `box` button with the `rotate` icon (the one Rotate on the Selection uses), between the Move handle and Edit Frame. It turns the Frame and its own beads a quarter clockwise about their centre, Width and Height swapping, as one Undo step; the canvas under it is not touched, so there is no covered-beads Message, and Edit stays as it was. Offered after a Row progress pass is done too: each Row direction's pointer turns a quarter clockwise with the Frame, so the same beads stay finished, and it never asks (ticket 0647).
- **Move handle (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473, 0490):** the Frame's move grip. A **horizontal line** in the gutter above the top edge, centred on it, 48px from the Frame line (below the Frame when there is no room above): a 40 × 6 pill in `ink` with a 2px `canvas` halo (`accent` while it is dragged), inside a 56 × 44 hit area for every pointer. Always visible and active, with the bar closed and once Row progress has started, so moving a Frame never needs the bar. It does not scale with the zoom and is kept inside the canvas box, at least 28px from its sides. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads, which travel with it on screen as it goes (the Row progress pointer travels with it); it is one announced Undo step. It is a button named "Move Frame" with the Tooltip "Drag to move the Frame together with its beads." and its place in the Tab order: the arrow keys move the Frame one bead, and Shift + arrows resize it, only while it or a handle has focus. The cursor is `grab` over it, `grabbing` while dragged. The `move-frame` icon is no longer used by the Frame (it stays in the set).
- **Frame panel (ticket 0473; redesigned in tickets 0553 and 0555, design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/DtuwrYB8pNEabwFCYCC8wB):** the Frame's own controls, in one panel that ignores zoom and pan. It is a panel like Menu and Project, in the same shell, density and parts (MenuPanel card): `panel-width` (520px, 585px at the app's 18px root), one of the three open at a time, Escape or a press outside closes it, it opens without focusing a field, so a tablet's keyboard stays down until one is tapped (ticket 0510). It hangs from the **Frame button in the Canvas strip**, 4px under it, and from nothing else. The Frame button is always there, with or without a Frame (ticket 0555).
  - **Title line:** `frame:` in the `label` style; at the right, 24px icon buttons Rotate Frame (`rotate`, `ink`; absent once a Row progress pass is done) and Remove Frame (`delete`, `danger`). Remove always asks first ("Remove Frame?"; "The Frame goes, and its beads go with it. Rows marked as done are cleared too. This can be undone."; one Undo brings it all back, beads included).
  - **`name:`** the Frame's own name (Inter 600 16/24, one line, ellipsis) with a pencil, edited in place like the Project's name (Save, Cancel, "n/40"); an empty name is allowed and shows "+ Name this frame" in `muted`. The name is stored with the Frame (ticket 0555).
  - **No Frame yet:** the title line has no icons, and right under it, above the export section (ticket 0650), come one `meta-small` line, "Set a frame around the drawing you want to export.", then **Frame from starter** (`frame`, `accent`, ›, pushes the Starter gallery; StarterGallery card), then **Select a frame** (`select`; ticket 0568: closes the panel and makes Select the tool, and the next drag becomes the Frame on release; Escape or another tool cancels), then **Fit a frame to the drawing** (`fit`; disabled with nothing drawn), then one `meta-small` line, "A frame sets the size, the exports and the bead count." Only the export section (below them) is besides.
  - **size** (PanelSection): Width × Height as two typeable 56px fields (`panel-row` tall, DM Mono 14 centred, 1px `field-line`, `radius-sm`), a muted ×, and "beads". They show the current numbers; they take digits only (1 and up, up to three digits); Enter or leaving both applies, each side growing or shrinking evenly about the Frame (the odd bead goes right or down; where rows alternate the top edge moves by pairs of rows), between four and 999 beads; Escape restores. Then the four edges in a 2 × 2 grid (Top, Bottom, Left, Right; `panel-row` cells, 12px column gap): a 14px glyph (a square with that edge ruled, `accent-strong` while it was the last one stepped), the edge's name (`control` 13) and a joined − | + Stepper (`picker-height`, 26px halves). + always adds a bead on that side; a press is one Undo step; holding repeats (Stepper card); an edge stops at four beads with the reason "The Frame can’t get any smaller on this side."; each button is named for its edge and axis ("Top edge: add one row"). Then **Estimated size**: the `ruler` icon, "Estimated size", "≈ 38 × 39 mm" in `meta` (always mm, however large, so it reads as Replace bead? states it, ticket 0687), and a 24px `info` button whose Tooltip holds the warning ("An estimate. Bead size × beads across and down. Thread, tension and small differences between beads mean the finished work often comes out a little different."); the old Note under it is gone. Collapsed summary: "24 × 30 · ≈ 38 × 39 mm". While a Row progress pass is done the fields are off (`faint`), the edge grid is hidden and one `meta-small` line says "Rows are marked done. Remove the frame to change the size." The section ends with **Select a frame** (`select`, ticket 0568), a `panel-row` that frames a new drag as above; it is off once a Row progress pass is done.
  - **export** (PanelSection, ticket 0568; always shown): **Picture** (`image`, `.png`) and **Printable chart** (`pdf`, `.pdf`), moved from Project; the maker's name printed on them is set in Project. Under them the **Export quality** QualitySlider (Low / Medium / High; PNG beads 45 / 60 / 90 px, default Medium; PDF 150 / 300 / 450 DPI; QualitySlider card). With a Frame it is the first section, right under the title line, before the name; with no Frame it comes after the ways to get one (ticket 0650), and both are disabled. The same section, Letters left out, is the Export panel the Canvas strip's Export button opens (ExportPanel card, ticket 0652), which shows Select a frame and Fit a frame to the drawing in place of the disabled buttons when there is no Frame.
  - **Letters** (ticket 0573; inside the export section, under Export quality, with a Frame only): a `label`-weight line "Letters" with an info button right after the word (Button card; named "What Letters does", ticket 0651) and an AppSwitch at the right (off by default, saved with the Project, no Undo step). The info button's Tooltip holds "Print each color's letter on its beads, so colors can be told apart on paper and in black and white."; there is no line under the switch. On, the Frame's beads in the editor, the PNG and the PDF pattern carry their Swatch letter (white on dark beads, `ink` on light; Inter 600, about 56% of the bead).
  - **bead** (PanelSection, ticket 0687; with a Frame, after size): the Frame's own bead section, the same as Project's canvas section's: its Bead shape (Symmetric | Real, applied at once to the Frame alone), then its Bead as three PanelPicker buttons, Brand, Type, Size (BeadPicker card). A Bead pick asks Replace bead? for the Frame, whose message gives the Frame's Estimated size with the new Bead and the old (ConfirmDialogs card). Collapsed summary: the Bead's name, "TOHO Cube 1.5mm".
  - **beads needed** (PanelSection, last until ticket 0655): the total in the header ("1 512 · ≈ 7.6 g", also its collapsed summary), then one `panel-row` per color: a 14px swatch, the code (`meta-small`, `muted`), the color name (`control` 13, ellipsis), the count (`meta`) and the grams (`meta-small`, `muted`, right). Five rows, then "Show all 8 colours" (`muted`, a chevron) that opens the rest in place (BeadsNeeded card).
  - **background** (PanelSection, ticket 0666; CanvasBackground card): the Frame's own Background, the same BackgroundColorPicker as Project's canvas section (the five colors and Custom color; in high contrast the one line that the canvas stays white), its radio group named "Frame background". Collapsed summary: a 12px chip and the name. Between beads needed and margin.
  - **margin** (PanelSection, the new last, ticket 0655; MarginSheet card): the margin color (BackgroundColorPicker: Auto, the five Canvas colors, Custom color; remembered on the device), the Switch "Name and size on the margin", and the `meta-small` line "The margin is 4 beads on every side. Nothing can be placed in it, and exports never show it." Collapsed summary: "auto" or the color's name.
- **Fit to drawing** is in the Progress bar and, with no Frame, in the Frame panel (ticket 0555); the size is the Frame panel's size section and the Tooltip at the Frame's corner.
- **Its own Technique (ticket 0492):** the Frame has its own Technique and Brick block, apart from the Canvas's: a peyote Frame sits on a loom Canvas and the other way round. It is drawn from the corner of the Canvas position it lies on in its own geometry, its first row always unshifted, so it moves by whole Canvas positions and its top edge resizes by a whole cycle of its own rows; its handles, rulers and Row progress marks follow it. The Technique choice and Brick block stepper in the Frame controls change the Frame and wait once a pass is done; the Canvas's are in the Project panel.
- **Nothing of the Frame in Project (ticket 0554):** the Frame's Technique is the Project's one Technique; the Estimated size, Frame from starter, Picture and Printable chart exports and Beads needed are all in the Frame panel.
- **Frame number:** a 22px chip ("1"; DM Mono 12 on `elevated`, 1px `line-strong`, radius 6) in the Frame row. Pressing it brings the Frame into view. Name: "Frame 1, bring it into view".
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. Piece rulers hide and the Frame's rulers show on all four sides, just outside it (Rulers card). The header reads "Star · 21×19" and the canvas strip "Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm". Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame only.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Row not done). Without one there is nothing to export as a picture or chart: Picture and Printable chart sit in the Frame panel disabled, under the line that says to set a frame and Frame from starter, Select a frame and Fit a frame to the drawing, and the Progress bar shows "Set Frame to start" with a Fit to drawing button. A Frame is made from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the Frame's own beads. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **A Custom frame (tickets 0592, 0593, ADR 0077):** a peyote Triangle or Warped Square sized by Rounds, a Star (five Warped Squares lying flat as rhombi, Seams round each arm and from its middle to its corners) or Kaleidocycle (six Triangles round a hole, Off-shape, with a Seam down each gap) sized by Part size, or a Kite (two wings, its Seam the spine) sized by Rows; or a brick-stitch Fringe earring (ticket 0597), its top and a strand under each base bead, a Seam between them, sized by Top width, Top rows and Fringe length with its Fringe style. Inside its bounding rectangle every bead is drawn at its centre, turned to its panel's angle, in the Project's Bead shape; the Seams are 1px lines in `piece-line` from the centre to each corner; the Off-shape round the shape and the centre where the Seams meet are the Canvas colour with no position marks, and a press there is refused with the margin outline's flash. The Frame panel shows a Stepper per setting (− value +, the Stepper card; labelled Rounds, Part size or Rows, or Top width, Top rows and Fringe length, then the Fringe style as a SegmentedControl of the setup's names) in place of Width, Height and the edge steppers, with the Estimated size of the shape under it; Edit Frame's resize handles, Remove row/column and Select a frame are not offered. A Selection over it (ticket 0594) is the Select tool's rectangle of the Frame's cells on screen, drawn as on any Frame, holding every bead whose centre lies inside it; Rotate Frame turns the whole shape a quarter with its beads and swaps its rectangle's sides.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
