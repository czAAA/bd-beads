# Frame

The rectangle that marks which beads are the Pattern: set over the open canvas before or after drawing, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **One Frame per canvas. It limits drawing in one place only: its margin.** Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas, stay editable, and can be exported later by moving the Frame over them.
- **Frame controls redesign (ticket 0473, planned):** the Edit Frame, Margin handle and Frame bar bullets below are the design for ticket 0473 (prototype: the "Canvas Margin Controls" canvas). Until it ships the app still draws the corner row and the mini margin steppers of tickets 0455 and 417. Why: the mini steppers lived inside the margin, so they shrank with the zoom and crowded each other, and a stylus missed them. The new controls never scale.
- **Margin (v18):** six bead positions of clear space on all four sides of the Frame (three until ticket 427, ADR 0056), measured from its outermost beads (102px at the smallest bead, 15px plus the 2px gap). No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there. Six holds the ruler gutter, the Frame line and handles and, in Edit Frame, the margin steppers at every bead size.
- **Margin look:** flat, no fill, nothing dimmed, no panel. At rest it is only a quiet gap in the grid: the empty-position dots do not draw inside it. While the Frame is being edited, and for 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin move out.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves every bead found in the margin to the nearest empty position outside it, with no travel animation, and a Message says what moved with Undo: "Frame set. 1 bead was in the margin and moved outside it." ("Frame moved.", "Frame resized."; "2 beads were in the margin..."; the count is beads, not Pieces). Beads inside the Frame are the Pattern and never move. Not decided: a piece with beads both inside the Frame and in the margin.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (tickets 417, 0452, 0473; ADR 0062 amended):** a toggle, not a mode. It opens the **Frame bar** (below) and shows the eight resize handles; closed, both are hidden and the Frame can still be moved (Margin handle). Two ways in, one switch: the **Frame** button in the Canvas strip, after Menu and Project (CanvasStrip card), and the **edit pencil** at the margin outline's top-right corner. The pencil is the `pencil` icon on a 32px `box` chip, 44px to hit for every pointer, centred 14px out and up from the corner so its hit area never touches the Margin handle, pinned to that corner (it moves with the Frame when the canvas is panned) and kept inside the canvas box; it does not shrink with the zoom. Open is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon (like the Rulers toggle), closed is the `box` look. The bar is closed whenever a Project opens; the choice is kept for the session only. Once a Row progress pass is done the switch is disabled, its Tooltip saying "Row progress has started, so the Frame can only be moved." The rulers and the dashed margin outline are always shown with a Frame; the size Tooltip at its bottom-right corner ("13×13 ≈ 2.1 × 2.1 cm") only while dragging or stepping. Tools work inside the Frame with the bar open, since the handles sit in the margin where nothing can be drawn. Eight handles resize it (9px, 1.5px `ink`, radius 2, filled with the canvas background), grabbed within 14px of a handle's centre for mouse and pen, 24px for touch, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; the nearest wins and corners beat edges. On touch: four 16px corner handles. A Selection carries the same eight handles but never shows the pencil, the handle or the bar. There is no key; Esc, Enter and a tap outside leave nothing.
- **Rotate Frame (ticket 0471, ADR 0063):** a `box` button with the `rotate` icon (the one Rotate on the Selection uses), between the Move handle and Edit Frame. It turns the Frame, its margin and the beads inside both a quarter clockwise about their centre, Width and Height swapping, as one Undo step; Pieces outside stay put, beads under the turned area are covered with a Message and Undo, and Edit stays as it was. Disabled once a Row progress pass is done ("Rows are marked done. Remove the Frame to rotate.").
- **Margin handle (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473):** the Frame's move grip, replacing the Move handle square of the corner row. A **horizontal line** in the middle of the **top margin**, halfway between the Frame line and the margin's outer edge: a 40 × 6 pill in `ink` with a 2px `canvas` halo (`accent` while it is dragged), inside a 56 × 44 hit area for every pointer. Always visible and active, with the bar closed and once Row progress has started, so moving a Frame never needs the bar. It does not scale with the zoom; at any zoom it keeps to the part of the top margin that is on screen (centred on the visible stretch, at least 32px from the canvas box's sides, and within the canvas box vertically), and goes only when the Frame and its margin are wholly out of view. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads, which travel with it on screen as it goes (the Row progress pointer travels with it); it is one announced Undo step. It is a button named "Move Frame" with the Tooltip "Drag to move the Frame together with its beads." and its place in the Tab order: the arrow keys move the Frame one bead, and Shift + arrows resize it, only while it or a handle has focus. The cursor is `grab` over it, `grabbing` while dragged. The `move-frame` icon is no longer used by the Frame (it stays in the set).
- **Frame bar (ticket 0473):** the Frame's own controls, in one panel that ignores zoom and pan. It floats at the **top centre of the drawing area**, 12px under the Canvas strip, on the side opposite the Tool pill's docked edge (Tool pill at the top: the bar docks 12px above the Progress bar instead), so panning cannot carry it away. 504px wide (the canvas box less a 8px gutter on each side where narrower; under 560px the four edge cells stack two by two), `pill` fill, 1px `line-strong`, radius-lg, `elevation-2` (light only), padding 8, 8 between its rows. **Row 1:** "Frame" in the `meta-tiny` role over the size ("24 × 30 beads", Inter 15 600, `ink`; the unit in `muted`), then Rotate Frame (`rotate` icon) and, after a 1px `line-strong` divider 4px either side, Remove Frame (`close` icon, `danger`); Remove always asks first ("Remove Frame?"; Beads stay, the rows marked done are cleared, one Undo brings it all back). **Row 2:** four edge cells in a grid, in the order Top, Bottom, Left, Right. A cell is a `surface` rectangle (1px `line`, radius-md, padding 6) holding a 16px glyph (a square with that edge ruled 4px `ink`, `accent` while that edge was the last one stepped), the edge's name (Inter 12 600) and a **− and + pair**; the last-stepped cell has a 2px `accent-strong` border. In every cell − comes first and + second, and + always adds a bead on that side. Each is 48 × 48 (`--frame-hit`), 4px from its partner, radius-md, `button` fill, 1px `button-line`, 24px icons; hover `pill`, pressed `board`; focus ring as every button. A press moves that edge one bead out (+) or in (−) and is one Undo step, announced like any Frame change; where rows alternate the top edge goes by a pair of rows; holding repeats as the Stepper card says. An edge stops at four beads: the − is disabled, `faint` on `surface`, with the reason "The Frame can’t get any smaller on this side." Each button is named for its edge and axis ("Top edge: add one row", "Left edge: remove one column"). While a Row progress pass is done the steppers and Rotate Frame do not exist (the bar then holds the size and Remove Frame). The Tool pill and the Selection bar steer clear of the bar the way they steer clear of each other (StackingOrder card).
- **Fit to drawing** is in the Progress bar and the Project sheet, and the size is the Frame bar's row 1 and the Tooltip at the Frame's corner (ticket 0473; before it, ticket 427 had Edit Frame with no bar).
- **Frame controls in Project (spec 0440):** Technique stays, and so do the Estimated size line and its Note under it. Edit Frame, Fit to drawing and Remove Frame are gone from Project: the Frame is edited and removed from the Selection bar and the Progress bar, where those buttons already are. The size steppers, the beads | mm switch and the side choice of ticket 410 stay gone: each edge changes itself on the canvas.
- **Frame number:** a 22px chip ("1"; DM Mono 12 on `elevated`, 1px `line-strong`, radius 6) in the Frame row. Pressing it brings the Frame into view. Name: "Frame 1, bring it into view".
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. Piece rulers hide and the Frame's rulers show on all four sides, inside the margin (Rulers card). The header reads "Star · 21×19" and the canvas strip "Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm". Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame only.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Row not done). Without one, Export (PNG or PDF in Project, or Export ▾) opens "Set Frame to export" (SaveBox card) and the Progress bar shows "Set Frame to start" with a Fit to drawing button. A Frame is made from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the beads inside the Frame. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
