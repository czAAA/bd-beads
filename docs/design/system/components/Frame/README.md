# Frame

The rectangle that marks which beads are the Pattern: set over the open canvas before or after drawing, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **One Frame per canvas. It limits drawing in one place only: its margin.** Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas, stay editable, and can be exported later by moving the Frame over them.
- **Margin (v18):** six bead positions of clear space on all four sides of the Frame (three until ticket 427, ADR 0056), measured from its outermost beads (102px at the smallest bead, 15px plus the 2px gap). No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there. Six holds the ruler gutter, the Frame line and handles and, in Edit Frame, the margin steppers at every bead size.
- **Margin look:** flat, no fill, nothing dimmed, no panel. At rest it is only a quiet gap in the grid: the empty-position dots do not draw inside it. While the Frame is being edited, and for 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin move out.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves every bead found in the margin to the nearest empty position outside it, with no travel animation, and a Message says what moved with Undo: "Frame set. 1 bead was in the margin and moved outside it." ("Frame moved.", "Frame resized."; "2 beads were in the margin..."; the count is beads, not Pieces). Beads inside the Frame are the Pattern and never move. Not decided: a piece with beads both inside the Frame and in the margin.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (tickets 417, 0452, ADR 0062):** a toggle on the Frame, not a mode. Whenever a Frame is made or a Project opens, an **edit toggle** (the `pencil` icon, `box` look, pressed when on, outline when off, 26px and 36px on touch, shrinking with the zoom like the steppers) sits in the corner row (below), just inside the top-right corner of the margin outline, clamped inside the canvas box. On, it shows the Frame's resize handles and margin steppers; off, it hides them. The choice is kept for the session only. Once a Row progress pass is done it is off and disabled, its Tooltip saying "Row progress has started, so the Frame can only be moved." The rulers and the dashed margin outline are always shown with a Frame; the size Tooltip at its bottom-right corner ("13×13 ≈ 2.1 × 2.1 cm") only while dragging or stepping. Tools work inside the Frame with edit on, since the handles sit in the margin where nothing can be drawn. Eight handles resize it (9px, 1.5px `ink`, radius 2, filled with the canvas background), grabbed within 14px of a handle's centre for mouse and pen, 24px for touch, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; the nearest wins and corners beat edges. On touch: four 16px corner handles. A Selection carries the same eight handles but never shows the toggle or steppers. Frame from Selection leaves edit on. There is no key, Dock slot or Frame sheet; Esc, Enter and a tap outside no longer leave anything.
- **Corner row (ticket 0455):** Move handle, Edit Frame and **Remove Frame** (`close` icon, `box` look) side by side in that order, like a window's buttons, 4px apart and 4px inside the margin outline's top-right corner, kept inside the canvas box. Remove Frame always asks first ("Remove Frame?"; Beads stay, the rows marked done are cleared, one Undo brings it all back). Each has a Tooltip.
- **Move handle (tickets 0452, 0455):** one square, always visible and active, also once Row progress has started: a `box` button with the `move-frame` icon (four arrowheads round a bead), the corner row's size (26px, 36px on touch), at its left end. Its Tooltip says "Drag to move the Frame together with its beads." Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads, which travel with it on screen as it goes (the Row progress pointer travels with it); it is one announced Undo step. The button (named "Move Frame") carries the Tooltip, passes a press on to the canvas to drag, and is its place in the Tab order: the arrow keys move the Frame and Shift + arrows resize it, only while a Frame handle or stepper has focus. The cursor is `grab` over it.
- **Margin steppers (ticket 417):** a mini − and + on each of the four edges, centred on the edge in the keep-out margin, **between the ruler numbers and the margin's outer edge** (midway between them), clamped so the pair stays inside the canvas box (ticket 427, ADR 0055), **shrinking with the zoom** (full size at 100% and up, half size at 50% and below) and **shown whatever the rulers show** (with Rulers off they sit right against the Frame's edge), and **gone once the Frame and its margin are scrolled wholly out of view**, like the Frame's other controls, 28px square at full size (36px on touch; `--selbar-height`), `box` look, 2px apart (side by side above and below the Frame, stacked left and right). A press moves that edge one bead out (+) or in (−) and is one Undo step, announced like any Frame change; where rows alternate the top edge goes by a pair of rows. An edge stops at one bead (the − is disabled with the reason "The Frame can’t get any smaller on this side."). Each is named for its edge ("Grow the Frame at the top"). While a Row progress pass is done they do not exist, as Edit Frame itself does not.
- **Edit Frame has no bar** (ticket 427, ADR 0055): Fit to drawing is in the Progress bar and Remove Frame in the corner row, and the size is the tooltip at the Frame's corner.
- **Frame controls in Project (spec 0440):** Technique stays, and so do the Estimated size line and its Note under it. Edit Frame, Fit to drawing and Remove Frame are gone from Project: the Frame is edited and removed from the Selection bar and the Progress bar, where those buttons already are. The size steppers, the beads | mm switch and the side choice of ticket 410 stay gone: each edge changes itself on the canvas.
- **Frame number:** a 22px chip ("1"; DM Mono 12 on `elevated`, 1px `line-strong`, radius 6) in the Frame row. Pressing it brings the Frame into view. Name: "Frame 1, bring it into view".
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. Piece rulers hide and the Frame's rulers show on all four sides, inside the margin (Rulers card). The header reads "Star · 21×19" and the canvas strip "Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm". Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame only.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Row not done). Without one, Export (PNG or PDF in Project, or Export ▾) opens "Set Frame to export" (SaveBox card) and the Progress bar shows "Set Frame to start" with a Fit to drawing button. A Frame is made from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the beads inside the Frame. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
