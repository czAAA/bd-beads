# Frame

A sheet of its own beads laid over the open canvas (ADR 0066), which holds the Pattern: made round a drawing before or after it, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **One Frame per canvas. It owns its beads and is opaque.** It hides what the canvas holds under it without changing or deleting it, and the canvas beads show again when the Frame moves off them. A press inside it paints the Frame, one outside paints the canvas, and a stroke stays on the layer it began on. It limits drawing on the canvas in one place only: its margin. Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas, stay editable, and are woven later by making a Frame round them (Frame from Selection, Fit to drawing), which lifts them into it.
- **Margin (v18, back in v19, ticket 0501, ADR 0066):** six bead positions of clear space on all four sides of the Frame's rectangle on the canvas, at every zoom. No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there; Erase still works. The Frame's own sheet is unaffected.
- **Margin look:** flat, no fill, nothing dimmed, no panel. At rest it is only a quiet gap in the grid: the empty-position dots do not draw inside it. While the Frame is being edited, and for 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin stay.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves no bead and shows no Message: a canvas bead that lies in the margin stays on the canvas, covered by it (not drawn), and shows again if the Frame moves off it.
- **Gutter (ticket 0490):** the band just outside the Frame line where its controls stand: the edit pencil, Remove Frame, the Move handle and the resize handles. It is measured in screen pixels, so the controls keep their size and their distance from the line at every zoom. A resize handle's square stands half its size plus 3px from the line (7.5px, 11px on touch); the pencil and the Move handle stand 48px (`--frame-control-offset`) from it, past the handles' reach, so none covers another. Each is kept inside the canvas box and flips to the other side of the Frame's line (a resize handle to the inside of its edge, the pencil and the Move handle to below the Frame) when there is no room outside it. A press in a control's hit area wins over the tool's press there.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (tickets 417, 0452, 0473, 0490; ADR 0062 amended):** a toggle, not a mode. The **edit pencil** in the gutter above the Frame's top-right corner shows the eight resize handles; off, they are hidden and the Frame can still be moved (Move handle). The pencil does not open the **Frame panel** (below), which has its own opener, the **Frame** button in the Canvas strip, after Menu and Project (CanvasStrip card; ticket 0510). The pencil is the `pencil` icon on a 32px `box` chip, 44px to hit for every pointer, centred on the corner's column (the Move handle slides clear of it on a small Frame), kept inside the canvas box (below the Frame when there is no room above) and never shrinking with the zoom. Open is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon (like the Rulers toggle), closed is the `box` look. The handles go off whenever a Project opens; the choice is kept for the session only. Once a Row progress pass is done no handles show, while the Frame button still opens the bar, which then holds the size and Remove Frame alone (ticket 0475). The rulers are always shown with a Frame; the size Tooltip at its bottom-right corner ("13×13 ≈ 2.1 × 2.1 cm") only while dragging or stepping. Tools work inside the Frame and right up to its edge with the handles on. Eight handles resize it, each in the gutter (9px, 1.5px `ink`, radius 2, filled with the canvas background), grabbed within 14px of a handle's centre for mouse and pen, 24px for touch, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; the nearest wins and corners beat edges. On touch: four 16px corner handles. **Remove Frame (ticket 0498):** a second `box` chip (`delete` icon, same 32px chip and 44px hit) right after the pencil, one hit area to its right (to its left when the canvas box has no room), with its Tooltip; it runs the same command as the Frame panel's Remove Frame, with the same confirmation (asked only when a Row progress pass is done). The pencil, Remove Frame and the Move handle are always there while a Frame is in view: with any tool, with a Selection, and after Row progress has started (only the resize handles go then). A Selection carries the same eight handles, standing on its own edge; where its handles and a control overlap, the Selection's handle wins the press. There is no key. Escape or a press outside the bar closes it, as it does Menu and Project, and that press does nothing else (ticket 0475); that includes a press on the Move handle, the pencil or a resize handle (ticket 0510).
- **Rotate Frame (ticket 0471, ADR 0063):** a `box` button with the `rotate` icon (the one Rotate on the Selection uses), between the Move handle and Edit Frame. It turns the Frame and its own beads a quarter clockwise about their centre, Width and Height swapping, as one Undo step; the canvas under it is not touched, so there is no covered-beads Message, and Edit stays as it was. Disabled once a Row progress pass is done ("Rows are marked done. Remove the Frame to rotate.").
- **Move handle (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473, 0490):** the Frame's move grip. A **horizontal line** in the gutter above the top edge, centred on it, 48px from the Frame line (below the Frame when there is no room above): a 40 × 6 pill in `ink` with a 2px `canvas` halo (`accent` while it is dragged), inside a 56 × 44 hit area for every pointer. Always visible and active, with the bar closed and once Row progress has started, so moving a Frame never needs the bar. It does not scale with the zoom and is kept inside the canvas box, at least 28px from its sides. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads, which travel with it on screen as it goes (the Row progress pointer travels with it); it is one announced Undo step. It is a button named "Move Frame" with the Tooltip "Drag to move the Frame together with its beads." and its place in the Tab order: the arrow keys move the Frame one bead, and Shift + arrows resize it, only while it or a handle has focus. The cursor is `grab` over it, `grabbing` while dragged. The `move-frame` icon is no longer used by the Frame (it stays in the set).
- **Frame panel (ticket 0473):** the Frame's own controls, in one panel that ignores zoom and pan. It is a panel like Menu and Project, in the same shell (MenuPanel card; ticket 0475): one of the three is open at a time, Escape or a press outside closes it, it opens without focusing a field, so a tablet's keyboard stays down until one is tapped (ticket 0510). It hangs from the **Frame button in the Canvas strip**, 4px under it like Menu and Project (a dropdown on every screen size), and from nothing else. 504px wide (the window less a 8px gutter on each side where narrower; under 560px the four edge cells stack two by two), the panel's own look (`overlay-fill`, 1px `overlay-line`, radius-lg, `elevation-3`, padding 8), 8 between its rows. **Row 1:** two boxed fields, **Width** and **Height**, with a muted × between them and the unit ("beads", `muted`) after. Each field has a `meta-tiny` caption, a 1px `line-strong` border, `surface` fill, radius-md and a 2rem height, so it reads as something to tap; it is up to 7rem wide. Both start **empty** with the current number as a `muted` placeholder, so a phone or tablet keyboard opens only on a tap. They take digits only (1 and up, no leading zero, up to three digits). Press Enter or leave both fields and the typed sides apply, each growing or shrinking evenly about the Frame (an empty field keeps its side; the odd bead goes right or down; where rows alternate the top edge moves by pairs of rows), between four and 999 beads; moving from one field to the other applies nothing; Escape clears what was typed; the fields are off once a pass is done (shown, `faint`, not clickable, with a Tooltip: "Rows are marked done. Reset progress to change the Frame size."), then Rotate Frame (`rotate` icon) and, after a 1px `line-strong` divider 4px either side, Remove Frame (`delete` icon, the bin, as every other remove; `danger`); Remove always asks first ("Remove Frame?"; "The Frame goes, and its beads go with it. Rows marked as done are cleared too. This can be undone."; one Undo brings it all back, beads included). **Row 2:** four edge cells in a grid, in the order Top, Bottom, Left, Right. A cell is a `surface` rectangle (1px `line`, radius-md, padding 6) holding a 16px glyph (a square with that edge ruled 4px `ink`, `accent` while that edge was the last one stepped), the edge's name (Inter 12 600) and a **− and + pair**; the last-stepped cell has a 2px `accent-strong` border. In every cell − comes first and + second, and + always adds a bead on that side. Each is 48 × 48 (`--frame-hit`), 4px from its partner, radius-md, `button` fill, 1px `button-line`, 24px icons; hover `pill`, pressed `board`; focus ring as every button. A press moves that edge one bead out (+) or in (−) and is one Undo step, announced like any Frame change; where rows alternate the top edge goes by a pair of rows; holding repeats as the Stepper card says. An edge stops at four beads: the − is disabled, `faint` on `surface`, with the reason "The Frame can’t get any smaller on this side." Each button is named for its edge and axis ("Top edge: add one row", "Left edge: remove one column"). While a Row progress pass is done the steppers stay but are disabled (same Tooltip) and Rotate Frame does not exist (the bar then holds the size, the disabled steppers and Remove Frame). The Move handle slides clear of the pencil where it would cover it.
- **Fit to drawing** is in the Progress bar and the Project panel, and the size is the Frame panel's row 1 and the Tooltip at the Frame's corner (ticket 0473; before it, ticket 427 had Edit Frame with no bar).
- **Its own Technique (ticket 0492):** the Frame has its own Technique, Brick block and Net size, apart from the Canvas's: a peyote Frame sits on a loom Canvas and the other way round. It is drawn from the corner of the Canvas position it lies on in its own geometry, its first row always unshifted, so it moves by whole Canvas positions and its top edge resizes by a whole cycle of its own rows; its handles, rulers and Row progress marks follow it. The Technique choice and Brick block stepper in the Frame controls change the Frame and wait once a pass is done; the Canvas's are in the Project panel.
- **Frame controls in Project (spec 0440):** Technique stays, and so do the Estimated size line and its Note under it. Edit Frame, Fit to drawing and Remove Frame are gone from Project: the Frame is edited and removed from the Selection bar and the Progress bar, where those buttons already are. The size steppers, the beads | mm switch and the side choice of ticket 410 stay gone: each edge changes itself on the canvas.
- **Frame number:** a 22px chip ("1"; DM Mono 12 on `elevated`, 1px `line-strong`, radius 6) in the Frame row. Pressing it brings the Frame into view. Name: "Frame 1, bring it into view".
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. Piece rulers hide and the Frame's rulers show on all four sides, just outside it (Rulers card). The header reads "Star · 21×19" and the canvas strip "Width 21 · Height 19 · 3.4 × 3.0 cm". Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame only.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Row not done). Without one, Export (PNG or PDF in Project, or Export ▾) opens "Set Frame to export" (SaveBox card) and the Progress bar shows "Set Frame to start" with a Fit to drawing button. A Frame is made from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the Frame's own beads. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
