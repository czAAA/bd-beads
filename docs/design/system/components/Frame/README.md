# Frame

A sheet of its own beads laid over the open canvas (ADR 0066), which holds the Pattern: made round a drawing before or after it, and required for Export and Row progress.

- **Up to four Frames in a Project (spec 1214, ADR 0066), each its own layer over the open canvas. A Frame owns its beads and is opaque, and no bead ever passes between layers.** It hides what the canvas holds under it without changing or deleting it, and the canvas beads show again when the Frame moves off them. A press inside a Frame paints that Frame, one outside every Frame paints the canvas, and a stroke stays on the layer it began on. Making a Frame (Frame from Selection, Fit to drawing, Set Frame around the picture) **copies** the beads under it into the new Frame and leaves the canvas's own where they are, under it; a smaller Frame **folds** the beads it gives up and a larger one unfolds them; Remove Frame always asks and never drops its beads onto the canvas (Remove, Remove and copy, Cancel). A Frame and its margin never meet another Frame and its margin. It limits drawing on the canvas in one place only: its margin. Beads outside the margin stay on the saved canvas and stay editable.
- **Margin sheet (ticket 0655; wins over the Margin look, Gutter, Edit Frame, Rotate Frame and Move handle bullets below where they differ):** the margin is drawn as a sheet of drafting paper in its own color (Auto: the Canvas color one step off), with an inner border, the Row progress, the Move bar and the tools (Rotate Frame, Edit Frame, Export, Margin color, Remove Frame) in its top band, a title block (name, Technique, maker, sizes, the lettered legend, Letters) in its bottom-left corner as seen, rulers along its left and top as seen (ticket 1170) and hot-corner resize handles while Edit Frame is on. Nothing of the Frame stands in the gutter any more. It has a full, a compact and a folded form by zoom. All of it is the MarginSheet card; built by tickets 0656 to 0658.
- **Margin (v18, back in v19, ticket 0501, ADR 0066):** four bead positions of clear space (six until ticket 0656) on all four sides of the Frame's rectangle on the canvas, at every zoom. No bead can be placed in it: Paint, Fill, Select and paste skip it, and the pointer shows `not-allowed` there; Erase still works. The Frame's own sheet is unaffected.
- **Margin look:** the Margin sheet above (ticket 0656): its own color, no Position marks in it, nothing dimmed. For 1s after a press inside it is refused, a 1px dashed `line-strong` outline (radius 12) marks its outer edge, fading in and out over `--duration-fast`, opacity only (`bb-frame-margin`; the dot gap is `bb-frame-clear`). 2px in high contrast. The Frame line and handles are unchanged.
- **Beads already in the margin stay.** Setting, moving or resizing the Frame moves no bead and shows no Message: a canvas bead that lies in the margin stays on the canvas, covered by it (not drawn), and shows again if the Frame moves off it.
- **Gutter (ticket 0490):** the band just outside the Frame line where its controls stand: the edit pencil, Remove Frame, the Move handle and the resize handles. It is measured in screen pixels, so the controls keep their size and their distance from the line at every zoom. A resize handle's square stands half its size plus 3px from the line (7.5px, 11px on touch); the pencil and the Move handle stand 48px (`--frame-control-offset`) from it, past the handles' reach, so none covers another. Each is kept inside the canvas box and flips to the other side of the Frame's line (a resize handle to the inside of its edge, the pencil and the Move handle to below the Frame) when there is no room outside it. A press in a control's hit area wins over the tool's press there.
- **Line:** 1.5px `ink`, radius 7, 7px outside the outermost beads, so the current row's rings (inside the beads' cells) sit inside it. No fill, and nothing outside is dimmed.
- **Edit Frame (tickets 417, 0452, 0473, 0490; ADR 0054):** a toggle, not a mode. The **edit pencil** in the gutter above the Frame's top-right corner shows the eight resize handles; off, they are hidden and the Frame can still be moved (Move handle). The pencil does not open the **Frame settings popover**, which the sheet's ··· button opens (MarginSheet card, Several Frames). The pencil is the `pencil` icon on a 32px `box` chip, 44px to hit for every pointer, centred on the corner's column (the Move handle slides clear of it on a small Frame), kept inside the canvas box (below the Frame when there is no room above) and never shrinking with the zoom. Open is an `ink` fill with a `canvas` icon (like the Rulers toggle), closed is the `box` look. The handles go off whenever a Project opens; the choice is kept for the session only. Once a Row progress pass is done no handles show, while the ··· button still opens the popover, which then holds the size and Remove Frame alone (ticket 0475). The rulers are always shown with a Frame; the size Tooltip at its bottom-right corner ("13×13 ≈ 2.1 × 2.1 cm") only while dragging or stepping. Tools work inside the Frame and right up to its edge with the handles on. Eight handles resize it, the Handle card's in `accent` on the Frame line (MarginSheet card), with the Handle card's reach (14px mouse and pen, a 44px touch area, capped at a third of the Frame's shorter side; corners beat edges, then the nearest wins). On touch: the four corners. **Remove Frame (ticket 0498):** a second `box` chip (`delete` icon, same 32px chip and 44px hit) right after the pencil, one hit area to its right (to its left when the canvas box has no room), with its Tooltip; it runs the same command as the popover's Remove Frame, with the same confirmation (always asked). The pencil, Remove Frame and the Move handle are always there while a Frame is in view: with any tool, with a Selection, and after Row progress has started (only the resize handles go then). A Selection carries the same eight handles, standing on its own edge and drawn as the Frame's hot corners in `marker` (ContextBar card, Area sheet, ticket 0807); where its handles and a control overlap, the Selection's handle wins the press. There is no key. Escape or a press outside the popover closes it, as it does Menu and Project, and that press does nothing else (ticket 0475); that includes a press on the Move handle, the pencil or a resize handle (ticket 0510).
- **Rotate Frame (ticket 0471, ADR 0052):** a `box` button with the `rotate` icon (the one Rotate on the Selection uses), between the Move handle and Edit Frame. It turns the Frame and its own beads a quarter clockwise about their centre, Width and Height swapping, as one Undo step; the canvas under it is not touched, so there is no covered-beads Message, and Edit stays as it was. Offered after a Row progress pass is done too: each Row direction's pointer turns a quarter clockwise with the Frame, so the same beads stay finished, and it never asks (ticket 0647).
- **Move handle (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473, 0490):** the Frame's move grip. A **horizontal line** in the gutter above the top edge, centred on it, 48px from the Frame line (below the Frame when there is no room above): a 40 × 6 pill in `ink` with a 2px `canvas` halo (`accent` while it is dragged), inside a 56 × 44 hit area for every pointer. Always visible and active, with the popover closed and once Row progress has started, so moving a Frame never needs the bar. It does not scale with the zoom and is kept inside the canvas box, at least 28px from its sides. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads, which travel with it on screen as it goes (the Row progress pointer travels with it); it is one announced Undo step. It is a button named "Move Frame" with the Tooltip "Drag to move the Frame together with its beads." and its place in the Tab order: the arrow keys move the Frame one bead, and Shift + arrows resize it, only while it or a handle has focus. The cursor is `grab` over it, `grabbing` while dragged. The `move-frame` icon is no longer used by the Frame (it stays in the set).
- **Frame panel (retired, ticket 1222):** the Frame's own controls were one panel from the strip's Frame button (tickets 0473, 0553, 0555). Spec 1214 retired it with that button: the size, a Custom frame's shape steppers and Fringe style, and Beads needed are in the Frame settings popover from the margin sheet's ··· (MarginSheet card, Several Frames); the Background is the second row of the Margin color popover; the ways to add a Frame are the Frames section of the Project panel (ProjectPanel card, Frames). Its Export section is the Export panel's, which has a Frame dropdown with several Frames (ExportPanel card). Replace Frame is gone: Remove Frame, then add one.
- **Fit to drawing** is, in the Project panel's Frames section and, with no Frame, in the Export panel (tickets 0555, 1222); the size is the popover's size and the Tooltip at the Frame's corner.
- **Its own Technique (ticket 0492):** the Frame has its own Technique and Drop, apart from the Canvas's: a peyote Frame sits on a loom Canvas and the other way round. It is drawn from the corner of the Canvas position it lies on in its own geometry, its first row always unshifted, so it moves by whole Canvas positions and its top edge resizes by a whole cycle of its own rows; its handles, rulers and Row progress marks follow it. The Technique choice and Drop stepper in the Frame's title block change the Frame and wait once a pass is done; the Canvas's are in the Project panel.
- **Nothing of the Frame in Project (tickets 0554, 0670, 1222):** the Frame's Technique, its settings and its Bead are in its title block (LayerSettings card), its Background is the Margin color popover's second row, and its size, Estimated size and Beads needed are in the ··· popover. The Project panel holds only the Frames section: the ways to add a Frame and one folded section per Frame (ProjectPanel card). The Picture and Printable chart exports are the Export panel's, which has a Frame dropdown once there are several Frames, and unfold under the Project panel's Export row as the Export panel's rows (ticket 1058), so a Project-wide export never needs a Frame's sheet.
- **Once set:** inside it, empty positions draw as full `bead-empty` beads. The Frame's rulers show on its left and top as seen (ticket 1170), just outside it, whatever the Rulers toggle says; the pieces around it keep theirs, which the toggle shows or hides (Rulers card, ticket 1012). Its size reads in its title block and in the head of the ··· popover ("Peyote · 11 × 100"). Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame they are on, each Frame its own.
- **What needs a Frame:** Export, Row progress (Row done, Back a row). Without one there is nothing to export as a picture or chart: the Export panel shows no export rows, only the line that says to set a Frame, with Drag a Frame and Fit a Frame to the drawing (ExportPanel card), and there is no sheet to hold Row progress. A Frame is made from the Project panel's Frames section (Frame from starter, Fit a Frame to the drawing, Drag a Frame), from a Selection (Frame on the Selection bar) or by Fit to drawing.
- **Rotate** no longer touches the Frame: it turns the Selection (ContextBar card, ticket 416, ADR 0052). Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Frame from Selection again.
- **Export** takes only the Frame's own beads. PNG and PDF never draw the Frame line.
- **A Custom frame (tickets 0592, 0593, ADR 0077):** a peyote Triangle or Warped Square sized by Rounds, a Star (five Warped Squares lying flat as rhombi, Seams round each arm and from its middle to its corners) or Kaleidocycle (six Triangles round a hole, Off-shape, with a Seam down each gap) sized by Part size, or a Kite (two wings, its Seam the spine) sized by Rows; or a Fringe earring (ticket 0597), its top in brick stitch, peyote or loom with its Drop (ticket 0814), its top and a strand under each base bead, a Seam between them, sized by Width, Top rows (past its point, a body under it, ticket 0814) and Fringe length with its Fringe style, each side's resize handle driving its own: left and right Width, top Top rows, bottom Fringe length, only the dragged edge moving; in a V a changed Width moves Fringe length two beads a strand gained or lost on a side, so the V still reaches the edges (ticket 0816). Inside its bounding rectangle every bead is drawn at its centre, turned to its panel's angle, in the Bead's own cell; the Seams are 1px lines in `piece-line` from the centre to each corner; the Off-shape round the shape and the centre where the Seams meet are the Canvas colour with no position marks, and a press there is refused with the margin outline's flash. The Frame settings popover shows a Stepper per setting (− value +, the Stepper card; labelled Rounds, Part size or Rows, or Width, Top rows and Fringe length, then the Fringe style as a SegmentedControl of the setup's names; ticket 0670, LayerSettings card), and its size section the Estimated size of the shape alone, in place of Width, Height and the edge steppers; Edit Frame's resize handles, Remove row/column and Drag a Frame are not offered. A Selection over it (ticket 0594) is the Select tool's rectangle of the Frame's cells on screen, drawn as on any Frame, holding every bead whose centre lies inside it; Rotate Frame turns the whole shape a quarter with its beads and swaps its rectangle's sides.
- **Mirror on a Custom frame (ticket 0706; the whole-Frame Mirror since ticket 0750, ContextBar and MarginSheet cards):** every counterpart is found through the shape's layout, the bead's centre reflected across the Axis through the layout's centre or turned about it, the bead under each point taking it as Paste lands one, a Seam or Off-shape dropped silently. On a round shape (Triangle, Warped Square, Star, Kaleidocycle) Radial's Fold is the side count, fixed and exact; the Axes are the shape's own symmetry, set when the Mirror goes there and not editable: a round shape Radial with its side count as the Fold, a Kite or Fringe earring its one straight line (Left–right, Top–bottom once turned a quarter); the Mirror bar shows a note naming it in place of the chips. The Axis lines, the ring and the sector lines are drawn about the layout's centre, from its first Seam.
- **Naming:** the mode is "Edit Frame". "Frame" alone names the thing.
- **Canvas look (tickets 1001, 1004; approved 2026-11-14):** the Frame's empty positions are tonal tiles by the Canvas's rule, from the Frame's own Background, in place of full `bead-empty` beads (in the app since ticket 1001), each with an edge just inside it so every empty position reads as a bead (BeadBoard card, Tonal tiles); and round beads in Open weave lie over the Frame's tiles (ticket 1010) (BeadBoard card, Canvas look). The Frame's line, margin sheet, bar and handles are unchanged.
- **Off-shape (ticket 1196; any Frame):** where the Frame holds no bead position (a Custom frame outside its shape, the empty half bead at the short end of each peyote or brick stitch row or column) and its Seams are drawn on screen in `frame-off-shape`, darker than every Frame background of the theme (blackish in dark, a few steps darker in light, a grey in high contrast; a Custom color darker than the token is darkened itself), with no position marks, so it reads as a place no bead can go. A press there is refused with the outline flash. Exports leave it white.
- The consumer provides the Frame's position and size in beads, the Bead's size for the measured size, and whether the Frame is being edited.
