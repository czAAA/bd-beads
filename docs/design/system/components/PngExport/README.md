# PngExport

The PNG export: one portrait picture, the whole framed Schema on its board at full width with the story under it.

- The chart: rulers on the top and left with a number every 10, a hairline every 10 beads, beads at `print-png-bead` (fewer past the pixel ceiling).
- The story under it: technique word, name, "by {maker}", bead and size and the total on the left; Beads needed in two columns (letter swatch, name, beads, grams) on the right; the X1 mark and "bd-beads" bottom left, date and time bottom right.
- A Schema wider than tall gets a shorter picture; the story stays under it. No wide layout, no watermark. Drawn in the app's language. File name `bd-beads-{pattern}.png`.
