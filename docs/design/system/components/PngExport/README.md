# PngExport

The PNG export: one portrait picture, the whole framed Schema on its board at full width with the story under it.

- The chart: rulers on the top and left with a number every 10, a hairline every 10 beads, beads at the size the Export quality chooses (QualitySlider card): 45, 60 or 90 px, 60 (Medium, `print-png-bead`) by default, and fewer past the one ceiling (400 megapixels, and 16 384 px wide), reduced to a whole number of px across.
- The story under it: technique word, name, "by {maker}", bead and size and the total on the left; Beads needed in two columns (letter swatch, name, beads, grams) on the right; the X1 mark and "openbeads" bottom left, date and time bottom right.
- A Schema wider than tall gets a shorter picture; the story stays under it. No wide layout, no watermark. Drawn in the app's language. File name `bd-beads-{pattern}.png`.
