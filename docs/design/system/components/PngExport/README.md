# PngExport

The PNG export: the whole Pattern on its board, with a narrow story column beside it.

- The chart takes about three quarters of the picture: rulers every 10, a hairline every 10 beads, beads at `print-png-bead` (fewer pixels past 16 megapixels).
- Story column: technique word, name, "by {maker}", bead and size, Beads needed with beads and grams and the Total, date and time, the X1 mark and "bd-beads". Under the chart for a Pattern wider than tall.
- The maker's name under the board, the accent line behind the board's corners, three marks; drawn in the app's language. File name `bd-beads-{pattern}.png`.

Hand-written from the Phase D sign-off.
