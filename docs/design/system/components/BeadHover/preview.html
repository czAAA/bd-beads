On the Pattern

  • Hovering a bead with no colour chosen draws the 2px bead-outline inside it; with Paint and a colour, the bead previews that colour at 60%. Touch has no hover: the bead changes on contact.
  • The cursor is a crosshair over the board, a grab hand while Space is held, and grabbing while panning or moving a picture in Convert image.
  • Painting has no animation: a bead takes its colour the instant the pointer reaches it.
