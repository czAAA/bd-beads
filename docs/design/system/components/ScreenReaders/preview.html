Landmarks and the Pattern

  • header (banner), the left column as aside "Tools", the canvas box as main; phone sheets as dialogs named by their title.
  • The drawing surface is role="img" named "Logo panel, 40 by 30 beads, 2 colors, row 12 of 30 done", updated when that changes; Beads needed is its text equivalent.
  • Toggles use aria-pressed, the Switch role="switch", disclosure rows aria-expanded.
