# MarginSheet

The Frame's keep-out margin drawn as a sheet of drafting paper: its own background and inner border, the Frame's tools in its top band, a title block in its bottom-left corner as seen with Row progress on a strip under it across the sheet (ticket 1176), and the rulers along its left and top (tickets 0655, 1170; design canvas "Keep-out margin variants", variant A "Drafting sheet", link on ticket 0655 in the private docs repo). It replaces the flat margin and the gutter controls of the Frame card. Built by tickets 0656 (width and color), 0657 (tools, title block, Row progress) and 0658 (zoom forms and folding).

## Width

- **4 bead positions on every side**, fixed, not a setting (was 6). The keep-out rules of the Frame card are unchanged; only the width is.
- 4 is what the content needs at 100% zoom, where a loom bead is 20px and the band 80px. The top band holds the 10px border inset, the 28px tool row, the column numbers (12px) and 7px to the Frame line: about 64px. The bottom band holds 7px or more from the Frame line, the title block's 38px row (its legend in two lines, ticket 1213; 26px until then) on Row progress's 26px strip (ticket 1176; the 48px two-row block until then) and the 10px inset: about 73px, held clear of the Frame's line and its outset from 80px of band. So the drafting sheet is 80px (ticket 1213; 72px, an 18px bead's 4, until then). The left band holds the row numbers (about 40px); the right band is clear. Three beads (54px) cannot hold the title block; five or more is empty paper.

## The sheet

- **Fill:** the margin color (below) on the whole band, outer corners `--margin-sheet-radius` (12px; 10px in the compact form), drawing geometry that keeps its value when the radius scale changes (ticket 1078). It is opaque over the Canvas and its Position marks; the Frame's own sheet sits on it as before.
- **Inner border:** 1px `margin-line`, inset `--margin-border-inset` (10px; 6px compact), corners `--margin-border-radius` (6px), like the border of a drawing. Nothing else is drawn on the paper: no zone letters, no crop marks.
- **Edge:** a 1px `margin-line` hairline round the outside, so the sheet reads on a Canvas of nearly the same color.
- **High contrast:** the sheet is white with a 2px black border and a 1px `margin-line` inner border.
- **Never in exports:** PNG and PDF print the Frame's beads only, as before.

## Margin color

- **Auto** (the default): the Canvas color one step off, toward white by 5% in dark and toward black by 5% in light, its hue kept: Slate `#3b3e43` gives `#45484c`, Night `#202020` gives `#2b2b2b`, Linen `#f7f3ec` gives `#ebe7e0`, Studio `#fafafa` gives `#eeeeee`. On a Custom Canvas color the step goes toward white when the Project is drawn in the dark look and toward black in the light look (CanvasBackground card). It follows the Canvas color as it changes: the Canvas layer's own Background since ticket 0666, whatever the Frame's.
- **Picked:** the standalone `BackgroundColorPicker` (CanvasBackground card, ticket 0653) with an **Auto** swatch first, then the theme's three Canvas colors (ticket 1000), then the Palette's Custom color tile. Auto is drawn split corner to corner, the Canvas color and the margin color, so it shows what it does. The swatch equal to the current Canvas color carries a 6px dot (`ink` at 70%) and the name "{color}, the Canvas color", and its Tooltip says so: "Canvas color" («Цвет холста») over the hex, chosen or not, where every other swatch's reads "Color" (ticket 0684); picking it leaves no margin visible. The chosen one is ringed with a check as in every BackgroundColorPicker.
- **Remembered on the device**, as the Canvas color is: the position for the three, one Custom color for both themes. Not an Undo step.
- **Three Canvas colors (ticket 1000; approved 2026-11-14, in the app since that ticket):** the picker offers Auto, the theme's three Canvas colors and Custom color, and a stored position moves by the CanvasBackground card's map (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 become 1, 2, 2, 1, 3).
- **Where:** the Frame panel's last section, **margin** (retired, ticket 1222: the color and the title block's switch are in the Margin color popover) (PanelSection, after settings, ticket 0852; summary "auto" or the color's name), and the **Margin color** button on the sheet, which opens the same picker in a popover under it (the shared popover look, 12px padding, `label` "margin color", the swatches, the name and hex line).
- **What sits on it:** text and icons are `ink` and `body`; the rulers take the nearest grey to the look's own `ruler` at 4.5:1 or more against the margin color, the 5th numbers at 7:1, the rule the Custom Canvas color uses.

## Top band

Left to right, 14px in from the sheet's top-left corner (10px compact), 4px apart:

1. **Expand the Frame** (`expand-frame`; while expanded **Back to the Canvas**, `collapse-frame`; ticket 1168, built by 1169): a 28px icon button (24px compact) in the sheet's very corner. It fits the Frame to the screen and slims the sheet to what it holds (Expand the Frame, below). Shown with every Frame, Custom frames too, with Row progress started or not.
2. **Row progress** was here, after a divider, until ticket 1176: it is now under the title block (Row progress strip, below).
3. **Move bar**, centred on the top band: the Frame card's Move handle (40 × 6 `ink` pill in a 56 × 28 hit area, `grab`), now on the paper instead of in the gutter. Same name, Tooltip, keys and Tab stop.
4. **Tools**, at the right, 28px icon buttons (`radius-md`, no fill, hover `ink` at 10%): **Mirror the whole Frame** (`mirror`, ticket 0750; below), **Rotate Frame** (`rotate`), **Edit Frame** (`pencil`; on: `ink` fill, `canvas` icon), **Export** (`export`, the Canvas strip's Export: opens the Export panel under this button, ExportPanel card), **Margin color** (`palette`), a divider, **Remove Frame** (`delete`, `ink` on the paper, `danger` on hover and in its confirmation). Each keeps the name, Tooltip, confirmation and disabled reasons it has in the Frame card. **Which of them stand on the band is one rule of the band's width** (ticket 1195; Zoom forms, below): as many as fit, the rest in the **···** Frame tools menu.

**Keyboard (ticket 0857):** the band is three Tab stops, in its order: Expand the Frame (ticket 1168), the Move bar (whose arrows move the Frame, so it stays a stop of its own) and the tools. ←/→, Home and End step along the tools and stop at the ends; the one last focused is the Tab stop. The tools stop is whatever stands on the band: its tools and, where some are in the menu, Frame tools. The title block's name ("Name this Frame" on an unnamed Frame) and its other controls come next, then Row progress, one stop, ←/→ along it (ticket 1176).

## Row progress strip (ticket 1176)

Row progress stands under the title block, on a strip across the sheet: the band keeps only the Frame's tools, and the progress a maker reads while weaving sits under the legend whose letters it counts.

- **The strip:** `--margin-strip` (26px, its rules included) tall, the width of the sheet inside its inner border, filled `margin-title` with 1px `margin-line` rules, its bottom corners rounded with the inner border (`--margin-border-radius`). Its buttons (the settings button, Back a row, Row done) have the same `--margin-border-radius` corners, squarish as the title block, not a pill (ticket 1245). The title block stands on it at the left, sharing its top rule, its own corner square. 2px padding at each end.
- **What it holds (tickets 1223, 1251),** left to right, 4px apart: the **settings** button (a 15px `settings` icon in a 24px icon button, "Row progress settings"; its list holds the four ways, Show row marks and Reset progress), Back a row (the quiet `text` button, its words and arrow), a divider, the counter as text ("Row 5 of 30", `control`; Pattern done included), the **track**, filling all the strip leaves (never under `--margin-track`, 64px, 4px either side), a divider and **Row done** (primary, 24px). Where the strip is short of room it first shortens the counter to "5/30", then gives up the track, then Back a row's words; the settings button, the counter and Row done are never dropped from it, and where even that does not fit the sheet the whole strip is not drawn (ticket 1245). Several Frames, below, has the whole of it.
- **One lit Row done (ticket 0981, 1223):** Row done is the one filled button (primary) on the strip, the Progress bar being gone.
- **Compact and folded:** where the bottom band has no room to stack the title block (its compact row, the row without the legend, or the name tab) on the strip, the two share one line, the title block at the left and the strip, the same strip, taking what it leaves (ticket 1223 built it so; the stacked strip needs a 76px band). Row progress leaves the name tab its whole name, giving up its track first, then Back a row's words; Row done and the counter stay as long as the strip is drawn (tickets 1223, 1245).
- **Bare:** no strip (Several Frames, below).

The edit pencil, Remove Frame chip and Move handle no longer stand in the gutter: nothing of the Frame is drawn outside its sheet except the size Tooltip.

The Homepage's What's inside demo Frame shows the same strip, Back a row included (Homepage card, ticket 1223).

### Mirror the whole Frame (ticket 0750, ADR 0082)

- **The button:** the first of the tools, left of Rotate Frame, the `mirror` icon. While the whole Frame is the Mirror's Area it is **selected**: filled `accent` (yellow in dark, orange in light) with its icon in `on-accent`, `aria-pressed="true"`, in every theme (high contrast too); off, the band's plain look. This is the band's own selected look, not the IconButton `selected` (`ink` fill) that Edit Frame uses: Edit Frame is a mode of the Frame, the Mirror a setting that stays on.
- **Tooltip:** "Mirror the whole Frame" («Отражать всю рамку») off, "Mirror off" («Выключить отражение») on.
- **A press** makes the whole Frame the Mirror's Area (a Canvas Area is dropped, its Mode and Axes kept, counts clamped; onto a Custom frame the Axes become the shape's own), enters Mirror mode and shows the Mirror bar above the sheet (ContextBar card); one Undo step. **Pressed while on** it turns the Mirror off, as Close mirror does.
- **Shown and hidden as Rotate Frame is**, in every form that has the tools; with Row progress started it stays (mirroring does not change the Frame). In the folded and bare forms it is the first item of the **Frame tools** menu, "Mirror the whole Frame", with a check (`check`, `accent-strong`) while on and `role="menuitemcheckbox"`.

## Expand the Frame (tickets 1168, 1169)

How the expanded sheet is laid out, what a move by hand does and what the press fits are ticket 1260's (As built, at the end), which wins over the bullets below where they differ.

Signed off 2026-11-20 (design canvas "Expand Frame to the screen", icon A, link on ticket 1168). For weaving from the screen: the Frame as large as the screen allows. Changed by ticket 1176: it only enlarges the Frame, the Tool pill and drawing staying as they are, and the Progress bar steps aside, the sheet's Row progress strip standing in for it.

- **The Progress bar steps aside** (ticket 1176): nothing of it is drawn but the room a home indicator's safe-area inset keeps; the sheet's Row progress strip is the one on screen, its Row done filled (ProgressBar card).
- **The press** fits the Frame with its sheet into the room under the Canvas strip, down to the screen's bottom, clear of the Tool pill as Fit is, centred there. The sheet fills the whole room on every side, so no Canvas shows at the fit (ticket 1194; until then a small Frame stopped at the 400% ceiling and left Canvas at the sides): the room the Frame leaves on the axis that does not bind is shared by the top band (at least what its row and numbers need) and the bottom strip. The zoom is the largest at which it fits, with no ceiling; Fit's 48px bead cap (`LARGEST_FIT_BEAD_PX`) does not hold here either (tickets 1176, 1194). A zoom in goes on to twice the fit, or 400% where that is more. The view glides there over `duration-base` with `ease-standard`; under reduced motion it jumps.
- **The Tool pill stays** (ticket 1176; it stepped aside until then), so drawing goes on while expanded. It moves to the free edge: the left for a Frame the screen's height binds, the bottom for one its width binds, and back to its own edge with Back to the Canvas (ToolPill card). The Selection bar and the Mirror bar show as ever (ContextBar card).
- **The sheet slims to what it holds**, at scale 1 at every zoom (never the 1 to 1.5 of Zoom forms below), so a closer zoom gives the room to the beads and not to the paper:
  - **Top:** the band's row `--margin-expanded-inset` (6px; `--margin-expanded-inset-compact`, 4px, under `bp-tablet`) in from the sheet's edge, the column numbers 3px under it, the Frame's line 3px under them.
  - **Sides:** a side that holds row numbers has them 3px off the line and is as wide as the widest of them and the inset; a side without numbers is the inset alone.
  - **Bottom:** the title block on the Row progress strip (ticket 1176), the inset from the line and from the sheet's edge, in its usual corner.
  - **No inner border.** The fill, the edge hairline and the outer corners (12px; 10px under `bp-tablet`) are the sheet's own.
  - **The sheet is at least as wide as its row:** a narrow Frame keeps the row whole, the sheet centred on the Frame (the fit counts it, ticket 1194).
  - The numbers grow with the bead (ticket 1194): DM Mono at about 40% of the bead's pitch on screen at the fit, from 12px to 28px, so a few large beads carry large numbers; they keep that size while zooming, every 5th in `body`, a number on every column and row (Ruler step 1) wherever it fits and the Ruler step and Ruler dots where it does not, and the current pass on its number's `marker` pill (its padding growing with them), or the tick where its number is not drawn. The sheet's top and left grow to hold them. They are drawn in every case, never only dots. The unexpanded sheet keeps its own sizes.
- **The form goes by width, never by zoom.** The row is the widest of full, compact and folded that holds every tool; where even the folded row does not, it keeps the tools that fit and the rest go under **···**, as in Zoom forms. It is never bare. The title block steps down: the two-row block, then its one row with pencils (Title block, On the strip), then the compact row, then a row of the name, the legend and Letters with its fold, then the tab. The roomy block is not used. The **Move bar is not drawn** and is no Tab stop: the view follows the Frame, so there is nothing to drag it to.
- **Drawing is as ever** (ticket 1176; until then only the Frame was in play): a press on the Canvas past the keep-out margin draws there, and the 4 keep-out positions and every keep-out rule are unchanged; only the drawing of the sheet changes. Pinch, the zoom buttons and panning still work, and the sheet stays slim at the new zoom, its numbers at their size.
- **Zooming out goes back** (ticket 1194): a zoom out (pinch, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel, Ctrl/⌘ + minus, the zoom buttons) that comes to rest under about 95% of the fit (fingers lifted, the wheel still for about 150ms) glides back to the view as it was before the press, the way Back to the Canvas does, and announces "Back to the Canvas"; one that rests above it springs back to the fit; a zoom in stays expanded. During the pinch the sheet stays slim. Under reduced motion it jumps.
- **Moving the view by hand goes back** (2026-11-22): a drag (the Hand tool, the middle or right button, Space), a wheel or trackpad scroll, a two-finger pan (fingers that carry the view about 24px while their spread zooms it by under 10%), a two-finger twist of the Canvas or Rotate canvas jumps back to the view as it was before the press, announced "Back to the Canvas", and the move goes on over it; the rest of a pinch keeps that zoom. A twist of the Frame and the edge scroll a drag carries stay expanded.
- **Back to the Canvas** (the same button, `collapse-frame`), or **Escape** when nothing else takes it (an open popover, panel, menu or Selection takes it first), puts back the view as it was before the press, the Progress bar and the sheet's zoom forms. Fit while expanded fits the expanded room. A changed screen size, a turned iPad included, fits again.
- **Not saved:** a new page or another Project opens unexpanded. Remove Frame goes back.
- **In every form:** the first item of the full, compact and folded rows; bare, it stands 4px to the left of the Move bar on its line (10px on a coarse pointer), as the Frame tools button stands to its right, so the Move bar stays centred. It is a button, not a menu item, in every form.
- **Name and Tooltip:** "Expand the Frame", and "Back to the Canvas" while expanded (`copy/frame.md`, Expand the Frame). The name changes with the state; there is no `aria-pressed`. The press is announced: "Frame expanded" and "Back to the Canvas".

## Rotate Frame waits under the pointer (ticket 1016)

The Frame turns about its middle (ADR 0052, ADR 0079), seen turning over `--frame-turn-duration` (150ms, `--ease-standard`; a jump under reduced motion), and the band with all its tools goes with it at once; the view does not scroll. So the next press needs no aim:

- **The copy.** A pointer's press on Rotate Frame leaves a copy of the button exactly where it was pressed, lifted off the paper: the band's button, at the band's scale, on an `elevated` pill (`space-4` padding, 1px `overlay-line`, `radius-lg`) with `elevation-3`. A press on it turns the Frame again by the same action as the band's button; four presses without moving the pointer turn the Frame four times.
- **It leaves** `--rotate-copy-wait` (200ms) after the pointer leaves it, fading out over `--rotate-copy-fade` (150ms, `--ease-in`); back over it within that time, it stays. On touch, which has no hover, it leaves `--rotate-copy-tap-hold` (1.6s) after the last tap.
- **Turned back** to where the copy stands, the band's own button arrives under it, and the copy goes once the turn has been seen, with no fade: the pointer is never over nothing.
- **From the Frame tools menu** (a coarse pointer, or a band too narrow for its tools), the menu closes on the press and a full-size copy stands centred where the item was pressed, by the same rules.
- **Keyboard and screen reader.** The copy has the button's name and Tooltip but is not a Tab stop, takes no focus and is hidden from a screen reader (`aria-hidden`), so Rotate Frame is there once: the keyboard's is the band's own button, whose focus goes with the band, and a press from the keyboard leaves no copy. Each turn is announced once.
- Rotate Frame from the Frame settings popover, whose item does not move, leaves a full-size copy where it was pressed.
- The Selection bar's Rotate waits under the pointer by the same rules (ContextBar card, ticket 1110).

## Edit Frame: hot corners

- While Edit Frame is on, the sheet's outer edge is outlined 1px dashed in `accent` at 55%, 3px outside it.
- **On a turned Frame** (ticket 1016) Edit Frame keeps its place in the band, enabled, so no tool shifts as the Frame turns. The handles stand on the line of the rectangle the Frame lies in; each moves the Frame's own edge that lies under it, by that edge's own step (on peyote and brick stitch the two-bead step shows on whichever screen edge the Frame's own top or left has become), and the edge across from it stays where it is on the Canvas. The size Tooltip and the title block give the size as seen.
- The eight resize handles are the Handle card's, in `accent`, on the Frame line: a bracket at each corner, a bar at each edge's middle, the corner under the pointer hot. Beside the hot corner the size Tooltip shows ("42 × 13 ≈ 6.7 × 1.7 cm"). Their reach is the Handle card's (14px for mouse and pen, a 44px touch area).
- Editing stays locked once a row is marked done, as before. The Edit Frame button stays in its place, disabled (`aria-disabled`, still focusable), its Tooltip giving the lock sentence "Rows are marked done. Reset progress to change the size." on hover, focus, a tap or a long press, as Reset progress says why before a row is done (ticket 0975). In the Frame tools menu its item is disabled with the same reason. After Reset progress it works again.

## Rulers and the current pass

- With a Frame, its rulers stand in the left and top bands as seen, at every turn (ticket 1170; three sides, none along the title block's edge, until then). Placement, sizes and the 5th-number rule are the Rulers card's; their color follows Margin color above.
- The **current pass** is marked on its own ruler number: a `marker` pill behind it, 2px round the number and fully rounded, the number weight 700 in `canvas` (Rulers card), in the band that numbers it (the left for a pass across the screen, the top for one down it). The numbers sit below the tool row in every form, so the mark never meets a tool. The pass itself keeps its ring and the finished passes their dimming (BeadBoard card).
- **Row done (ticket 0729):** the pill glides from the pass's number to the next one's over `duration-base` with `ease-standard`, the two numbers crossfading their color; the tick glides along the Frame's line the same way. When the last pass is done the pill and the tick fade out over 150ms (`ease-in`) and Pattern done plays over the Frame, inside the sheet (Motion section, Row progress). Under reduced motion they jump.
- Where the pass has no number drawn (the compact and folded forms, Rulers off, a Frame turned a quarter, whose numbers count the cells it covers, or a Ruler step that skips it), the mark is a **tick**: 3px thick in `marker`, rounded, from the middle of the Frame's line in to the beads, at the pass; on the left and right sides for a pass across the screen, on the top for one down it. Inside the line, it never stands under a tool.
