# Glossary: Editing a Schema

Editing, sizing, counting and the clipboard and history. Part of the glossary split out of `CONTEXT.md` (ticket 380); the index is [README.md](README.md).

**Eraser**:
The 4th Tools-group tool, selectable by clicking its own button alongside Paint, Fill and Select: its primary press/tap erases a single bead under the pointer, dragging to erase a line (ticket 176 — renamed from "Erase" and switched from its original flood-erase primary behavior, so it works on touch/phone). Behaves like every other drawing command — one undo step per stroke, respects the Row progress lock. The Eraser is the only way to erase by pointer: the right mouse button no longer erases under Paint or Fill (ticket 541); it moves the Canvas, like the Hand tool, under every tool. Its key is `4` (ticket 292; it was `E` from ticket 250). `Del` never picks the Eraser: it only empties the beads of the Selection.
_Avoid_: erase mode, clear tool

**Clear**:
Resets the open Project to how it was when first created at its size: every cell empty and Row progress's pointers back at the first row (it stays on with the Frame), after a confirmation. The Project's name, size, Technique, Bead and rotation are kept. One undo step, which brings back both the grid and Row progress. Unlike other drawing commands it ignores the Row progress lock, since clearing progress is part of what it does.
_Avoid_: delete all, reset, wipe

**Schema size**:
How big a Schema is: its Frame's width × height, counted in beads (a Project with no Frame has no Schema size yet). It is set in the Frame section, in beads or mm (the unit is remembered on the device, not in the Project); a size in mm always rounds up to the next whole bead and is not remembered. A stepper press adds or removes one bead in either unit. Not limited in size beyond what the device can hold (see ADR 0019, which removed the cap ADR 0026 set).
_Avoid_: dimensions, resolution, physical size, columns × rows

**Rotate**:
Turns the Selection a quarter turn clockwise about its centre, on its layer (in the Frame the result is trimmed to the Frame, ticket 491); the turned rectangle becomes the Selection and the Frame is untouched (ADR 0052, which replaces the Frame turn of ADR 0026 and the view-only turn of ticket 171; both come back as their own controls, **Rotate Frame** and **Rotate canvas**, spec 0470, and this Rotate stays Selection-only). It is a drawing edit: beads under the turned footprint are replaced, one Message with Undo says the turned fragment landed on top of existing beads (shown only when something was covered), and the keep-out margin and finished rows trim it silently. On peyote and brick stitch the turned top row snaps to an even row. Turning the whole drawing is Select all, Rotate, then Set Frame again. It sits on the Selection bar, and its key is `Shift+R`. Row progress doesn't lock it (ticket 0647): on the Canvas it is always offered; in the Frame it shows disabled, saying "This Selection reaches into finished rows", where the Selection holds a bead of a finished pass, as the turn would leave those beads in place and draw their turned copies too (Select all with a Frame usually does), and `Shift+R` does nothing there.
_Avoid_: flip, spin, orientation

**Beads needed**:
The count (and Estimated weight) of beads per color inside the Frame only; with no Frame it says "Set Frame to count beads." Beads outside the Frame are never counted. A color whose swatch has a catalog code (see Palette) is listed with it, "Red · DB0723", on screen, in the print and in the exports, so it can be bought (ticket 0538).
_Avoid_: shopping list, bead count

**Estimated size**:
A Schema's width and height in mm/cm, worked out from its Schema size and Bead as one bead's size multiplied by the bead count, and never stored. Always presented as an estimate, since real work comes out a little different.
_Avoid_: real size, actual size, physical size

**Estimated weight**:
The grams of beads a Schema needs, per color and in total, shown in Beads needed: the bead count multiplied by an average weight of one bead of the Frame's Bead, derived on the spot and never stored. Always presented as an estimate (an info tooltip says so and shows the average used), since real beads vary by color and finish. Hidden for a Bead with no weight. The per-bead weights are provisional until a dealer confirms them (ticket 155).
_Avoid_: real weight, exact weight

**Remove row/column**:
A Tools-group tool, next to Eraser, that removes the specific row or column the Selection marks out, from any index, shifting the beads after it to close the gap, as one undo step. With a Frame it acts on the Frame and shrinks it by one; with no Frame it acts on the Piece area the line belongs to, which ends one line shorter, with a new empty line outside it. Beads outside the Frame or Piece area stay where they are. Enabled only when the Selection is exactly one whole row or column of the Frame or Piece area (a drag with the Select tool across the whole Frame or Piece area makes one; a ruler number no longer does, ticket 447). Once Row progress has a pass done it still works, keeping what is woven (ticket 0647, ADR 0055), with "row" and "column" worded the way the rows run, as the Progress bar's labels are: a line of a Piece area, or a Frame line that touches no finished pass, goes without asking; a finished row asks first ("Row 5 is already woven. Removing it moves Row progress back to row 12.", adding "The rows after it shift." on peyote and brick stitch) and moves the pointer back by that row's passes (one, or two for a later peyote row); a column across finished rows asks first ("This takes a bead out of each of the 12 woven rows.") and they stay finished. One Undo brings back the line and the pointer. Its key is `Shift+Del`, which asks the same way; plain `Del` only empties the Selection's beads and never changes the Frame's size.
_Avoid_: delete row, delete column, shrink

**Replace Bead**:
Swaps a layer's Bead for a different catalog entry, from that layer's Layer settings (the Project panel's Canvas section, the Frame panel; tickets 0670, 0687, ADR 0079), after a confirmation that gives the Bead's size in mm before and after and, for the Frame, its Estimated size; the Palette follows the woven layer's. The Schema size and every painted cell stay as they are, whichever unit the Project was created in — see ADR 0007. Each layer is drawn at its own Bead's real size.
_Avoid_: change bead, swap bead, resize pattern

**Custom color**:
A paint color chosen freely with the Custom color picker, a wheel, a brightness slider and a hex field the app draws inside the colour popover under the Custom button. The picker only previews while it is open; leaving it (a press outside, Escape or Enter) takes the color once: it becomes the paint color and joins the Palette as a new swatch (unless its hex is already a swatch; there is no cap), and from then on it is an ordinary Palette swatch. Pressing an existing Palette swatch instead picks that swatch and takes nothing, and a picker opened and left untouched takes nothing. Cells keep the hex, so a Project opens with its colors on a device that lacks the swatch.
_Avoid_: user color, extra palette color, one-off color

**Selection**:
Also called a **fragment** (ticket 0526): both words mean the same rectangle, and copy the person reads may use either, where fragment reads better (the rotate toast, "The turned fragment landed on top of existing beads."); code identifiers, test ids and the Select tool's name keep Selection. One rectangle of a Project's cells, or, after a Piece-tool tap, the exact shape of one Piece (ADR 0051; there is no Lasso, no Point tool and no Add / Subtract). Three things make it: dragging with the Select tool, tapping a bead with the Piece tool (exactly that bead's Piece: no empty cell, no hole and no bead of another Piece inside its bounding rectangle, so it is not rectangular unless the Piece is), or Select all (the Frame's inside when there is a Frame, else the rectangle round every placed bead). A drag, resize or move draws any rectangle, across the Frame's edge and its keep-out margin alike, and may begin in the margin, which is the Canvas's (ticket 0659); a Piece tap still keeps out of the margin (tickets 428, 433, 0501). Ruler numbers are display-only (ticket 447): a press on one selects nothing and does what the active tool does at that spot, and the number has no hover or pointer cue. It is drawn as its outer border in the Selection colour (traced round the painted cells for a shape, round each hole too). A rectangle has the Frame's eight handles (four larger ones on touch): dragging a handle resizes it, dragging inside moves the outline, and neither touches a bead. A shaped Selection has no handles and can't be resized or moved; a Select drag or another Piece tap replaces it. Its **Selection size** is its columns × rows in beads (the bounding rectangle's, for a shape), shown at the left of the Selection bar, with its measured size, "12×8 · 4.2 × 2.6 cm" (say "Selection size", not "dimensions"). It survives switching between Select and Piece; choosing Paint, Fill, the Eraser or Hand drops it (the beads stay); Clear selection (Del) empties every bead in it as one undo step. Exactly one is active at a time: a new drag or tap replaces the previous one, and leaving Select and Piece, switching or creating a Project, arming Paste (a Copy keeps it, ticket 415), or pressing Escape while nothing is copied clears it (an Escape with no Selection, paste preview or open disclosure left to dismiss switches to the Paint tool instead). It marks out cells, it does not change them — selecting never paints anything. Shift and Alt do nothing with a gesture. A Selection lives on one layer (ticket 491, ADR 0066): the one the drag or tap began on, drawn in that layer's geometry. Whatever its rectangle covers, it selects only what that layer shows (ticket 0659): begun in the Frame, only the Frame's cells; begun on the Canvas, only the Canvas clear of the Frame and its margin, so the Frame's cells, the Canvas beads it hides and any left in the margin are never part of a Canvas Selection. Cut, Copy, Fill, Clear selection and Rotate act on that layer only, and on a shape's own cells only (Fill recolours just the Piece's beads; Rotate turns the shape, which stays selected); Frame uses a shape's bounding rectangle.
_Avoid_: region, highlighted area, selected block, set of beads, Lasso

**Piece tool**:
The Tools-group tool that selects a whole Piece with one tap, no dragging (ticket 414; the polygon icon, key `6`). Acts on the Canvas for a Canvas bead and on the Frame for a Frame bead (ticket 491; Name Piece is the Canvas's only, Pieces being Canvas concepts). Tapping a bead selects every bead that touches it by a side or a corner (the rule that makes a Piece, like Fill's reach); the Selection is exactly that Piece's beads. Tapping empty space clears the Selection. With a Frame only the part of the Piece on the tapped bead's side counts (inside the Frame, or on the Canvas clear of its keep-out margin, ticket 433), trimmed to a legal shape; a tap on a bead in the margin is refused with the not-allowed cue; a tap on a bead with no Piece on that side changes nothing. Beads of other Pieces inside its bounding rectangle are not selected. Shares its Selection with Select: switching between the two keeps it.
_Avoid_: Lasso, Point tool, magic wand

**Select all**:
The Selection bar's first item (shown with the Select tool active, once a Selection exists) and Ctrl/⌘+A (ticket 428, ADR 0051; ticket 431 removed the Tool sheet tile and key `7`). The standalone Select all button at the canvas box's top-centre was removed with the Dock (ticket 439, ADR 0046): a touch-only user must first drag a Selection before the bar offers it (a Menu entry can be a later ticket). With a Frame it sets the Selection to the Frame's inside, on the Frame layer (always allowed, ticket 433); without one, to the bounding rectangle of every placed bead, on the Canvas layer (the box Fit to drawing finds). It makes Select the active tool; with no Frame and an empty canvas it does nothing.
_Avoid_: select everything

**Copy**:
Copies only the Selection's drawn beads (from its layer) into an in-session clipboard as bare row, column and colour, trimmed to their bounding box (empty cells inside stay holes), and arms paste mode at once (ticket 437, ADR 0016): the Selection is cleared, the tool becomes Select and the first drawn bead (leftmost of the top row) is the anchor a stamp lands on the pointer's bead. A Selection with no drawn bead copies nothing and shows a Message. Cut arms paste mode the same way (ticket 447). The clipboard is an editing-session aid like the undo stack, never saved with the Project, but its own lifecycle is deliberately looser (see ADR 0016): it clears only when a new Copy replaces it or a new Selection is made, and survives a Project switch, a tool switch, and leaving paste mode.
_Avoid_: duplicate, clone

**Cut**:
Copy, then empty the Selection's beads, as one undo step (Ctrl/Cmd+X, the Selection bar's scissors; tickets 415, 447). Then, as Copy does, the Selection is cleared, the tool becomes Select and paste mode is on, so hovering previews the cut piece and a click stamps it. Acts on the Selection's layer and honours the Row progress lock and the keep-out margin like Clear selection.
_Avoid_: remove, move

**Fill tool**:
The Tools-group tool that floods a region with the current color in one press (ticket 25; key `2`), as one Undo step: the pressed bead and every bead of its color joined to it, or the empty positions joined to an empty one, as the layer's Weave joins them. Inside the Frame it stops at the Frame's edge; on the Canvas it keeps to **Fill reach**. Not the Selection bar's Fill, which fills a chosen rectangle.
_Avoid_: bucket, flood fill

**Fill reach**:
How far a Fill tool press on the Canvas may go (ticket 0715, ADR 0026; decided in the grilling session of 2026-10-30). Through beads: the same-colored beads joined to the pressed one as far as they go, on screen or off. Into empty space: only an enclosed hole, the empty region joined to the press as long as it stays on screen (the positions the renderer counts as in view at the press); a region that reaches the screen's edge is open space, and the press is refused with the toast "Nothing encloses this spot. Select an area to fill it." Under the **Fill cap**: a press that would paint more than 250,000 positions (about a 500 × 500 patch) is refused with "Too big to fill. Zoom in, or select an area." A refused press changes nothing, shows the not-allowed pointer for a moment, and has no Undo step and no Mirror counterparts. Inside the Frame none of this applies: its edge bounds the Fill, with no cap. To fill open space, select an area and use the Selection bar's Fill.
_Avoid_: fill limit, fill bound

**Fill (Selection)**:
The Selection bar's Fill: every position in the Selection, empty ones too, takes the current color on the Selection's layer (inside the Frame, only its own cells), as one undo step; the keep-out margin and finished rows trim it silently, as Paste (ticket 415). Not the Fill tool, which floods a region.
_Avoid_: paint selection

**Clear selection**:
Empties every bead of the Selection's layer in it as one undo step (Del, the Selection bar's last button; ticket 415). If the Selection holds every placed bead (Select all), it opens the Delete all confirmation and runs Delete all instead, so Row progress resets and no finished row is left behind. Not Clear, the Project panel's whole-diagram wipe, though both end in the same confirmation when everything is selected.
_Avoid_: delete selection, empty selection

**Frame (from Selection)**:
The Selection bar's Frame: offered only when the Project has no Frame; makes the Frame the Selection's rectangle and lifts the Canvas beads inside it into the Frame, as one undo step; a named Piece it takes whole gives the Frame its name (tickets 415, 489, ADR 0066; Edit Frame follows in 417). Where the weave snaps the rectangle outward (a start on an unshifted row, whole cycles), one toast says how many empty rows or columns each edge gained and why, with no Undo; nothing is said when nothing moved (ticket 570).

**Paste**:
Paste mode (ADR 0016) is on right after Copy or Cut: a click, or a click-and-drag (one stamp at the press point, never a Selection), stamps the copied block with its anchor bead on the clicked cell, as one undo step, and can be repeated until the clipboard is replaced or cleared; Ctrl/Cmd+V stamps at the cell under the pointer from any tool and re-arms paste mode. A stamp lands in the geometry of the layer under the pointer, Canvas to Frame and back alike (ticket 491); one landing in the Frame is clipped to it, and one Message says how many beads were clipped. The Canvas has no edge to clip to, and the block's empty cells are holes: they leave the destination's own color alone. While paste mode is on, hovering previews the block where it would land, and a click stamps it as a single undo step. Select all is hidden. While the clipboard holds something there are two ways back in that stamp nothing (ticket 447): a **Paste** icon in the Selection bar, after Cut, and a **Paste** tool in the Tool pill right after Mirror (key `8`, ADR 0084); both only enter paste mode, and the tool shows as the lit one while it is on. They are absent while the clipboard is empty. Escape or another tool leaves paste mode and keeps the clipboard (on touch also Undo or a second tap on Select); a new Copy or Ctrl/Cmd+V re-arms it, and Shift+arrow leaves it and makes that Selection.
_Avoid_: place, insert, apply

**Mirror**:
The live drawing aid that lands every drawing gesture (a Paint or Eraser stroke, a Fill, a Paste) at its counterparts under the current Axes, inside its Area, as one Undo step (ADR 0082; decided in the grilling sessions of 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-31; it returns what ADR 0069 retired, in a new home). **There is one Mirror per Project**, saved with it, off by default, and making one always replaces the one before. Three ways make it: the Mirror tile (key `7`, its place in the pill, ADR 0084) and a drag on the Canvas; the **whole-Frame Mirror** icon on the Frame's margin; or a Selection and the Selection bar's Mirror. The tile, right after Piece, enters **Mirror mode**'s pick: the Select tool's own gesture and outline (one shared gesture), and the drag's rectangle is the Area at once, with the Mirror bar to set it up; a drag that begins in the Frame or its keep-out margin is refused with the not-allowed cue, the Frame mirroring whole or not at all. The tile is lit while the Mirror is on; a Paint, Fill, Eraser or Paste press (or Escape, which falls to Paint) leaves the pick and the Mirror stays on. Not a Tool: Paint, Eraser, Fill and Paste stay the active tool and simply land everywhere at once, and the hover preview shows every counterpart. A counterpart stays on the stroke's layer, and one that would land outside the Area, in the keep-out margin, on a Seam or Off-shape, or in a finished row is dropped silently, as Paste drops; a gesture outside the Area is not mirrored. The Area's outline, the Axis lines (thin, dashed, in the Selection colour) and the Radial centre (a small ring) show whatever tool is active, are not hidden by the Rulers toggle, and never appear in exports. Close mirror (on the Mirror bar) turns it off and drops its Area; the Mode, Axes and counts are kept for the next one. Setting, moving, resizing and closing the Area are one Undo step each; the bar's settings are not Undo steps. An older file with a Canvas Area opens with the Mirror there, else with a Frame and Mirror on as the whole-Frame Mirror, else off.
_Avoid_: mirror tool, symmetry, symmetry tool, kaleidoscope (Kaleidocycle is a Frame shape), copy mode, Mirror current, Mirror popover (retired names)

**Mirror bar**:
The Mirror's own bar (ContextBar card): the Selection bar's component with Mirror's items, in order the Area's size ("12×8 · 4.2 × 2.6 cm"), the Mirror mode (Mirror | Repeat), the Axis chips, the Steppers that apply and Close mirror. It shows whenever the Mirror is on, whatever tool is active: 6px above a Canvas Area (the Selection bar's placement), above the margin's top band for the whole-Frame Mirror. Like the Selection bar it hides during a gesture, pan or pinch and shrinks to its size readout while the Area is resized; while a Selection exists it gives way to the Selection bar (only one bar is on screen) and comes back when the Selection clears. It holds everything the retired Mirror popover held.
_Avoid_: Mirror popover, Mirror panel, mirror toolbar

**Mirror mode**:
How the Mirror lays a counterpart: **Mirror** (the default), a reflection (A | A′), or **Repeat**, the same way round (A | A). One switch for every Axis, Radial included: Mirror with Radial is a kaleidoscope, Repeat with Radial a plain turn. A segmented control on the Mirror bar; it has no Off position, Close mirror being the way out. Also the name of the tile's pick, the state where a drag makes the Area.
_Avoid_: copy mode, flip mode, symmetry kind

**Axis**:
What the Mirror maps across: **Left–right** (a vertical line), **Top–bottom** (a horizontal line), or **Radial** (a centre with a **Fold**). Toggle chips on the Mirror bar: the two straight ones may be on together, Radial only alone (Radial on turns the straight Axes off and remembers them, off brings them back). A straight Axis runs through the Area's centre and a direction may have several, its **Axis count** (N axes cutting the Area into N+1 equal strips, 1 to the Area's beads across that direction minus 1, an axis through a bead's middle mirroring it onto itself); resizing the Area smaller clamps a count that no longer fits, and a direction one bead across is off with its reason. Radial's centre is the Area's centre, the overlay drawing it as a small ring, in Mirror mode with the Fold's mirror lines through it (the first vertical and full, the others soft) and in Repeat mode with its sector rays. Fold is Radial's image count, on a grid **2 or 4 only**, the two turns a grid takes exactly (a half turn, a quarter turn): the counterpart is found by row and column about the centre as Rotate turns a fragment (ADR 0052), so on peyote and brick it keeps its index and takes the grid's half-bead shift, the same by count though not by look. A quarter turn needs a square Area: in one that is not square (a rectangle on the Canvas or the whole Frame) Fold 4 is off with its reason, and an Area made or resized out of square clamps a Fold 4 to 2, as it clamps a count; any other Fold that comes to a grid (a round shape's side count, an older file) is taken as 4. Any other count would leave the nearest bead to take a turned centre, which does not map back (drawing A lands on B, drawing B does not land on A), so a grid never offers one; six or eight sides are a round Custom frame's. On a Custom frame (Triangle, Warped Square, Star, Kaleidocycle, Kite, Fringe earring) the whole-Frame Mirror's Axes are the shape's own symmetry (`LayoutSymmetry`: its fold and its first mirror line, turned with Rotate Frame), set when the Mirror goes there and not editable: a round shape Radial with its side count as the Fold, a Kite or Fringe earring its one straight line; every counterpart is geometric, the bead's centre reflected or turned about the layout's centre and the bead under that point taking it (`beadUnder`), a Seam or Off-shape dropped silently. The Mirror bar then shows the Mode and a note naming the Axis. A Mirror moved to another Area keeps its Mode and Axes, its counts clamped; moved off a Custom frame it takes Left–right.
_Avoid_: mirror line, guide, symmetry line, strips (for the setting), sectors (for the setting), Axis handle (retired)

**Area**:
The rectangle the Mirror's Axes run inside, and the only place it mirrors: a rectangle on the Canvas, made by a drag in Mirror mode or from a Selection (the Selection bar's Mirror), or the **whole Frame**, from the Frame margin's whole-Frame Mirror icon; the Frame never holds a smaller one. Drawn as the Select tool's Selection (the 1.5px marker rectangle with eight handles, which resize and move it), though it is not one. One per Project, as the Mirror is; a new one replaces it. The whole-Frame Mirror icon (the `mirror` icon, left of Rotate frame in the margin band, in its `selected` yellow while on, with its Tooltip; a checked item in the Frame tools menu when the band folds) moves the Mirror to the whole Frame, dropping a Canvas Area, enters Mirror mode and shows the Mirror bar; pressed again it turns the Mirror off.
_Avoid_: selection (for it), region, zone, mirror box, Select area, Clear the area (retired)

**Mirror (Selection bar)**:
The Selection bar's command, after Rotate, always shown: it makes the Selection's rectangle (a shape's bounding rectangle) the Mirror's Area, replacing any Mirror, clears the Selection and shows the Mirror bar, exactly as a drag in Mirror mode does. Disabled on a Frame Selection, with its reason (the Frame mirrors whole, from its margin). The one-shot copy of a fragment to its counterparts it used to be (ticket 0707, `mirrorLayerSelection`) is retired.
_Avoid_: Flip, mirror selection, reflect

**Undo**:
Steps the grid back to how it was just before the most recent step-worthy edit — a whole dragged Paint/erase stroke, a Fill, a Paste, a Frame change (Frame from Selection, an Edit Frame drag or stepper, a Move handle drag with its beads), Fit to drawing or Remove Frame (which brings the Frame back with its progress, and restores the Canvas beads it cleared), a Rotate, a Remove row/column, a Replace Bead, adding, removing or moving a Palette swatch — restoring it in full even where the edit has since been covered by Row progress's finished-row lock; Undo replays history rather than drawing, so the lock never blocks it. Row direction, moving the Row progress pointer, Select, and Copy are not edits and are never undo steps. An editing-session aid like the clipboard: never saved with the Project, and reset whenever the open Project changes. It reaches back as far as a memory cap allows (ticket 0677): past it the oldest steps are dropped, so a long session of whole-sheet Fills keeps its last few dozen and one of strokes its last thousands. Available anywhere in the editor via Ctrl/Cmd+Z, except while typing in a form field.
_Avoid_: revert, step back

**Redo**:
Steps forward through whatever Undo has stepped back from, re-applying each undone edit in order; Undo and Redo can be alternated freely without losing or duplicating a step. A new edit that actually changes the grid clears it — the same edits that count as an Undo step in the first place, so one that lands on nothing (e.g. aimed only at finished rows) leaves it alone. Reset alongside Undo whenever the open Project changes. Available anywhere in the editor via Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y, except while typing in a form field.
_Avoid_: repeat, step forward
