# Glossary: Canvas and Frame

The Project, the Canvas, the Frame, Pieces, zoom and the rulers. Part of the glossary split out of `CONTEXT.md` (ticket 380); the index is [README.md](README.md).

**Project**:
Everything saved under one name: one Canvas, the Frame (none or one) laid over it with its own beads, the Pieces and any unframed Schemas, plus a Technique for the Canvas and one for the Frame, the Bead, rotation, Row progress, the Project's name and its Maker's name. What a person creates, opens, saves, lists in the library, exports as a file and shares. A Project with no Frame has no framed Schema yet (ADR 0050, ticket 262).
_Avoid_: design, drawing, chart, pattern, schema (for the whole saved thing)

**Schema**:
A Piece that has a Frame or a name; the word for finished work. A **framed Schema** is the beads the Project's Frame holds, finished and ready for progress: what the PNG and PDF exports contain, what Beads needed counts, and what Row progress works on. It is the Frame's own, apart from the Canvas's Pieces (ADR 0066): a Frame made round a Piece lifts its beads out of the Canvas into the Frame, so no Piece is cut. An **unframed Schema** is a named Piece not yet framed; a Project holds any number of them. A Piece is named with **Name Piece** (ticket 406): select beads of exactly that Piece and choose Name in the selection bar or the Tool sheet; the name is optional text of at most 40 characters like the Maker's name, an empty name takes it off, and one Undo step covers either. The name is drawn under the Piece, kept with the Project (the project file, version 3, keeps it by the position of one bead of the Piece) and follows the Piece through edits: a named Piece that joins an unnamed one absorbs it; a bead that would join two named Pieces is refused and nothing is painted; a Piece that splits keeps its name on the larger part (more beads), the smaller part becoming a plain Piece; erasing every bead of a named Piece deletes its name; Rotate (of a Selection), Remove row/column and a change of Technique carry it along (a Technique change that joins two named Pieces keeps the name of the one with more beads). A named Schema is **framed** with the Selection bar's Frame: select the Piece (Piece tool), choose Frame, and the Frame snaps to its rectangle and takes the Piece's beads and its name (ticket 407, ADR 0066); doing the same on another named Schema moves the Frame there, and the Schema left behind goes back to the Canvas, named and unframed. A Frame that is removed leaves its beads and its name on the Canvas (ticket 497): the name stays on the Piece. A Frame is refused, with a Message naming the Schema, when it would cut through a named Piece, taking it whole or not at all, so a name is never cut; an unnamed Piece may still be cut. Setting a Frame over beads does not name them. A Frame still makes a framed Schema without a name (ADR 0050, ticket 404). The Frame's name can also be typed, changed or cleared in the Frame panel (ticket 555, one Undo step, allowed after Row progress has started); it is the same name, drawn under the Frame, and it is not used in the export file names. The code has no `Schema` type: a framed one is the Frame's beads (`beadsInFrame`), an unframed one is `namedPieces` (`domain/pieceNames.ts`). The Homepage says Schema only for this, never for the whole Canvas.
_Avoid_: pattern, chart (all replaced by Schema, ADR 0050); for the whole saved thing, which is a Project; for an unnamed Piece with no Frame

**Canvas**:
The open area a Project is drawn on: an endless field of bead positions with no board and no fixed size (see ADR 0026), laid out on the Technique's Grid. A Project has exactly one. Positions are addressed by row and column, negative included, and only painted ones are stored. The person draws anywhere, and moves around with the Hand tool, the wheel, two fingers or Space + drag.
_Avoid_: open canvas, board, infinite grid, sheet

**Grid**:
The endless arrangement of positions on the Canvas set by the Technique: loom and square stitch rows stack, peyote rows nest, brick stitch rows are offset by half a bead, a bead apart, in blocks of one or more rows (Brick block). It has no edge and no size; the Frame, not the Grid, gives a Schema its size.
_Avoid_: board, for the beads themselves or for the Schema

**Position marks**:
How the Grid's positions are drawn on the Canvas: as squares (one shared 1px line in the gap between neighbouring positions, shaped like the Technique's bead, and round every position of the Canvas, a drawn one and one inside the Frame included, ticket 0458). The Dots style and the choice between styles were removed (ticket 0545). Inside the Frame an empty position is an empty bead instead, over whatever the Canvas holds there; the keep-out margin has none. Never in exports.
_Avoid_: grid dots, background dots

**Piece**:
A small, independent set of beads that touch by a side or a corner, drawn on the Canvas. Pieces form, merge and split as beads are painted and erased. Each Piece belongs to exactly one Piece area. A Piece that has a Frame or a name is a Schema.
_Avoid_: island, cluster, group, shape, fragment (a fragment is a Selection: a rectangle that may hold beads of several Pieces)

**Piece area**:
The rectangle around one or more Pieces, with, while there is no Frame, its own rulers. Each Piece's area counts as everything within one bead outside its own bounds; Piece areas whose rectangles overlap, lie inside or touch once that margin is included join, repeated until none do, so Pieces with one empty bead between them share one rectangle and one set of rulers while two or more empty beads keep them apart, and a large Piece never shows smaller Pieces' rectangles inside or against its own. The margin only decides what joins; the drawn rectangle stays around the beads. A Piece area shows its rectangle and rulers only when its joined rectangle spans more than 4 beads in total (width times height, so a single row of 5 counts and 2x2 does not); smaller ones are not drawn, and beads, Pieces, Export and Beads needed are unchanged. Forms, merges and splits as beads are painted and erased. With no Frame, a whole row or column of one can be selected by a Select-tool drag across it and removed with Remove row/column. The Rulers toggle shows or hides it; the Frame is never hidden by it.
_Avoid_: group, cluster, island

**Frame**:
The one small sheet per Canvas, on whole Canvas positions, that holds the beads of the Schema (ADR 0066). It owns its beads, counted from its own first row and column, and its own Technique and Brick block (ticket 492): it is drawn, hit, ruled, counted, exported and printed in them, from the corner of the Canvas position it lies on, whatever the Canvas is woven in. Its first row is always an unshifted one, so it moves by whole Canvas positions and its top edge resizes by a whole cycle of its own rows. It is opaque: what the Canvas holds under it is hidden, not changed or deleted, and shows again when the Frame moves off it. A press inside it paints, erases and fills the Frame, a press outside does the same to the Canvas, and a stroke stays on the layer it began on. Plus a **keep-out margin** (below): drawing on the Canvas stays possible beyond the margin and is saved with the Project, but Export, Beads needed and Row progress read only the Frame's beads. The first Frame set on a Project turns Row progress on by itself; the Frame then stays resizable until a pass is done, and its size is held still from then until Row progress is turned off or the Project is cleared (ticket 411); it can still be moved, with its beads and the pointer, by the Move handle (ticket 0452, ADR 0054). With a Frame the Schema has a size (the Schema size) . Made, moved, resized and removed as Undo steps. Frame from Selection and Fit to drawing lift the Canvas beads inside the new Frame into it (one step; a named Piece's name goes with them); the Move handle carries the Frame's beads and leaves the Canvas as it is; resizing never changes a bead's color and never removes one. Woven in the same Weave as the Canvas (Technique and Brick block alike), a Frame made smaller lays the beads it gives up on the Canvas where they stand (ticket 513), over what the Canvas holds there, which shows again where the Frame had no bead; one grown over Canvas beads lifts them in, so a Piece dragged out of the Frame and back is whole again, and it takes a named Piece whole or not at all, stopping at its edge until it covers it (a Message names it), while a named Piece it already hides part of stays on the Canvas, whole and hidden. Woven otherwise, a Frame made smaller keeps what it gives up as Folded beads and one grown back shows them again, its own Folded bead winning where the Canvas has one too; the Canvas beads it grows over are hidden, never removed. By handle drag this is done once, on release, and one Undo restores the Frame, its beads and the Canvas and Remove Frame removes only the Frame: its footprint on the Canvas is cleared and the Frame's beads are dropped in, in the Canvas's Technique and geometry cell by cell like Paste, so what was drawn under the Frame is gone and the picture looks as the Frame did (it asks for confirmation only when a Row progress pass is done, since progress goes with the Frame), and one Undo brings the Frame back. Resized from the Frame row or sheet, the size is applied once: each press of Width or Height shows on the canvas but is not committed until the row or sheet closes (or another command reads the Frame), as one Undo step. A Project from before the Frame opens with a Frame the size of its old grid. **Footprint** (ticket 514, ADR 0066): the Canvas positions the Frame hides, takes presses from and clears on Remove Frame, which are its own rectangle where Canvas and Frame are woven alike and otherwise the rows whose middle lies inside what it draws (`frameFootprint`): a peyote Frame draws shorter than the loom rows it names, a loom Frame on a peyote Canvas draws taller. The rows it names but does not cover are ordinary Canvas, drawn, erased and marked like the rest, and share their position with the Frame's own last rows, so a press names its layer as well as its position (`pointToHit`). Remove Frame clears the footprint, then lays the Frame's beads at their own row and column; a Canvas bead in the strip stays unless a Frame bead lands on it.
_Avoid_: border, crop, artboard, page, grid size; in code, Frame for any other rectangle of positions, which is a `Rect` (ticket 0617): the Frame itself is `FrameSheet`

**Layer**:
The Canvas or the Frame: a set of beads woven in one Weave. A Project has one or two, and a press, a stroke, a Selection or a paste acts on one layer at a time. Its **Layer geometry** (ADR 0078, ticket 0628) answers where its beads are, whatever lays them out: its positions, whether a position holds a bead, the beads touching one, where a bead lies and at what angle, which bead a point is on, the box round its edge, which beads a view of part of it draws, whether its beads keep rows and columns (which rulers number), and its Seams. A grid's answers come from its Weave (the Canvas, open, or a Rectangular Frame), a Custom frame's from its layout. Editing, drawing, the hit test, the exports and a Starter's bead count ask it, never whether the Frame has a layout (tickets 0629, 0630, 0632).
_Avoid_: sheet, level

**Folded beads**:
The Frame's own beads beyond its edge: what a Frame woven unlike the Canvas, or a Custom frame, gives up when it is made smaller, kept with the Frame instead of dropped onto the Canvas, and shown again when it grows back over them. Saved with the Project and carried by every Frame command (moved, turned, re-woven and shifted with the Frame's other beads; Remove row/column takes the whole line, its Folded beads too), they are not drawn, exported, counted or woven; Remove Frame, or a Frame made elsewhere, does with them what it does with the Frame's other beads.
_Avoid_: hidden beads, trimmed beads, offcut (and Off-shape, which is a part of a Custom frame with no positions)

**Keep-out margin**:
The 6 bead positions all the way round a set Frame's footprint, outside its line (tickets 261, 0501, 514, ADR 0066: it hangs off what the Frame draws, so it is joined to it in every Technique; 3 positions until ticket 427, and removed by ticket 0490 until 0501 brought it back), drawn flat: a gap in the Position marks (Squares' lines stop at its edge, and run round the Frame's own positions), with a dashed outline that is always shown with the Frame, and that also flashes for 1s after a press in it is refused; the pointer shows not-allowed there. Nothing can be drawn there: Paint, Fill, Paste and Rotate all leave it empty (a stroke across it paints only the beads outside it, still one Undo step); Erase still works on anything. It cannot be selected either (tickets 428, 433): a Selection is wholly inside the Frame, wholly clear of the margin, or round the Frame and its margin whole, never anything else, whichever way it is made (one rule, `selectionAllowed`); a drag cannot start in it and is clamped when it ends there, Shift+arrow stops one cell before it, a ruler number selects only the Frame's part of its line, and a Piece tap selects only the tapped bead's side. Beads held in a margin are out of reach of every Selection except one round the whole Frame (so Cut, Clear selection and Copy touch them only then, while Rotate still leaves the margin empty), while Erase still clears them. A Canvas bead that lands in the margin when the Frame is made or resized stays on the Canvas, covered by the margin (not drawn there, not counted), and shows again if the Frame later moves off it or is resized. Moving the Frame carries its own beads: beads held in the margin stay where they are, and whatever is under the destination stays where it is on the Canvas, hidden under the Frame and there again as soon as the Frame no longer lies over it (a move off, Remove Frame, a resize); a named Piece under the Frame keeps its name. The margin is 6 positions at every zoom (ticket 459 grew it when zoomed out for the corner controls; ticket 473 took those controls out of it). It lies on the Canvas round the Frame's sheet and counts the Canvas's positions; a Frame in another Technique does not change it. Removing the Frame removes the margin. Export, Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame's beads only, so they ignore the Canvas. Designed to become 4 positions drawn as a **Margin sheet** (ticket 0655, built by tickets 0656 to 0658): see below.

**Margin sheet** (ticket 0655; MarginSheet card):
The keep-out margin drawn as a sheet of drafting paper: 4 bead positions on every side, filled with the margin color (Auto: the Canvas color one step off; or one picked with the background color picker, remembered on the device), with an inner border. Its top band holds the Frame's Row progress, its Move bar and its tools; its bottom-right corner holds the **title block**: the Frame's name, Technique and maker's name, its sizes, the lettered color legend and the Letters switch. It has a full, a compact and a folded form, chosen by how many screen pixels the band has at the zoom. Never in exports.
_Avoid_: border, paper margin, frame card

**Custom frame** (spec 0590; Triangle and Warped Square, ticket 0592; Star, Kaleidocycle and Kite, ticket 0593; Selection, Rotate Frame, exports and Row progress, ticket 0594):
A Frame that is not a rectangle on one Grid: a fixed layout of bead positions given by its kind and its own settings (how many rows or rounds, whatever that kind needs), made of parts woven at different angles or in different Techniques, as a maker beads it. The first kinds are the five geometric peyote shapes: Warped Square, Triangle, Kite, Star and Kaleidocycle (Triangle and Warped Square worked in rounds from the centre, sized by Rounds; the Kite two peyote wings on a herringbone spine, worked in rows from its point, sized by Rows; the Star and Kaleidocycle drawn as their finished face but made of parts (the Star five warped squares, the Kaleidocycle twenty-four triangles, of which its face shows six), sized by Part size, each panel knowing its part and making order); the **Fringe earring** is one too: a brick-stitch top with hanging strands, its settings Top width, Top rows, Fringe style (straight, V, later looped) and Fringe length. It is still the Project's one Frame, set, added and replaced like any Frame, and its kind and settings are saved with the Project. It is built of **panels**: small sheets, each in one Technique at its own angle and place, built by the kind's rule from its settings (the Star five diamonds at 72°, the Kaleidocycle six triangles round a hole, a fringe earring a brick top and its strands); a bead belongs to exactly one panel. Its footprint is its bounding rectangle, as for any Frame, with the keep-out margin round it; inside that rectangle, the **Seams** and the **Off-shape** area hold no positions and cannot be drawn on (a refused press flashes the outline, as on the keep-out margin). Its settings can be changed later in the Frame panel (shrinking keeps what it gives up as Folded beads, without asking, and growing shows them again or adds empty rounds or rows; the steppers commit once, as one Undo step, when the Frame panel closes), held still once a Row progress pass is done. Every tool works on it except the resize handles, Remove row/column and Frame from Selection. A Selection over it is a rectangle on screen holding every bead whose centre lies inside it, from any panel; Paste and Rotate put each bead on the nearest free position of the panel under it, at that panel's angle, and a bead that lands on a Seam or Off-shape is dropped, a Message saying how many. Rotate Frame turns the whole layout a quarter turn, beads included. What Remove Frame does with the beads is a property of the Frame's kind: a rectangular Frame drops them onto the Canvas, a Custom frame removes them with itself (asking first when it shows beads, its Folded beads going without a word; one Undo brings it back). A plain rectangular Frame in one Technique is not a Custom frame: the open Canvas makes that.
_Avoid_: Shaped Frame, shape, expert mode, mixed technique (for the Frame itself), template

**Frame kind** (ticket 0591, ADR 0077):
What sort of Frame it is: **Rectangular** (the Frame as it has always been) or one of the Custom frame kinds (Warped Square, Triangle, Kite, Star, Kaleidocycle, Fringe earring; Medallion later). One entry in a typed registry, like the Starters', that holds everything that differs by kind: its settings, how its panels are built, its Layer geometry and Estimated size, which tools it allows, what Remove Frame does with its beads, its Row order and how it is exported. Code asks the Frame's kind rather than testing for it. A new Custom frame shape is its layout builder, its entry, a golden layout and its words (ADR 0078, ticket 0632). Saved with the Project; a Frame with none, as a Rectangular one is saved, reads as Rectangular.
_Avoid_: frame type, shape, template

**Seam** (ticket 0592):
The line between two panels of a Custom frame: no bead position, cannot be drawn on, and drawn as a thin line so the panels read apart.
_Avoid_: gap, border, margin (the keep-out margin is outside the Frame)

**Off-shape** (ticket 0592):
The part of a Custom frame's bounding rectangle outside its shape (between a Star's points, round a Triangle): no bead position, cannot be drawn on, drawn in the Canvas color with no position marks. It is inside the Frame, so it hides the Canvas under it, unlike the keep-out margin round the Frame.
_Avoid_: margin, keep-out, empty area

**Edit Frame**:
The Frame's edit toggle (tickets 0452, 0473, 0510, ADR 0054). The **edit pencil** in the Frame's gutter, above its top-right corner (32px chip, 44px hit, never scaling with the zoom; kept inside the canvas box, flipping to below the Frame when there is no room above), shows or hides the eight resize handles (four on touch), each a small square in the gutter just outside the Frame's edge at a fixed pixel size at every zoom, flipping to the inside of an edge that has no room outside it in the canvas box; a press on a handle wins over the tool's press there. It does not open the **Frame panel**, which is a separate panel opened by the **Frame** button in the Canvas strip alone. The handles are hidden whenever a Project opens or the Frame is removed, and Frame from Selection leaves them hidden; the choice is kept for the session only. Tools work inside the Frame as usual, since the handles sit in the gutter, outside the Frame and its keep-out margin. Once Row progress has started the pencil shows no handles, while the Frame button still opens the bar, which then holds the size and Remove Frame alone (ticket 0475). The rulers and the dashed margin outline are always shown; the size tooltip shows only while dragging or stepping. A Selection never shows rulers or a margin outline; the pencil, **Remove Frame** (a chip right after the pencil, the same command and confirmation as the bar's, ticket 0498) and the Move handle stay with every tool, with a Selection and after Row progress has started, and where a Selection's handles overlap one of them the Selection's handle wins the press.
_Avoid_: Set Frame (the old mode, which also drew a Frame by dragging), crop, resize, set size, Edit Frame mode

**Frame panel**:
The panel Edit Frame opens (tickets 0473, 0475), in the same shell as the Menu, Project and Export panels and one of the four at a time: Escape or a press outside closes it. It hangs from the Frame button in the Canvas strip as a dropdown on every screen size (ADR 0046), and opens without focusing a field, so a tablet's keyboard stays down until a field is tapped (ticket 0510). It is mounted with Menu and Project from the app shell (AppPanels), not from the canvas, so nothing in the canvas can move it (ticket 0511). Row 1: the size, a Width and a Height field (empty, the current size as placeholder; digits only) that grow or shrinks both sides evenly about the Frame (the odd bead going right or down; on peyote and brick stitch the top edge moves by pairs of rows), Rotate Frame and Remove Frame (always asks first). Row 2: Top, Bottom, Left and Right cells, each a − and + pair of 48px buttons: one bead per press (a pair of rows on the top edge where rows alternate), hold to repeat, one Undo step, down to four beads. Its Width and Height fields and its Top, Bottom, Left and Right − buttons never delete beads: what a smaller Frame gives up drops onto the Canvas (ticket 513) or, woven unlike the Canvas, is kept as Folded beads. From ticket 568 its first section is **Export** (Picture and Printable chart), always shown and off while the Project has no Frame, with a hint to set one, and a **Select a frame** row (with or without a Frame) makes Select the tool so that the next drag becomes the Frame on release, Escape cancelling.
_Avoid_: Frame toolbar, Frame sheet

_Avoid_: Set Frame (the old mode, which also drew a Frame by dragging), crop, resize, set size, Edit Frame mode

**Export panel**:
The small panel the Canvas strip's **Export** button opens (the `share` icon, the strip's last button; ticket 0652), in the same shell as the Menu, Project and Frame panels and one of the four at a time. With a Frame it holds the Frame panel's Export section (Picture, Printable chart and Export quality), the same component; with no Frame it says to set a frame and offers Select a frame and Fit a frame to the drawing. Letters stays in the Frame panel.
_Avoid_: Share panel (it only exports files), Export menu

**Rotate Frame**:
A button in the Frame panel, before Remove Frame (spec 0470). Turns the Frame and its own beads a quarter turn clockwise about the Frame's centre, within the Frame's own geometry (ADR 0066); its Width and Height swap. The Canvas under it is not touched, so nothing is covered and no Message is shown; the top row snaps to the start of a cycle of rows on peyote and brick stitch, one Undo step. Row progress turns with it (ticket 0647, ADR 0052): each Row direction's pointer and the current direction move a quarter turn clockwise, so five rows done from the top become five columns done from the right and the same beads stay finished; a Custom frame's order turns with its layout, so its pointer stays. Nothing is lost, so it never asks, and one Undo brings back the Frame and its pointers. The bar stays as it was. It shares its icon with Rotate and has no key.
_Avoid_: Frame turn (the ADR 0026 Frame turn that moved Pieces)

**Rotate canvas**:
The Canvas strip button before the Rulers toggle (spec 0470, planned). A quarter turn clockwise of the whole picture, like turning a real canvas: only the Project's rotation changes, no bead, Frame, name or margin moves, so peyote and brick stitch turn without any row snapping. A view setting like Row direction: saved with the Project, not an Undo step, not in exports, not blocked by Row progress, and independent of Row direction. Its icon is the Rotate icon with a small canvas badge; no key.
_Avoid_: Rotate view, turn picture

**Move handle**:
The Frame's move grip (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473, ADR 0066): a horizontal line in the Frame's gutter above its top edge (below it when there is no room above), always there, handles on or off, also after Row progress has started. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads (ADR 0054): while it is dragged the beads travel with the Frame on screen, not only its outline; the Canvas under the destination is hidden, not cleared, and shows again when the Frame moves off it, the move is not limited, and while the pointer touches the canvas box's edge the view scrolls that way, so the Frame stays in hand; it goes by a pair of rows on peyote and brick stitch, and the Row progress pointer travels with the Frame. One announced Undo step; arrow keys move it a bead and Shift + arrows resize, while it has focus. It sits in the **gutter**, a band just outside the Frame's edge measured in screen pixels, so it keeps its size and its distance from the edge at every zoom; it is kept inside the canvas box (flipping to below the Frame when there is no room above), slides clear of the pencil, and wins over a tool's press in its hit box. Resizing never touches a bead.
_Avoid_: Frame drag, drag inside the Frame

**Hand tool**:
The tool (5) that moves the view by dragging and never changes a bead. Space + drag, the middle mouse button and the right mouse button (ticket 541) do the same from any tool; a right-button press that does not move the Canvas does nothing.
_Avoid_: pan mode, grab tool

**Pen mode** / **Mouse mode**:
The input mode: which pointer draws with the current Tool and which one moves the canvas. In Pen mode the pen draws, and a finger or mouse drags the canvas like the Hand tool, so a palm resting on an iPad does not paint. In Mouse mode (the default) a finger or mouse draws and the pen drags the canvas. Told apart by each event's pointer type, not by the Tool; two-finger pinch, the wheel and Space + drag work in both. One toggle picks it (the Tool pill's last button), offered only once a pen has been seen (the first pen event of a visit, kept on the device), and that first pen switches to Pen mode unless a mode was chosen before; the choice is kept on the device.
_Avoid_: stylus mode, touch mode, palm rejection

**Rulers toggle**:
The button (and R, and the Canvas strip's Rulers button) that shows or hides every ruler and size marking on the canvas, and the Piece areas' rectangles — each Piece area's, the Frame's and the Frame's size tooltip while it is set — kept as a preference on the device like the theme. Rulers show column numbers above and row numbers left of each Piece area, from 1, or the Frame's on all four sides once a Frame is set.
_Avoid_: ruler switch, numbers toggle

**Canvas zoom**:
The zoom the app's own controls and gestures change (zoom buttons, pinch and Ctrl/⌘ + wheel over the canvas, Ctrl/⌘ + plus, minus and 0), applied to the canvas alone. It is the only zoom there is: the Dock and every sheet keep their size (ADR 0034).
_Avoid_: scale, magnify, page zoom (a different thing)

**Page zoom**:
The browser's own whole-page zoom (pinch on the page, double-tap, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel outside the canvas, the browser's zoom menu). The app locks it so the chrome never changes size.
_Avoid_: UI scale, browser scale

**Zoom floor**:
The smallest zoom, 10%, the same on every screen: zoom out, pinch, wheel and Fit all stop there, so a Schema wider or taller than the screen can be seen whole. Fit never goes above 100%.
_Avoid_: min zoom, bead-min floor

**Ruler step**:
How many beads apart the rulers show a number, chosen from 5, 10, 50, 100 and so on as the smallest that leaves every number clear at the current zoom; the beads between are drawn as Ruler dots. The Ruler step applies from 50% zoom up; below 50% a ruler shows only its last number (the column count along the columns, the row count along the rows, on every Frame side and every Piece ruler), and the beads without a number keep their dots.
_Avoid_: label interval, tick spacing

**Ruler dot**:
The small mark a ruler draws for a bead that has no number at the current Ruler step; every 5th is a bolder dot, and, a dot, like a number, is display-only (ticket 447). At a bead pitch too small for a dot per bead, only the 5th-bead dots remain.
_Avoid_: tick, marker

**Ruler layout**:
Everything the rulers are for one view of the canvas, worked out once from the Project and the Surface view: the boxes that carry rulers, the numbers and Ruler dots to draw, and what a point of the viewport selects. The overlay draws it; ruler numbers are display-only (ticket 447), so nothing asks it what a press selects; the Ruler step is chosen once per axis per box inside it. The print rulers (fixed step, no dots) are not part of it.
_Avoid_: ruler view, ruler geometry

**Canvas color**:
The background of the drawing area, picked by the person in the Project panel (its Canvas section; the Homepage has none): five in light, five in dark, stored as a number so a change of theme keeps the position. Or the person's own Custom color (ticket 0653), from the Palette's Custom color picker: one color for both themes, remembered while a numbered one shows. A preference on the device like the theme: not saved with a Project and not in exports, which always print on the light board. High contrast has one white canvas, so its Canvas section has no swatches, only a line saying so.
_Avoid_: board color, background theme
