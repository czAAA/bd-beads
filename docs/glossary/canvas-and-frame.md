# Glossary: Canvas and Frame

The Project, the Canvas, the Frame, Pieces, zoom and the rulers. Part of the glossary split out of `CONTEXT.md` (ticket 380); the index is [README.md](README.md).

**Project**:
Everything saved under one name: its layers (one Canvas and up to four Frames laid over it, each with its own beads and Layer settings), the Pieces and any unframed Schemas, the Palette, the Project's name (the Canvas's too) and its Maker's name (ADR 0079). What a person creates, opens, saves, lists in the library, exports as a file and shares. A Project with no Frame has no framed Schema yet (ADR 0050, ticket 262).
_Avoid_: design, drawing, chart, pattern, schema (for the whole saved thing)

**Schema**:
A Piece that has a Frame or a name; the word for finished work. A **framed Schema** is the beads one of the Project's Frames holds, finished and ready for progress: what a PNG or PDF export contains (one Frame each), what Beads needed counts, and what Row progress works on. It is that Frame's own, apart from the Canvas's Pieces (ADR 0066): a Frame made round a Piece copies its beads into the Frame and the Canvas keeps the Piece (ticket 1215). An **unframed Schema** is a named Piece not yet framed; a Project holds any number of them. A Piece is named with **Name Piece** (ticket 406): select beads of exactly that Piece and choose Name in the selection bar or the Tool sheet; the name is optional text of at most 40 characters like the Maker's name, an empty name takes it off, and one Undo step covers either. The name is on a tag standing above the Piece's top-left corner, always that corner, and the Piece is outlined by a thin border (SchemaTag card, tickets 0721, 0733): the name is written on the tag, a long one ending in an ellipsis with its Tooltip; a click, tap, Enter or Space opens it into a paper tag with the name, the size in beads and mm, the bead and color counts, Rename (Name Piece's editor) and Frame (as the Selection bar's Frame). Tags are page markup over the canvas, the same size at every zoom, one Tab stop ("Schema names") after the canvas, and never in an export; a framed Schema and a plain Piece have none. The name is kept with the Project (the project file, version 3, keeps it by the position of one bead of the Piece) and follows the Piece through edits: a named Piece that joins an unnamed one absorbs it; a bead that would join two named Pieces is refused and nothing is painted; a Piece that splits keeps its name on the larger part (more beads), the smaller part becoming a plain Piece; erasing every bead of a named Piece deletes its name; Rotate (of a Selection), Remove row/column and a change of Technique carry it along (a Technique change that joins two named Pieces keeps the name of the one with more beads). A named Schema is **framed** with the Selection bar's Frame: select the Piece (Piece tool), choose Frame, and the Frame snaps to its rectangle and takes the Piece's beads and its name (ticket 407, ADR 0066); doing the same on another named Schema adds another Frame there, if it clears every other Frame's margin (ticket 1222). A Frame that is removed takes its beads and its name with it, leaving the Canvas as it was (ticket 1215). A Frame may cut through a named Piece, which stays whole and named on the Canvas under it. Setting a Frame over beads does not name them. A Frame still makes a framed Schema without a name (ADR 0050, ticket 404). The Frame's name can also be typed, changed or cleared on its chip in the margin sheet's band (ticket 555, one Undo step, allowed after Row progress has started; clearing it gives "Frame N" back, ticket 1222); it is the same name, shown in the Margin sheet's band (not drawn under the Frame, ticket 0719), it is the title printed on the PNG and PDF exports (an unnamed Frame's print the Project's name), and it is in the PNG and PDF file names, `{project}-{frame}-{maker}` (e.g. `summer-rose-ada.pdf`); the PDF's name adds an underscore and a word for each print switch that is on (`summer-rose-ada_large-beads.pdf`), shown as "Saves as" in the Export panel, whose pencil names one export by hand with no word added (ticket 1096), while the project file, holding the whole Project, is `{project}-{maker}.openbeads`; a part with nothing to write is left out (tickets 0708, 0720). The code has no `Schema` type: a framed one is the Frame's beads (`beadsInFrame`), an unframed one is `namedPieces` (`domain/pieceNames.ts`). The Homepage says Schema only for this, never for the whole Canvas.
_Avoid_: pattern, chart (all replaced by Schema, ADR 0050); for the whole saved thing, which is a Project; for an unnamed Piece with no Frame

**App page** (ADR 0085, ADR 0086, ticket 0756):
What fills the canvas box between the Canvas strip and the page's footer: the editor's Canvas, the Homepage, or Convert image. Every App page has the same three parts, the strip as its header (Menu always first), its content, and the Progress bar of steps as its footer (the editor's is empty since ticket 1223), so the box keeps its shape from page to page. The editor's side panels (Project, Export) show on the editor alone; Menu works on every page. A page that holds work may register a **leave guard**: a Menu action that leaves it (New Project, a pick from the Projects library, Import, Homepage) awaits the guard, which may ask the person first; a page with nothing to lose registers none, and leaving is at once. The browser's Back on a page other than the editor is that page's Close, and a reload or closing the tab asks first while the page holds a guard (ticket 0861). The Menu knows nothing of which page it is on. A page's content is never a modal, so it can open a confirmation.
_Avoid_: screen, view, layout

**Canvas**:
The open area a Project is drawn on: an endless field of bead positions with no board and no fixed size (see ADR 0026), laid out on its own Technique's Grid. The first layer, with every Layer setting of its own; a Project has exactly one, and its name is the Project's. Positions are addressed by row and column, negative included, and only painted ones are stored. The person draws anywhere, and moves around with the Hand tool, the wheel, two fingers or Space + drag. A Fill tool press on it keeps to **Fill reach** (editing.md): beads as far as they join, empty space only as a hole the screen holds whole, under the Fill cap.
_Avoid_: open canvas, board, infinite grid, sheet

**Grid**:
The endless arrangement of positions on the Canvas set by the Technique, each bead laid along its Thread direction and nothing nesting: loom rows and columns stack straight, peyote's columns are staggered (every other column half a bead down) and brick stitch's rows are (every other row half a bead sideways), both in blocks of one or more lines (Drop). It has no edge and no size; the Frame, not the Grid, gives a Schema its size.
_Avoid_: board, for the beads themselves or for the Schema

**Position marks**:
How the Grid's positions are drawn on the Canvas. In light and dark each empty position is a tonal tile, its cell 0.75px in with the Technique's corner, filled in the Background stepped 6.5 L* toward ink; the grid is the gap between two tiles, with no line (ticket 1001). Inside the Frame an empty position is the same tile from the Frame's own Background, with an edge 0.75px wide just inside it, the Background stepped twice as far toward ink, so a row of empty positions never reads as one bar; the keep-out margin has none. A shown reference image lies over the tiles and leaves them as they are (ticket 1008). High contrast and the draft look keep squares instead (one shared 1px line in the gap between neighbouring positions, shaped like the Technique's bead, round every position, ticket 0458), and inside the Frame an empty bead. The Dots style and the choice between styles were removed (ticket 0545). Never in exports. The editor also lays them, with the Background, under the drawing (ticket 0717), so while the canvas is panned or zoomed whatever the moving drawing uncovers shows empty Canvas, never a bare patch.
_Avoid_: grid dots, background dots

**Calm gap** (ticket 1007; BeadBoard card):
The gap between tonal tiles, one color everywhere on the Canvas: the Background moved 42% of the way to the tile's color (about 3.8 L*, 1.11:1), never the tile's color. Replaced Grid focus (ticket 1002), which eased the gap by distance from the work and had a toggle and `G`. Not in high contrast, the draft look, exports or over a reference image.
_Avoid_: grid focus, grid fade, vignette

**Piece**:
A small, independent set of beads that touch by a side or a corner, drawn on the Canvas. Pieces form, merge and split as beads are painted and erased. Each Piece belongs to exactly one Piece area. A Piece that has a Frame or a name is a Schema.
_Avoid_: island, cluster, group, shape, fragment (a fragment is a Selection: a rectangle that may hold beads of several Pieces)

**Piece area**:
The rectangle around one or more Pieces, with its own rulers (ticket 1012: with a Frame set too, the Canvas around it). Each Piece's area counts as everything within one bead outside its own bounds; Piece areas whose rectangles overlap, lie inside or touch once that margin is included join, repeated until none do, so Pieces with one empty bead between them share one rectangle and one set of rulers while two or more empty beads keep them apart, and a large Piece never shows smaller Pieces' rectangles inside or against its own. The margin only decides what joins; the rectangle stays around the beads. A Piece area shows its rectangle and rulers only when its joined rectangle spans more than 4 beads in total (width times height, so a single row of 5 counts and 2x2 does not); smaller ones are not drawn, and beads, Pieces, Export and Beads needed are unchanged. Forms, merges and splits as beads are painted and erased; the count follows each edit, working out again only the Piece areas it filled or emptied beads in, so an edit on a big layer costs what it touched (ticket 0731). With no Frame, a whole row or column of one can be selected by a Select-tool drag across it and removed with Remove row/column. The Rulers toggle shows or hides it (its rulers and its rectangle); the Frame's rulers and line are never hidden by it. A Canvas bead covered by the Frame or its Keep-out margin is not drawn and not counted, so it joins no Piece area and carries no ruler or rectangle: a Piece area is worked out from the beads that show. A layer too big to walk keeps its one box round every bead that shows until its Pieces are walked in idle time (ticket 0716), with a Frame set too.
_Avoid_: group, cluster, island

**Table** (spec 1182, ticket 1185; spec 1234):
The one plane pan and zoom move, that every layer lies on, measured in millimetres and belonging to no layer. The Canvas lies on it by an origin and a quarter turn; each Frame by its own centre and a quarter turn (0°, 90°, 180° or 270°), never by Canvas rows and columns: nothing snaps a Frame to the Canvas's grid, and no edit, turn or setting of one layer moves or turns another, not even by a rounding. What a layer is read by (a Frame's Row direction as seen, its rulers, its sizes as seen, its exports) goes by its own quarter turn on the Table. Decided 2026-11-25 (spec 1234).
_Avoid_: world, scene; lie alike, on the Canvas's grid, off the grid (retired with spec 1234: no layer is placed against another)

**Table turn** (spec 1234):
The angle the whole Table is turned on screen, by a two-finger twist: every layer together, each keeping its turn relative to the others. Any angle, easing onto a quarter turn of the screen within about 10°, glued to the fingers (ticket 1227). A view: saved with the Project, never an Undo step, never in an export, the PDF or a thumbnail; Fit and Delete all set it back to 0°. It never changes a Row direction, a ruler or a size as seen. A twist never turns only the Canvas or only a Frame: Rotate canvas and Rotate Frame turn one layer, by quarter turns. No layer is ever off a quarter turn from another, so nothing leans. Code catches up in ticket 1238.
_Avoid_: canvas twist, Frame twist, lean, view rotation

**Frame**:
A small sheet laid over the Canvas, lying on the Table by its own centre and quarter turn (spec 1234), that holds the beads of a Schema (ADR 0066). A Project holds up to four (`MAX_FRAMES`, spec 1214, ADR 0103; the Frames section refuses a fifth), each a layer of its own (ADR 0079), kept in the order made. A layer: it owns its beads, counted from its own first row and column, and its own Layer settings (Technique and its settings, Bead, background; ticket 492, ADR 0079): it is drawn, hit, ruled, counted, exported and printed in them, whatever the Canvas is woven in, and each of its cells is named by the Frame and its own row and column. Its first row is always an unshifted one, so its top edge resizes by a whole cycle of its own rows; it moves freely, following the pointer. Its sheet (the Frame and its keep-out margin) is opaque and covers what lies under it in layer order: what the Canvas holds there is hidden, not changed, not counted, and shows again when the Frame moves off it, and a Canvas bead half under the sheet's edge shows half. A press goes to the layer drawn on top under the pointer: inside a sheet the Frame, elsewhere the Canvas, and a stroke stays on the layer it began on. Plus a **keep-out margin** (below): drawing on the Canvas stays possible beyond the margin and is saved with the Project, but Export, Beads needed and Row progress read only the Frame's beads. Each Frame has its own Row progress, on from the moment it is made; the Frame then stays resizable until a pass is done on it, and its size is held still from then until it is removed or the Project is cleared (ticket 411); it can still be moved, with its beads and the pointer, by the Move handle (ticket 0452, ADR 0054). With a Frame the Schema has a size (the Schema size) . Made, moved, resized and removed as Undo steps. Frame from Selection, Fit to drawing and Set Frame around the picture copy the beads you see inside the new Frame into it (one step; a Custom frame lays them on its layout, each as a Paste lands it); the Canvas keeps every bead, hidden under the Frame, and a named Piece keeps its name there while the Frame takes the name too. A Frame and the Canvas, or one Frame and another, are separate sheets and no bead ever passes between them (ticket 1215, spec 1214). The Move handle carries the Frame's beads and leaves the Canvas as it is; resizing never changes a bead's color and never removes one. A Frame made smaller always **folds** the beads it gives up, whatever the two Weaves are (Folded beads, below), and one grown back shows them again; a growing Frame takes nothing from the Canvas, which only hides what it covers, and a named Piece under it keeps its name and stays whole. By handle drag this is done once, on release, and one Undo restores the Frame and its beads. Remove Frame always asks first, for every Frame, a Custom frame included, and names Row progress once a pass is done: **Remove** takes the Frame with all its beads, Folded beads too, and its passes; **Remove and copy** does the same and puts the beads on the clipboard with Paste mode started, as after Cut, to be pasted anywhere and re-woven cell by cell as any Paste is; Cancel changes nothing. Nothing is ever dropped onto the Canvas, so no line-up comes first, and one Undo brings the Frame back with its beads, Folded beads and progress. Resized from the Frame settings popover, the size is applied once: each press of Width or Height shows on the canvas but is not committed until the popover closes (or another command reads the Frame), as one Undo step. **Name** (spec 1214): every Frame has one, shown on its sheet and in the Frames section: "Frame N", N the lowest number no Frame goes by, unless it was made from a named Piece, which gives its name (`nextFrameName`); clearing a name gives "Frame N" back, and two Frames may share a name. **Where a new Frame goes**: one from a Starter lands round the middle of the view, nudged to the nearest place clear of every other Frame's Keep-out margin, else to the right of the last Frame (`landedClear`, ticket 1222); one from a Selection, Fit to drawing or Set Frame around the picture would meet another Frame's margin and is refused, with a toast naming that Frame. Delete all empties every layer and keeps the Frames. A Project from before the Frame opens with a Frame the size of its old grid. A new Frame made from the Canvas starts where its copied beads are, with their stagger, and lies on its own from then on.
_Avoid_: border, crop, artboard, page, grid size; footprint (retired with spec 1234: what a Frame covers is its sheet as drawn); in code, Frame for any other rectangle of positions, which is a `Rect` (ticket 0617): the Frame itself is `FrameSheet`

**Last edited Frame** (spec 1214, ticket 1220):
The Frame most recently painted on or changed: the Project's one answer to "which Frame" wherever something wants a single one. Saved with the Project (`Project.lastEditedFrame`, file version 17), and read through `lastEditedFrameId`, which gives the first Frame while none was edited, or once the named one is gone, and none in a Project without a Frame. A stroke or any edit that changes a Frame's layer sets it (`withLastEdited`, in the one Edit path), and so do Undo and Redo, to the Frame whose layer they put back; an edit that touches several Frames at once (a Palette-wide change) leaves it as it is when it is among them. Export's Frame dropdown starts on it (shown only with more than one Frame; Export is always of one Frame) and the Project library's thumbnail draws it.
_Avoid_: active Frame, current Frame, selected Frame


**Layer**:
The Canvas or a Frame: a sub-canvas with everything a canvas has, its own id, name, Weave, Bead, background, beads, Letters switch, Piece names and **orientation** (its quarter turn on the Table, spec 1234; the Table turn turns them all together on screen), each changed on its own in its Layer settings (ADR 0079). A Project holds an ordered list of them, the Canvas first; up to five, and a press, a stroke, a Selection or a paste acts on one layer at a time. Its **Layer geometry** (ADR 0078, ticket 0628) answers where its beads are, whatever lays them out: its positions, whether a position holds a bead, the beads touching one, where a bead lies and at what angle, which bead a point is on, the box round its edge, which beads a view of part of it draws, whether its beads keep rows and columns (which rulers number), and its Seams. A grid's answers come from its Weave (the Canvas, open, or a Frame with no shape), a Custom frame's from its layout. Editing, drawing, the hit test, the exports and a Starter's bead count ask it, never whether the Frame has a layout (tickets 0629, 0630, 0632).
_Avoid_: sheet, level

**Layer settings** (ADR 0079, ticket 0670):
The one section that sets a layer: its Technique and that Technique's settings, its Bead and its background, plus a Custom frame's shape settings. The same section wherever a layer is set: the New Project form (both layers start from it; it has no background, ticket 0881), the Project panel's Canvas section, the chips of the Frame's margin sheet and (ticket 0671) the Starter setup. A Bead picked there is the Bead of that layer, confirmed by Replace Bead, which no longer asks which layer. A new layer starts as a copy of the layer it is made from or with, or of a Starter. What changes what is woven (Technique, its settings, Bead, shape settings) is an Undo step; what changes only how it looks (background) is not, and is saved with the Project all the same. On a Frame, only what moves positions waits while a Row progress pass is done.
_Avoid_: layer properties, layer options

**Folded beads**:
The Frame's own beads beyond its edge: what a Frame gives up whenever it is made smaller, whatever the Weaves of the Frame and the Canvas are, since no bead ever passes to the Canvas (ticket 1215); a Custom frame folds what its layout no longer has a position for. They are kept with the Frame and shown again when it grows back over them. Saved with the Project and carried by every Frame command (moved, turned, re-woven and shifted with the Frame's other beads; Remove row/column takes the whole line, its Folded beads too), they are not drawn, exported, counted or woven; Remove Frame takes them away with the Frame's other beads, and Remove and copy leaves only the beads shown on the clipboard.
_Avoid_: hidden beads, trimmed beads, offcut (and Off-shape, which is a part of a Frame with no positions)

**Keep-out margin**:
4 of the Frame's own bead positions all the way round its drawn edge, outside its line (spec 1234; the owner plans to rework it) (tickets 261, 0501, 514, 0656, ADR 0066: it hangs off what the Frame draws, so it is joined to it in every Technique; 3 positions until ticket 427, 6 until ticket 0656, and removed by ticket 0490 until 0501 brought it back), drawn as the **Margin sheet** below: no Position marks in it (Squares' lines stop at its edge, and run round the Frame's own positions), and a dashed outline that flashes for 1s after a press in it is refused; the pointer shows not-allowed there. Nothing can be drawn there: Paint, Fill, Paste and Rotate all leave it empty (a stroke across it paints only the beads outside it, still one Undo step); Erase still works on anything. It cannot be selected either (tickets 428, 433): a Selection is wholly inside the Frame, wholly clear of the margin, or round the Frame and its margin whole, never anything else, whichever way it is made (one rule, `selectionAllowed`); a drag cannot start in it and is clamped when it ends there, Shift+arrow stops one cell before it, a ruler number selects only the Frame's part of its line, and a Piece tap selects only the tapped bead's side. Beads held in a margin are out of reach of every Selection except one round the whole Frame (so Cut, Clear selection and Copy touch them only then, while Rotate still leaves the margin empty), while Erase still clears them. A Canvas bead that lies in the margin when the Frame is made or resized stays on the Canvas, covered by the margin (not drawn there, not counted, never copied into the Frame), and shows again if the Frame later moves off it or is resized. Moving the Frame carries its own beads: beads held in the margin stay where they are, and whatever is under the destination stays where it is on the Canvas, hidden under the Frame and there again as soon as the Frame no longer lies over it (a move off, Remove Frame, a resize); a named Piece under the Frame keeps its name and stays whole. The margin is 4 positions at every zoom (ticket 459 grew it when zoomed out for the corner controls; ticket 473 took those controls out of it). It is the Frame's own, counted in the Frame's positions: a 15/0 Frame has a slimmer margin than an 11/0 one, and nothing about the Canvas changes it (spec 1234; until ticket 1237 it counts the Canvas's positions). Removing the Frame removes the margin. Export, Beads needed and Row progress count the Frame's beads only, so they ignore the Canvas. Drawn as a **Margin sheet** (ticket 0655; its width and color built by ticket 0656, its tools by 0657, its zoom forms by 0658): see below. Zoomed far out (a band under 31px on screen, about 39%) the sheet is not drawn at all and only its Move bar stays (ticket 0682): the 4 positions are still kept clear, and a refused press still flashes the outline. Each Frame has its own (ticket 1218, spec 1214): every rule here holds in any Frame's margin, a press lands on the Frame under it or on the Canvas, and **Frames never meet**: a move or resize that would bring one Frame plus its margin into another's stops at the last place allowed, and a Rotate Frame that would is refused with a Message naming that Frame.

**Margin sheet** (ticket 0655; MarginSheet card):
The keep-out margin drawn as a sheet of drafting paper: 4 bead positions on every side, filled with the margin color (Auto: the Canvas's background one step off; or one picked with the background color picker, remembered on the device), with an inner border. Its top band holds Expand the Frame, **who the Frame is** (its name, Technique, the Technique's settings and the maker's name as editable chips, and the fold; compact and folded, the name alone; ticket 1258), its Move bar and its tools: in every form with a tools row, as many as fit at their spacing (44px targets on a touch screen) stand on the band and the rest go under the **···** Frame tools button, least used first (Margin color, Mirror the whole Frame, Rotate Frame, Edit Frame last), the button shown only when it holds something (2026-11-21 grilling; replacing ticket 0976's touch rule); its bottom edge, from the bottom-left corner as seen, is one line: the size in beads then in cm as plain facts, the Bead's chip, the lettered color legend, the Letters switch and the Frame's **Row progress** (ticket 1258; a title block with a strip under it until then), the facts standing over Row progress where the sheet is too narrow for one line; compact keeps the legend and Row progress, folded Row progress alone. The chips in the band and the facts are what **title block** still names in Name and size on the margin and Fold the title block. The rulers stand in its left and top bands. Both stay there at every Canvas turn and with the Frame turned by Rotate Frame (ticket 1170; until then they were on the Frame's own bottom-right corner, carried round by the Canvas's turn, ticket 0728). It has a full, a compact and a folded form, chosen by how many screen pixels the band has at the zoom, and a bare one under that, where nothing of it is drawn but the Move bar, above the middle of the Frame's top edge, with the **···** Frame tools button beside it, whose menu holds every Frame tool (tickets 0682, 1164). It turns with its Frame's quarter turn and with the Table turn (spec 1234). Never in exports.
_Avoid_: border, paper margin, frame card

**Expand the Frame** (tickets 1168, 1169, 1176, 1259, 1260; MarginSheet card, As built 1260 and 1259):
The margin sheet's first button (`expand-frame`), for weaving from the screen. The sheet covers the drawing area: the Frame's facts and Beads needed in a left rail, its tools with their words in a right rail (for a wide Frame or a narrow screen, both in a band along the top), its rows in the **window** between, and its Row progress strip across the bottom; Export and Margin color unfold in the rail, and there is no ··· and no Move bar. The view glides to the whole Frame where its beads then are 28px or more (26px up to 1500px wide), else to that pitch with the rows running on along the longer way. The Tool pill stays on its edge (ticket 1259): four places round the Frame show while it is dragged, only the side it is on keeps room for it, and drawing works as unexpanded. A move by hand along the rows scrolls them and stays expanded; one across them (any, where the whole Frame is in view), a twist of the Canvas or Rotate canvas goes back at once to the view as it was, the move going on over the Canvas (a twist of the Frame and the edge scroll a drag carries stay expanded). So does **Back to the Canvas** (`collapse-frame`), Escape when nothing else takes it, and a zoom out that comes to rest under about 95% of the expanded fit, while one above it springs back and a zoom in stays. Not saved: a reload or another Project opens unexpanded, and Remove Frame goes back.
_Avoid_: full screen, focus mode, zen mode

**Custom frame** (spec 0590; Triangle and Warped Square, ticket 0592; Star, Kaleidocycle and Kite, ticket 0593; Selection, Rotate Frame, exports and Row progress, ticket 0594):
A Frame that is not a rectangle on one Grid: a Frame with a **Frame shape**: a fixed layout of bead positions given by its shape and its own settings (how many rows or rounds, whatever that shape needs), made of parts woven at different angles or in different Techniques, as a maker beads it. The first shapes are the five geometric peyote ones: Warped Square, Triangle, Kite, Star and Kaleidocycle (Triangle and Warped Square worked in rounds from the centre, sized by Rounds; the Kite two flat peyote wings on a herringbone spine, worked in rows from its point, each from one wing's end to the spine pair and out along the other and the next back, a wing gaining a bead every two rows (2, 2, 4, 4, 6 … beads a row), every bead at its Bead's real proportions so the wings lean as the Bead makes them, sized by Rows (ticket 0874); the Star and Kaleidocycle drawn as their finished face but made of parts (the Star five warped squares, each centred on a notch between two arms and folded over the outline there, its close corners the star's centre front and back and its far corners the two arms' tips, so its face shows each arm as a ridge row with a half-arm of shorter rows either side and each square's partial last round along one arm's ridge, the back beaded as the face's mirror, so counts, Row progress and the Word chart take the back from the face, a count each bead twice but the five tips once, ticket 0875; the Kaleidocycle twenty-four triangles, four faces of six, its Frame one face: six of the 24, its counts that face's with the whole piece's beside them, and each other face a Frame of its own, ticket 0876; joined as the Kali Primer joins it, ticket 0877: the face is one whose triangles are hinged in Mirror pairs, so its Row progress ends with each triangle's Strip and Tetra, the three Mirror pairs laced with Hinge Pins, then the three ring joins, and its whole piece counts the beads of the joins too, each on its own line: the Edge Beads (Part size − 1 a side of each Tetra), the Hinge Beads (Part size − 2 a half-Hinge row, two a Strip), and in 15/0, their own bead size, a Triangle Tip at each of the 72 tips and Part size − 1 Hinge Pins a Hinge), sized by Part size, each panel knowing its part and making order); the **Fringe earring** is one too: a beadwoven top with hanging strands, its settings Width, Top rows, Fringe style (straight, V, later looped) and Fringe length (ticket 0814). **Width** (stored as `topWidth`) is the width of the whole earring: one strand hangs under each bead of the top's base row, so the top and the fringe always share it. The top is always a **point** that tapers from the Width (in brick stitch a bead a row, half a bead in on each side, to a pair in Width − 1 rows; fewer Top rows cut it off): Top rows stop at the point's height, so no full-width body ever grows under it (ticket 0814 had one; it was taken out), Top rows past it, from a handle, a stepper or an older file, cut to the point with the beads it gives up kept as Folded beads, and a changed Width keeps a whole point whole. Its top is woven in **brick stitch**, **peyote** or **loom** (its Technique in Layer settings, the earring Starters' setup offering it with its Drop; the designs are made in brick stitch 1-drop, so another turns Use design off with a note), laid out in blocks of Drop as on a plain Frame, a taper step one stitch (N beads on N-drop): brick stitch's point a stitch shorter a block of Drop rows, half a bead in on each side; peyote's columns a stitch shorter a block out from the middle one, its Width an odd number of columns (of blocks on N-drop, stepping by two, an even one made odd on switching with a note saying why), Top rows the middle column's beads, each strand hanging from its column's lowest bead (on 1-drop at two heights, half a bead apart); loom's point a bead in on each side a row, in stepped edges, to one or two, its strands straight under each base bead. Switching the top's Technique or Drop keeps each bead's row and column, as on any Frame: beads with no place in the new top become Folded beads, the fringe untouched, one Undo step, held still while Row progress is on. It is a Frame, inheriting every Frame property and action (ADR 0079): set, added and replaced like any Frame, its Layer settings its own, its shape and settings saved with the Project. It is built of **panels**: small sheets, each in one Technique at its own angle and place, built by the shape's rule from its settings (the Star two half-arms a square, ten round the centre, the Kaleidocycle six triangles round a hole, a fringe earring its top and its strands); a bead belongs to exactly one panel. A round shape (Triangle, Warped Square, Kaleidocycle; the Star's rows lie straight along its ridges instead, a little further apart than peyote's so five arms lie flat, ticket 0875) is charted as a bead artist charts it (ticket 0723): each side's beads packed in columns at right angles to it, every bead a width across and as deep as its Bead's height over its pitch (a Delica 11/0's where the Bead is unknown), gaps opening along the Seams; so replacing the Frame's Bead lays it out again, its sheet following, every bead keeping its name and colour. Its sheet is its bounding rectangle, as for any Frame, with the keep-out margin round it; inside that rectangle, the **Seams** and the **Off-shape** area hold no positions and cannot be drawn on (a refused press flashes the outline, as on the keep-out margin). Its settings can be changed later in the Frame settings popover (shrinking keeps what it gives up as Folded beads, without asking, and growing shows them again or adds empty rounds or rows; the steppers commit once, as one Undo step, when the popover closes), held still once a Row progress pass is done. Every tool and Frame action works on it, done through its shape (ADR 0079, ticket 0669): the resize handles step its size setting (on a Fringe earring each side its own, ticket 0814: the left and right handles Width, the top handle Top rows, the bottom handle Fringe length, a V's tip moving with it; in a V a changed Width, by a handle or a stepper, moves Fringe length two beads for each strand the V gains or loses on a side, so its outer strands keep their length and the V reaches the edges, ticket 0816; only the dragged edge moves, the one across from it staying where it is on the Canvas; once Rotate Frame has turned it, a handle drives the setting of the side of the shape it sits on), Frame from Selection fits the shape round the Selection, and Technique offers the Techniques the shape supports; only Remove row/column, which needs plain rows, is not offered. A Selection over it is a rectangle on screen holding every bead whose centre lies inside it, from any panel; Paste and Rotate put each bead on the nearest free position of the panel under it, at that panel's angle, and a bead that lands on a Seam or Off-shape is dropped, a Message saying how many. Rotate Frame turns it as it turns any Frame. Remove Frame asks first as for any Frame (Remove, Remove and copy, Cancel) and takes the beads with it, its Folded beads too; one Undo brings it back (ticket 1215). A Frame with no shape is not a Custom frame: the open Canvas makes that.
_Avoid_: Shaped Frame, expert mode, mixed technique (for the Frame itself), template; Rectangular frame (for a Frame with no shape, which is just a Frame)

**Frame shape** (ticket 0591, ADR 0077, ADR 0079):
What makes a Frame a Custom frame: Warped Square, Triangle, Kite, Star, Kaleidocycle or Fringe earring (Medallion later). A Frame without one is just a Frame. One entry in a typed registry, like the Starters', that holds everything that differs by shape: its settings, the Techniques it supports, how its panels are built, its Layer geometry and Estimated size, how each Frame action is done on it, its Row order and how it is exported. Code asks the Frame's shape rather than testing for it. A new shape is its layout builder, its entry, a golden layout and its words (ADR 0078, ticket 0632). Saved with the Project; a Frame saved without one reads as a plain Frame. The code calls it `kind` until ticket 0668.
_Avoid_: Frame kind, Rectangular (as a kind), frame type, template

**Seam** (ticket 0592):
The line between two panels of a Custom frame: no bead position, cannot be drawn on, and drawn as a thin line so the panels read apart.
_Avoid_: gap, border, margin (the keep-out margin is outside the Frame)

**Off-shape** (ticket 0592; any Frame, 2026-11-21 grilling):
The part of a Frame's rectangle that holds no bead position: on a Custom frame, its bounding rectangle outside its shape (between a Star's points, round a Triangle); on a peyote or brick stitch Frame, the half bead at the short end of each staggered row or column. It cannot be drawn on and is drawn darker than the Frame's background (blackish in dark, a few steps darker in light), with no position marks, so it reads as a place no bead can go; the Seams match it. It is inside the Frame, so it hides the Canvas under it, unlike the keep-out margin round the Frame. Screen only: exports leave it white.
_Avoid_: margin, keep-out, empty area, stagger gap

**Edit Frame**:
The Frame's edit toggle (tickets 0452, 0473, 0510, ADR 0054). The **edit pencil** in the Frame's gutter, above its top-right corner (32px chip, 44px hit, never scaling with the zoom; kept inside the canvas box, flipping to below the Frame when there is no room above), shows or hides the eight resize handles (four on touch), each a small square in the gutter just outside the Frame's edge at a fixed pixel size at every zoom, flipping to the inside of an edge that has no room outside it in the canvas box; a press on a handle wins over the tool's press there. It does not open the **Frame settings popover**, which the sheet's ··· button opens. The handles are hidden whenever a Project opens or the Frame is removed, and Frame from Selection leaves them hidden; the choice is kept for the session only. Tools work inside the Frame as usual, since the handles sit in the gutter, outside the Frame and its keep-out margin. Once Row progress has started the pencil shows no handles, while the ··· button still opens the popover, which then holds the size and Remove Frame alone (ticket 0475). The Frame's rulers and the dashed margin outline are always shown, whatever the Rulers toggle says (ticket 1012); the size tooltip shows only while dragging or stepping. A Selection never shows rulers or a margin outline; the pencil, **Remove Frame** (a chip right after the pencil, the same command and confirmation as the Frames section's, ticket 0498) and the Move handle stay with every tool, with a Selection and after Row progress has started, and where a Selection's handles overlap one of them the Selection's handle wins the press.
_Avoid_: Set Frame (the old mode, which also drew a Frame by dragging), crop, resize, set size, Edit Frame mode

**Frame settings popover** (ticket 1222, spec 1214; replaces the Frame panel and the Canvas strip's Frame button, both retired): what the margin sheet's **···** button opens, one 21rem popover hung under it, always shown on the sheet in every form, with Escape or a press outside closing it. It holds everything about that one Frame, top to bottom: its head (name over Technique and size, "Peyote · 11 × 100"); every band tool as a Menu item (Mirror the whole Frame, Rotate Frame, Edit Frame, Export, Margin color, a rule, Remove Frame), each a second trigger of the band's own control; its size, Width and Height fields (empty, the current size as placeholder; digits only) that grow or shrink both sides evenly about the Frame (the odd bead going right or down; on peyote and brick stitch the top edge moves by pairs of rows), then Top, Bottom, Left and Right cells, each a − and + pair: one bead per press (a pair of rows on the top edge where rows alternate), hold to repeat, one Undo step, down to four beads, the edges named as the Frame lies on screen after Rotate Frame, then its Estimated size (a Custom frame has its shape steppers and Fringe style in their place; rows marked done turn them off and say so, with Reset progress); and its **Beads needed**. Nothing in it deletes beads: what a smaller Frame gives up is kept as Folded beads (ticket 1215). It opens from elsewhere too: the Frame stays toast's Open Frame opens that Frame's. Its Background is the second row of the Margin color popover; its name and Technique are also chips in the sheet's band, and its Bead a chip in the sheet's bottom edge.
_Avoid_: Frame panel, Frame toolbar, Frame sheet

**Frames section** (ticket 1222, spec 1214): the Project panel's part for the Frames, right after the name. **add a frame** is open with no Frame and folded once there is one: Frame from starter (the Starter gallery as a Subpanel), Fit a Frame to the drawing and Drag a Frame (Select is the tool and the next drag becomes the Frame on release, Escape cancelling), all three disabled at four Frames with "A Project holds up to 4 Frames." Then "frames" (ticket 1250), open while there is one Frame, folded at two or more: a row for each, in the order made, with its picture, name, Technique and size, and three icons always shown: Show this Frame (brings it into view), Export this Frame… (Export, on this Frame) and Remove Frame… (the same question as the sheet's). Each way of adding a Frame adds one, never replaces one; Frame from Selection and Piece frame are in the Selection bar and add too.
_Avoid_: Frame list, Frames panel

**Export panel**:
The small panel the Canvas strip's **Export** button opens (the `export` icon, the strip's last button; tickets 0652, 0847), in the same shell as the Menu and Project panels and one of the three at a time. With a Frame it holds the Export section (Picture, then Printable chart with its page count, then Export quality and the Large beads and Row by row switches, Large beads only where it makes the chart's beads bigger; tickets 0916, 0957, 1095, 1125), the same component; with no Frame it says to set a frame and offers Select a frame and Fit a frame to the drawing; a Frame selected from it brings it back once set (ticket 0847). Letters is in the bottom edge of the Frame's margin sheet. With more than one Frame it has a **Frame** dropdown over its rows (ticket 1222), starting on the Last edited Frame or the Frame whose sheet's Export opened it, and exports that one Frame.
_Avoid_: Share panel (it only exports files), Export menu

**Export settings** (ticket 1210): the Export panel's fold, under Name on exports and closed at first, holding Export quality and every print switch (Cover page, Black and white, Empty grid, Chart border, Large beads, Row by row). Whether it is open is remembered on the device. The Export panel is one component wherever it opens (the Canvas strip, a Frame's sheet, the Selection bar), so the fold and Name on exports are in all three.
_Avoid_: advanced options, more settings

**Rotate Frame**:
A tool on the Frame's margin sheet and in its settings popover, before Remove Frame (spec 0470). Turns one Frame alone a quarter turn clockwise about its own centre, on the Table, like a sheet of A4 turned on an A1 (ADR 0052, ADR 0079, ticket 0667; spec 1234): it steps the Frame's quarter turn, its beads keep their own row and column and its grid turns with them, so nothing is rewritten in any Technique and no row snaps. Its sheet turns, Width and Height swapping as seen, and stays where it is on the Table. Where the turn would bring it into another Frame's margin it is refused, with a Message naming that Frame. It turns by quarter turns only: a two-finger twist turns the whole Table, never the Frame alone (Table turn; decided 2026-11-25, code catches up in ticket 1238, until then a twist that starts on the Frame turns it to any angle). The Canvas under it is not touched, so nothing is covered; one Undo step. Row direction stays as seen: a Frame woven top to bottom is woven top to bottom after the turn (ticket 1183). With no pass done it just turns; once a pass is done it asks first (ticket 1184; ConfirmDialogs card, Turn the Frame?): start over in that way (every pointer back at the first pass), keep the rows woven (the way turns along with the Frame, every pointer stays and the same beads stay finished, as its passes are the same beads), or Cancel, which leaves the Frame unturned. The question is the domain's (`rotateFrameQuestion`), like Remove line's. A Custom frame never asks: its order turns with its layout and its pointer stays. Each turn is one Undo step, which brings back the Frame's orientation and its Row progress. It is resized as seen (ticket 1016): Edit Frame stays in its place in the band, each resize handle moves the Frame's own edge lying under it (by that edge's own step) with the edge across from it staying on the Canvas, and the popover's Width, Height and edge steppers work as seen; a turned Frame never trades beads with the Canvas, so what a smaller one leaves out becomes Folded beads and a growing one takes nothing. A press is seen turning over 150ms and leaves a copy of the band's Rotate Frame where it was pressed (MarginSheet card). Turned upright again, it starts on a row and column where a Frame may start. While it lies turned its rulers count the cells it covers, and Remove Frame takes its beads with it, its Folded beads too, as for any Frame (tickets 0667, 1215). The bar stays as it was. It shares its icon with Rotate and has no key.
_Avoid_: Frame turn (the ADR 0026 Frame turn that moved Pieces)

**Rotate canvas**:
The Canvas strip button before the Rulers toggle (spec 0470). Turns the Canvas alone, like a real canvas on a table under the sheets laid on it (ticket 1188; spec 1234): a quarter turn clockwise about the middle of the drawing area. Only the Canvas's quarter turn and origin change; no bead, name or margin moves, so peyote and brick stitch turn without any row snapping. Every Frame stays exactly where it is on the Table, its rows, Row direction, rulers, handles, margin sheet and title block as they were; the Canvas beads it now covers are hidden, not changed, and show again when the Canvas turns back. A two-finger twist no longer turns the Canvas alone: it turns the whole Table (Table turn; decided 2026-11-25, code catches up in ticket 1238). Pan and pinch move and zoom every layer together. Exports follow the Frame alone (ticket 1186). A view setting like Row direction: saved with the Project, not an Undo step, not blocked by Row progress. It turns the Canvas's placement on the **table** (the plane pan and zoom move; ticket 1185), never the view's scroll over it. Its icon is the Rotate icon's arc with a bead of the woven layer's Type (Round, Delica, Cube) and a thread through its hole, the bead turned as the Canvas is, so it shows which way the thread runs now (ticket 1082); no key. A new peyote Canvas starts turned a quarter (the Technique's `startOrientation`), so its staggered columns show as staggered rows; every other Technique starts upright, and so does a Project made by Convert image, whatever its Technique, so it opens the way the converter showed it (ticket 0965).
_Avoid_: Rotate view, turn picture

**Move handle**:
The Frame's move grip (tickets 0452, 0455, 0473, ADR 0066): a horizontal line in the Frame's gutter above its top edge (below it when there is no room above), always there, handles on or off, also after Row progress has started. Dragging it moves the whole Frame with its beads (ADR 0054): while it is dragged the beads travel with the Frame on screen, not only its outline; the Canvas under the destination is hidden, not cleared, and shows again when the Frame moves off it, the move is not limited, and while the pointer touches the canvas box's edge the view scrolls that way, so the Frame stays in hand; it goes by a pair of rows on peyote and brick stitch, as many whole beads as the pointer has gone since the press (ticket 1150: not to the bead under the pointer, which is a row up or down at every other staggered column), and the Row progress pointer travels with the Frame. One announced Undo step; arrow keys move it a bead and Shift + arrows resize, while it has focus. It sits in the **gutter**, a band just outside the Frame's edge measured in screen pixels, so it keeps its size and its distance from the edge at every zoom; it is kept inside the canvas box (flipping to below the Frame when there is no room above), slides clear of the pencil, and wins over a tool's press in its hit box. Resizing never touches a bead.
_Avoid_: Frame drag, drag inside the Frame

**Hand tool**:
The tool (5) that moves the view by dragging and never changes a bead. Space + drag, the middle mouse button (ticket 541) and a right-button drag (ticket 1074, ADR 0098) do the same from any tool. A right-button press waits: once the pointer has moved 8 px with the button down (the Tool pill's drag threshold) it is the middle button's pan until the button lifts; lifted before that it is a right click, which does nothing until it opens the Radial toolbox (ticket 1064). A pen's barrel button reports the right button and follows the same rule.
_Avoid_: pan mode, grab tool

**Pen mode** / **Mouse mode**:
The input mode: which pointer draws with the current Tool and which one moves the canvas. In Pen mode the pen draws, and a finger or mouse drags the canvas like the Hand tool, so a palm resting on an iPad does not paint. In Mouse mode (the default) a finger or mouse draws and the pen drags the canvas. Told apart by each event's pointer type, not by the Tool; two-finger pinch, the wheel and Space + drag work in both. One toggle picks it (the Tool pill's last button), offered only once a pen has been seen (the first pen event of a visit, kept on the device), and that first pen switches to Pen mode unless a mode was chosen before; the choice is kept on the device.
_Avoid_: stylus mode, touch mode, palm rejection

**Rulers toggle**:
The button (and R, and the Canvas strip's Rulers button) that shows or hides the Piece areas' rulers and rectangles on the Canvas (its rectangle too), kept as a preference on the device like the theme. It does the same with a Frame set as without one (ticket 1012): each Piece area of more than 4 beads on the Canvas around the Frame gets its column numbers above and row numbers to the left, from 1. It never touches the Frame's own rulers: its numbers, Ruler dots and line, and its size tooltip while it is dragged or stepped, are always shown, the numbers on its margin sheet at the sheet's scale while a Piece's follow the zoom (a Custom frame has none of its own, and a Frame zoomed out past its sheet's full form shows no numbers). Off until switched on; held, R too, while the Canvas has no bead, whether or not there is a Frame, since with only a Frame there is nothing for it to switch (tickets 0921, 1012), and lit only while it can be pressed.
_Avoid_: ruler switch, numbers toggle

**Canvas zoom**:
The zoom the app's own controls and gestures change (zoom buttons, pinch and Ctrl/⌘ + wheel over the canvas, Ctrl/⌘ + plus, minus and 0), applied to the canvas alone. It is the only zoom there is: the Dock and every sheet keep their size (ADR 0034). While a big pattern is zoomed or panned its drawing is moved and scaled and drawn sharp once the gesture settles (tickets 349, 0689); the empty Canvas look lies under it the whole time, and in the draft look the drawing reaches half a screen past the view, so nothing bare shows (ticket 0717). Beads a long pan reaches are drawn mid-gesture, in the full look on a draft-look stand-in under the drawing, so they show before it stops (ticket 0732).
_Avoid_: scale, magnify, page zoom (a different thing)

**Page zoom**:
The browser's own whole-page zoom (pinch on the page, double-tap, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel outside the canvas, the browser's zoom menu). The app locks it so the chrome never changes size.
_Avoid_: UI scale, browser scale

**Zoom floor**:
The smallest zoom, 10%, the same on every screen: zoom out, pinch, wheel and Fit all stop there, so a Schema wider or taller than the screen can be seen whole. Fit shows the Frame with its Margin sheet whole, clear of the Tool pill (ticket 0850); a small Frame's sheet it zooms in on until it fills that room one way, up to the largest bead size, 48px a bead (ticket 0964). With no Frame, Fit never goes above 100%. Fit (the button and ⌘0) also puts the Table turn back to 0° first (ticket 1227, spec 1234; a view setting, no Undo step), then it zooms to fit; each layer's own quarter turn stays as it is. An expanded Frame is only refitted, its turns being its own.
_Avoid_: min zoom, bead-min floor

**Ruler step**:
How many beads apart the rulers show a number, chosen from 5, 10, 50, 100 and so on as the smallest that leaves every number clear at the current zoom; the beads between are drawn as Ruler dots. The Ruler step applies from 50% zoom up; below 50% a ruler shows only its last number (the column count along the columns, the row count along the rows, on every Frame side and every Piece ruler), and the beads without a number keep their dots.
_Avoid_: label interval, tick spacing

**Ruler dot**:
The small mark a ruler draws for a bead that has no number at the current Ruler step; every 5th is a bolder dot, and, a dot, like a number, is display-only (ticket 447). At a bead pitch too small for a dot per bead, only the 5th-bead dots remain.
_Avoid_: tick, marker

**Ruler layout**:
Everything the rulers are for one view of the canvas, worked out once from the Project and the Surface view: the boxes that carry rulers (the Frame's, then each Piece area's), the numbers and Ruler dots to draw (a Frame's always, at its margin sheet's scale; a Piece's with the Rulers toggle on, at the zoom's), and what a point of the viewport selects. The overlay draws it; ruler numbers are display-only (ticket 447), so nothing asks it what a press selects; the Ruler step is chosen once per axis per box inside it. The print rulers (fixed step, no dots) are not part of it.
_Avoid_: ruler view, ruler geometry

**Background**:
The color a layer is drawn on, one of its Layer settings (ADR 0079, ticket 0666; the Homepage has none): three in light, three in dark (ticket 1000), stored as a number so a change of theme keeps the position, or the person's own Custom color (ticket 0653), from the Palette's Custom color picker, one color for both themes. Saved with the Project, a view and not an Undo step, and not in exports, which always print on the light board. A Frame's Off-shape is drawn darker than it (the token `frame-off-shape`, ticket 1196; a Custom color darker than the token is darkened itself). The device remembers the last one picked as the background a new Project starts with; an older Project opens with it. High contrast has one white canvas, so it offers no swatches, only a line saying so. Each is picked in its layer's Layer settings (ticket 0670): the Canvas's in the Project panel's Canvas section, the Frame's from its margin sheet; the New Project form has none (ticket 0881), so a new Project starts on the device's last pick; the margin's Auto follows the Canvas's. Before ticket 0666 it was the **Canvas color**, one device preference not saved with a Project.
_Avoid_: board color, background theme, Canvas color (for any layer's)

**Reference image** (ticket 0790):
One picture a Project may hold, laid on the Canvas over every layer's Background and under every bead, to bead over (tracing) or keep beside the pattern as an example. It pans, zooms and turns with the Canvas; its opacity is one of four steps (25, 50, 75, 100%). Shown and hidden from the Canvas strip, set in the Project panel; resized and turned from its corner and Turn handles whenever it is shown (ticket 0799), moved by its **Picture bar**'s Move grip (ticket 0883) or in **Adjust**, so no tool's press on the picture itself moves it. Replace image is in the Project panel and on the Adjust bar. A view, like Background: saved with the Project and in its file, not an Undo step, never printed. While it is shown, Pick color from Canvas takes the nearest bead color to it where there is no bead (ticket 1119). ReferenceImage card.
_Avoid_: background image (Background is a layer's color), underlay, tracing image, template

**Picture bar** (ticket 0883):
The small pill over a shown Reference image's top edge: the Move grip, Set Frame around the picture, Convert to beads and ··· (Opacity, Replace, Remove). It moves the picture without Adjust. ReferenceImage card.
_Avoid_: image toolbar, picture menu

**Set Frame around the picture** (ticket 0883):
The picture bar's action that sets the Frame to the beads the Reference image covers: adds a Frame, one Undo step (a Frame already there stays: Remove it first to have one in its place).

**Convert to beads** (ticket 0883):
The picture bar's action that turns the Reference image into beads where it lies, at the size it covers on the Canvas, in Exact colors by default: one Undo step. Not Convert image, which is the full page; All settings opens that page from here.
_Avoid_: trace, auto-bead, import picture
