# Text fit checks

Part of the testing docs; the rules every test follows are in [`docs/testing.md`](../testing.md).

Text that pokes out of its box, is cut off, or a Tooltip that is cut off, in any of the seven languages, is caught by tests that sit beside the code and run with the other tests (ticket 552). There is no whole-app run for it.

1. **Label widths** (`src/i18n/labelFit.test.ts`, plain Node): `textWidth` (`src/testUtils/textWidth.ts`) sets a string in the app's bundled fonts, reading the faces and unicode ranges from `src/styles/fonts.css`, and the test checks every key of a declared group against the room its place gives it, in all seven languages (so far What's inside's tab names, tickets 0797, 0825; each budget names the CSS its room comes from and the layout tier, and a failure names the key, language, tier, width needed and width allowed). Known overruns are in its `PENDING`, each deleted by the ticket that fixes it; one that fits again fails until removed. Chinese has no bundled font, so its glyphs count as one em and the result is an estimate. To cover a place, add a `Budget`. A language added to the app fails the type check until it is listed. The same file reports a group of translations that no mounted component reads and that no budget covers (a text with no declared room): mount the component that shows it, or list the group in `CHECKED_ELSEWHERE` with a reason.
2. **Component fit** (`*.browser.test.ts`, the `browser` project): `src/components/componentFit.browser.test.ts` (the fixed-size controls), `panelsFit.browser.test.ts` (the strip, the Menu, Project and Frame panels, lists, forms and dialogs of their own), `statesFit.browser.test.ts` (what a person reaches by acting: the colour popover, toasts, the save-failed notice, notes, panels expanded, the convert-image box, the empty library, the Selection bar), `src/components/shell/dialogsFit.browser.test.ts` (the shell's dialogs, opened through the dialog registry, with and without a Project) and `src/homepage/homepageFit.browser.test.ts` (the Homepage, each of its sections, What's inside's fit at 1440 × 900, a tablet and a phone, and its tour's 13 rows applied in the real demo editor) mount a component at the layout's width edges (`FIT_WIDTHS`; the Homepage at nine widths) in two languages (`FIT_LOCALES`, ticket 0606): English, the shortest text, and "longest", a test-only language whose every string is the widest of its seven translations, measured in the UI font (`src/testUtils/longestText.ts`). Text runs out where it is longest, and no real language is longest everywhere (Russian is for about a quarter of the strings), so one "longest" mount shows what any language would. It is shown in place of Russian, so its counts and numbers follow Russian's rules. What it cannot show is a layout fact other than width, such as a script's glyph height (`ProjectHead.browser.test.ts` tries every real language for that). One mount per language is resized through the widths (`problemsAcrossWidths`), so a failure names the width; the Homepage is mounted afresh at each width, as What's inside changes with it. A case's `act` (an expanded panel, an opened list) runs once, at the language's widest width, and the page is then narrowed with that state kept They fail on text that pokes out of its box or the screen, is cut by an ellipsis, or wraps in a control meant to be one line (`textFitInPage.ts`; user-typed Project names and the canvas's own backdrop and rulers are skipped). To add a component, add a case to a `CASES` list (`FitCase` in `src/testUtils/browserFit.ts`; its fourth item is what a person does to it first, such as expanding a panel). Text that does not fit yet goes in `src/testUtils/fitPending.ts` with the ticket that fixes it; a stale entry fails. `mountAt` sets the language on the shared preference (the app reads it from storage once), and `browserFit.browser.test.ts` guards that every mount really is in the language asked for, that "longest" holds the widest wordings, and that Russian is itself again after it. The Frame panel is also mounted at 390px in its own width (the phone's dropdown less its gutters and padding) in every real language (`FramePanel on a phone`, ticket 0864): an edge name with no room leaves its cell for its glyph's Tooltip, so a language's own words decide what shows, which "longest" would not.
3. **Tooltips**: every case of `describeFits` also opens each Tooltip it finds (`.app-tooltip`, no list of triggers) at 320 px, in English by hover, keyboard focus and a touch long press and in "longest" by hover alone, as whether a Tooltip opens does not depend on the language (`tooltipProblems`, `waysIn`), and checks the bubble against the screen, every ancestor that clips it and its own box (`measureOpenBubble`, `src/testUtils/hoverInPage.ts`). A failure names the trigger, its Tooltip text, the pixels cut and the way it was opened; known ones are in `fitPending.ts` under "<case> Tooltips". `src/components/ui/tooltips.browser.test.ts` tries the registry actions' Tooltips on their own (ticket 0607). They are all one component whose bubble is capped at a maximum width, so what can differ is only the shape of what it holds and how much text: the name alone, the name with its key chip, with a body line, off with the reason in place of the body, and placed above the trigger (the others open below it). Each shape is tried at the left and right edges in both languages, on the action whose Tooltip has the widest line in that language, measured in the UI font when the test runs, so the choice follows the copy; a shape no action has any more fails. Whether a Tooltip opens depends on neither the action nor the language, so hover, keyboard focus and a touch long press are each tried once, on one action, in English, and only the hover test waits for the hover delay (the others open by focus). Known limit: an action that is neither the widest of its shape nor in a mounted component is not opened on its own; as the bubble's width is capped and the screen's edges are what cut it, its text is not expected to fail where a wider one passes.
4. **Guards** (`src/testUtils/hoverSource.test.ts`, plain Node, they read the templates): no native `title` and no hand-made tooltip that is not listed in `src/testUtils/hoverExemptions.ts` (one way to make hover text); and every component whose template shows a translated text or makes a Tooltip is mounted by a browser fit test, or used in the template of one that is (`exercisedComponents`), or listed in `NOT_MOUNTED` with a reason. A new component with text and no case fails the guard.

Known limit: no check sees the assembled app. A bug that only shows in the combined layout (a panel clipping a Tooltip, two parts competing for room) is caught only where a component that holds the clipping ancestor is mounted. Tooltips in a state a case does not reach are not opened: give the case an action that reaches it.
